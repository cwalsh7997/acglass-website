A Key Commercial Market in
Broward County.

Plantation is central Broward — Class-A corporate office along University Drive, the Westfield Broward mall, and dense multifamily. Broward HVHZ, Miami-Dade NOA required.

Plantation falls within the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) — every opening requires Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) documentation and full Florida product approvals for all glazing systems. ACG's team handles HVHZ work every week — Miami-Dade NOA packages, Florida product approvals, engineer-of-record coordination built to pass Broward County plan review without revisions.
