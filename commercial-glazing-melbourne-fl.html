Common Questions

Melbourne Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG have Melbourne project experience? +

Yes. ACG completed Hardy World 4.0 in Melbourne - a commercial retail project requiring impact storefront glazing. We have direct experience with Brevard County building department requirements and can mobilize quickly for Melbourne-area projects.

Does Melbourne require wind-rated commercial glazing? +

Yes. Melbourne is on Florida's Atlantic coast, and Florida Building Code requires wind-resistant glazing systems meeting Brevard County's coastal exposure requirements. ACG ensures every system meets FBC wind design requirements for the project's wind zone and occupancy category.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Melbourne? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, Slimpact, TGP fire-rated glass, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. System selection is driven by the project spec, wind zone, and design intent.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for a Melbourne project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope - system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown - within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions.
