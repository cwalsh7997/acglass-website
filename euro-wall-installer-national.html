Code Framing - Florida HVHZ

HVHZ installation: what changes in Miami-Dade and Broward.

Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) - Miami-Dade and Broward Counties - imposes some of the most demanding fenestration requirements in the United States. Every Euro-Wall product installed in the HVHZ must carry a Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) or a Florida Product Approval (FPA) covering large-missile impact per ASTM E1996 and cyclic pressure testing per ASTM E1886. ACG manages the documentation chain from submittal to inspection.

Impact Glass Specification

Laminated safety glazing per ASTM E1996 large-missile criteria. PVB or SGP interlayer. Tempered outer lite typical. ACG coordinates glass specification with Euro-Wall and the project structural engineer of record.

Frame Reinforcement

HVHZ installations require reinforced Euro-Wall frame profiles - heavier-wall extrusions, closer anchor spacing, and sill track anchored to a structural substrate meeting the NOA installation requirements.

Third-Party Testing

Miami-Dade product approval requires testing by an approved third-party laboratory. ACG works exclusively with Euro-Wall's NOA-carrying product configurations and does not field-engineer deviations that would void the NOA.

Inspection Protocol

HVHZ installations require special inspector observation at frame anchor installation and pre-close rough-in. ACG schedules and coordinates with the permit-issuing authority as part of standard project management.

Code Framing - Tennessee

Tennessee non-HVHZ: thermal performance over impact.

Tennessee operates under IBC 2018 with Tennessee amendments and IECC Climate Zone 4A. Without HVHZ wind-driven-rain requirements, the performance priority shifts to thermal control. ACG's Tennessee supply and consulting (furnishing materials and consulting) will service Middle Tennessee projects requiring Euro-Wall systems with:

  • Thermally broken aluminum frames (required for IECC CZ 4A compliance)
  • Low-E insulating glass units (IGUs) - dual or triple pane per energy model
  • ASCE 7-16 design wind speed 115 mph basic wind - structural anchor design accordingly
  • Air and water infiltration testing per ASTM E283 / E331 - values used in energy models
  • Condensation resistance factor (CRF) documentation for Healthcare and Education occupancies

Reference: IBC 2018, Chapter 24 - Glass and Glazing · Tennessee State Fire Marshal code adoption schedule.

Installation Process

How ACG installs a Euro-Wall system.

Euro-Wall installations require precision rough-opening dimensioning, substrate verification, and hardware calibration. ACG follows a documented eight-step field process for every opening.

01

Submittal Coordination

ACG generates shop drawings from architectural plans, coordinates with the Euro-Wall factory for final system configuration, and routes submittals through the GC's project management platform (Procore or equivalent). Approval cycle typically 10-15 business days.

02

Rough Opening Verification

Field-verify rough opening dimensions and substrate plumb/level/square prior to ordering. Euro-Wall systems have narrow tolerance ranges at sill, head, and jamb. ACG absorbs this coordination rather than passing it upstream.

03

Factory Coordination & Lead Time

Euro-Wall products are manufactured to order. Typical lead time is 12-16 weeks from approved shop drawings. ACG provides a confirmed delivery window to the GC schedule so weathering-in is not held up.

04

Substrate & Sill Prep

Sill track substrate must be structurally sound, level to ±1/8" per 10 feet, and properly flashed before Euro-Wall components arrive. ACG performs or coordinates pre-installation substrate work.

05

Frame & Track Installation

Head and sill tracks installed per NOA or product approval anchor schedule. Jamb conditions sealed and flashed per Euro-Wall installation manual and the project waterproofing specification.

06

Panel Hang & Glazing

Panels hung on track hardware, glazed if not pre-glazed at factory, and hardware adjusted for smooth operation across all opening configurations (fully open, partially open, fully closed and locked).

07

Weather-Strip & Seal

All perimeter seals, interlocking panel gaskets, and sill-to-floor transitions completed per the Euro-Wall installation manual. Water test performed on finished assembly before sealant cure.

08

Commissioning & Owner Training

Hardware settings adjusted for panel travel, locking, and flush-close. Owner and property manager receive operating instructions for panel stacking, lock engagement, and routine maintenance schedule.
