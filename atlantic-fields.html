The Development

Discovery Land Company's first Florida community.

Atlantic Fields is the East Coast debut of Mike Meldman's Discovery Land Company — a 1,500-acre private golf community in Martin County's Hobe Sound, less than 30 minutes north of Palm Beach International Airport. Developed in partnership with the Becker Holding Corporation on a former citrus farm and the historic Hobe Sound polo grounds, the community contains 317 home sites built around a Tom Fazio-designed 18-hole championship course, an innovative 10-hole short course, and a Discovery Performance Center.

It shares an entrance drive with Michael Jordan's Grove XXIII and borders the unspoiled 6,000-acre Atlantic Ridge Preserve State Park. The course opened for play in late 2025. ACG has been the glazing contractor of record on three completed projects inside the gates with general contractor Proctor Construction — and additional scopes are coming.

FAQ

Atlantic Fields, answered.

Where is Atlantic Fields located?

Atlantic Fields is in Hobe Sound, Florida — Martin County, on the Treasure Coast. The community spans 1,500 acres at 2645 Southeast Bridge Road, less than 30 minutes from Palm Beach International Airport. It shares an entrance drive with Michael Jordan's Grove XXIII and borders the 6,000-acre Atlantic Ridge Preserve State Park.

Who is the developer of Atlantic Fields?

Atlantic Fields is being developed by Discovery Land Company in partnership with the Becker Holding Corporation. It is Discovery Land Company's first community on Florida's east coast and the East Coast debut of the brand. Mike Meldman is the chairman and founder of Discovery Land Company.

Who designed the golf course?

The 18-hole championship course at Atlantic Fields was designed by Tom Fazio, one of the most awarded golf-course architects in the world. The 10-hole short course features more than 120 routing options and 37 tee boxes. The championship course opened for play in late 2025.

Who is the glazing contractor at Atlantic Fields?

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is the glazing contractor of record on the projects ACG has executed at Atlantic Fields, working under general contractor Proctor Construction. ACG has completed three buildings inside the community to date — the Sales Center, the Golf House, and the Discovery Performance Center — and is bidding additional scopes.

How many homes are at Atlantic Fields?

Atlantic Fields will have 317 single-family custom-estate home sites on its 1,500-acre footprint. Lots start at $6 million per the developer; homes are custom-designed by individual architects and homebuilders selected by each owner. Discovery Land has indicated lot sales have been strong.

What amenities does Atlantic Fields have?

Atlantic Fields offers a Tom Fazio championship course, a 10-hole short course, the Discovery Performance Center for golf and wellness, an equestrian center on the historic Hobe Sound polo grounds, a Lake Club, a Family Park with kids' club and waterpark, tennis/pickleball/padel courts, an organic farm with farm-to-table dining, and Discovery's signature Outdoor Pursuits program.

When did the Tom Fazio course open?

The 18-hole championship course at Atlantic Fields debuted in late 2025. The 10-hole short course opened earlier as part of the property's first phase of golf operations.
