Frequently asked questions

What is ACG's primary service area?

Florida statewide. Three offices - West Palm Beach (HQ), Naples, Tampa.

Do you serve my city?

If your project is in Florida, almost certainly yes. ACG covers 80+ cities across 14 counties and four regions. If your city isn't listed by name, send drawings - the list is a guide, not an exhaustive registry.

What's ACG's travel radius?

Within Florida, no practical limit. No project site is more than two hours from a named PM and field crew between the three offices.

Do you take projects outside Florida?

Yes, selectively. Coastal Alabama, Georgia, Carolinas, Caribbean - project-specific, through GC partner relationships. ACG holds Florida CGC #1531993 only.

How do I find out if ACG has worked in my city before?

Each named city above links to a dedicated page with example projects, AHJ notes, and ACG's coverage approach. For cities without a dedicated page, project references for the surrounding county are available during prequalification.

Do you do residential work in these areas?

No. ACG is commercial only - Division 08 commercial new construction and commercial renovation. Owner-direct restaurant glazing and multifamily projects qualify. Residential glass repair, single-family window replacement, and shower-door installation are not part of ACG's scope.

Your city's on the list. Or it's a 90-minute drive from one of ours. Either way - send the drawings.
