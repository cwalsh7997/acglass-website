iFLY Miami · Allapattah / River District
The project

Two scopes. One of them flies.

iFLY Miami is Miami's first indoor skydiving facility — a 9,569 SF destination at 1501 NW North River Dr in the Allapattah / River District, opened in March 2026 with general contractor Parkway Construction.

The flight experience depends entirely on a precisely engineered wind tunnel: a cylindrical glass chamber generating 150+ mph vertical airflow. The glass is not decorative — it is load-bearing, pressure-rated, and the primary surface through which spectators watch every flight.

ACG delivered both work packages: the exterior commercial glazing envelope — storefront with entry doors and curtainwall sections, impact-rated for Miami-Dade's High Velocity Hurricane Zone — and the specialty cylindrical wind tunnel glass installation, one of the most technically exacting specialty glass installs ACG has executed.

Scope notes

What wind tunnel glass has to do.

Pressure-rated for dynamic load

Not the one-time hit of an impact test — sustained positive pressure from high-velocity air recirculating at operational speeds, all day.

Curved to precise tolerances

Precision-curved panels forming a true cylinder, typically 12–14 feet in diameter, with minimal aerodynamic disruption at the joints — tolerances measured in millimeters.

Structurally glazed and sealed

A continuous seal system that holds under the thermal cycling and vibration of industrial-scale airflow equipment. Any gap, deflection or seal failure affects structural integrity and flight performance.

Optically clear, 360°

The spectator experience is the commercial model. No distortion, no visual obstruction from framing, no fogging under the tunnel's temperature differentials.

The two scopes could not be installed independently — the flight chamber and the exterior storefront share structural steel. ACG coordinated with Parkway Construction and the tunnel equipment manufacturer to sequence the glazing around the mechanical rough-in, managing two technically distinct packages under a single subcontract without disrupting the critical path.
