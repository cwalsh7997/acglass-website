Tradewinds Hobe Sound

The clubhouse storefront package for a new multifamily community in coastal Martin County - front-of-house entries, garden-room glazing, and window openings in ESWindows impact-rated systems.

Location Hobe Sound, FL Sector Multifamily Scope Clubhouse storefront Systems ESWindows

Sheet A-101 · Project

The clubhouse is the centerpiece. The glass defines it.

Tradewinds is a new multifamily community in Hobe Sound - a coastal Martin County submarket between Stuart and Jupiter. Three-story residential buildings surround a standalone amenity campus anchored by a clubhouse, pool, and pavilion structures.

The clubhouse is the visual centerpiece of the property. Double-height ceilings, a wrap of floor-to-ceiling glass on the garden-room side, and a pair of frameless all-glass storefront entries define the amenity experience residents see every day.

ACG delivered the clubhouse storefront package, partnering with ESWindows on the glazing products - the front-of-house storefront entries, the garden-room glazing, and the clubhouse window openings, specified to match the clean, modern aesthetic the developer set for the amenity buildings.

Sheet A-301 · Scope notes

Coastal glazing, sequenced for TCO.

Coastal wind zone

Hobe Sound falls within Martin County’s coastal wind zone. ESWindows impact-rated systems carry the Florida Building Code approvals and NOA documentation this exposure requires.

Built for salt air

Product finish and framing details were specified to hold up against coastal salt air over the life of the building - not just through the warranty period.

Frameless entries

A pair of frameless all-glass storefront entries sets the front-of-house arrival, matched to the double-height garden-room glazing beyond.

Sequenced for TCO

Amenity packages live or die on schedule coordination - the clubhouse certifies as the project nears lease-up, with every trade compressed into the final weeks. ACG sequenced shop drawings, material staging, and install crews to land the scope without holding up TCO.
