Common Questions

Pompano Beach Glazing FAQ.

Is Pompano Beach in the HVHZ? +

Yes. Pompano Beach is within Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), which requires all glazing systems to carry Miami-Dade NOA documentation or Florida Product Approval. ACG installs only HVHZ-compliant systems and manages the full submittal package.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Pompano Beach? +

ACG installs ESWindows, ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, Slimpact, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. Every system carries the required Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval for Broward County projects.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for Pompano Beach? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and competitive pricing — within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions.

Does ACG handle Broward County permits and submittals? +

Yes. ACG manages the full submittal package — shop drawings, Florida product approvals, NOA documentation, and engineer-of-record coordination. Our packages are built to pass Broward County plan review the first time.
