FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of interior glass partitions does ACG install?

ACG installs aluminum-framed glass partitions, frameless glass walls, demountable systems, sliding glass barn doors, and fire-rated interior glass assemblies. Systems are available in clear, frosted, tinted, and decorative glass options.

Can interior glass partitions meet acoustic requirements?

Yes. ACG specifies and installs interior glass systems rated for STC 35-50 depending on the system configuration and glass type. Laminated glass and specialized gaskets significantly improve acoustic performance.

Does ACG install interior glass in existing buildings?

Yes. ACG frequently installs interior glass partitions in tenant improvement and renovation projects. Demountable systems are particularly well-suited for existing buildings as they minimize disruption to adjacent spaces.
