Interior Glass Systems

Office Fronts and Partitions
That Close Out Clean.

Interior glass partition systems live at the intersection of finish work and technical installation — they need to be perfectly plumb and level, they need to transition cleanly to ceiling grid systems and flooring, and they need to look pristine on occupancy day.

ACG installs frameless and framed partition systems from floor to ceiling or slab to slab. Fully framed systems for acoustic performance. Partially framed or frameless systems for clean, contemporary aesthetics. Switchable privacy glass for conference rooms and executive offices. Sliding glass panels for space flexibility.

We coordinate with your GC's interior finishing trades — ceiling grid contractors, flooring installers, and drywall contractors — to ensure clean transitions at every interface. Our installations are measured, cut, and installed with the precision that finish work demands.

Acoustic Systems

High-Performance Acoustic Partitions

STC-rated glass partition systems for private offices and conference rooms.

Standard glass partitions don't provide meaningful sound attenuation — you can have a glass office front that looks open and modern while still achieving STC 45+ acoustic performance. ACG installs laminated acoustic glass in framed systems with acoustic gaskets and edge details that eliminate sound flanking paths.

Frameless Systems

Frameless Glass Partition Systems

Minimal hardware, maximum glass for contemporary commercial interiors.

Frameless glass partition systems achieve the cleanest possible aesthetic — glass secured with minimal point-fixed hardware or proprietary channel systems rather than visible aluminum framing. ACG installs frameless systems with precision tolerances and hardware quality that maintains the clean look over years of use.
