The Resort

Seven private acres
on the Atlantic.

Eau Palm Beach is one of South Florida's most iconic oceanfront properties - seven private acres of Atlantic beachfront in Manalapan, formerly the Ritz-Carlton Palm Beach. It rebranded in 2013 and has held a Forbes Five-Star rating for more than a decade, one of the rarest distinctions in American hospitality.

The resort encompasses 309 guestrooms and suites, a 42,000-square-foot spa, multiple dining outlets including the signature Polpo restaurant, and a member place in Preferred Hotels & Resorts' Legend Collection - reserved for the highest echelon of independent luxury. The guest who stays at Eau notices thread count. They notice lighting. They notice glass.

The Assignment

A design-build
by ownership.

Eau's renovation wasn't delivered through a general contractor. Ownership and resort management ran the project themselves - a design-build program that put ACG in the room with the principals who cared about the outcome most, working straight to their standard, on their timing, without a layer in between.

Seven signature areas. Seven scopes. Each with its own design intent, its own timeline, and its own standard of execution. The phases ranged from statement pieces visible from the beach to the precise details that only professionals notice. All of it had to be built inside an occupied, operating Five-Star resort - around dinner service at Polpo, around spa appointments, around guests checking in.

The photo of Connor Walsh at the resort's new front doors, with the Eau monogram mat at his feet, captures the arrangement. Not a sub-sub. A trusted partner, owner-direct, on seven private acres of the Atlantic.

The Standard

What Five-Star means
for glass.

A Forbes Five-Star rating isn't awarded - it's earned, and it's re-evaluated every year. The criteria cover hundreds of touch points: the response time on a guest request, the temperature of the towels, the condition of every surface in every space.

Glass is everywhere in a resort. It's the lobby entry. It's the restaurant wall. It's the spa window. It's the view from the guestroom. At this standard, glazing isn't infrastructure - it's design. Every frame aligned. Every seal invisible. Every pane crystal clear, without distortion or imperfection.

Guests at this level notice glass the way they notice thread count. They can't always name what they're seeing, but they feel it when it's right - and they definitely notice when it isn't.

Field Notes

Eau Palm Beach,
answered.

Where is Eau Palm Beach Resort & Spa?

Eau Palm Beach is located at 100 S Ocean Blvd, Manalapan, Florida 33462 - seven private acres of Atlantic Ocean beachfront in Palm Beach County. The resort is approximately 15 minutes south of Palm Beach International Airport and minutes from the Town of Palm Beach.

What was ACG's scope on the renovation?

ACG executed a seven-phase glazing renovation across the resort: the new custom front entrance doors, Polpo restaurant's bi-fold ocean wall, Polpo's arched interior windows, the Kids Club and ice cream shop floor-to-ceiling glazing, the Eau Spa additions, the updated rear facade entrances, and the overall sequencing discipline that kept the resort operating through every phase.

Was there a general contractor on the project?

No. The renovation was delivered as an owner-led design-build. ACG worked directly with resort ownership and management - no GC layer - on each of the seven phases.

What glazing systems were used?

The Polpo bi-fold ocean wall is a Euro-Wall system. Other phases use impact-rated storefront and curtainwall with custom framing to accommodate the resort's arched openings and decorative-glass entrance doors.

How did ACG work inside an operating Five-Star resort?

The work was sequenced around resort operations - around Polpo's dinner service, around Eau Spa appointments, around guest arrivals. Cranes and heavy work were scheduled before guest hours; daily punch-list closure happened before turndown. The resort never lost a reservation or a star during any phase of the renovation.

Is Eau Palm Beach still Forbes Five-Star?

Yes. Eau Palm Beach has held a Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star rating for more than a decade and is a member of Preferred Hotels & Resorts' Legend Collection, reserved for the highest echelon of independent luxury properties.

Can ACG take on similar luxury resort glazing work?

Yes. Eau Palm Beach is ACG's reference project for Forbes Five-Star hospitality glazing. We've since delivered for additional luxury hotels, oceanfront clubs, and private communities across Florida.
