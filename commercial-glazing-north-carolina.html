North Carolina Market

North Carolina's
Construction Boom.

North Carolina has become one of the most active commercial construction markets in the Southeast, driven by three powerful forces: Charlotte's financial and corporate sector growth, the Research Triangle's technology and life sciences explosion, and sustained population growth generating commercial and healthcare development statewide.

Charlotte's Midtown and South End construction boom is producing some of the Carolinas' most complex glazing scopes. Bank of America's ongoing campus expansion, Truist Financial's headquarter build-out, and the wave of mixed-use towers rising in South End are generating sustained demand for curtainwall, window wall, and commercial storefront. Charlotte's Uptown corridor is one of the fastest-growing high-rise markets in the Southeast — and ACG is positioned to deliver the full glazing scope on Charlotte's most demanding tower projects.

Research Triangle Park represents one of the most significant technology and life sciences construction programs in the country. Apple's $1 billion North Carolina campus in Research Triangle Park, Google's expanding Durham and RTP presence, Amazon's East Coast operations build-out, and Meta's data center expansions are generating enormous glazing demand. The Raleigh downtown mixed-use explosion and Durham's American Tobacco District and downtown redevelopment add hospitality, retail, and office glazing scopes to the RTP market.

North Carolina's healthcare construction market is one of the most active in the Southeast. Duke Health's ongoing campus expansion, UNC Health's new hospital and clinic build-out, WakeMed's capital program, and Atrium Health's Charlotte market growth are generating hundreds of millions of dollars in annual healthcare glazing work. Healthcare glazing requires specialized expertise — fire-rated assemblies, infection control glass, and clinical entrance systems engineered for high-traffic hospital environments.

Wilmington and coastal North Carolina require impact-rated glazing systems due to their hurricane wind exposure. The NC Building Code enforces ASCE 7 wind load requirements in coastal jurisdictions, and Wilmington's AHJ demands complete product approval and submittal documentation matching Florida-equivalent coastal standards. ACG's Florida coastal expertise transfers directly to Wilmington and Brunswick County commercial projects.

Greensboro, Winston-Salem, and the Triad region have seen consistent healthcare, logistics, and light industrial commercial construction. Asheville's mountain market has grown rapidly with hospitality and mixed-use development. ACG serves North Carolina from its Southeast network — AI-managed coordination enabling efficient project delivery statewide.

Common Questions

North Carolina Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve North Carolina commercial construction? +

Yes. ACG is a national commercial glazing contractor serving North Carolina through its Southeast network. Our AI-managed operations platform enables full project coordination for Charlotte, Raleigh-Durham, Wilmington, and statewide scopes. We follow our GC partners wherever they build.

Does coastal NC require impact-rated glazing systems? +

Yes. Wilmington and coastal North Carolina are in high-wind exposure zones requiring impact-rated or tested-and-labeled glazing assemblies. The NC Building Code enforces ASCE 7 wind load requirements in coastal jurisdictions, and Wilmington's AHJ demands complete product approval and submittal documentation. ACG's Florida coastal expertise transfers directly to these requirements.

Can ACG handle Charlotte Uptown high-rise curtainwall? +

Yes. ACG has experience with slab-to-slab curtainwall systems used in Charlotte's Uptown and South End high-rise corridor. Our AI-managed scheduling platform sequences curtainwall to match multi-story construction schedules, and our submittal teams manage structural engineering coordination for high-rise glazing. Charlotte tower projects welcome.

What glazing systems does ACG install on NC projects? +

ACG installs YKK AP, Kawneer, Oldcastle BuildingEnvelope, Wausau Window, ESWindows, CGI Windows, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. Coastal NC projects are specified with impact-rated assemblies per local wind zone and AHJ requirements. System selection is driven by the project spec and architect's design intent.

How quickly can ACG turn around a scope for a NC project? +

Send us your drawings and ACG returns a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and competitive pricing — within 48 hours. Charlotte, Raleigh, Wilmington, or any North Carolina location: same 48-hour turnaround commitment.
