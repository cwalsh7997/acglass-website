Euro-Wall. Installer specs.

ACG installs Euro-Wall on Florida commercial work. Installer and specifier language only. Not an authorized-dealer listing. The official locator does not list American Commercial Glass.

Written by Connor Walsh, President. Manufacturer HTML fetched 2026-09-03 from euro-wall.com.

Relationship

What ACG is, and is not.

Products

facts.html lists Euro-Wall as installer. ACG is not A-Christian Glass (ACG) in Delray Beach. That shop is the one the official Euro-Wall locator currently lists. Do not treat this page as proof of a dealer listing.

Manufacturer HQ on euro-wall.com: 2200 Murphy Court, North Port, FL 34289. Phone 888.989.EURO (3876). Email info@euro-wall.com. Products page states the line is crafted in the USA. Commercial page names offices, retail, restaurants, and hotels as applications.

Numeric design pressures, NOA numbers, Florida Product Approval numbers, and lead times are omitted. Manufacturer HTML shows Design Pressure Charts and a Florida Product Approval resource link, but no numbers in the fetched HTML. Existing ACG Euro-Wall pages publish DP tables and a 14 to 20 week lead-time range. Those figures are not copied here.

Systems on euro-wall.com

What the manufacturer HTML states.

  • Vista Multi Slide

    Impact and non-impact sliding door system. Interlock less than 1 inch. Up to 8 panels in a single stack direction. Performance risers on the page: Vista RF (riser free), Vista HT (High Tide 5/8 inch), Vista WM (Weather Max 1 inch). Center-meet astragal described as galvanized-steel reinforced. Standard finishes: Kynar liquid paint or powder coat. Warranty: 10 years from date of purchase, manufacturing defects. Impact tests listed: ASTM E283-04, ASTM E331-00, TAS 202-94, TAS 201-94, TAS 203-94, AAMA 1303-05.

    Source: euro-wall.com Vista Multi Slide (fetched 2026-09-03)

  • Vista Fold

    Impact and non-impact folding door system. Panels 14 ft tall and 52 in wide available. Configurations listed: straight, segmented, radius, split heights, and 90-degree postless corner units. Standard finishes: Kynar liquid paint or powder coat. Warranty: 10 years from date of purchase, manufacturing defects. Impact tests listed: ASTM E283-04, ASTM E331-00, TAS 202-94, TAS 201-94, TAS 203-94, ASTM F842-14.

    Source: euro-wall.com Vista Fold (fetched 2026-09-03)

  • Vista Pivot

    Impact and non-impact pivot door. Maximum 168 in high by 100 in wide. Matching impact-rated transom and sidelight units listed. Standard finish coating meets AAMA 2605. Powder coat or Kynar finishes available. Warranty: 10 years from date of purchase, manufacturing defects. Impact tests listed: ASTM E283-04, ASTM E331-00, TAS 202-94, TAS 201-94, TAS 203-94, AAMA 1304-18.

    Source: euro-wall.com Vista Pivot (fetched 2026-09-03)

  • Vista DS (direct set)

    Impact and non-impact fixed window system. Profile stated as 1-1/4 inch. Panels up to 14 ft tall and up to 9 ft wide. Multiple panels connect with a 1-1/4 inch meeting rail. Kynar liquid paint or powder coat. Warranty: 10 years from date of purchase, manufacturing defects. Impact tests listed: ASTM E283-29, ASTM E331-00(23), TAS 202-94, ASTM E330-14(21), ASTM F588-17(R23), TAS 201-94, TAS 203-94.

    Source: euro-wall.com Vista DS (fetched 2026-09-03)

  • Other commercial names on the manufacturer commercial page

    Also listed, without extra sizes in the fetched HTML: thermally broken impact and non-impact folding door; impact-rated thin-line window; thermally broken impact and non-impact casement window; impact and non-impact fixed window; multi-directional folding door. Dedicated pages for those SKUs were not fetched in this pass.

    Source: euro-wall.com commercial products (fetched 2026-09-03)
