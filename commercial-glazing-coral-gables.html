Coral Gables Market

The City Beautiful's
Most Demanding Glazing Market.

Coral Gables is Miami-Dade County's most architecturally distinctive commercial market. The City Beautiful's design review board sets aesthetic standards that exceed code compliance — glazing systems here must be visually precise, not just structurally adequate. Miracle Mile commercial corridor, the Ponce de Leon Blvd office corridor, Alhambra mixed-use, the University of Miami commercial edge, the Merrick Park luxury retail corridor, and the ongoing office tower construction in the core downtown Coral Gables area all fall within this jurisdiction.

HVHZ applies throughout. Every glazing system in Coral Gables must carry a valid Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance. But unlike other Miami-Dade markets, the technical compliance is just the starting point. Design-forward projects here demand glazing subs who can coordinate with architects on custom finish requirements, profile configurations, and visual details — while still delivering the NOA-compliant, submittal-ready packages that Miami-Dade County plan review requires.

ACG operates at this intersection of design precision and technical compliance. We work with the full ACG manufacturer roster — all with extensive architectural finish and color programs — and we manage the coordination between the architect's design intent and the County's code requirements from the earliest stages of submittal.

Common Questions

Coral Gables Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG have experience navigating Coral Gables' design review requirements for glazing? +

Yes. ACG understands that Coral Gables is not a standard Miami-Dade County jurisdiction — the City Beautiful has its own architectural and design review board requirements that go well beyond code compliance. We coordinate with the project architect on glazing aesthetics, finish specifications, and visual compatibility before finalizing system selection, ensuring NOA-compliant systems that also satisfy Coral Gables' design review process.

Can ACG specify systems that satisfy both Coral Gables' design standards and HVHZ requirements? +

Yes. ACG specifies NOA-approved systems from manufacturers who offer architectural finish options, custom color programs, and profile configurations that satisfy Coral Gables' aesthetic requirements while meeting full HVHZ code compliance. This is a coordination challenge that we navigate routinely — design precision and code compliance are not a trade-off we ask GCs to make.

Which ACG office serves Coral Gables commercial glazing projects? +

ACG's West Palm Beach office serves Coral Gables and the full Miami-Dade commercial market. We're active on projects along Miracle Mile, Ponce de Leon Blvd, and the Alhambra corridor, with established supply relationships and inspector familiarity in Miami-Dade County.

Does ACG carry HVHZ certification for Coral Gables commercial glazing projects? +

Yes. Every glazing project in Coral Gables falls within Miami-Dade County's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. ACG is HVHZ-certified and installs only NOA-approved systems in Miami-Dade County. Our certifications and documentation meet Miami-Dade County plan review requirements on the first submission — no revision cycles on the HVHZ compliance side.

How does ACG manage the submittal process for Coral Gables commercial glazing projects? +

ACG manages the complete submittal package: shop drawings, Miami-Dade NOA documentation, Florida product approvals, architectural finish specifications, and engineer-of-record coordination. We build our packages to satisfy both Miami-Dade County plan review and Coral Gables' architectural review process — so your glazing scope doesn't become a revision loop on either front.
