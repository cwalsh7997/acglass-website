The Market

Pelican Bay is a commercial glazing market.

Pelican Bay is one of Florida's most distinguished luxury master-planned communities, covering more than 2,000 acres of Gulf-front and near-Gulf Naples real estate. The commercial footprint includes Waterside Shops (one of Florida's premier luxury retail centers), The Ritz-Carlton Naples, multiple Class A luxury multifamily towers (Bay Colony, The Crescent, Marbella, Breakwater, Bay Laurel), and an extensive amenity network of beach clubs, tennis centers, and fitness facilities.

Pelican Bay sits in Exposure Category C for most of its footprint, with Exposure D along Gulf-facing elevations at Bay Colony, The Ritz-Carlton, and the beach dune walkovers. Every commercial and multifamily permit requires Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance with Florida Product Approval documentation. Post-Hurricane Ian (2022) and Hurricane Milton (2024), the hardening and rebuild cycle has emphasized full impact glazing across the community's commercial and amenity buildings.

ACG's Naples office serves Pelican Bay with the commercial subcontractor infrastructure this caliber of community requires. Our ESWindows flagship partnership delivers Exposure D-capable storefront, window wall, and sliding door systems with the architectural-grade finishes Pelican Bay's architects and owners specify. For the hospitality and amenity spaces where operable walls define the lifestyle — The Ritz-Carlton cabanas, beach clubs, restaurant patios — we install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems.

Pelican Bay FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Does Pelican Bay require impact windows on commercial and multifamily buildings?

Yes. Pelican Bay sits in Collier County's wind-borne debris region. Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening. The Pelican Bay Foundation and Collier County both enforce full FBC compliance on all new construction and major renovations.

What exposure category applies to Pelican Bay commercial projects?

Most of Pelican Bay is Exposure Category C. Bay Colony, The Ritz-Carlton, Gulf-facing condominium towers, and the beach dune areas are Exposure D. Florida Product Approval documentation must match the correct exposure. DP ratings from DP40 inland to DP80+ Gulf-facing.

How much do Pelican Bay commercial impact windows cost?

Pelican Bay commercial impact window pricing runs $75-$180+ per square foot installed, depending on system type, finish specification, and exposure. Luxury multifamily window wall $85-$155/SF. Waterside Shops retail storefront $80-$130/SF. Euro-Wall multi-slide on private club and resort amenity: $20,000-$30,000+ per opening.

What products does ACG install at Pelican Bay?

ESWindows 8000 storefront, 9000 window wall (up to DP80+ for Gulf-facing), SGD-2020 impact sliding doors, and Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems. Architectural-grade anodized aluminum and PVDF powder-coat finishes standard on Pelican Bay projects. Full Florida Product Approval documentation with every specification.

Does ACG have Naples-area office and experience?

Yes. ACG operates a Naples office serving Pelican Bay, Old Naples, 5th Avenue South, Mercato, Marco Island, and all Collier and Lee Counties. Our commercial glazing focus — zero residential — matches Pelican Bay's spec expectations. Referenced projects include Wild Blue Clubhouse (Naples) and Atlantic Fields Performance Center (Hobe Sound).
