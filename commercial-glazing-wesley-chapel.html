Wesley Chapel Market

Pasco County's Commercial Boom
Needs a Glazing Sub That Shows Up.

Wesley Chapel has become one of the most active commercial construction markets in the entire Southeast. The SR-54 and SR-56 corridors have seen relentless retail and mixed-use development. The Wiregrass Ranch area continues to add medical, office, and large-format retail. KRATE at the Grove has driven ancillary commercial development throughout the surrounding district. And the medical corridor anchored by AdventHealth Wesley Chapel and BayCare has generated a steady pipeline of medical office, MOB, and outpatient facility construction that shows no signs of slowing.

ACG's Tampa office — approximately 30 minutes from Wesley Chapel's commercial core — serves Pasco County GCs with local crew availability, established supply chain relationships, and the operational infrastructure to handle multiple concurrent projects. We're not a one-project-at-a-time sub. We maintain capacity across the Tampa Bay region, which means when you need a glazing sub for your next Wesley Chapel bid, we're ready to price and ready to perform.

Pasco County's Building and Construction Services department runs a permit and inspection process that rewards contractors who show up prepared. ACG submits complete shop drawing packages, Florida Product Approval (NOA) documentation, and engineering calculations at first submission — reducing review cycles that cost your schedule. We know what the county inspectors are looking for, and we build our submittals accordingly.

Common Questions

Do you pull permits in Pasco County for commercial glazing work?

Yes. ACG is a licensed glazing contractor in Florida and handles the full permit application process with Pasco County's Building and Construction Services department. We submit shop drawings, product approval documentation (NOA), and all required calculations to get through plan review without revision cycles that slow your schedule.

How quickly can ACG mobilize for a Wesley Chapel commercial project?

Our Tampa office is approximately 30 minutes from Wesley Chapel. Once a contract is executed and the permit is approved, we can typically mobilize field crews within 1–2 weeks depending on material lead times. Our scheduling means your superintendent gets real-time crew and delivery updates — not a phone tag game.

What types of commercial projects does ACG typically glaze in Wesley Chapel?

Wesley Chapel's commercial growth is diverse. We work across retail storefronts and strip centers along SR-54 and SR-56, medical office buildings and hospital-adjacent facilities, mixed-use and multi-family podium projects in Wiregrass and KRATE-area developments, and big-box retail. We bring the same process rigor whether it's a 3,000 SF inline tenant or a full curtainwall package on a mid-rise.
