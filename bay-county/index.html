Bay County commercial glazing market.

Every commercial structure built since 2019 is current FBC code, post-Michael standard, 140 mph design wind, full WBDR impact. Bay County permitting and code notes.

  • Bay County Builders Services permits unincorporated areas.
  • City of Panama City, City of Panama City Beach, and City of Mexico Beach have their own building departments.
  • Coastal Bay County is full WBDR — 140 mph design wind on the mainland coast, 140–150 mph open-coast on Panama City Beach.
  • Post-Hurricane Michael (Oct 2018), inspector scrutiny is unusually high — bid letters need NOA references and anchor calc summaries.
