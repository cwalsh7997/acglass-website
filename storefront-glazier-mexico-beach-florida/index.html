Mexico Beach commercial glazing questions, answered.

Was Mexico Beach really destroyed by Michael?

Yes. Hurricane Michael made landfall just east of Mexico Beach as a Cat 5 on October 10, 2018. Most of the town's commercial structures were destroyed or severely damaged.

Is every Mexico Beach commercial project new construction?

Effectively yes - almost everything commercial post-2018 is new build. Impact NOA and current FBC compliance is the baseline.

Lead time on Mexico Beach commercial?

10-14 weeks for impact storefront. The supply chain into rural Bay County adds 1-2 weeks vs. metro markets.

Who permits Mexico Beach?

City of Mexico Beach has its own building department. Bay County Builders Services handles unincorporated areas.

Can you handle small-restaurant reconstruction packages?

Yes. Small-restaurant impact storefront and entrance is inside our standard scope.
