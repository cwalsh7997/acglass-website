Project LIFT

A purpose-built vocational and skills training center in Hobe Sound, FL, serving the Martin County community. ACG delivered the complete ESWindows glazing package — storefront, glass bay doors, and windows — for Hooks Construction.

Location Hobe Sound, FL GC Hooks Construction Systems ESWindows storefront & glass doors Completed 2024
01
Sheet A-011 · The project

Built for the work bays.

Project LIFT is a purpose-built community facility in Hobe Sound — a vocational and skills training center designed to serve the Martin County community. The building combines open program space with office and support areas, clad in bold blue and gray metal panels with a standing-seam metal roof.

Hooks Construction managed the build and brought ACG in to handle the complete ESWindows glazing package: the commercial storefront at the main entry, the large-format glass and aluminum overhead-style door systems on the work bay openings, and the fixed commercial windows on the office section of the building envelope.

The large glass bay doors are the defining feature of this building's glazing package — wide aluminum frames, full-height glass panels, and the clean line of the opening edge. Every unit installed plumb and operating correctly at TCO. Hooks ran a tight site and ACG matched the pace.
