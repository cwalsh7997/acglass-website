About the Manufacturer

About Trulite Glass & Aluminum

Trulite Glass & Aluminum Solutions is one of the largest glass fabricators and aluminum systems distributors in North America. With manufacturing and distribution facilities across the United States, Trulite supplies commercial glass, architectural glazing products, and aluminum framing systems to glazing contractors throughout the country — including in every major Florida market.

For ACG, the Trulite partnership is primarily a supply chain relationship. Trulite's national distribution network means ACG can access a broad range of commercial glass products — from standard float and tempered glass to specialty architectural glass, insulating glass units, and laminated safety glass — with competitive lead times and pricing. When a project specification calls for a glass product or aluminum system that Trulite manufactures, ACG sources directly through this relationship.

Trulite's product range also includes aluminum storefront and window systems, providing ACG with a second-tier commercial framing option for projects where Trulite's aluminum systems match the specification and budget requirements better than other manufacturers in ACG's partner network.

Why ACG Uses Trulite

Why Trulite for
Commercial Projects

Nationwide Supply Chain Depth

Trulite's national manufacturing and distribution footprint means product is accessible from multiple distribution points. On projects where material availability or lead time is a constraint, Trulite's supply network provides options that a regionally focused manufacturer cannot match.

Broad Product Range

Trulite supplies architectural glass in virtually every configuration — tempered, laminated, insulating, spandrel, and specialty coated glass. When a project's glass specification is detailed, Trulite's fabrication capabilities can typically accommodate it without a custom order that extends lead times.

Competitive Commercial Pricing

Trulite's scale as one of North America's largest glass fabricators translates to competitive material pricing. For cost-sensitive commercial projects where glass and aluminum make up a significant portion of the glazing scope, sourcing through Trulite gives ACG the ability to sharpen its bid without sacrificing product quality.

Products ACG Specifies

Trulite Products
ACG Uses

Trulite's product range spans glass fabrication and aluminum framing systems. These are the categories ACG draws on most frequently when specifying Trulite for commercial projects in Florida.

Glass Fabrication

Commercial Tempered & Laminated Glass

Trulite fabricates tempered safety glass, laminated glass, and insulating glass units for commercial glazing applications. ACG specifies Trulite-fabricated glass in commercial storefront and curtainwall applications where the glass specification calls for a standard product that benefits from Trulite's fabrication scale and consistent quality control.

Architectural Glass

Insulating Glass Units & Specialty Coatings

Trulite manufactures insulating glass units with a range of coating and spacer options for commercial energy performance requirements. For projects where the architect specifies a particular solar control or low-emissivity coating, Trulite's fabrication capabilities cover standard and specialty options across most commercial IGU configurations.

Aluminum Systems

Commercial Storefront & Aluminum Framing

Trulite distributes commercial aluminum storefront and window framing systems. For retail, office, and light commercial projects where a Trulite aluminum system matches the specification and the project doesn't require a specialized impact or curtainwall solution, Trulite's framing products provide a reliable and cost-competitive option for ACG's commercial storefront scope.

Spandrel & Opaque Glass

Spandrel Glass & Decorative Panels

Spandrel glass is a common specification requirement on multifamily and office buildings with window wall or curtainwall systems — the opaque panels that cover floor slabs and structural elements between vision glass. Trulite fabricates spandrel glass panels in standard and custom sizes and colors, providing ACG with a reliable source for this component on larger commercial scopes.

ACG & Trulite

The ACG–Trulite
Relationship

ACG maintains Trulite as an active manufacturer partner because of the supply chain advantages Trulite's national scale provides. Trulite is one of nine manufacturer brands in ACG's partner network — each selected because their products match specific project type requirements in Florida's commercial glazing market.

When a project calls for commercial storefront or architectural glass products where Trulite's offering is the right specification fit — whether for technical, budget, or lead-time reasons — ACG sources through this relationship. The result is a direct material supply chain with the scale and reliability of one of North America's largest glass fabricators behind it.

ACG's partner network includes ESWindows, Eurowall, YKK AP, PGT Innovations, Trulite, Allegion, TGP (Technical Glass Products), Slimpact, and Vitro Glass. No single manufacturer covers every project type. ACG selects the right product for each project based on specification requirements, code compliance, lead time, and budget — Trulite is a key option in that selection process.
