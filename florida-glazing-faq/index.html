What is HVHZ-rated glass?

Glazing tested for Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone: Miami-Dade and Broward only. Must pass TAS 201/202/203.

Read full answer →

Where is HVHZ applied in Florida?

Miami-Dade and Broward Counties only. Palm Beach County is not in the HVHZ.

Read full answer →

What's the difference between storefront and curtain wall?

Storefront is single-story, span-by-span. Curtain wall is multi-story, hung from the slab edge.

Read full answer →

What's the difference between window wall and curtain wall?

Window wall sits on the floor slab. Curtain wall hangs from the slab edge. Window wall costs less.

Read full answer →

Is impact glass better than hurricane shutters?

For most upscale commercial, impact glass wins on appearance and operational simplicity. Shutters cost less but require deployment.

Read full answer →

How long does commercial glass installation take?

6-16 weeks from contract to substantial completion. Material lead time is the dominant variable.

Read full answer →

What is a Miami-Dade NOA?

Notice of Acceptance - a document issued by Miami-Dade County certifying a product passes HVHZ testing.

Read full answer →

What's the difference between Florida Product Approval and Miami-Dade NOA?

FL # is statewide approval issued by FL DBPR. NOA is issued by Miami-Dade and required in HVHZ counties.

Read full answer →

What's the difference between tempered and laminated glass?

Tempered is heat-treated for strength. Laminated is two layers bonded to an interlayer. Different code requirements.

Read full answer →

What is low-E glass and why does Florida need it?

Glass with a metallic coating that reflects heat. Required by FL Energy Code to meet SHGC ≤ 0.25 in South Florida.

What's the difference between ESWindows and ESWindows storefront?

Both are top commercial aluminum storefront brands.

What is structural silicone glazing?

A curtain wall technique where glass is bonded to aluminum with silicone, eliminating exterior pressure caps.

What is spandrel glass?

Opaque glass used in curtain walls at slab lines to conceal interior structure.

What are ADA requirements for storefront doors?

32-inch clear width, 5-pound max opening force, level landings, accessible hardware.

What is fire-rated glazing?

Glass tested to maintain integrity during a fire for 20-120 minutes. Required at rated walls and exits.

What is smart glass?

Glass that electronically controls light transmission - tinting or switching opaque on demand.

What about glass railings for FL commercial?

Need structural laminated glass, 50 lb/ft load capacity, and approved post-and-shoe systems.

Can commercial glass be repaired or replaced?

Most damage requires replacement. Glass cannot be patched or welded. Surface scratches under 1/16 inch may be polished.

How do I evaluate Florida commercial glaziers?

Six-criterion framework: license, bonding, HVHZ experience, portfolio fit, response speed, warranty terms.

How do I find a good commercial glazier in South Florida?

Verify license, check bonding, document HVHZ submittal experience, match portfolio to your project type.

What's the best glass for FL restaurant storefronts?

Laminated impact for HVHZ; low-iron for indoor-outdoor concepts; low-E for brand-quality urban restaurants.

What is blast-resistant glazing?

Heavily laminated glass for federal courthouses, military, security - ASTM F1642 + GSA Performance Conditions.

What about automatic door operators for FL commercial?

Required for most ADA compliance. Stanley, ASSA ABLOY Besam, Horton are common.

What aluminum storefront series should I spec?

ESWindows ES-9500, ESWindows ES-8000T (HVHZ standard), ES-CS1325 (heavy-duty), ES-CS1325 (high-wind). Each has different face dimensions and wind ratings.

What are FL Building Code glass requirements?

FBC 2023 governs wind, impact (HVHZ/WBDR), energy (SHGC ≤ 0.25 South FL), and ADA accessibility.

Insulated seal (10 yr), low-E coating (5-10 yr), aluminum finish (10-20 yr), installer workmanship (1-5 yr).
