Storage Rentals of America

A three-story self-storage facility in Vero Beach, FL, with a glazed office tower at the main entrance. ACG's glazing package for Conkling & Lewis Construction combined Trulite 3300 storefront with a veneer wall system.

Location Vero Beach, FL GC Conkling & Lewis Construction Systems Trulite 3300 storefront & veneer wall Completed 2025
01
Sheet A-011 · The project

The entrance tower carries the whole building.

Storage Rentals of America is a national self-storage operator known for well-built, architecturally considered facilities - and the Vero Beach location is no exception. Three stories, a bold red-and-white brand identity, and a glazed office tower at the main entrance that anchors the building's street presence.

ACG worked as the glazing subcontractor to general contractor Conkling & Lewis Construction (ACG's subcontract was with A Day Designs, LLC). The package paired Trulite 3300 storefront with a veneer wall system across the building's elevations.
