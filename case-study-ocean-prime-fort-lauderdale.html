Scope narrative

Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale is the flagship Las Olas Marina location of a multi-unit fine-dining concept. The restaurant sits at 171 Las Olas Circle. ACG's work here was one Euro-Wall door/opening. ACG did not install the restaurant's full folding and sliding waterfront facade.

American Commercial Glass furnished and installed that single Euro-Wall opening. This page does not claim a complete glazing scope, a full-height folding wall along the primary waterfront elevation, or that Euro-Wall was the only manufacturer used on the building.

The restaurant sits in Broward County - HVHZ jurisdiction. The Euro-Wall opening ACG installed required a current Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) documenting TAS 201 large-missile impact, TAS 202 structural air pressure, and TAS 203 cyclic wind pressure testing.

Technical highlights

Technical highlights of ACG's Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale opening:

  • One Euro-Wall opening: ACG furnished and installed a single Euro-Wall door/opening. ACG is not claiming the restaurant's remaining glazing.
  • Miami-Dade NOA: Euro-Wall systems carry Miami-Dade NOAs for HVHZ use. ACG verified the applicable NOA for the opening it installed.
  • TAS 201/202/203 compliance: As required for all HVHZ glazing, the Euro-Wall system was tested and documented under Florida's three-protocol impact and structural test suite. The NOA specifies the exact glass make-up (laminated, PVB interlayer, specific thickness) that must be used to maintain HVHZ approval.
  • Design pressure: Fort Lauderdale waterfront exposure generates higher DP requirements relative to inland sites. The opening ACG installed had to sit inside the tested NOA envelope for that site.

Schedule, safety, and coordination

Hospitality construction schedules - especially for flagship restaurant openings - are driven by the operator's opening date, not by the construction team's convenience. ACG coordinated Euro-Wall delivery and installation sequencing with Buckeye Hospitality Construction's overall schedule, providing advance notice of fabrication lead time at the time of award so the GC could build the opening window into the master schedule without surprises.

Euro-Wall systems require experienced installation crews familiar with the panel hardware, threshold conditions, and seal sequencing specific to the manufacturer's products. ACG's Euro-Wall installation team has completed multiple Florida hospitality projects with the same folding wall system, reducing installation risk at the field stage.

HVHZ submittals at Broward County require complete NOA documentation before permit issuance. ACG submitted the HVHZ-compliant package early in preconstruction to avoid permit delays that would cascade into the operator's opening schedule.

Frequently asked questions

What is Euro-Wall?

Euro-Wall is an architectural glazing manufacturer that produces folding wall, sliding wall, and pivot door systems for commercial and hospitality applications. Euro-Wall systems for Florida HVHZ use carry Miami-Dade NOAs confirming TAS 201, 202, and 203 impact and structural test compliance. ACG is an Euro-Wall installer in Florida.

Is Fort Lauderdale in the HVHZ?

Yes. Fort Lauderdale is in Broward County, and all of Broward County is designated HVHZ under the Florida Building Code. Every exterior glazing system installed in Fort Lauderdale must carry a current Miami-Dade NOA documenting HVHZ impact compliance.

What is a Miami-Dade NOA?

A Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) is the product approval document issued by Miami-Dade County's Product Control Section, certifying that a glazing system passed TAS 201 missile-impact, TAS 202 structural, and TAS 203 cyclic-load testing. NOAs are required for all glazing in HVHZ (Miami-Dade and Broward counties).

Can Euro-Wall systems be used in HVHZ?

Yes, subject to the specific NOA in effect for the product configuration. Euro-Wall manufactures HVHZ-rated folding and sliding wall systems with current Miami-Dade NOAs. ACG verifies that the specific frame size, glass make-up, and anchorage conditions of each project fall within the tested NOA envelope before submitting for permit.

Why use a folding wall at a waterfront restaurant?

A folding wall allows the full facade to open, eliminating the visual barrier between the interior dining room and an exterior terrace or waterfront. When the panels fold back, the restaurant reads as a single indoor-outdoor space. When the panels close, the same facade provides an HVHZ-rated impact barrier against storm conditions. No other glazing product provides both functions in a single frame.

What is TAS 201 impact testing?

TAS 201 is Florida's large-missile impact test: a 9-pound 2x4 wood projectile fired at the glazing system at 50 feet per second. To pass, the projectile cannot penetrate through the assembly - the glass may crack but must remain in the frame. Passing TAS 201 is one of three requirements for Miami-Dade NOA approval.

Does ACG install Euro-Wall systems in HVHZ?

Yes. American Commercial Glass (FL CGC #1531993) is an Euro-Wall installer in Florida. ACG has completed Euro-Wall hospitality projects in HVHZ and non-HVHZ Florida jurisdictions and provides HVHZ-compliant NOA submittal packages as a standard part of every Florida HVHZ project.
