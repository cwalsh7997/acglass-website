Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale is the Cameron Mitchell Restaurants flagship at Las Olas Marina, 171 Las Olas Circle. Architect: Kobi Karp Architecture.

ACG furnished and installed a single Euro-Wall door/opening on this job. ACG did not install the restaurant's full folding and sliding waterfront facade, and this page does not claim a complete Euro-Wall package.

Photographs on this page show the restaurant and marina. They are context for the building, not a record that ACG glazed the entire elevation.

GC takeaway

Las Olas Marina is a Fort Lauderdale waterfront site in Broward's High Velocity Hurricane Zone. Ocean Prime sits on that marina. ACG's contracted work here was one operable Euro-Wall opening - furnish and install - not the restaurant's full folding and sliding waterfront facade. This page does not publish a square-footage package or a product-approval number for the opening.

That is the useful takeaway for a GC pricing hospitality glass on a marina: a single operable opening is a defined Division 08 scope. The photographs show the restaurant and docks as building context, not as a record of a full-elevation install.
