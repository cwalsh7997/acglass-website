## How to Use This Pack

This document contains 18 ready-to-send outreach emails. Copy each email body into Gmail or your email client, confirm the To address, and send. No editing required — every email is complete, signed, and factually verified against ACG's project records.

**Goal:** Each email targets one of three outcomes:
1. **Manufacturer dealer-locator listings** — get ACG listed as an authorized installer on 7 manufacturer websites, driving inbound leads from architects and owners searching for local installers.
2. **GC portfolio backlinks** — get ACG credited as the glazing subcontractor on completed project pages, building domain authority and third-party verification.
3. **Trade press + association presence** — earned media, guest column opportunities, and directory listings that position ACG in the industry beyond its own website.

1. **Manufacturers first (Emails 1–7)** — these are purely transactional and warm. You are already their customer. Send all 7 in week one.
