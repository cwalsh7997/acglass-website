Core Competencies

What ACG installs.

01

Commercial Storefronts

Pre-glazed and field-glazed storefront systems. Florida-approved, impact-rated. Primary product partner: ESWindows.

02

Curtainwall Systems

Stick-built and unitized curtainwall for commercial, multifamily, and mixed-use construction up to mid-rise.

03

Window Wall & Impact

Florida Building Code-approved window wall and impact-rated window and door systems for HVHZ and non-HVHZ zones.

04

Folding & Moving Glass Walls

Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems for hospitality, amenity, and luxury multifamily. Authorized dealer and installer.

05

Automatic Entrance Systems

Sliding, swinging, and revolving automatic doors. ADA-compliant. Back-of-house service included.

06

Fire-Rated Glazing

UL-listed fire-rated assemblies for stair and corridor enclosures, healthcare, and institutional projects.

07

Interior Glass Partitions

Demountable office partitions, frameless all-glass systems, and architectural interior glazing.

08

Specialty & Custom Glazing

Decorative frit, laminated interlayers, back-painted glass, switchable privacy glass, digital print applications.

Differentiators

Why GCs select ACG.

Pre-Glazed Storefront Capability

ACG's flagship product partnership with ESWindows gives GCs access to factory pre-glazed storefront systems — glass installed in the frame at the manufacturing plant, delivered to site assembled. Faster installation, cleaner weathering-in, fewer field variables.

AI-Managed Operations

ACG runs a documented operating system across estimating, submittals, procurement, scheduling, and closeout. Every VIP GC interaction (DeAngelis Diamond, Verdex, Proctor, Curran Young, Rycon, Suffolk) is tracked and owner-monitored.

Three Florida Offices

West Palm Beach headquarters, Naples office serving Southwest Florida, and Tampa office supporting Central Florida expansion. Regional coverage without the regional overhead.

Owner-Level Decision Making

Every project has an owner-level decision-maker one phone call away. No pass-the-buck on change orders, deliveries, or scheduling conflicts. Connor Walsh (President) or Rielly Walsh (CEO) is reachable directly.
