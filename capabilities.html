What is the difference between a storefront and a curtain wall?

The difference between a storefront and a curtain wall is height and performance: storefront is a single-span system used below the fourth floor, while curtainwall spans multiple floors and meets higher air, water, and structural performance. American Commercial Glass installs both across Florida.
