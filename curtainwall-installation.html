Curtainwall is the most complex glazing scope on the building — and the most consequential when it fails. ACG brings the engineering rigor, manufacturer relationships, and field execution to deliver curtainwall systems that perform over the long term.

Curtainwall Expertise

The Curtainwall Sub GCs
Actually Trust.

Curtainwall failures are catastrophic — water infiltration behind the system, structural movement that breaks seals, improper drainage that saturates walls over years. The consequences of a bad curtainwall sub don't show up on Day 1. They show up at Year 5, after the warranty is gone.

ACG approaches curtainwall differently. We review structural drawings, understand the building's movement expectations, and design our systems to accommodate them. We coordinate with the waterproofing contractor before installation begins. We specify the right sealants and gaskets for the thermal and movement conditions the building will experience.

Our Eurowall curtainwall systems are engineered for Florida's coastal exposure conditions — wind loads, thermal cycling, and hurricane impact where required. Shop drawings include full thermal analysis, drainage plane details, and anchor conditions coordinated with structural engineering.

System Types

Stick-Built Curtainwall

Field-assembled curtainwall for maximum flexibility and cost efficiency.

Stick-built curtainwall assembles framing members in the field, allowing accommodation of irregular building conditions and non-standard dimensions. Eurowall stick systems are engineered for Florida wind loads with thermal break options for energy compliance. Ideal for projects where unitized pricing isn't justified or field conditions require flexibility.
