The Project

CubeSmart Self Storage at 4255 Davie Rd Ext is a multi-story, climate-controlled storage facility in Davie, FL - a commercial building that required a polished, high-quality exterior to match the brand standards of one of the country's largest self-storage operators.

The building features a prominent curtainwall and storefront glazing package on the main facade - the multi-panel window groupings that define the upper floors, the glass-enclosed lobby entrance, and the full storefront system fronting the vehicle access lane. All of it executed in PGT aluminum systems.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the complete PGT glazing package: curtainwall framing on the upper facade, commercial storefront at the main lobby entrance, and the secondary glazed openings throughout the building's exterior envelope.

Self-storage and commercial projects like this one demand the same precision as any other scope - the GC has a hard TCO date, the tenant has a specific opening schedule, and there is zero tolerance for glazing delays. ACG met every milestone.

The Team

Delivered for DC Construction as general contractor, using PGT aluminum curtainwall and storefront systems - a premium product that pairs performance with the clean aesthetic CubeSmart's brand requires.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Davie location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
