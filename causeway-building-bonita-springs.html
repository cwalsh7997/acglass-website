The project

Bay after bay, identical.

Causeway Building is a multi-tenant retail and light commercial strip center at 28219 Bonita Crossings Blvd — a Southwest Florida infill development housing a range of retail, service, and specialty businesses. The building's gray corrugated metal exterior and colorful awnings are anchored by a consistent row of ESWindows dark-framed storefront systems across every bay. Fort Construction managed the build and brought ACG in to execute the full storefront package: consistent framing, consistent profiles, consistent installation quality from bay one to the end of the run.

Scope notes

Precision and sequencing over a long run.

01

Every bay, one system

ACG supplied and installed the ESWindows commercial storefront system across all tenant bays: entry door units, fixed side lites, and the transoms above each opening.

02

Identical framing, consistent reveals

With multiple bays requiring identical framing and consistent reveals across a long run, precision and sequencing were as important as product quality. Every door swings correctly, every frame sits plumb, every bay matches its neighbors.

03

Clean punch at TCO

When a GC gets to TCO on a multi-tenant retail project, they don't want to hear about glazing punch list items. Fort Construction didn't on this one. Site supervision ran out of ACG's Naples office.
