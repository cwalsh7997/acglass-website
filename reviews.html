How ACG Collects Reviews

Earned, not chased.

Every ACG review comes from a GC, architect, or owner ACG has delivered a completed scope for. There is no incentive, no reciprocal arrangement, no third-party review platform. Two to three days after the punch list is signed, the PM gets one email: if ACG hit the mark, a Google review link. If anything fell short, direct escalation to Connor Walsh. That’s the process.

If ACG has worked on your project and you’d like to share feedback, the review links are above — or email connor@acglass.com directly.
