ACG Dealer Program

Quote ES Windows.
Win More Work.
ACG-Branded.

If you install ESWindows on residential or light-commercial work, ACG can set you up as a dealer. You quote at ACG dealer pricing, your customer sees an ACG-branded quote, and you get back-end estimating help on anything you can't price yourself. Apply below — most approvals take 2 business days.

Dealer Application

Once approved, you'll receive an ESWindows login with your dealer pricing baked in, plus access to the ACG dealer portal at acglass.com/dealer.

By submitting, you agree to ACG following up by email or phone about your application. We don't share your information.

What ACG Dealers Get

Pricing
Dealer pricing baked into your ESWindows login
When you log into the ESWindows quoting tool, prices already include your dealer markup. You quote, you add tax, that's the customer price.
Customer-facing quote
ACG-branded PDF for your customers
Upload your ESWindows quote CSV to the dealer portal and get back a clean, ACG-branded PDF you can send straight to your customer.
Estimating support
Back-end help on anything weird
Custom sizes, mulled units, tricky impact ratings — email plans to ACG and we'll come back with a quote you can pass through.
Approval
Most decisions in 2 business days
Submit the form, we'll reach out for a quick conversation and a license check, and if it's a fit you'll have credentials shortly after.
How the ACG Dealer Program Works

Simple, No Surprises.

1
You apply

Fill out the application above. We're looking for licensed contractors and qualified installers — typically window/door specialists, glaziers, GCs with self-perform glazing, or remodelers with consistent ESWindows volume.

2
We talk and verify

A short call to understand your typical projects, confirm license/insurance, and agree on a markup tier. We don't ghost — you'll hear back either way.

3
Credentials issued

You get an ESWindows login with your dealer pricing already applied, plus an ACG dealer portal login at acglass.com/dealer.

4
You quote & sell

Build quotes on ESWindows, generate ACG-branded PDFs from the dealer portal, and ping us when you need help on anything outside the standard catalog.

Already a Dealer

Sign in to the dealer portal

If you already have ACG dealer credentials, head to the portal to generate branded customer PDFs and access your account.
