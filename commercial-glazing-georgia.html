Georgia Market

Georgia Is the Southeast's
Construction Engine.

Georgia has emerged as one of the dominant commercial construction markets in the Southeast — driven by corporate relocations, port expansion, and sustained population growth across the Atlanta metro and coastal corridor.

Atlanta's Midtown and Buckhead high-rise corridors are running some of the Southeast's most complex glazing scopes. Mixed-use towers, corporate campus redevelopment, and the Atlanta Beltline's multi-block commercial and residential projects are generating sustained demand for curtainwall, window wall, and commercial storefront across the metro.

The suburban Atlanta technology corridor — Alpharetta, Dunwoody, and Kennesaw — is seeing major corporate campus construction from Google, Microsoft, NCR, and dozens of mid-sized technology companies establishing Southeast headquarters. These projects require sophisticated glazing scopes: floor-to-ceiling curtainwall, high-performance window wall, and complex entrance systems designed to meet both aesthetic specifications and Georgia's energy code requirements.

Savannah is one of the fastest-growing ports in the United States. The Port of Savannah's expansion has triggered a massive wave of warehouse, logistics, distribution, and supporting commercial construction across the Coastal Empire. Downtown Savannah's commercial district continues to grow alongside the historic district, generating hospitality, retail, and mixed-use glazing scopes that require sensitivity to the city's architectural character while meeting modern wind load requirements.

Georgia's wind requirements vary significantly by location. Coastal Savannah and Brunswick are in higher wind exposure zones requiring impact-rated or tested-and-labeled glazing assemblies — the same fundamental requirements ACG manages daily in Florida. Inland Atlanta and Augusta operate under lower wind requirements but still demand code-compliant, engineered systems with complete submittal packages.

Augusta's healthcare and government construction market is generating consistent glazing demand — the Augusta University Medical Center expansion, Fort Gordon's infrastructure build-out, and the suburban commercial corridor along Washington Road. Columbus has seen growth driven by Fort Benning (now Fort Moore) and the surrounding defense industry supply chain.

ACG's GC partners — including Rycon Construction and Verdex Construction — operate throughout the Southeast, including Georgia. ACG's AI-managed logistics platform enables full project coordination for Georgia scopes from our Florida offices, with field crews deployed to match the construction sequence.

What are Georgia's building code glazing requirements? +

Georgia follows the International Building Code with state amendments. Wind requirements vary significantly — coastal Savannah and Brunswick face higher wind exposure requiring impact-rated or tested-and-labeled assemblies, while inland Atlanta operates under lower wind requirements. ACG engineers system specifications to the correct wind zone, exposure category, and local AHJ requirements for each project location.

What glazing systems does ACG install on Georgia projects? +

ACG installs YKK AP, Kawneer, Oldcastle BuildingEnvelope, Wausau Window, ESWindows, CGI Windows, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. System selection is driven by the project specification, architect's design intent, and Georgia's local code requirements — not convenience or stock availability.
