The Delray Beach Market

One of Florida's Most Vibrant
Commercial Markets.

Delray Beach punches well above its size for commercial construction activity. Atlantic Avenue's restaurant and retail corridor — one of Florida's most recognized urban commercial streets — generates constant redevelopment, tenant improvement, and new construction. The glazing specifications here are demanding: architectural storefronts, high-visibility curtainwall, and impact systems that must perform aesthetically as well as structurally.

The Delray Marketplace, the Congress Avenue commercial corridor, and the Federal Highway multifamily boom extend the market well beyond the beach. Delray Beach Medical Center's campus generates sustained healthcare construction with the fire-rated assemblies, impact curtainwall, and institutional-grade glazing specs that come with hospital-level projects.

Coastal and barrier island properties in Delray Beach fall within the High Velocity Hurricane Zone — HVHZ product approvals, NOA documentation, and third-party testing are required. Inland properties follow standard Florida product approval under FBC Chapter 16. ACG identifies the applicable zone for every project before the scope is assembled, so there are no product substitutions at submittal or failures at inspection.

ACG's West Palm Beach HQ is 15 miles north on I-95. That's the closest major commercial glazing sub in the South Palm Beach County market — and it shows in response time, mobilization speed, and knowledge of the building department process.

Common Questions

Delray Beach Glazing FAQ.

How close is ACG's West Palm Beach office to Delray Beach? +

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is approximately 15 miles north of Delray Beach on I-95 — the closest major commercial glazing sub serving the South Palm Beach County market. That proximity means same-day site visits, faster mobilization, and direct familiarity with Palm Beach County's building department, permit review process, and inspection timeline.

Is Delray Beach in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone? +

Coastal areas of Delray Beach — east of Federal Highway and along the barrier island — fall within the HVHZ under Florida Building Code Chapter 16 and require full NOA documentation for glazing products. Properties west of Federal Highway generally follow standard Florida product approval requirements. ACG identifies the correct zone for every project during scope preparation — there are no product substitutions at submittal and no surprises at inspection.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Delray Beach? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall curtainwall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, and Allegion entrance hardware. For HVHZ projects in coastal Delray Beach, our product selection process ensures NOA documentation is complete before the submittal package is assembled — not assembled reactively after a plan review comment.

How quickly can ACG turn around a scope for Delray Beach? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions. The South Palm Beach County market is competitive; we match its pace.

Does ACG handle submittals and HVHZ documentation for Delray Beach? +

Yes. ACG manages the complete submittal package — shop drawings, Florida product approvals, HVHZ NOA documentation, and engineer-of-record coordination. Our packages are designed to pass Palm Beach County plan review without revisions, keeping your Atlantic Ave, Congress Ave, or Linton Blvd project on schedule.
