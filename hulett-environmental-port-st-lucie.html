Hulett Environmental · Port St. Lucie, FL
The project

A headquarters built around daylight.

Hulett Environmental Services is one of Florida's largest pest control companies, with operations spanning the state. This is their Port St. Lucie headquarters - a purpose-built commercial office and operations facility on the Treasure Coast, delivered by Hooks Construction.

The building required a complete ESWindows commercial glazing package: the main entry storefront, the clerestory window band across the upper facade, and the full commercial window package throughout the office interiors. Clean white exterior, dark aluminum frames, and enough natural light inside to make the office function the way a headquarters should.

The conference room shows what happens when a glazing scope is done right: an entire wall of ESWindows commercial windows, floor-to-ceiling light, clean frames - and not a single gap or misaligned unit in sight.
