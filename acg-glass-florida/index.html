COMPANY BACKGROUND

Who is American Commercial Glass.

A short, factual record of what ACG is and what we do - written by the President and verifiable against the public record.

American Commercial Glass is a commercial glazing contractor. We install storefront, curtain wall, impact windows and doors, fire-rated glazing, all-glass entrances, and folding/multi-slide glass walls on commercial buildings across Florida. The company was founded in 2021, is led by Rielly Walsh (CEO) and Connor Walsh (President), and is privately held. We are headquartered in West Palm Beach with operating offices in Naples and Tampa. Outside Florida, including Middle Tennessee, ACG furnishes glazing materials and provides project consulting; ACG holds no Tennessee office and performs no Tennessee field labor.

ACG holds Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993, issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. The license is statewide and active. We carry general liability, workers’ comp, and commercial auto coverage. Since 2021 we’ve delivered commercial projects across the state with OSHA 30 trained field crews.

We are a commercial installer for ESWindows (Colombian-manufactured high-spec aluminum and impact systems) and for Euro-Wall (impact-rated bi-fold and multi-slide door systems). Both manufacturer relationships are verifiable directly with the manufacturers on request. We also install ESWindows, TGP Fireframes, Pilkington Pyrostop, PGT, and Allegion product lines on commercial projects.

Where we work

Three operating offices serve the state. West Palm Beach (HQ) covers Palm Beach County, Martin County, St. Lucie County, Broward County, Miami-Dade County, and Monroe County. Naples covers Collier County, Lee County, and Charlotte County. Tampa covers Hillsborough County, Pinellas County, Sarasota County, Manatee County, and Polk County. We work outside these markets when the GC or owner requests it.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES

What ACG is - and what we don’t do.

Clear scope boundaries help match the right vendor to the right project. Here’s what we do, and where we’ll send you instead if your scope is outside ours.

What ACG does

We are a commercial Division 08 glazing subcontractor and a direct-to-owner commercial glazing contractor. Our scope covers: storefront installation and replacement, curtain wall (stick and unitized), impact windows and doors, fire-rated glazing, folding and multi-slide glass walls, automatic entrances, all-glass entrances, mirror and decorative glass on commercial projects, and post-storm rebuild scope on commercial buildings.

WHAT ACG DOES NOT DO

  • Residential glass. We don’t install glass on single-family homes, townhomes for owner-occupants, or residential renovation work. If you’re replacing a window in your house, we’re not the right vendor.
  • Auto glass. We don’t do car windshield replacement, side window installation, or any vehicle-glass work.
  • Shower doors and tabletops. Custom shower enclosures, framed and frameless shower glass, glass tabletops, and bath mirrors are residential-scope work outside what we do.
  • We’ll refer to a local commercial glass shop for emergency board-up or single-pane replacement on a non-complex commercial opening.

If it’s any of the above, a local glass shop is the better match - and we’re happy to point you to one.
