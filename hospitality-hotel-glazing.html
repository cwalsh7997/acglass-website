The whole hotel envelope, on one subcontract.

A resort is rarely a single glazing scope. It is a tower of window wall, a lobby of curtainwall and automatic entrances, restaurant storefronts, and pool and spa glass, all riding a schedule that does not move. We run the whole package so your super makes one call, not five.

Hotel Glazing

Most GCs split this across four
subs. We keep it on one.

Hotel and resort glazing is complex — the typical hospitality project has a tower with window wall systems, a lobby with curtainwall or storefront, automatic sliding entries, pool deck enclosures with specialty glass, restaurant storefronts, and spa facilities with specialty privacy glass.

Most GCs split this scope across multiple subs and spend the project coordinating between them. ACG handles it all — one contract, one schedule, one point of contact who knows where every piece is in the supply chain.

Florida's coastal resort market adds HVHZ requirements to every system on the building. ACG's hospitality experience includes projects in Miami Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Naples, and throughout Florida's coast — we know what the inspectors look for and how to document our systems properly.

Hotel Guest Room Window Wall
