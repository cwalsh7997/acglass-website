Building code context

Georgia follows the IBC with state amendments administered through the Georgia State Minimum Standard Codes. Atlanta's urban commercial market demands curtainwall engineering, structural silicone glazing, and high-rise facade experience — all of which translate directly from ACG's Florida Class-A office portfolio.

Licensing

Georgia requires state-level general contractor licensing via the Georgia State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors. Tennessee provides trade-exam reciprocity for Georgia's commercial contractor license. ACG will file for the Georgia license in 2026 once the Tennessee license is in place.
