Port St. Joe commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Port St. Joe in Gulf County?

Yes. Port St. Joe is the county seat of Gulf County, FL — distinct from the Bay County panhandle market.

Was Port St. Joe affected by Hurricane Michael?

Yes, severely. Storm surge and wind damage were significant. Commercial reconstruction post-2018 is current FBC standard.

Do you do historic district storefront work in downtown Port St. Joe?

Yes. Historic district storefront alterations have design review — we structure submittals with profile, finish, and divided-lite details to clear review.

Is WindMark Beach a St. Joe Company development?

Yes. WindMark Beach is one of St. Joe's smaller Gulf County coastal developments — coastal vernacular commercial, 140 mph design wind, full WBDR.
