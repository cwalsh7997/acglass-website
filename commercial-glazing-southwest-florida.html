SWFL Local Operations

Southwest Florida's
Local Glazing Partner.

Southwest Florida — Naples, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Sarasota — represents one of the country's most consistently active coastal construction markets. Post-Ian recovery, new development, and population-driven commercial expansion are all driving sustained glazing demand.

ACG's Naples office serves Southwest Florida with local crews, established supply chains, and deep familiarity with Collier and Lee County building departments. We're not commuting from South Florida on every job — we live and build in this market.

HVHZ requirements in Collier and Lee counties are stringent — Florida's coastal construction codes apply rigorously here, especially post-Ian. ACG's systems are specified and tested for the actual exposure conditions Southwest Florida buildings face.
