The ACG Model

Supply + Install —
Why It Matters.

Most glazing procurement in commercial construction involves a gap: the GC or owner buys glass products from a distributor or manufacturer, then separately contracts an installer to put them in. That split creates coordination problems — damaged material on site with no clear owner, installation questions about products the installer didn't source, and a warranty that covers the product or the installation but not both.

ACG eliminates that gap. We source the aluminum systems, glass, hardware, sealants, and ancillary components directly from manufacturers — and we install them. One contract. One warranty covering both product and installation. One party who answers the phone when there's a question about why something was specified, how it was installed, or what's covered when there's a warranty claim ten years from now.

This is how commercial glazing should work — and it's how every ACG project works from day one. The 48-hour scope turnaround is possible because ACG knows what it's supplying before it bids. There's no separate supplier to check with, no second loop through a distributor's pricing system. We know the product, we know the lead time, and we can price it accurately in 48 hours.

Process

How Commercial Glass
Installation Works with ACG.

01

Send Drawings

Send your project drawings — architectural plans, specifications, and any existing scope documents. ACG reviews them and returns a complete scope, system recommendations, and pricing within 48 hours. No meeting required, no site visit needed to generate the initial scope.

02

Submittal Package

Once awarded, ACG generates the complete submittal package — shop drawings, Florida Product Approval documentation, anchor calculations, and any NOA documentation required. Packages are designed to pass plan review without revisions. ACG manages the review cycle directly with the building department and engineer of record.

03

Procurement & Scheduling

ACG orders material directly from manufacturers as soon as submittals are approved. Lead times are tracked in ACG's system and integrated into the GC's master schedule. Material delivery milestones are communicated proactively — not discovered as a problem at the week-before-install meeting.

04

Installation & Close-Out

ACG installs to the approved submittals and the project schedule. Final inspection, punch list closure, and warranty documentation are delivered at project completion. The GC gets a clean close-out package — not a list of warranty contacts to chase individually for a product and an installer who are now pointing at each other.

Common Questions

Commercial Glass Installation FAQ.

What does supply and install mean for commercial glass? +

ACG sources the glass products, aluminum systems, hardware, and materials directly from manufacturers and installs them under a single subcontract. No materials middleman — ACG buys direct from ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, and other manufacturers. This eliminates the coordination gap that exists when a GC buys materials from one source and contracts a separate installer. ACG owns the warranty for both product and installation.

What commercial glass systems does ACG install in Florida? +

ACG installs commercial storefront (standard and impact-rated), all-glass entrance systems, curtainwall (stick-built and unitized), window wall systems, impact windows and doors for Florida coastal zones, and automatic entrance systems. Every exterior system carries Florida Product Approval documentation as required by the Florida Building Code.

Does ACG serve all of Florida? +

Yes. ACG operates from three Florida offices — West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa — providing statewide coverage. South Florida, Southwest Florida, the Gulf Coast, and the Tampa Bay market are all within ACG's primary service area. Orlando, Jacksonville, and emerging statewide markets are served from these bases.

How quickly can ACG provide a commercial glass installation scope? +

ACG returns a complete scope — system recommendations, Florida code compliance documentation, and pricing — within 48 hours of receiving project drawings. This is enabled by ACG's operations, which processes drawing sets and generates scope documents without the manual bottlenecks that slow most glazing contractors to a 7–14 day response cycle.
