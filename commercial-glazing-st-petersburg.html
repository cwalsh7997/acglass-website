Our Services

Commercial Glazing Services in St. Petersburg

Full-service glazing solutions for St. Petersburg's dynamic waterfront market — from downtown mixed-use towers to beach resort properties.

Impact Storefront Systems

FBC-approved impact storefront for St. Petersburg's waterfront retail and commercial properties. ES-8000 and ES-9000 systems with laminated impact glass meeting Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements for Pinellas County.

Curtainwall & Window Wall

High-performance curtainwall and window wall systems for St. Petersburg's downtown high-rises and waterfront mixed-use developments. Engineered for coastal wind loads and salt-air durability along Tampa Bay.

Sliding & Folding Glass Walls

Eurowall MultiSlide and VistaFold systems for St. Petersburg's hospitality and luxury waterfront projects. Impact-rated operable walls maximizing bay and Gulf views while meeting Florida Building Code.

Impact Windows

Florida Product Approved impact windows for St. Petersburg commercial, institutional, and multifamily projects. Laminated glass assemblies engineered for Pinellas County's coastal wind zone with superior thermal performance.

Fire-Rated Glazing

TGP and SAFTI fire-rated glazing for St. Petersburg healthcare facilities, museums, educational buildings, and commercial corridors. Complete fire-rated assemblies meeting UL certification requirements.

Automatic Entrances

Allegion and Stanley automatic entrance systems for St. Petersburg's hotels, hospitals, and commercial buildings. Sliding, swinging, and revolving door systems with ADA compliance and hurricane-rated options.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG serve St. Petersburg's downtown waterfront projects?

Yes. ACG provides specialized glazing for St. Petersburg waterfront mixed-use, hospitality, and residential projects, including coastal durability and premium detail coordination.

What waterfront durability standards apply to St. Petersburg?

St. Petersburg's waterfront environment requires corrosion-resistant finishes and high-performance glazing. ACG specifies materials suitable for coastal/bayfront exposure and provides installation expertise.

Can ACG handle tight downtown construction schedules?

Yes. ACG coordinates rapid installation in downtown retail and hospitality environments, working around traffic and tight schedules to minimize business disruption.

What glazing systems does ACG install in St. Petersburg?

ACG installs impact storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet Pinellas County Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements.

What is the typical timeline for a St. Petersburg commercial glazing project?

Timelines vary by scope. Pinellas County permitting typically takes 2-4 weeks for commercial glazing. Standard storefront projects run 6-10 weeks from approved submittals; curtainwall and window wall systems require 12-16 weeks. ACG provides detailed schedules during pre-construction.

Does ACG offer value engineering for St. Petersburg projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering during pre-construction to optimize glazing specifications for Pinellas County's code requirements and coastal conditions. This includes system alternatives that meet performance requirements at competitive pricing, energy code optimization, and coordination with architects.
