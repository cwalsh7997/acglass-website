The St. Petersburg Market

St. Pete Is in the Middle of
a Commercial Renaissance.
ACG Is Building Through It.

St. Petersburg's transformation from a quiet Gulf Coast retirement city to one of Florida's most dynamic urban markets has been swift, sustained, and substantial. The downtown waterfront has experienced a wave of new residential towers, hotel renovations, and mixed-use developments that show no signs of slowing - and every one of those projects needs a glazing sub who can handle both the curtainwall on a 20-story tower and the retail storefronts at grade.

The scale and duration of this project will generate glazing scope for years. ACG is positioned to be on it from the first building permit.

The EDGE District and Grand Central Avenue corridor continue to mature as mixed-use destinations - new restaurants, boutique retail, galleries, and multifamily projects are filling in the neighborhoods west and south of downtown. Midtown St. Pete is experiencing significant commercial investment, with new retail, medical, and community facilities. The Skyway Marina District commercial area is active with boating-related retail and mixed-use.

St. Pete's peninsula location - bounded by Tampa Bay to the north and east, and the Gulf of Mexico to the west - means coastal wind exposure is a real design consideration. Impact-rated glazing is increasingly specified throughout Pinellas County, not just on the beachfront. ACG's submittals are built to pass Pinellas County plan review the first time.

Common Questions

St. Petersburg Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve St. Petersburg and South Pinellas commercial projects? +

Yes. ACG's Tampa office serves St. Petersburg and all of South Pinellas County with local field crews, local supply relationships, and direct familiarity with Pinellas County's building department and permit process. St. Pete's construction renaissance has made it one of our most active markets, and our crews are regularly working on projects throughout the downtown core, the EDGE and Grand Central districts, and South Pinellas County.

What glazing systems does ACG install in St. Petersburg? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. For St. Pete's downtown tower projects, we commonly work with PGT and Euro-Wall curtainwall systems with full structural engineering coordination and pre-construction submittal packages built for Pinellas County's review process.

Are impact-rated systems required in St. Pete commercial buildings? +

Most of St. Petersburg falls within Florida's wind-borne debris region. The city's peninsula location - bounded by Tampa Bay and the Gulf of Mexico - means coastal wind exposure is a design reality. Impact-rated glazing is increasingly specified throughout Pinellas County, not just on beachfront properties. ACG's submittal packages are engineered for Pinellas County plan review from the first submission, with Florida Product Approvals and NOA documentation for every system installed.

Does ACG handle curtainwall for downtown St. Pete tower projects? +

Yes. ACG installs curtainwall and window wall for mid-rise and high-rise projects in downtown St. Pete - including the ongoing waterfront tower construction, the Tropicana Field redevelopment area, and the Historic Gas Plant District projects. We bring full structural engineering coordination, pre-construction submittal packages, and scheduling to every large-scale curtainwall scope. Send us the plans and we'll return a detailed scope in 48 hours.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for a St. Pete project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope - system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown - within 48 hours. From a Grand Central District retail storefront to a downtown St. Pete curtainwall package, our operations support fast, accurate takeoffs for any project size.
