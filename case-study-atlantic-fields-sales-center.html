Scope narrative

The sales and welcome center at a Discovery Land Company community occupies a specific role in the development sequence: it is typically the first structure completed and the building where prospective members are introduced to the community's vision before the course, clubhouse, and other amenities are finished. As such, it must present at the same quality level the finished community will ultimately deliver — an opening statement in glass and materials that communicates the standard everything else will meet.

American Commercial Glass installed the commercial glazing package at the Atlantic Fields Sales Center as part of the multi-structure Atlantic Fields engagement with Proctor Construction. The scope included impact-rated storefront systems at the building's primary elevations, glazed entry systems creating an inviting and architecturally refined front-of-house arrival experience, and specialty glazed elements consistent with the Welcome Center's design program.

Sales and welcome centers at private clubs are designed to maximize the visual impact of the surrounding landscape — large glass openings that frame views of the course, the canopy of mature trees, or the water features that define the community. ACG coordinated glass make-up selection to maximize view clarity while meeting Florida Energy Code SHGC compliance and WBDR impact requirements.

Martin County WBDR requirements were documented in the Florida Product Approval submittal package prepared by ACG for the Sales Center permit — consistent with the multi-building FPA documentation approach across the Atlantic Fields development.

Technical highlights

Technical highlights of the Atlantic Fields Sales Center glazing scope:

  • View-optimized glass: Neutral low-E glass make-up selected to minimize reflective or tinted appearance that would interfere with landscape views from the interior — balancing SHGC compliance with maximum view clarity at a member-facing presentation facility.
  • Entry design: Glazed entry systems at the welcome center arrival — impact-rated aluminum door systems with sidelites and transoms consistent with the architectural entry expression and WBDR impact requirements.
  • Storefront systems: ESWindows impact-rated aluminum storefront with Florida Product Approval for Martin County WBDR. Thermally broken frames for Florida Energy Code compliance.
  • Florida Product Approval: FPA documentation per ASTM E1886/E1996 for all exterior glazing, at calculated design pressures for the Martin County coastal exposure.

Schedule, safety, and coordination

Sales and welcome centers in private club developments are on the critical path of the development program — when Discovery Land Company begins membership sales for Atlantic Fields, the sales center must be open and operational. ACG coordinated with Proctor Construction to ensure the Sales Center glazing installation was completed on the schedule the membership program required.

As the first structure completed in a phased development, the sales center glazing may occur before full site infrastructure is in place — access roads, utilities, and other trade infrastructure may still be under construction when the glazing crew mobilizes. ACG coordinated access and logistics with Proctor Construction's site management to confirm safe material delivery and installation access.

What is the Atlantic Fields Sales Center?

Why does a sales center have high glazing requirements?

A private club sales and welcome center must present at the quality level of the completed community — prospective members are evaluating the community's standards based on what they see in the sales center. Large, clear glass openings that frame landscape views, refined entry systems, and architectural frame finishes all contribute to the member's first impression of the community's quality standard.

What is view-optimized glass?

View-optimized glass refers to neutral-tone or clear low-E glass specifications that minimize the green, blue, or gray tint introduced by some low-E coatings, while still meeting Florida Energy Code SHGC requirements. At member-facing club facilities where landscape views are a primary design element, ACG works with the architect to select glass make-ups that maximize view clarity within the code-required thermal performance envelope.

Is Hobe Sound in a hurricane-prone area?

Yes. Hobe Sound is in Martin County on Florida's Treasure Coast — a WBDR (Wind-Borne Debris Region) coastal jurisdiction under the Florida Building Code. All exterior glazing in Martin County commercial buildings must be impact-rated with Florida Product Approval. Martin County is not HVHZ (which is limited to Miami-Dade and Broward counties), but it is subject to Florida coastal impact glazing requirements.
