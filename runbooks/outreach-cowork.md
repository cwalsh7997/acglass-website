# Outreach: Weekly Cowork Loop

**Time estimate:** 90 minutes per cycle, weekly.
**Prereqs:** Outreach sheet in the ACG SEO Ops workbook, Gmail signed in as connor@acglass.com.

This is the partner-outreach loop ACG runs to local design firms, smaller GCs, and adjacent trade subs (electrical, mechanical) — the partners where one introduction can produce a year of recurring referrals. It is intentionally NOT a manufacturer or press-relations loop — those are owner-only and never run through this workflow.

---

## Rule (read first)

**Manufacturer outreach AND press outreach are owner-only.** Never queue an outbound to ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, Aldora, AGC Inc., trade-press editors, or industry-association staff through this loop. Those go through Connor directly.

This loop is for:
- Architects (especially residential-to-commercial mid-tier firms in FL)
- Smaller GCs (sub-$50M annual revenue) we want as repeat customers
- Adjacent trade subs whose schedule we cross frequently
- Property management / facilities companies on commercial portfolios

---

## The weekly prompt (use VERBATIM)

Open Claude/Perplexity/whichever your reasoning tool of the week is. Paste this prompt with the week's three target contacts filled in.

```
You are an outreach research assistant for American Commercial Glass.

CONTEXT FACTS — these are the only facts about ACG you may use:
- Florida-licensed Division 08 commercial glazing subcontractor (CGC #1531993)
- HQ West Palm Beach, offices in Naples and Tampa, Nashville opening Q3 2026
- Authorized installer for ESWindows and Euro-Wall
- Restaurant and hospitality specialty
- 350+ commercial projects, $3M/$6M bonding via Arch Insurance
- AI-managed operations, 48-hour scope letters
- Founders Connor Walsh (President) and Rielly Walsh (CEO)

DO NOT invent any other facts about ACG.

For each contact below:
1. Research the contact / firm from public sources. Note the firm's recent commercial work in Florida (last 12 months) and where geographically it overlaps with ACG's footprint.
2. Draft a SHORT outbound email — ≤120 words, plain prose, no bullet points, no marketing claims, opens with one specific observation from your research, closes with one direct ask (15-minute call OR a project lookalike sample).
3. Sign 'Connor' (lowercase 'C' is intentional).
4. No banned phrases: "leverage", "synergy", "world-class", "best-in-class", "ecosystem", "game-changing", "delve into", "I'd be happy to", "Certainly", "Great question".

CONTACTS THIS WEEK:
1. <Name> — <Title> — <Firm> — <Email>
2. <Name> — <Title> — <Firm> — <Email>
3. <Name> — <Title> — <Firm> — <Email>

Return each draft as: ## Contact N <name>, then a one-line research note, then the email body in a code block.
```

---

## Then: drop into Gmail as DRAFT

Critical rule: **draft only, never send**. Connor reviews every outbound from this loop. The workflow places it in the Drafts folder; you click Send (or don't) yourself.

Manual steps:
1. Copy the email body.
2. Gmail → **Compose**.
3. To: contact email.
4. Subject: hand-write a 5–7 word subject — never use a generated subject.
5. Paste body. Verify the signature is "Connor" exactly (no "Best regards," or other closer).
6. Click **Save** (not Send). The draft sits.

## Outreach sheet column schema

The **Outreach** tab of the ACG SEO Ops sheet has these columns (header row, then one row per contact):

| # | Column | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| A | `Date Added` | YYYY-MM-DD when the contact entered the loop |
| B | `Contact Name` | Full name |
| C | `Title` | Their title |
| D | `Firm` | Company name |
| E | `Email` | Contact email |
| F | `LinkedIn URL` | Optional — for your own reference |
| G | `Firm Footprint` | One-line note on overlap with ACG geography |
| H | `Source` | How you found them (referral, conference, list import, etc.) |
| I | `Draft Created` | YYYY-MM-DD draft saved to Gmail |
| J | `Sent` | YYYY-MM-DD if you actually sent the draft |
| K | `Reply` | Y/N |
| L | `Outcome` | Free text — call scheduled, no-reply, "pass", etc. |
| M | `Next Touch` | YYYY-MM-DD for a follow-up, or blank |
| N | `Notes` | Anything else |

Three contacts per week → 156/year → ~20 % reply rate is the working benchmark.

---

## Banned moves

- Sending the draft without reading and editing. The whole point is that you are in the loop.
- Adding manufacturers or press contacts to this sheet. They go through Connor's private outreach, not this loop.
- Using AI-generated subject lines. Subjects are hand-written every time. AI is allowed for body drafting only.
- Pasting more than 3 contacts at once into the prompt. The research quality drops sharply past 3.
