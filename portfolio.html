The Residences at Marina Bay

Project Hero Image
Location Fort Lauderdale, FL
Category Multifamily Residential
Scope Window Wall Systems & Storefronts
Systems Impact Windows, Commercial Glazing

The Residences at Marina Bay is a 32-story, 485-unit luxury tower featuring a comprehensive window wall and storefront glazing system. Our team managed the installation of over 8,000 linear feet of aluminum framing and 65,000 SF of impact-rated glass, coordinating with the developer's tight construction schedule and demanding architectural specifications.

The project required precision coordination between our crews, the structural steel teams, and the mechanical trades to ensure flawless installation on this high-visibility downtown property.
