GC bid FAQ

What Florida license covers ACG commercial glazing work?

American Commercial Glass holds Florida CGC #1531993 issued by the Florida DBPR, operates under NAICS classification 238150 (Glass and Glazing Contractors), and maintains industry-standard general liability, automobile, and workers compensation insurance.

How fast does ACG return a bid on documented scopes?

Documented scopes get a detailed proposal or budget direction back within 48 hours - with an exclusions list, not surprises.

Where are ACG's Florida offices?

American Commercial Glass operates statewide in Florida from three offices: West Palm Beach (HQ, 700 S Rosemary Ave Ste 204), Naples (4850 Tamiami Trail N, Ste 301), and Tampa (3031 N Rocky Point Dr W, Ste 600).

How do I invite ACG to bid?

Send drawings on Send Us Plans, email connor@acglass.com, call (772) 486-7711, or invite ACG on BuildingConnected or Procore. ACG bids Division 08 only.

Is ACG woman-owned?

American Commercial Glass is woman-owned - Rielly Walsh, CEO, is the 51% majority owner.
