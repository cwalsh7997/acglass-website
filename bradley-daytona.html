The Bradley Daytona

The complete glazing package - every window, door, and storefront opening - for a 300-unit luxury apartment community on Daytona Beach's LPGA Blvd growth corridor.

The project

300 units. Every opening.

The Bradley Daytona is a 300-unit luxury apartment community at 1501 NW N River Dr, near the LPGA Blvd and I-95 interchange - one of the most active growth corridors in Volusia County. At three stories and 300 units, it stands among the most extensive multifamily glazing projects ACG has delivered in Central Florida.

Scope notes

Hundreds of openings, one schedule.

01

Impact windows at volume

Impact-rated windows for every residential unit - from 1-bedroom plans at 685-797 SF up to 3-bedroom plans at 1,321 SF. Three hundred units means hundreds of individual openings, each measured, ordered, and installed in sequence.

02

Amenity & leasing storefront

Storefront systems for the leasing office and clubhouse, plus all interior and exterior doors - serving a resort-style amenity package: 24-hour fitness center, yoga and spin studio, resort pool, dog park, pet spa, and co-working space.

03

Logistics as craft

For Daytona Beach commercial glazing at this scale, ACG managed the full scope from submittals through punch list - keeping fabrication lead times aligned with Waypoint's multi-building schedule so no glazing delay held up a certificate of occupancy. Supervision ran out of ACG's West Palm Beach office.
