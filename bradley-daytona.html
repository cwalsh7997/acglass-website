The Project

The Bradley Daytona is a 300-unit luxury apartment community at 1501 NW N River Dr, near the LPGA Blvd and I-95 interchange in Daytona Beach — one of the most active growth corridors in Volusia County. At 3 stories and 300 units, it stands among the largest new multifamily glazing projects ACG has delivered in Central Florida.

The community offers a resort-style amenity package — 24-hour fitness center, yoga and spin studio, resort pool, dog park, pet spa, co-working space, and lake views. Unit types range from 1-bedroom at 685–797 SF up to 3-bedroom at 1,321 SF. ACG delivered the complete glazing package covering every window, door, and storefront opening across the entire development.

The Scope

Full supply and install — impact-rated windows for every residential unit, storefront installation systems for the leasing office and clubhouse, and all interior and exterior doors. Three hundred units means hundreds of individual openings, each requiring precise measurement, ordering, delivery coordination, and installation sequenced around a multi-trade schedule across multiple buildings.

For Daytona Beach commercial glazing of this scale, logistics matter as much as craft. ACG managed the full scope from submittals through punch list — keeping fabrication lead times aligned with Waypoint's construction schedule and ensuring no glazing delay held up a certificate of occupancy.

The Team

Waypoint builds large-scale multifamily across the Southeast and holds their subcontractors to a high standard: perform at volume, stay on schedule, and don't slow the job. ACG delivered.

The glazing package on The Bradley Daytona included multifamily complex glazing. ACG specified, supplied, and installed PGT impact windows — manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Daytona location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
