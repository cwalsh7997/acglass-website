Florida Code & HVHZ Compliance

HVHZ, wind load, and NOA — what it actually means.

The High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) designation applies to Miami-Dade and Broward counties under the Florida Building Code. Every glazing system installed in HVHZ must carry a Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance or Florida Product Approval with verified impact test results. ACG handles the submittal and coordination process for ESWindows systems on HVHZ projects.

HVHZ Counties and Wind Load Requirements

Miami-Dade and Broward counties require that fenestration products be tested and listed under an active Miami-Dade NOA. Wind speed design criteria in HVHZ typically exceeds 170 mph (Exposure D per ASCE 7). Design pressures for glazing in HVHZ commercial projects commonly range from ‑60 psf to ‑130 psf depending on building height, roof zone, and wall zone classification. ESWindows ES8000 is rated to DP +130/‑130 psf LMI.

Miami-Dade NOA HVHZ Compliant FBC Chapter 17

Impact Testing: ASTM E1996 and ASTM E1886

Products in HVHZ must pass ASTM E1886 (impact test method) and ASTM E1996 (performance specification for missile impact). Large Missile Impact (LMI) applies to floors 1–3 and uses a 9-pound 2x4 lumber missile at 50 ft/s. Small Missile Impact (SMI) applies at floor 4 and above, using 10 steel ball bearings at 130 ft/s. ESWindows ES8000, ES-7525, and GW-7000 all carry LMI ratings. ACG coordinates the impact test documentation required for permit submittals in HVHZ jurisdictions.

What ESWindows products does ACG install on commercial projects?

ACG installs the ES8000 DirectSet aluminum storefront (DP +130/‑130 psf, LMI-rated, NOA 24-0321.07), the ES8000T thermally broken storefront (NOA 21-0914.03), the ES50 commercial window line, and ES Curtainwall systems including the ES-7525 stick curtainwall (impact and blast rated) and the GW-7000 unitized curtainwall (Miami-Dade certified, LMI + SMI at DP +100/‑140 psf).
