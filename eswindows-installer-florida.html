Sheet ES-301 · Florida code & HVHZ compliance

HVHZ, wind load, and NOA — what it actually means.

HVHZ counties & wind load

Miami-Dade and Broward require fenestration tested and listed under an active Miami-Dade NOA. Design criteria typically exceed 170 mph (Exposure D per ASCE 7); glazing DPs commonly run -60 to -130 psf by height and wall zone. ES8000 is rated to DP +130/-130 psf LMI.

ASTM E1996 / E1886 impact testing

Large Missile Impact (floors 1–3): a 9-lb 2x4 at 50 ft/s. Small Missile Impact (floor 4+): ten steel balls at 130 ft/s. ES8000, ES-7525 and GW-7000 all carry LMI ratings. ACG coordinates the test documentation for HVHZ permit submittals.

Active ESWindows NOAs on ACG projects

NOA 24-0321.07 — ES8000 storefront, LMI, exp. Apr 2028. NOA 21-0914.03 — ES8000T thermal, LMI, exp. Aug 2031. NOA 23-0724.09 — ES-7000 window wall, LMI, exp. Jul 2028. GW-7000 — unitized curtainwall, Miami-Dade certified, LMI + SMI.

Questions specifiers ask before they bid.

Who is an authorized ESWindows installer in Florida?

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is an authorized ESWindows installer operating across Florida from offices in West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa. ACG installs ESWindows commercial systems — including the ES8000 storefront, ES50 window line, and ES Curtainwall — on commercial projects statewide. License: FL CGC #1531993.

What ESWindows products does ACG install on commercial projects?

ACG installs the ES8000 DirectSet aluminum storefront (DP +130/‑130 psf, LMI-rated, NOA 24-0321.07), the ES8000T thermally broken storefront (NOA 21-0914.03), the ES50 commercial window line, and ES Curtainwall systems including the ES-7525 stick curtainwall (impact and blast rated) and the GW-7000 unitized curtainwall (Miami-Dade certified, LMI + SMI at DP +100/‑140 psf). ACG also installs the ES-7000/7100 window wall (DP +125/‑150 psf) on projects requiring pre-glazed window wall systems.

Does ESWindows have Miami-Dade NOA approval?

Yes. Multiple ESWindows products carry active Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) approvals for High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) use. The ES8000 storefront holds NOA 24-0321.07 (LMI, exp. April 2028). The ES8000T thermal storefront holds NOA 21-0914.03 (LMI, exp. August 2031). The GW-7000 unitized curtainwall is Miami-Dade certified for LMI and SMI. ACG handles NOA submittal documentation for applicable Florida projects in Miami-Dade and Broward counties.

Is ACG a certified ESWindows commercial installer?

ACG is an authorized ESWindows installer for commercial projects. ACG does not claim a higher certification tier than "authorized installer" — that is the manufacturer relationship designation that authorizes ACG to install ESWindows systems with factory technical support.

What is the lead time for ESWindows on a Florida commercial project?

Lead times depend on product type, finish, and glass specification. Standard ESWindows storefront systems (ES8000 series) typically run 8–14 weeks from approved shop drawings. Curtainwall and unitized systems require longer fabrication windows. Custom finishes and glass specifications add to lead time. ACG initiates the submittal and approval process early in the project schedule to protect the glazing lead time from becoming a schedule driver. Send plans early for a lead-time estimate specific to your project's product mix.

Where is ACG's coverage area for ESWindows installation?

ACG operates from three Florida offices — West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa — and covers commercial projects statewide. Regular ESWindows project coverage includes Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Martin, St. Lucie, Indian River, Okeechobee, Lee, Collier, Charlotte, Sarasota, Manatee, Hillsborough, Pasco, Pinellas, Orange, Osceola, Seminole, Brevard, Volusia, and Duval counties.
