About the Manufacturer

About ESWindows

ESWindows (a brand of Tecnoglass) is a Miami-based manufacturer of high-performance impact-rated windows, doors, and commercial glazing systems. Founded in Colombia and operating a major U.S. distribution hub at 3550 NW 49th Street in Miami, ESWindows is one of the most widely specified impact glazing brands in Florida — particularly in HVHZ and coastal markets where the performance standards are highest.

ESWindows holds Florida Product Approvals across its full product line and Miami-Dade NOAs (Notices of Acceptance) for systems deployed in High Velocity Hurricane Zones. Their product catalog covers everything from residential single-hung windows to heavy-duty commercial curtainwall and window wall systems — all engineered for large missile impact (LMI) and small missile impact (SMI) certification.

For commercial glazing contractors like ACG, ESWindows is a primary supply partner. Their Miami warehouse enables fast lead times for Florida projects, and their regional sales team works directly with contractors on technical submittals, shop drawings, and specification compliance.

Systems ACG Installs

ESWindows Product Lines
ACG Installs

ACG installs ESWindows systems across commercial, multifamily, hospitality, and luxury residential project types. Below are the primary product lines we specify and install.

Commercial

Storefronts — ES-8000 / ES-9500

The ES-8000 is ACG's go-to ESWindows commercial storefront for retail, office, and institutional projects. Rated +130/-130 psf, pre-glazed, 5" depth. Impact-rated (LMI) with Florida Product Approval. Suitable for HVHZ applications. The ES-9500 provides an alternative 5" pre-glazed system for projects requiring additional design flexibility.

Multifamily

Window Wall — ES-7000 / ES-WW1032

ACG installs ESWindows window wall systems on multifamily and mixed-use projects. The ES-7000/7100 delivers +150/-150 psf LMI and SMI performance for high-rise coastal applications. The ES-WW1032 is a heavy-duty 10.5" depth system rated +185/-185 psf — one of the highest-performing window wall products on the market.

Residential / Mixed

Impact Sliding Doors — ES-6500 / DM-12 / SGD2020

ACG installs ESWindows impact sliding door systems on luxury residential, hospitality, and mixed-use projects. The ES-6500/6600 heavy-duty sliders are rated +120/-120 psf LMI+SMI. The DM-12 delivers +125/-165 psf. The ES-SGD2020 Prestige system provides minimal sightlines for design-forward projects.

Curtainwall

Curtainwall — ES-7525 / ES-CS1325 / GW-7000

For large-scale commercial curtainwall scope, ACG specifies ESWindows stick and unitized systems. The ES-7525 is a 7.5" stick curtainwall rated +100/-100 psf LMI+SMI+Blast. The GW-7000 unitized curtainwall carries Miami-Dade certification and is rated +100/-140 psf — suitable for the most demanding Florida coastal projects.

Doors

Commercial Swing Doors — ES-9000 / ES-9100

ESWindows commercial entrance doors are a standard specification on ACG's storefront projects. The ES-9000/9100 single swing door is rated +130/-130 psf LMI+SMI — matching the performance of the ES-8000 storefront frame. Available in a full range of hardware configurations and anodized or powder-coated finishes.

Specialty

Thermally Broken Systems — ES-8000T / ES-P252 / GW-400

When thermal performance is a specification requirement, ACG specifies ESWindows thermally broken systems. The ES-8000T is a pour-and-debridge thermally broken storefront rated +100/-120 psf with an active NOA. The GW-400 lift-and-slide system is thermal and LMI+SMI rated at +100/-130 psf.

Technical Compliance

Florida Product Approval &
HVHZ Compliance

Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) encompasses Miami-Dade and Broward counties — two of the most restrictive building code jurisdictions in the United States. Every glazing product installed in these counties requires Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) documentation, not just a Florida Product Approval.

ESWindows holds Miami-Dade NOAs on multiple systems in their product line, including the HC-200 casement, the GW-5250 window wall, the GW-7000 unitized curtainwall, and the HR-2600 sliding window. ACG's team is fluent in Florida Product Approval documentation and NOA requirements — we pull the right certificates during submittal, not during inspection.

For projects outside HVHZ, ESWindows systems carry the standard Florida Product Approval numbers required under the Florida Building Code. ACG provides complete submittal documentation — product cut sheets, NOAs, installation drawings, and testing reports — as part of the standard scope.
