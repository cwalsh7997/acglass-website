The Ocala Market

Healthcare, Logistics, and One of
Florida's Fastest-Growing Markets.

Ocala is one of Central Florida's fastest-growing commercial markets — driven by a convergence of healthcare investment, logistics and distribution expansion, the massive World Equestrian Center development, downtown revitalization, and a residential population growth rate that is reshaping Marion County's entire commercial footprint.

The healthcare cluster along SE 17th Street — HCA Florida Ocala Hospital, AdventHealth Ocala, and the expanding network of medical office buildings, specialty clinics, and outpatient facilities — represents the most significant institutional glazing demand in Marion County. Hospital-grade construction requires fire-rated glass assemblies, occupied-space scheduling discipline, and submittal packages engineered to meet both Florida Building Code and Joint Commission standards. ACG brings the same healthcare glazing expertise we apply on major hospital campuses statewide to every Ocala medical project.

The I-75 corridor through Ocala has become one of Florida's most active logistics and distribution zones — FedEx, Amazon, Chewy.com, and a growing roster of major distribution operations have chosen Ocala's central location between Tampa and Jacksonville as the hub for their Florida operations. These large-format industrial and logistics facilities require commercial storefront, curtainwall, and specialty glazing systems installed on aggressive timelines. ACG's AI-managed logistics are built for this kind of schedule precision.

The World Equestrian Center — one of the largest equestrian venues in the world, with ongoing commercial development on the mixed-use campus — has catalyzed hotel, retail, restaurant, and office development in western Marion County. The Ocala Downtown redevelopment brings additional commercial glazing demand to the urban core. The Villages' Marion County expansion area is generating steady suburban commercial construction along the SR-200 and US-27 corridors.

ACG serves Ocala from its Tampa office, 75 miles southwest via I-75 — the same corridor that connects Ocala's distribution centers to Tampa's port and logistics network. Our field teams make the Ocala market a direct extension of Tampa operations, not a distant service call.
