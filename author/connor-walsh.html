About Connor Walsh

Bio text below is a placeholder skeleton. Connor to review, revise, and approve all copy before publication.

Connor Walsh is the President and Co-founder of American Commercial Glass (FL CGC #1531993), a Florida-based commercial glazing contractor serving general contractors, developers, and owners across multifamily, hospitality, restaurant, retail, public-sector, and private-club projects. He co-founded American Commercial Glass with his sister, Rielly Walsh, who serves as CEO.

Based in Florida, Connor leads commercial glazing scope, GC and developer relationships, manufacturer partnerships, and the company's Nashville market expansion. His day-to-day work spans preconstruction estimating, submittal coordination, field problem-solving, and the manufacturer relationships that give ACG access to the six product lines it installs: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

American Commercial Glass holds Florida Certified General Contractor license #1531993. The company operates across Florida's major commercial construction markets, with service areas including West Palm Beach, Naples, Tampa, Miami-Dade, Broward, and the Treasure Coast, with a Nashville office expansion underway for 2026.

Connor's approach to project execution centers on single-source Division 08 contracting — providing the GC or developer with one accountable party for all glazing, storefront, curtainwall, automatic entrance, and framing scope on a project. This model reduces RFI volume, eliminates subcontractor coordination gaps, and gives the owner a clear warranty path across the full glass envelope.

Markets served

Connor manages ACG's commercial glazing work across the following project types throughout Florida:

  • Multifamily residential — mid-rise and high-rise apartment and condo glazing, balcony systems, amenity storefront
  • Hospitality — hotel lobbies, resort amenities, indoor-outdoor bar and dining enclosures via Euro-Wall folding glass wall systems
  • Restaurant and food & beverage — operable glass walls, storefronts, slim-profile framing for full-service and fast-casual concepts
  • Retail — high-visibility storefront systems for street-level and lifestyle centers
  • Public sector — government and institutional glazing projects requiring Florida Building Code compliance and competitive bid documentation
  • Private clubs and amenity spaces — custom folding glass walls and high-end curtainwall for club facilities

Manufacturer partnerships

American Commercial Glass maintains approved-installer relationships with six manufacturer partners. Connor manages these relationships and uses them to source the right system for each project's performance, aesthetic, and budget requirements.

  • ESWindows — commercial storefront and curtainwall systems; impact-rated Series ES425 and ES750 for Florida HVHZ and WBDR applications
  • Euro-Wall — folding and sliding glass wall systems for indoor-outdoor hospitality and amenity applications
  • PGT WinGuard — impact-resistant windows and doors with Miami-Dade NOA for HVHZ projects
  • Allegion — automatic entrance hardware and door systems integrated with glazing scope
  • TGP (Technical Glass Products) — fire-rated glazing systems where code requires fire-and-impact-rated assemblies
  • Slimpact — slim-profile impact systems for restaurant and retail applications where narrow sightlines are required
