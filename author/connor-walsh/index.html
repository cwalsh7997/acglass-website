Connor Walsh

President & Co-founder, American Commercial Glass

Connor Walsh is President and co-founder of American Commercial Glass (ACG), the Florida commercial storefront glazing contractor based in West Palm Beach. Connor leads operations, bid engineering, and GC relationships. He co-founded ACG with his wife Rielly Walsh in 2021 and has scaled the company across commercial projects spanning restaurant, hotel, medical, school, retail, and office building work.
