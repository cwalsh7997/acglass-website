Hardy World 4.0 · Melbourne, FL
The project

One glazing language, every bay.

Hardy World 4.0 is a large-scale multi-tenant retail and commercial center in Melbourne, FL — a development designed with a strong street presence and a consistent commercial glazing language across every tenant bay.

ACG was brought on to deliver the complete storefront glazing package across the entire development. With multiple tenant bays, an anchor-tenant end unit and a mix of storefront profiles across varying facade conditions, this was a coordination-heavy scope that required disciplined sequencing from shop drawings through closeout.

ACG supplied and installed all aluminum storefront systems across the center — dark-framed glazed storefronts on the main tenant facades, fixed-lite windows on the secondary bays, and the large dark glass package on the anchor end unit that dominates the corner of the building.
