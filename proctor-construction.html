Why Proctor Construction Hires ACG.

ACG is a repeat commercial glazing subcontractor for Proctor Construction across high-end multifamily, private golf clubs, and hospitality projects on Florida's East Coast.

Proctor projects demand coastal-grade envelope performance with architectural precision. ACG delivers Euro-Wall SGD2020 large-format sliders, ESWindows commercial storefront, and custom curtain wall — every opening detailed for coastal exposure, signed off in submittal, and installed by ACG field teams.

Proctor Construction website: www.proctorcc.com

Sectors: Private golf clubs, luxury multifamily, institutional, and high-end commercial.
