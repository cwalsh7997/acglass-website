The Jupiter Market

Gateway Between Palm Beach
and the Treasure Coast.

Jupiter sits at one of Florida's most strategically positioned commercial intersections — the I-95/PGA Boulevard corridor where Palm Beach County transitions into the Treasure Coast. What that means for GCs: institutional-quality tenants, affluent demographics, and a construction market that demands the same glazing precision you'd find on a Boca Raton high-rise.

The Abacoa town center continues its mixed-use evolution. Roger Dean Chevrolet Stadium and the surrounding sports campus generate hospitality and retail development. Jupiter Medical Center's ongoing campus expansion drives sustained healthcare construction. The Sandhill Crane Business Center and the Palm Beach Gardens medical city just south round out a submarket with unusually deep institutional demand.

Most inland Jupiter areas fall outside the High Velocity Hurricane Zone, but coastal and barrier island properties carry Florida product approval requirements under Florida Building Code Chapter 16. ACG's estimating process flags the applicable exposure zone on day one — no product substitutions at submittal, no surprises at inspection.

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is 20 miles south on I-95 — the closest major commercial glazing sub operating in Jupiter. We know Palm Beach County's plan review and inspection processes, and our shop drawing packages are engineered to pass the first time.

Common Questions

Jupiter Glazing FAQ.

How close is ACG's West Palm Beach office to Jupiter? +

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is approximately 20 miles south of Jupiter — the closest major commercial glazing sub in the northern Palm Beach County market. That proximity translates to faster mobilization, quicker site visits, and no travel markups buried in the bid price. We also have direct familiarity with Palm Beach County's building department and permit review process.

Is Jupiter in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone? +

Most inland Jupiter areas — including Abacoa, Jupiter Farms, and the I-95 corridor — are not in the HVHZ. However, coastal zones along the barrier island and near the Intracoastal do carry Florida product approval requirements under Florida Building Code Chapter 16. ACG's estimating team identifies the applicable wind zone for every project during scope preparation, so product selections are code-compliant from the start — not corrected at submittal or inspection.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Jupiter? +

ACG installs ESWindows, CGI Windows, YKK AP, Eurowall curtainwall, Slimpact curtainwall, TGP fire-rated glass assemblies, and Allegion entrance hardware. For healthcare projects on the Jupiter Medical Center campus, we have direct experience with the specific fire-rated and specialty glazing requirements common in hospital and medical office construction.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for a Jupiter project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and competitive pricing — within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions. The northern Palm Beach County market is competitive; we don't make GCs wait a week for a number.

Does ACG manage permits and submittals for Jupiter projects? +

Yes. ACG manages the full submittal package — shop drawings, Florida product approvals, NOA documentation where required, and engineer-of-record coordination. Our packages are designed to pass Palm Beach County plan review without revisions. We track permit status proactively and keep your super informed, so the glazing scope never becomes the bottleneck on your schedule.
