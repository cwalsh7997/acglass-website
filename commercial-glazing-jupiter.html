Commercial Glazing Solutions for Jupiter

ACG delivers the full range of architectural glazing systems for Jupiter commercial projects, from beachfront hospitality and waterfront mixed-use to corporate office parks and medical facilities in northern Palm Beach County.

Storefront Systems

Kawneer Trifab and YKK AP ES-8000/ES-9000 storefront framing for Jupiter retail, restaurant, and commercial spaces. Thermally broken frames with impact-rated glazing options meeting Florida Building Code requirements for Palm Beach County, including HVHZ standards for coastal projects.

Impact Window Systems

Florida Building Code-compliant impact window and fixed glazing systems for Jupiter commercial construction. Coastal projects within the HVHZ require Miami-Dade NOA-approved products, while inland projects meet Wind-Borne Debris Region standards with large missile impact ratings at 150-175 mph design pressures.

Window Wall Systems

Unitized and stick-built window wall systems for Jupiter mid-rise and high-rise construction. Pre-glazed panels reduce on-site labor and accelerate enclosure schedules for waterfront condominiums, hotels, and mixed-use towers along the Jupiter Inlet and Intracoastal Waterway.

Sliding & Folding Glass Walls

Fire-Rated Glazing

Automatic Entrances

Code Compliance

Florida Building Code Requirements for Jupiter

Jupiter sits in northern Palm Beach County where building code requirements vary by proximity to the coast. Coastal Jupiter falls within the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), requiring Miami-Dade NOA-approved glazing products. Inland areas fall within the Wind-Borne Debris Region with standard large-missile impact requirements.

Design wind speeds in Jupiter range from 150 to 175 mph under the Florida Building Code (FBC 7th Edition, 2023). ACG engineers every glazing package to meet or exceed the specific wind speed and code zone requirements for each project location, whether HVHZ or standard Wind-Borne Debris Region.

All ACG impact systems carry Florida Product Approvals, and HVHZ projects use exclusively Miami-Dade NOA-listed products meeting the enhanced testing protocols required in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. Our team manages shop drawings, product approvals, and engineering calculations to streamline permitting with Palm Beach County building departments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG serve corporate office parks in Jupiter?

Yes. ACG provides comprehensive glazing for Jupiter and Palm Beach Gardens office parks, including multi-building installations, tenant improvements, and Class A finishes.

What impact requirements apply to Jupiter commercial projects?

Jupiter is outside the HVHZ but may require impact-rated glazing depending on building height and location per the Florida Building Code. ACG provides appropriate systems for all Jupiter applications.

Can ACG handle beachfront hospitality projects?

Yes. ACG provides specialized glazing for Jupiter beachfront hospitality, including waterfront durability, premium finishes, and coordinated installation around operational constraints.

What commercial glazing systems does ACG install in Jupiter?

ACG installs the complete range of commercial glazing systems throughout Jupiter and northern Palm Beach County, including storefront framing (Kawneer, YKK AP), impact-rated window and curtainwall systems, unitized window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP, SAFTI FIRST), and automatic entrances (Allegion, Stanley). All systems meet Florida Building Code requirements including HVHZ standards for coastal projects.

What is the typical timeline for a Jupiter commercial glazing project?

Project timelines vary by scope and system type. Storefront projects typically take 6-10 weeks from approved shop drawings to completion. Window wall and curtainwall projects on mid-rise buildings typically run 12-20 weeks for fabrication plus 8-16 weeks for installation. ACG provides detailed schedules during preconstruction and coordinates with your project timeline to meet critical enclosure milestones.

Does ACG offer value engineering for Jupiter projects?

Yes. ACG regularly works with general contractors and developers during preconstruction to value-engineer glazing packages without sacrificing performance or aesthetics. This can include alternate framing systems, glass makeup optimization, and installation method adjustments that reduce cost while maintaining full Florida Building Code compliance for Palm Beach County HVHZ and Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements.
