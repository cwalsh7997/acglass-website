Who runs ACG

A company is its operators.

Large glazing subcontractors hide behind layers of project engineers and regional managers. The GC who needs an answer never gets the person who actually makes the call. ACG is the opposite of that. The two people running the company are the same two people answering the phone.

01 - Chief Executive Officer

Rielly Walsh

CEO · Co-founder · 51% majority owner

Education

Middle Tennessee State University - B.S., Concrete Industry Management. MTSU is one of only four schools in the United States offering the specialized CIM program - a degree built directly with the American Society of Concrete Contractors and funded by the concrete industry itself. It is the closest thing in U.S. higher education to a trade-grade construction degree.

Prior experience

The Estates at Aqualina - lead superintendent, exterior stoneworks. Aqualina is the ultra-luxury oceanfront tower in Sunny Isles Beach where the stone facade, tile balconies, and exterior finishes set the building's entire identity. Running the exterior stone package there meant coordinating specialty trades, European material lead times, and luxury-grade punch standards - on a site where nothing short of perfect gets signed off.

At ACG

Rielly is CEO, co-founder, and the company's 51% majority owner - the basis of ACG's woman-owned status. She makes the calls on strategy, growth, and long-term direction: the Naples and Tampa office expansions, the operating discipline that sets ACG apart from legacy subcontractors, and the flagship manufacturer relationships with ESWindows and Euro-Wall.

Her background in concrete and stone is the unusual edge: most glazing executives have never worked the exterior envelope from the other side. Rielly has. She reads a building the way the GC does - in layers, sequences, and interfaces.

The aviation background is not window-dressing. It is the reason ACG ships detailed submittals early, flags risk before it bites, and treats every scope the way a captain treats a flight plan - signed off, double-checked, and executed without drama.

How we operate

Two co-founders. One operating standard.

01

Owner-level accountability

Every project has a principal-in-charge. No account executive layer, no regional manager buffer. The person you call is the person making the decision.

02

Preflight discipline

Submittals, shop drawings, and long-lead orders go out early and double-checked. Problems are flagged in the estimate stage, not in the field.

Answer-first reference · Leadership facts

Questions about ACG leadership, answered.

Who is the CEO of American Commercial Glass?

Rielly Walsh is the Chief Executive Officer and co-founder of American Commercial Glass. She holds 51% majority ownership of the company, which qualifies ACG as a Woman-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE). She studied Concrete Industry Management at Middle Tennessee State University — one of only four U.S. universities offering the CIM degree — and served as lead superintendent for the exterior stoneworks at The Estates at Aqualina in Sunny Isles Beach before joining ACG in 2022.

Who is the president of American Commercial Glass?

Connor Walsh is the President of American Commercial Glass and the qualifier of record for Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993 (Florida DBPR license lookup, 2026). Before founding ACG, he built Liberty Impact Windows & Doors to over $10M in annual sales in four years. He was previously a commercial-rated private jet pilot, captaining the Bombardier Challenger 601, Cessna Citation 650, and Gulfstream GIII.

What is the relationship between Rielly Walsh and Connor Walsh?

Rielly Walsh and Connor Walsh are the two co-founders of American Commercial Glass and are married. ACG operates as a family-owned, owner-operated commercial glazing contractor, with Rielly as CEO and majority owner and Connor as President and license qualifier.

Who is the license qualifier for CGC #1531993?

Connor Walsh, President, is the qualifying agent for Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993 issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. The qualifier holds direct responsibility to the licensing board for the licensee's contracting work; ACG's qualifier is an owner, not a hired third party.

Is American Commercial Glass a woman-owned business?

Yes. American Commercial Glass is a Woman-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE) by ownership because Rielly Walsh, CEO, is the 51% majority owner. WBENC formal certification is in progress as of 2026, and ACG can provide ownership documentation to procurement teams requiring diversity sourcing verification.

What construction standards does ACG leadership work to?

ACG leadership prices and delivers commercial glazing scopes to Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2026) requirements, Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) documentation (Miami-Dade NOA search, 2026), and, where projects require, federal AT/FP standards including UFC 4-010-01 (DoD Antiterrorism via WBDG, 2024) and ASTM F1642 (Airblast test method, ASTM 2022). Both Rielly and Connor are directly involved in scope reviews on projects governed by these references.
