Commercial vs Residential

Why a commercial glazier — not a residential impact dealer.

Most West Palm Beach search results for "impact windows" surface residential installers — companies that sell single-family-home retrofit packages, often financed through My Safe Florida Home grants. That work is different from ours.

ACG installs commercial storefront, curtainwall, and window-wall systems for general contractors and developers building offices, hotels, retail centers, restaurants, government buildings, and mixed-use projects. We bid AIA contracts, provide bonds where required, deliver shop drawings stamped by Florida-licensed engineers, and pass commercial inspections on the first walk-through.

If you're a homeowner replacing windows, a residential impact dealer is the right call. If you're a GC, owner, or architect with a commercial project: send drawings and we'll respond in 48 hours.
