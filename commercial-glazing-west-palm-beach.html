Code Compliance

Palm Beach County Building Code Requirements

Palm Beach County’s coastal areas fall within the HVHZ, requiring Miami-Dade NOA-approved glazing with large missile impact ratings. Inland areas are in the Wind-Borne Debris Region with slightly reduced but still significant requirements. The Florida Building Code 7th Edition (2023) governs all construction with design winds of 150–175 mph in coastal exposures.

ACG’s deep HVHZ expertise ensures every Palm Beach County project — whether on the Island, downtown, or inland — receives the right level of protection. All systems carry Florida Product Approvals or Miami-Dade NOAs as required, with complete engineering packages for permit. Salt air corrosion resistance is standard on all coastal specifications.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Palm Beach County in the HVHZ?

Palm Beach County is not within the High Velocity Hurricane Zone but does require impact-rated or protected glazing in coastal areas based on wind speed maps and the Florida Building Code.

Does ACG serve Boca Raton and Delray Beach?

Yes. ACG provides commercial glazing services throughout Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Jupiter, and Palm Beach Gardens.

What glazing systems does ACG install in West Palm Beach?

ACG installs impact-rated storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). Systems carry Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approvals depending on HVHZ zone location.

What is the typical timeline for a Palm Beach County glazing project?

Storefront projects run 2–4 weeks for installation. Window wall and curtainwall range from 4–12 weeks. Palm Beach County permit review runs 2–4 weeks for HVHZ coastal projects and 10–15 business days inland; ACG submits complete NOA packages to expedite approval.

Does ACG handle luxury and resort glazing in Palm Beach?

Yes. ACG provides premium glazing packages for Palm Beach’s luxury market, including oceanfront condominiums, resort hotels, and high-end retail. Our team specifies premium finishes, marine-grade hardware, and architectural glass options that meet both HVHZ code requirements and luxury design standards.

Does ACG provide value engineering for Palm Beach County projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering during preconstruction to optimize glazing budgets while maintaining code compliance. This includes alternative system selections, glass makeup optimization, and installation sequencing that reduces costs without compromising HVHZ or wind-borne debris requirements.
