How a multifamily scope ships

Four phases. One contractor. No subcontractor stack.

01

Plans land

Drop drawings at info@acglass.com or the intake form. We acknowledge inside 2 business hours, bid inside 48 hours on complete RFQ packages.

02

Submittal package

Shop drawings, product data, NOA documentation, structural calcs, sealant letters — complete on first submission. 10-15 business day turnaround.

03

Material order

Aluminum extrusions, laminated glass, hardware ordered on signed contract — not permit issuance. Saves 2-3 weeks of schedule.

04

Self-performed install

ACG W-2 employees install every assembly. One contractor warrants the entire envelope. Weekly look-aheads every Friday. 24-hour RFI response.
