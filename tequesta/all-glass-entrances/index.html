All-Glass Entrances in Tequesta, FL.

Tequesta all-glass entrance work. Frameless and minimally framed all-glass entrance systems serve as the architectural moment at retail, hospitality, restaurant, office lobby, and institutional building entries in Tequesta. ACG installs all-glass entrances with the hardware (top and bottom pivots, side patches, automatic openers) coordinated as a single-source assembly with the glass make-up, the operator, and the floor and head condition. The result: an entrance that operates daily for years without alignment issues or hardware service calls.

System options in Tequesta. Common configurations: single or double frameless pivot doors, herculite-style entrances with header and floor patches, automatic sliding doors with full-glass side panels, and pivot doors with sensor-operated openers. Tequesta all-glass entrances must meet FBC Section 2406 safety glazing requirements and CPSC 16 CFR 1201 Category II.

ACG provides material + labor + submittal documentation + operator coordination in the all-in number. Lead time is 6-10 weeks from approved shop drawings, with 1-2 days of field installation.

Standards & Documentation

Hardware, glass, and operator coordination.

Hardware sources. ACG specifies all-glass entrance hardware from Allegion (Dorma, FSB), CRL, and TGP depending on the project. Pivots are bottom-mounted floor closers or top-pivot offset depending on the floor condition and operating cycle. For high-traffic Tequesta retail or restaurant entries we specify floor closers rated for daily operation cycles in the 200,000+ range.

Glass make-up. Tequesta all-glass entrances use tempered or tempered-laminated configurations meeting safety glazing requirements per FBC Section 2406. ACG specifies the appropriate make-up and provides Florida Product Approval (FL#) or NOA documentation as required.

Operator and access control. ACG coordinates with the GC electrical and access control contractors on automatic operator installation, sensor placement, and access control integration. We pull operator specs from the manufacturer and verify power and control runs in pre-construction so the entrance commissions on first try.
