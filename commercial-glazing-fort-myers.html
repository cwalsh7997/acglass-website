Commercial Glazing Services in Fort Myers

Full-service glazing solutions for Fort Myers and Lee County's growing commercial market — from downtown riverfront projects to suburban medical campuses.

Impact Storefront Systems

FBC-approved impact storefront for Fort Myers' commercial and retail properties. ES-8000 and ES-9000 systems with laminated impact glass meeting Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements for Lee County.

Curtainwall & Window Wall

High-performance curtainwall and window wall systems for Fort Myers' waterfront developments and mid-rise mixed-use projects. Engineered for coastal wind loads and salt-air durability along the Caloosahatchee River corridor.

Sliding & Folding Glass Walls

Eurowall MultiSlide and VistaFold systems for Fort Myers' resort hospitality and luxury waterfront projects. Impact-rated operable walls maximizing river and Gulf views while meeting Florida Building Code.

Impact Windows

Florida Product Approved impact windows for Fort Myers commercial, institutional, and multifamily projects. Laminated glass assemblies engineered for Lee County's coastal wind zone with superior thermal performance.

Fire-Rated Glazing

TGP and SAFTI fire-rated glazing for Fort Myers healthcare facilities, educational buildings, and commercial corridors. Complete fire-rated assemblies meeting UL certification requirements.

Automatic Entrances

Allegion and Stanley automatic entrance systems for Fort Myers' medical centers, hotels, and commercial buildings. Sliding, swinging, and revolving door systems with ADA compliance and hurricane-rated options.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG have industrial glazing experience in Fort Myers?

Yes. ACG installs curtainwall, storefronts, and window systems for Fort Myers industrial facilities, manufacturing plants, and logistics centers. We understand the durability and specification requirements for industrial applications.

What impact requirements apply to Fort Myers commercial projects?

Impact requirements vary by location within Lee County. Coastal areas and certain wind zones require impact-rated glazing per the Florida Building Code. ACG provides code-compliant systems for all Lee County wind zones.

Can ACG handle large office park installations?

Yes. ACG has extensive experience with multi-building office park projects, including phased installations, coordinated delivery schedules, and consistent quality across multiple structures.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Fort Myers?

ACG installs impact storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet Lee County Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements.

What is the typical timeline for a Fort Myers commercial glazing project?

Timelines vary by scope. Lee County permitting typically takes 2-4 weeks for commercial glazing. Standard storefront projects run 6-10 weeks from approved submittals; curtainwall and window wall systems require 12-16 weeks. ACG provides detailed schedules during pre-construction.

Does ACG offer value engineering for Fort Myers projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering during pre-construction to optimize glazing specifications for Lee County's code requirements and coastal conditions. This includes system alternatives that meet performance requirements at competitive pricing, energy code optimization, and coordination with architects.
