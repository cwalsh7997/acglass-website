Fort Myers Is Southwest Florida's
Commercial Hub.

Fort Myers is driving commercial construction across all of Southwest Florida - and the demand for qualified glazing subs who understand Lee County's building requirements has never been stronger. Downtown Fort Myers redevelopment continues to add Class-A office and mixed-use density. The US-41 commercial corridor runs active retail and medical projects year-round.

Common Questions

Fort Myers Glazing FAQ.

How close is ACG's nearest office to Fort Myers? +

ACG's Naples office is approximately 30 miles south of Fort Myers - making it the closest major commercial glazing sub office in the Southwest Florida region. ACG crews mobilize to Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Estero, and Gateway projects regularly, with no out-of-market travel premiums. We're not dispatching from Miami or Tampa - Naples is our home base for Southwest Florida.

What's ACG's track record of completing projects in Lee County? +

ACG has completed multiple commercial glazing projects in Lee County across a variety of project types. Our Lee County portfolio includes the HCA Cape Coral Emergency department (healthcare, impact systems), Estero Vista (multifamily, window wall), and Ilumina at Gulf Coast Town Center (commercial, curtainwall). These projects span the range of glazing scopes Lee County GCs bring us - from complex hospital work to large-scale multifamily.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Lee County? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. Southwest Florida's coastal exposure and wind zone designations require systems with the correct Florida product approvals and NOA documentation - ACG specifies products that meet Lee County's building code requirements and passes plan review complete the first time. System recommendations are driven by the project spec and design intent, not by what's most convenient to stock.

How does Lee County's permit process work for commercial glazing? +

Lee County's building department requires complete Florida product approval documentation, engineer-of-record coordination for structural glazing, and wind load compliance calculations for all glazing systems. ACG manages the full submittal package - shop drawings, Florida product approvals, NOA documentation, and EOR coordination. Our packages are built to Lee County plan review standards and are submitted complete, minimizing back-and-forth that costs your schedule.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for a Fort Myers or Cape Coral project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope - system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown - within 48 hours. ACG's estimating process eliminates the manual bottlenecks that slow down other glazing subs. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions, no three-week wait for a number.
