What This Scope Includes

Life safety is non-negotiable.

Fire-rated glass is one of the most technically specific glazing scopes on any project. ACG delivers the full assembly — glass, framing, doors, hardware, and UL documentation — under one accountable subcontractor.

  • 20-minute fire-protective glass (openings less than 100 SI)
  • 45-, 60-, 90-, and 120-minute fire-resistive glass assemblies
  • UL-listed fire-rated framing systems (steel and aluminum-clad)
  • Fire-rated doors and transom assemblies
  • Full-wall fire-resistive glazed partitions
  • Temperature-rise-rated (CPT) assemblies for stair enclosures
  • UL listing documentation and submittal packages
  • Coordination with fire-stopping and continuity-of-protection details

FAQ

Questions GCs ask before they bid.

20-min vs. 60-min vs. 90-min — how is the right rating specified?+

The rating is specified by the architect per Florida Building Code occupancy classification and wall location. Corridors typically require 20-min. Exit enclosures and area-separation walls typically require 60–90-min. Stairwells and high-hazard separations can require 90–120-min. ACG reads the spec and confirms the rating per opening.

What is the difference between fire-protective and fire-resistive glass?+

Fire-protective glass (20–45 min) limits flame and smoke spread. Fire-resistive glass (60–120 min) also blocks radiant heat and is tested to ASTM E119. Fire-resistive is more expensive but required in certain applications (stair enclosures, high-hazard separations).

Does ACG handle the fire-rated doors too?+

Yes. ACG installs the full assembly — glass, framing, door, and UL-listed hardware — under one scope. Mixing subs on fire-rated assemblies is how UL listings get broken.

Is TGP the only fire-rated system ACG uses?+

TGP is our primary fire-rated partner. We also install SAFTI-FIRST products where specified. Both carry active UL listings for Florida commercial applications.

How does the AHJ inspection process work?+

ACG provides the UL listing documentation and fire-rating assembly sheets to the AHJ at rough-in and final inspection. If the AHJ questions anything, ACG coordinates directly with TGP or SAFTI engineering to resolve on site.
