TGP Fire-Rated Systems

TGP Glass. Certified
Ratings. Zero Shortcuts.

Technical Glass Products (TGP) manufactures the most comprehensive range of fire-rated glass and framing systems in North America. Pilkington Pyrostop and Pyrodur products. SuperLite II-XL. FireFloor. The full spectrum from 20-minute fire protection ratings through 120-minute fire resistance ratings.

The distinction between fire protection (W rating) and fire resistance (F and T ratings) matters enormously for code compliance. A W-rated assembly limits flame spread but doesn't limit radiant heat transmission — in many locations, a T-rated assembly that limits surface temperature is required. ACG's project managers understand these distinctions and help GCs specify the right system for each application.

IBC and Florida Building Code provisions for fire-rated glazing are complex — opening size limitations, distance from sprinkler heads, integration with fire-rated wall assemblies, and coordination with fire door scheduling all affect what's permissible. We work with your architect and AHJ to get the right assembly specified before the submittal process begins.
