What We're Ready to Be Quoted On

Topical expertise for journalists and researchers.

Connor Walsh is available for on-record commentary in the areas below. All statements are grounded in active field experience and current code practice.

Florida HVHZ glazing — High-Velocity Hurricane Zone requirements under FBC 8th Edition; Miami-Dade and Broward County NOA documentation; curtainwall, window wall, and storefront in HVHZ conditions.
Hurricane impact code compliance — ASCE 7-22 wind load design, Florida Product Approval (FPA) documentation, design pressure ratings, impact vs. non-impact code paths for commercial construction.
FBC Risk Category IV essential facilities — Glazing specification and installation for hospitals, emergency operations centers, fire stations, and emergency shelters under the Florida Building Code's highest risk category.
AI in construction estimating and operations — ACG operates custom AI tools (Sub.ai, jobcost.ai) for estimating workflow and job cost management. Practical field perspective on where AI adds traction and where it doesn't.
Single-subcontractor Division 08 delivery — How one subcontractor handling the full Division 08 scope (storefront, curtainwall, windows, doors, hardware) reduces coordination risk for GCs and owners.
Tennessee non-HVHZ commercial glazing (post-Q3 2026) — IBC 2018 (TN amendments), IECC Climate Zone 4A, ASCE 7-16 at V=115 mph. Thermal-broken aluminum and high-performance IGU systems for Middle Tennessee. Nashville office opens Q3 2026.
Commercial folding-door installation — Euro-Wall specialty — ACG is a Florida authorized installer for Euro-Wall folding and sliding door systems; impact-rated and HVHZ configurations available with Florida Product Approval where applicable.
Restaurant and F&B glazing — High-traffic commercial storefronts, open-wall dining concepts, indoor/outdoor threshold design for restaurant and food & beverage venues in Florida hospitality markets.
