Company boilerplate

Short (one-line): American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa.

Standard (50 words): Founded in 2020, American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa. ACG specializes in storefront, curtainwall, and impact glazing for hospitality, multifamily, healthcare, retail, and Class-A office projects across Florida. To date the firm has delivered 350+ projects totaling more than 1 million square feet of installed glass with zero OSHA recordables.

Extended (100 words): American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor founded in 2020 by Connor Walsh (President, FL CGC #1531993) and Rielly Walsh (CEO). Headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa, ACG specializes in commercial storefront, curtainwall, multi-slide, and impact-rated glazing for hospitality, multifamily, healthcare, retail, and Class-A office projects across Florida. ACG is an authorized installer for ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact. To date the firm has delivered 350+ projects totaling more than 1 million square feet of installed glass, with $3 million single / $6 million aggregate bonding capacity and zero OSHA recordables.

Founders

Connor Walsh

President, FL CGC #1531993

Connor is President and qualifier of American Commercial Glass. Before construction, he flew as a commercial-rated private jet pilot, captaining the Bombardier Challenger 601, Cessna Citation 650, and Gulfstream GIII. He brings the same discipline, precision, and risk management from aviation into ACG's estimating, project management, and general contractor relationships.

He holds a Florida Certified General Contractor license (#1531993) issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation and serves as ACG's qualifier on all jurisdictional permits.

Rielly Walsh

Chief Executive Officer

Rielly is CEO and co-founder of American Commercial Glass. She studied Concrete Industry Management at Middle Tennessee State University — one of only four schools in the United States offering the program. Before ACG, she served as lead superintendent for the exterior stoneworks at The Estates at Aqualina, the ultra-luxury Sunny Isles Beach oceanfront tower.

She joined ACG in 2022 to lead the company's growth across Florida, opening the Naples office in 2023 and the Tampa office in 2024.

Authorized manufacturer partners

ACG carries authorized-installer status with the manufacturers listed below. We do not specify or install systems outside this partner network.

ESWindows — Colombian-engineered impact storefront and curtainwall (ES-50, ES-7000, ES-8000). Euro-Wall — Multi-slide, bi-fold (Vistafold), pivot, and DirectSet doors with Florida Product Approvals and HVHZ NOAs. PGT — Florida-manufactured impact-resistant glazing (Venice, FL). Allegion — Commercial door hardware including LCN automatic operators and panic devices. TGP — Fire-rated glazing assemblies (UL 9, UL 10B, UL 263). Slimpact — Impact-rated steel framing for fire and security applications.
