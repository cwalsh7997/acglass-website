The Project

Estero Vista is a large-scale luxury apartment community in Fort Myers, FL - a multi-building campus built by Envirostruct featuring four-story residential buildings, a clubhouse and leasing office, detached garages, and a central pond and amenity area.

Projects at this scale - multiple buildings, hundreds of units, a leasing office with its own glazing requirements - demand a glazing sub that can manage volume without letting quality slip. ACG delivered the complete impact window and door package across every structure on the campus, coordinating phased installation with Envirostruct's construction sequencing.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed impact windows and doors across all residential buildings - the horizontal slider windows in each unit, the sliding glass doors on every balcony, and the fixed windows throughout. On the clubhouse, ACG handled the full storefront package including the glass entry doors you see from the interior: clean frames, smooth operation, and a finished product that matches the premium positioning of the community.

Every unit consistent. Every balcony door aligned. Every window sealed. At a development this size, consistency across the run is the job.

The glazing package on Estero Vista included community center envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows, Euro-Wall - manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Estero location pulled supervision out of our Naples office.
