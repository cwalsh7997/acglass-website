The Project

Atlantic Fields Golf House is the social and dining centerpiece of the Atlantic Fields private golf community in Hobe Sound, FL — a Toll Brothers development set around a championship course. The building is a two-structure complex: the main Golf House with its signature window wall dining room, and a separate pro shop. Both designed to open completely to the Florida landscape.

Built by Proctor Construction, this project required ACG to execute across multiple glazing systems — a feat of coordination that most glazing subs aren't equipped for. Window wall. Operable lift-and-slide panels. Storefront entrances. Interior glazing. We handled all of it.

The Window Wall

The main dining room features a full slab-to-slab window wall along the golf course elevation. The lower panels are a custom operable lift system — each glass panel rises vertically on a chain-and-sprocket mechanism, operated by hand crank, and locks in the fully open position to convert the dining room into an open-air pavilion overlooking the fairway.

When open, there is no boundary between the dining room and the terrace. The golf course is the fourth wall. That's what this system does when it's specified, coordinated, and installed correctly.

The Pro Shop

The pro shop features a large pivot-and-fold entry system — a wood-clad panel that swings open to create a wide-open retail entry connecting the shop directly to the exterior terrace and practice area. The surrounding storefronts are full-height glass with clean white aluminum framing consistent with the main building's architectural language.
