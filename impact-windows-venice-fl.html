The Market

Venice is a commercial glazing market.

Venice covers a diverse commercial market from the Venice Island historic downtown (Northern Italian-inspired architecture, boutique retail, restaurants) to Wellen Park (master-planned mixed-use with Atlanta Braves Spring Training complex) to Casey Key and Nokomis Gulf-front hospitality. The commercial footprint has expanded significantly in recent years with Wellen Park development and North Port residential growth spilling into commercial demand.

Venice's coastal exposure is significant. Hurricane Milton (October 2024) and Hurricane Ian (2022) both impacted Sarasota County's southern shoreline. Casey Key and the Gulf-facing portions of Venice Island are Exposure Category D; inland Venice and Wellen Park are Exposure C. All commercial projects require Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance, Florida Product Approval documentation, and appropriate DP ratings.

ACG serves Venice from our Tampa office (65 miles north) and Naples office (60 miles south). The ESWindows flagship partnership provides commercial storefront, window wall, and sliding door systems with factory pre-glazed capability. For the Venice hospitality market — Casey Key resorts, Venice Island boutique hotels, Nokomis beachfront — we install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems. Downtown Venice historic preservation work requires a commercial glazier who understands both modern impact standards and historic district permitting nuance.

Venice FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Does Venice require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. The City of Venice and Sarasota County both sit in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening. Venice Island, Casey Key, and the Gulf-facing portions are Exposure Category D with the highest design pressures; inland Venice and Wellen Park are Exposure C.

What exposure category applies to Venice commercial projects?

Venice commercial projects fall in Exposure C or D depending on location. Venice Island (downtown) and Gulf-front Casey Key / Nokomis are Exposure D with DP60-80+ typical. Inland Venice, Wellen Park, and North Port are Exposure C with lower but still substantial design pressures. Florida Product Approval documentation must confirm the appropriate exposure.

How much do commercial impact windows cost in Venice?

Venice commercial impact window pricing runs $65-$180 per square foot installed. Inland projects (Wellen Park, North Port) at the lower end of the range; coastal Venice Island and Casey Key 10-15% higher due to Exposure D requirements and salt-air-tolerant finishes. A mid-size retail rollout: $170,000-$360,000 installed.

Can ACG handle historic district permitting on Venice Island?

Yes. Venice Island historic downtown has specific architectural and permitting requirements. ACG coordinates with the general contractor and owner's architect to spec impact-rated glazing that satisfies both FBC requirements and historic preservation design standards. Our commercial focus means we've handled historic district work elsewhere in Florida including downtown West Palm Beach.

Does ACG serve Wellen Park specifically?

Yes. Wellen Park is one of the fastest-growing master-planned communities in Florida and the commercial build-out — town center, medical, hospitality, multifamily — is an active market. ACG serves Wellen Park from our Tampa and Naples offices with full commercial glazing capability, ESWindows product depth, and the documentation rigor large-scale master-planned community GCs expect.
