ESWindows

Impact-rated window and door systems engineered to Florida Building Code requirements with no compromise. ESWindows' product testing and HVHZ approvals represent the performance standard for coastal projects where hurricane protection meets design intent.

Why ACG specifies them: ESWindows delivers industry-leading impact ratings verified against Florida's strictest code requirements. Their manufacturing consistency, documented testing protocols, and responsive engineering support align with ACG's zero-tolerance approach.

Typical applications: Multifamily coastal residential, mixed-use commercial in high-wind zones, premium hospitality with HVHZ requirements, any envelope where impact rating is code-mandated or design-critical.

Eurowall

European precision engineering applied to window wall and curtainwall systems. Eurowall's thermal performance, tight envelope integration, and manufacturing tolerances set the benchmark for complex commercial facades demanding structural integrity and architectural clarity.

Why ACG specifies them: Eurowall systems are engineered for high-complexity projects where thermal performance and design precision are non-negotiable. ACG values their engineering rigor, product modularity, and ability to solve complex envelope integration challenges.

Typical applications: High-rise residential towers, commercial office with stringent thermal codes, premium mixed-use developments, hospitality where envelope performance and aesthetics demand excellence.

Allegion

Global leader in automatic entrance and access control systems. Allegion's portfolio spans sliding, swinging, and revolving doors engineered for durability, ADA compliance, and seamless integration with building access and fire safety systems.

Why ACG specifies them: Allegion's comprehensive product range and access control integration capabilities solve complex entry requirements across institutional, healthcare, and hospitality sectors. ACG values their reliability and parts availability across the Southeast.

Slimpact

Why ACG specifies them: Premium projects increasingly demand impact rating without sacrificing design language. Slimpact eliminates that conflict. ACG specifies Slimpact when projects require Florida's strictest wind and debris ratings while maintaining clean, modern sightlines.

Technical Glass Products

Industry standard for fire-rated glazing assemblies. TGP's tested and labeled systems set the performance benchmark for transparent fire protection, from 20-minute to 2-hour ratings across fire-protective and fire-resistive applications.

Why ACG specifies them: Fire-rated glazing represents the most code-sensitive scope in commercial construction. TGP's rigorous testing protocols, comprehensive labeling, and documentation provide confidence across architects, building officials, and insurers.

Typical applications: Interior corridor glazing, stairwell enclosures, exterior fire-rated walls, institutional facilities, healthcare buildings, any project where building code mandates transparent fire protection.
