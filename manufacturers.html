Our Approach

Why Our Partnerships Matter

Factory-Certified Installation

ACG crews maintain active factory certifications with every manufacturer. Your warranty is valid, your installation meets spec, no gaps between engineered and built.

Direct Engineering Support

Our manufacturer relationships are collaborative. ACG works directly with each partner's engineering teams to resolve challenges before they reach the field.

Supply Chain Reliability

Strategic partnerships mean priority access to materials, accurate lead times, and the purchasing power to keep your project on schedule.

Code Compliance Confidence

Every system comes with complete documentation — test reports, NOA approvals, FL product approvals, and fire ratings. Pass inspection the first time.

Competitive Pricing

Volume relationships translate directly to competitive pricing. ACG's purchasing power means premium systems without premium markups.

Full Warranty Protection

Factory-certified installation activates full manufacturer warranty. Protected by both manufacturer product warranty and ACG workmanship guarantee.

Need System Selection Guidance?

ACG's manufacturer partnerships mean your project gets the right system — specified correctly, sourced efficiently, and installed to factory standards.
