Sheet M-501 · Related questions

Asked by GCs and architects.

Q-01

Which manufacturers does ACG install?

Systems from seven partners: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP (Technical Glass Products), Slimpact, and Aldora — each matched to the project's performance, code, and hurricane requirements across Florida and the Southeast.

Q-02

Is ACG a PGT installer?

Yes. ACG installs PGT impact-rated commercial systems across Florida, matching products to each project's wind-load and HVHZ requirements as FL CGC #1531993.

Q-03

Does ACG install TGP fire-rated glass?

Yes. ACG installs TGP fire-rated glass and framing on commercial projects across Florida — each fire-protective or fire-resistive assembly matched to the opening's required rating, with sequencing coordinated through Procore.
