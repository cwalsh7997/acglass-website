What drives the city-to-city variance

  • Wind code zone (HVHZ vs WBDR vs standard FBC) drives 20-30% of variance via glass spec and engineering requirements.
  • Labor pool size and rates — South Florida glazier hourly rates run 12-18% above Tampa, 20-25% above Orlando.
  • Material logistics — Miami and Fort Lauderdale have local ESWindows/ESWindows/ESWindows stocking distributors. Pensacola and Tallahassee ship from Birmingham or Atlanta.
  • Brand-quality finish expectations — Worth Avenue and Design District retail pulls finishes 30-50% above standard commercial.
  • Permit cycle time — longer permit cycles in HVHZ counties tie up bid validity periods and influence bid pricing.
