East Hillsborough Market

Brandon and Riverview:
Hillsborough County's Fastest-Growing Suburbs.

The eastern Hillsborough corridor from Brandon to Riverview has been one of the most active commercial construction markets in the Tampa Bay area for the past several years. Brandon Town Center and the surrounding retail and commercial district continue to attract new tenants and redevelopment. The Bloomingdale Avenue corridor has generated a steady flow of medical office, retail, and restaurant construction. US-301 through Riverview has become a major commercial spine, with strip centers, stand-alone retail, medical facilities, and light industrial all active in the market.

Riverview's residential population growth has driven parallel commercial expansion — the FishHawk Ranch and FishHawk Trails areas have generated substantial demand for neighborhood commercial, medical, and mixed-use development. Sun Coast Community Health Center, BayCare outpatient facilities, and multiple medical office buildings have added healthcare glazing work to what was already a strong retail-driven market.

ACG's Tampa office is within 20–30 minutes of the Brandon commercial core and serves the full east Hillsborough market. We're experienced with Hillsborough County Building Services' permit and inspection process — our submittals are complete at first submission, which means plan review doesn't become a schedule delay for your project. We know what the county needs to see, and we deliver it up front.

FAQ

Commercial Glazing in Brandon & Riverview —
Common Questions

Does ACG handle Hillsborough County permits for Brandon and Riverview projects?

Yes. ACG is a licensed glazing contractor in Florida and manages the full permitting process through Hillsborough County's Building Services department. We submit complete packages — shop drawings, Florida Product Approval (NOA) documentation, and engineering calculations — at first submission to avoid review cycles that delay your project schedule.

What's ACG's lead time for pricing a Brandon or Riverview commercial glazing project?

Send us your plans and we return a detailed scope with system recommendations and itemized pricing within 48 hours. We don't do vague allowances or ballpark numbers — you get a scope specific enough for your estimating team to carry with confidence. Brandon and Riverview projects fall within our Tampa office's primary service area, so response time is fast.

Can ACG handle both storefront and curtainwall on the same Brandon or Riverview project?

Yes. Many commercial projects in the Brandon and Riverview corridor mix storefront systems at grade level with curtainwall or window wall on upper floors. ACG provides full-scope glazing packages — we coordinate all systems, manage the single submittal, and deliver one installation team. Working with one glazing sub rather than splitting the scope reduces coordination overhead for your PM and simplifies inspection scheduling.
