The Bonita Springs & Estero Market

At the Growth Epicenter of
Southwest Florida.

Bonita Springs and Estero sit at the intersection of Lee and Collier counties — and at the commercial growth epicenter of Southwest Florida. The Coconut Point mixed-use corridor, Miromar Outlets and the Three Oaks Pkwy commercial district, the I-75 interchange developments, and the luxury multifamily explosion along the Gulf Coast have all converged to make this one of the most active submarkets in the state.

The healthcare corridor is a particular driver: NCH Bonita Springs and Lee Health Coconut Point have anchored sustained institutional construction that demands fire-rated glass assemblies, impact-rated curtainwall, and specialty glazing at a hospital-grade level. The US-41 mixed-use development — retail pads, professional office, medical office, and multifamily stacked along a single arterial — creates high-density glazing demand across every product category.

ACG has executed here, not just prospected here. The Causeway Building and Imperial Crossings are two completed Bonita Springs projects in our portfolio — real evidence of delivery in this specific submarket. Our Naples office is 15 miles south, handling operations for both Lee and Collier county work from a single base.

Ben Hill Griffin Parkway's corridor connecting Estero to Bonita Beach Road is one of the most active commercial development corridors in the region. ACG knows it well.
