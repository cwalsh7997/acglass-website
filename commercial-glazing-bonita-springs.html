Code Compliance

Florida Building Code Requirements for Bonita Springs

Bonita Springs and southern Lee County fall within the Wind-Borne Debris Region under the Florida Building Code (FBC 7th Edition, 2023). All commercial glazing must meet impact-resistance standards or be protected by approved missile-impact systems.

Design wind speeds in Bonita Springs range from 130 to 150 mph, with higher speeds along Bonita Beach and Lovers Key barrier islands. ACG engineers every glazing package to meet or exceed the specific wind speed requirements for each project location.

All ACG impact systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet the large-missile impact test (ASTM E1886/E1996) required throughout the Wind-Borne Debris Region. Our team manages shop drawings, product approvals, and engineering calculations to streamline permitting with the Lee County building department.

Does ACG specialize in luxury residential glazing?

Can ACG handle resort property renovations?

What are the impact requirements for Bonita Springs?

Bonita Springs and Estero projects generally require impact-rated glazing per Florida Building Code. ACG provides Miami-Dade-approved impact systems for all Bonita Springs commercial projects.

What commercial glazing systems does ACG install in Bonita Springs?

ACG installs the complete range of commercial glazing systems throughout Bonita Springs and southern Lee County, including storefront framing (Kawneer, YKK AP), impact-rated window and curtainwall systems, unitized window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP, SAFTI FIRST), and automatic entrances (Allegion, Stanley). ACG provides detailed schedules during preconstruction and coordinates with your project timeline to meet critical enclosure milestones.
