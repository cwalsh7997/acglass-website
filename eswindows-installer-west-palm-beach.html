ESWindows Product Line

Three systems, every commercial application.

ESWindows manufactures a focused commercial product line designed for the Florida market. Unlike residential window manufacturers who have added commercial products as an afterthought, ESWindows' product engineering is built around commercial building codes, large-format openings, and the Florida Product Approval documentation requirements that general contractors and building departments require.

ESWindows 8000-series is the commercial storefront platform. It is available pre-glazed at the factory or field-glazed, and it covers design pressures from DP30 through DP70 standard, with HVHZ variants available for Miami-Dade and Broward projects. The factory pre-glazed option is the most significant differentiator in the ESWindows line: it eliminates field-glazing labor, reduces the risk of field installation defects, and compresses the schedule on storefront packages where the glazing subcontractor is frequently on the critical path.

ESWindows 9000-series is the commercial window wall system, rated DP50-DP80+ for both punched opening and continuous facade run applications. It is the correct system for mid-rise multifamily, Class A office towers, and commercial projects where the architectural intent calls for a flush, thermally broken facade rather than a structural silicone storefront appearance. Design pressures through DP80+ make the 9000-series viable on high-exposure coastal projects in Palm Beach County.

ESWindows SGD-2020 is the Prestige impact sliding glass door. At DP +90/-150 psf with LMI+SMI rating and FL Product Approval FL22267, it exceeds the performance of most competing impact sliding door products. For Palm Beach County projects — where amenity floor builds are high-specification and owners expect products with visible quality — the SGD-2020 occupies a category above standard contractor-grade sliding doors.

Does West Palm Beach require impact-rated glazing for commercial buildings?

Yes. Palm Beach County falls within Florida's wind-borne debris region. The Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved protection systems on all commercial openings. Coastal sites along the Intracoastal or Atlantic carry Exposure D design pressures, which require higher-rated systems than inland sites.

Factory pre-glazing shifts quality control to a controlled factory environment, reduces field labor hours, and delivers units that are ready to set on the day they arrive.
