The Boynton Beach Market

One of Mid-PBC's Most Active
Commercial Markets.

Boynton Beach sits in the heart of Palm Beach County's commercial development activity. The Renaissance Commons mixed-use corridor has been one of the county's most sustained multi-phase construction projects - a live-work-play development that has generated years of glazing work across retail, residential-over-retail, office, and hospitality. The Boynton Beach Mall redevelopment represents one of the region's most significant retail-to-mixed-use transformation projects, with a construction pipeline that will extend for years.

Healthcare is a powerful demand driver in Boynton Beach. Bethesda Hospital - now part of Bethesda Health - and JFK Medical Center anchor a healthcare corridor along Congress Avenue that has generated sustained institutional construction: medical office buildings, outpatient facilities, imaging centers, and the specialty glazing specifications they demand. Fire-rated assemblies, security glazing, and impact-rated curtainwall are recurring requirements.

Boynton Beach is not in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. That designation is Miami-Dade and Broward only. Boynton Beach, including the barrier island and inland areas, is a wind-borne debris region, so Florida Product Approval is the code path. ACG's estimating team identifies the applicable wind zone at scope preparation.

ACG's West Palm Beach HQ is 12 miles north - the closest major commercial glazing sub in the mid-PBC market.

Common Questions

Boynton Beach Glazing FAQ.

How close is ACG's West Palm Beach office to Boynton Beach? +

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is approximately 12 miles north of Boynton Beach on I-95 - the closest major commercial glazing sub in the mid-Palm Beach County market. That proximity means same-day site visits, faster mobilization, and direct familiarity with Palm Beach County's building department and permit review process.

Is Boynton Beach in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone? +

Boynton Beach is not in the HVHZ. That designation is Miami-Dade and Broward only. The barrier island and inland Boynton Beach are a wind-borne debris region, so Florida Product Approval is the code path. ACG identifies the correct zone for every project before the scope is assembled.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Boynton Beach? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall curtainwall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. For healthcare projects at Bethesda Hospital and JFK Medical Center, we have direct experience with the fire-rated and specialty glazing requirements common in hospital and medical office construction.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for Boynton Beach? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope - system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown - within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions. The mid-Palm Beach County market is competitive; we don't make GCs wait a week for a number.

Does ACG handle permits and submittals for Boynton Beach projects? +

Yes. ACG manages the complete submittal package - shop drawings, Florida product approvals, HVHZ NOA documentation where required, and engineer-of-record coordination. Our packages are designed to pass Palm Beach County plan review without revisions, keeping your Bethesda Hospital, Renaissance Commons, or Congress Ave project on schedule.
