Wave Haven Cocoa Beach

The complete ESWindows impact glazing package across a two-structure mixed-use development on the Space Coast — dark-framed commercial storefront plus the full residential window and door package, under Certified General Contractors.

Location Cocoa Beach, FL Sector Mixed-use GC Certified General Contractors Systems ESWindows
01
Sheet A-101 · Project

Two building types. One glazing sub.

Wave Haven is a mixed-use development in Cocoa Beach, FL — a two-structure project combining a commercial building and a row of residential townhome units on the Space Coast. The commercial building anchors the development with bold architecture: an orange-cedar wood-clad upper facade, dark storefront glazing at street level, and a second-floor covered terrace.

Certified General Contractors brought ACG in to deliver the complete ESWindows impact glazing package across both structures — a scope that required coordinating different system types and installation sequences across two distinct building types on the same project.

On the commercial building, ACG installed ESWindows dark-framed storefront systems at ground level — full-height glass entries, fixed lites, and the glazed storefront wrapping the retail and dining spaces. On the townhomes, the complete impact window and door package: dark-framed fixed windows on the upper and lower floors, sliding glass doors opening to the private balconies, and impact-rated entry doors.
