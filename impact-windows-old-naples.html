The Market

Old Naples is a commercial glazing market.

Old Naples is Naples' most historic and exclusive district, extending from the Naples Pier south through Port Royal and Aqualane Shores. Commercial density is intentionally limited - boutique retail on Third Street South, private clubs, fine dining, and historic inn-style hospitality. The commercial grammar is architectural subtlety - details that respect the district's mid-century modern and Florida vernacular architecture while meeting current Florida Building Code performance.

Old Naples sits in Exposure Category D along Gulf Shore Boulevard and Gulf-facing elevations. Third Street South and inland portions are typically Exposure C. Hurricane Ian (2022) brought major storm surge through Gulf Shore Boulevard and the Pier area, and Hurricane Milton (2024) added further damage. Commercial rebuild and hardening work across Old Naples requires full FBC 8th Edition compliance with documented Florida Product Approvals and DP-appropriate ratings.

ACG serves Old Naples from our Naples office with ESWindows flagship product depth - including the architectural-grade anodized and PVDF finishes that match Old Naples' material palette. For hospitality amenity projects where operable walls are required at private club and restaurant openings, we install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems - the same product used on Atlantic Fields.

Old Naples FAQ

Common questions - answered.

Does Old Naples require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. The City of Naples sits in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening. Historic district projects still require full FBC compliance - historic preservation standards address architectural detail, not performance.

What exposure category applies to Old Naples commercial projects?

Old Naples Gulf Shore Boulevard, the Pier area, and Gulf-facing elevations are Exposure Category D. Third Street South, Cambier Park, and inland Old Naples are generally Exposure C. DP ratings range from DP40-DP60 inland to DP60-DP80+ Gulf-facing. Florida Product Approval documentation must confirm the appropriate exposure.

How did Hurricanes Ian and Milton affect Old Naples commercial buildings?

Hurricane Ian (September 2022) brought major storm surge through Gulf Shore Boulevard and the Pier area, with significant commercial damage. Hurricane Milton (October 2024) added further impact. The rebuild and hardening cycle across Old Naples has emphasized full FBC 8th Edition compliance, Exposure D documentation, and in many cases HVHZ-level Miami-Dade NOA products for additional resilience.

Can ACG coordinate with Old Naples architects and historic district requirements?

Yes. ACG works through the general contractor and owner's architect on historic district and architecturally sensitive projects. Our ESWindows partnership provides the architectural-grade finish options and minimal-sightline frame profiles that Old Naples architects typically specify. Full FBC documentation packages on every project.
