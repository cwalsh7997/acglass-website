The Project

Storage King USA operates self-storage facilities across the country. The Winter Haven location features a two-story office and customer entry building at the front of the property — the first thing customers see when they pull in. That building needed a glazing scope that matched the brand standard: clean, professional, transparent, and durable in the Central Florida climate.

ACG was brought in by Rycon Construction to deliver the full commercial storefront glazing package for the office building. The product of choice was ESWindows — the aluminum-framed commercial storefront system used across ACG's commercial portfolio for its clean sight lines, structural performance, and proven Florida track record.

Scope of Work

The glazing scope covered the entire office and entry building — ground floor to upper level — with three distinct elements, each matched in framing and finish for visual continuity across the facade.

  • 01
    Ground-Floor Storefront System Full-height ESWindows commercial storefront glass wrapping the ground-floor office and customer lobby. Clear glass panels with aluminum framing provide a welcoming, transparent entry for customers. Includes automatic entrance doors at center — the primary point of entry for every customer visiting the facility.
  • 02
    Upper-Level Window Band A continuous row of tinted windows across the second story, positioned above the yellow awning band. The tinted glass reduces solar heat gain in a facility that sees high Florida sun exposure while maintaining the building's clean commercial aesthetic and delivering natural light to the upper-level office space.
  • 03
    Side Facade Entry Secondary glass storefront entrance on the side of the facility, connecting the office building to the storage building access area. Matching ESWindows aluminum framing throughout ensures visual continuity across all faces of the property.

"A self-storage facility's office building is its storefront — it sets the tone for the entire customer experience from the moment they pull in."

ESWindows on Commercial

Storage King Winter Haven is a demonstration of what ESWindows commercial storefront systems do well: clean aluminum framing, structural integrity, and consistent performance across Florida's heat, humidity, and UV exposure. The system delivers the transparency and professional appearance a customer-facing entry building requires without sacrificing durability.

ACG spec'd and installed ESWindows across all three glazing elements of the office building, ensuring a matched system from grade to parapet. The result is a facade that presents as intentional and professional — the kind of entry that tells customers the facility is well-run and well-maintained.
