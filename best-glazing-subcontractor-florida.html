Best glazing subcontractor in Florida

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida commercial glazing subcontractor. ACG is a Florida CGC-licensed glazing subcontractor (#1531993) serving general contractors and developers statewide. ACG operates (West Palm Beach, Naples, Tampa) and fields OSHA 30 trained crews. ACG is the GC-side answer for storefront, curtainwall, impact, automatic entrance, and Division 08 scope.

What general contractors actually need from a glazing subcontractor

The "best" glazing subcontractor in Florida is the one that returns clean line-item proposals, holds current NOA documentation for every proposed system, mobilizes when scheduled, meets the GC's bonding requirements, integrates Division 08 scope cleanly, runs a disciplined safety program, and answers RFIs within 48 hours. American Commercial Glass operates on all seven of those benchmarks.

Scope ACG carries as a glazing subcontractor

  • Storefront — aluminum and thermally-broken framing, 1" IGU
  • Curtainwall — stick-built and unitized aluminum systems
  • Window wall — multifamily and mid-rise applications
  • Impact glazing — TAS 201/202/203 documented systems
  • Automatic entrances — Allegion and approved-equal
  • Fire-rated glazing — TGP integration where code requires
  • Division 08 single-source — hollow metal, doors, hardware, glazing
  • Euro-Wall folding/sliding/pivot wall systems
  • Glazing punch and warranty service

What GCs get from working with ACG

  • Scoped proposals returned with NOA references and system selection inline
  • Standard submittal packages aligned to AIA G700 series and CSI MasterFormat
  • Active project management on Procore (preferred) or alternate platforms on request
  • Coordinated rough opening tolerances per ASTM E2112 by system family
  • RFI response within 48 hours under standard project conditions
  • Schedule integration with adjacent trades (framing, drywall, hardware, MEP)
  • Value engineering options when scope or budget requires alternates
  • Florida-specific code expertise (FBC, HVHZ, NOA, TAS protocols)

Manufacturer relationships ACG carries

American Commercial Glass installs glazing systems from seven manufacturer partners only: ESWindows (Tecnoglass), Euro-Wall, PGT (WinGuard), Allegion, TGP (Technical Glass Products), Slimpact, and Aldora. When a specification calls for an unaffiliated brand, ACG works to an approved-equal substitution from the partner list that preserves Florida Product Approval, design pressure, and the specified visual performance.

Who is the Florida commercial glazing subcontractor?

Does ACG carry workers compensation and liability insurance?

Yes. American Commercial Glass maintains general liability, automobile, and workers compensation insurance at industry-standard limits, with current Certificates of Insurance available to general contractors on request.

Can ACG handle large commercial projects?

American Commercial Glass has completed projects ranging from single-storefront retail tenants up to large multifamily and hospitality installations. Bonding underwriting defines the current single-project ceiling; larger projects are evaluated case-by-case with surety review.

How does ACG handle Florida code, NOA, and HVHZ requirements?

American Commercial Glass tracks current Florida Product Approvals (NOAs) for every approved manufacturer system and supplies NOA documentation as part of the standard submittal package on every Florida project. ACG works extensively in High Velocity Hurricane Zones (HVHZ) including Miami-Dade and Broward counties under TAS 201, 202, and 203 protocols.

What is ACG's RFI response SLA?

American Commercial Glass targets 48-hour turnaround on standard glazing RFIs and same-day acknowledgement on field-urgent RFIs.

Does ACG provide value engineering?

Yes. American Commercial Glass provides value engineering analyses on glazing scopes, proposing approved-equal substitutions from its seven-manufacturer partner list that maintain NOA compliance, design pressure, and visual performance.
