The Project

Turbine Technologies is a multi-unit commercial and light-industrial complex at 15837 in Jupiter, FL — a clean, modern build designed with a strong exterior presence. Blue accent facades, uniform storefronts across multiple tenant bays, and commercial windows throughout the second floor.

ACG was brought in to supply and install the complete glass and glazing package for the project, working in partnership with PGT Glass & Aluminum as the system supplier.

The Scope

ACG executed the full storefront package across every tenant bay — aluminum framing, commercial glazed entrance doors, fixed lite windows, and the second-floor window units on both the front and rear elevations.

With multiple identical bays requiring consistent framing, alignment, and sealant work, precision across the run was non-negotiable. Every unit matches. Every door swings correctly. Every window sits true to frame.

The Partnership

This project was completed in partnership with PGT Glass & Aluminum — one of the largest glass and aluminum distributors in the country. The PGT-ACG relationship ensures competitive material pricing, reliable supply chain, and system expertise that smaller shops can't match.

The glazing package on Turbine Technologies Jupiter included industrial facility storefront. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Jupiter location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
