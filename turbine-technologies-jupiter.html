Turbine Technologies Jupiter

The complete glass and glazing package for a multi-unit commercial and light-industrial complex in Jupiter - uniform storefronts across every tenant bay, supplied in partnership with PGT Glass & Aluminum.

Location Jupiter, FL Sector Commercial · Industrial Delivery Owner-direct Systems PGT Completed 2024
01
Sheet A-101 · Project

Multiple identical bays. Zero tolerance for drift.

Turbine Technologies is a multi-unit commercial and light-industrial complex in Jupiter, FL - a clean, modern build with a strong exterior presence: blue accent facades, uniform storefronts across multiple tenant bays, and commercial windows across the second floor.

ACG was brought in directly by ownership to supply and install the complete glass and glazing package, working in partnership with PGT Glass & Aluminum as the system supplier.

The scope ran the full storefront package across every tenant bay - aluminum framing, commercial glazed entrance doors, fixed-lite windows, and the second-floor window units on both the front and rear elevations. With multiple identical bays requiring consistent framing, alignment, and sealant work, precision across the run was non-negotiable: every unit matches, every door swings correctly, every window sits true to frame.
