The Project

Indiantown High School is a public high school in Martin County, FL — a new construction educational facility serving the Indiantown community. The school features a large commons and cafeteria area, administrative and academic wings, and the kind of open, modern interior design that requires substantial interior glass work to separate spaces while maintaining visibility and natural light throughout.

ACG was brought in to execute the interior glass scope — the partition walls, storefront systems, and interior glazing that define the school's spaces from within.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the interior glass package throughout the facility: the glass partition walls separating the commons from the media center and adjacent spaces, the interior storefront systems at administrative and secure access points, and the blue-tinted glazed storefront visible from the main commons area.

Institutional and educational construction runs on strict schedules tied to school calendars — a missed TCO date on a school project has real consequences. ACG coordinated with the construction team to deliver the interior glass scope on time, with every unit plumb and every door operating correctly from day one.

The glazing package on Indiantown High School included school envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows impact, TGP fire-rated — manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Indiantown location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
