Palm Beach County Market

ACG's Headquarters Is
Right Here in West Palm Beach.

Palm Beach County is not a market ACG serves from a distance - it is the market ACG was built on. ACG's headquarters sits at 700 S Rosemary Ave #204 in downtown West Palm Beach, steps from the Rosemary Square mixed-use district and a short walk from the Intracoastal Waterway. Every estimator, project manager, and field lead operates out of this office. When a GC calls about a West Palm Beach project, they're talking to the home team.

Downtown West Palm Beach is in the middle of one of the most sustained commercial development runs in its history. The CityPlace redevelopment - now branded as Rosemary Square - has become a model for mixed-use urbanism in South Florida, drawing national retailers, Class A office tenants, and residential developers to the corridor. The waterfront along Flagler Drive has seen a wave of high-rise residential and mixed-use construction that shows no sign of slowing. Each building in that pipeline represents a complex glazing scope: curtainwall, storefront, impact systems, and lobby glass - all in Palm Beach County's wind-borne debris region. Palm Beach County is not in the HVHZ.

The financial services and technology migration to Palm Beach County has been transformative. Firms relocating from New York and the Northeast - drawn by Florida's tax climate and quality of life - have catalyzed a wave of Class A office construction and high-spec tenant improvement work throughout West Palm Beach and Palm Beach Gardens. The demand for premium commercial glazing - architecturally precise curtainwall, high-performance storefronts, and sophisticated interior glass systems - has risen in lockstep with that tenant quality. ACG has been here for all of it.

ACG's completed projects span the length and breadth of the county. The Lake Park Innovation Center established a new benchmark for what a commercial build-out in northern Palm Beach County can look like - a technically demanding scope executed on a compressed schedule. The Turbine Technologies facility in Jupiter required the kind of precision industrial glazing that most subs aren't equipped to handle. The 736 Lagoon Drive project demonstrated ACG's ability to execute high-end coastal residential-scale commercial work under Palm Beach County wind-borne debris requirements. These are not case studies assembled for marketing purposes - they are the regular output of a team that has made Palm Beach County its operating base for over a decade.

Eastern Palm Beach County - the coastal strip from Lake Worth Beach north through Riviera Beach, Palm Beach Island, and into Jupiter - is not in the HVHZ. That designation is Miami-Dade and Broward only. Palm Beach is a wind-borne debris region, so impact-rated glazing with Florida Product Approval is the code path. Submittal packages still have to pass Palm Beach County building department review. ACG's team has been navigating PBC plan review since the company's founding. We know what the department requires, and we submit packages that pass the first time.

Common Questions

Palm Beach County Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG have a Palm Beach office? +

Yes - West Palm Beach is ACG's headquarters. ACG is located at 700 S Rosemary Ave #204, West Palm Beach, FL 33401, in downtown WPB adjacent to the Rosemary Square district. This is not a satellite office or regional branch - it is the company's primary operations base. Estimators, project managers, and field supervisors all work out of this office. When you send ACG plans for a Palm Beach County project, the team responding is the home team - not a regional coordinator routing your project to someone else.

What areas of Palm Beach County does ACG serve? +

ACG serves the full Palm Beach County commercial construction market. Active service areas include downtown West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Tequesta, Riviera Beach, Lake Park, Juno Beach, North Palm Beach, Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, Greenacres, Lake Worth, Lantana, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach, and Boca Raton. The entire county - coastal HVHZ zones and inland - is ACG's operating territory. There is no corner of PBC that requires out-of-market logistics or premium dispatch pricing.

How fast is ACG's scope turnaround for Palm Beach County projects? +

ACG returns a detailed scope - system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown - within 48 hours of receiving drawings. ACG's estimating process eliminates the manual bottlenecks that cause delays at other glazing subs. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions, no three-week wait. Send plans Monday, have a complete scope Wednesday. Because Palm Beach County is ACG's home market, there's no escalation path or regional coordination delay - the estimating team is right here at 700 S Rosemary Ave.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Palm Beach County? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. For Palm Beach County projects, ACG specifies systems with Florida Product Approval. Miami-Dade NOA documentation is used when the specification calls for it. System recommendations are driven by the project spec and architect's design intent - not by what's most convenient to stock or what carries the best margin for the sub. ACG works to the spec.

Does ACG handle submittals and shop drawings for Palm Beach County glazing projects? +

Yes. ACG manages the complete submittal package - shop drawings, Florida product approvals, NOA documentation, and engineer-of-record coordination. ACG's West Palm Beach team knows the Palm Beach County building department requirements in detail. Submittal packages are built to PBC standards and submitted complete - minimizing revisions and delays that cost your schedule. ACG also tracks submittals proactively and provides status updates to your GC team without being asked. As the home-market team, ACG's knowledge of PBC plan review nuances is built from years of firsthand experience - not a reference document.
