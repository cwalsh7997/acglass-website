Kentucky's commercial glazing demand is concentrated in Louisville (Norton Healthcare, UofL Health, Baptist Health, plus the bourbon-distillery hospitality boom and I-65 distribution corridor) and Lexington (University of Kentucky health and academic facilities, strong veterinary and equine-industry development).

Building code context

Kentucky follows the Kentucky Building Code (KBC), which adopts the IBC with state modifications. Louisville and the I-65 corridor are tornado-zone markets requiring impact-resistant glazing specifications on most Class-A commercial work — mechanics that translate directly from our Florida HVHZ experience.

Licensing

Kentucky does NOT require a state-level general contractor license. Contractors register the business at the state level and pull local city/county permits as needed. Louisville (Louisville Metro) and Lexington (LFUCG) each have local contractor registration requirements that ACG handles on a project-by-project basis.
