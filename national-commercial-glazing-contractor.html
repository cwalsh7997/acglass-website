National vs. Regional

What Separates a National Glazing Sub from a
Regional Shop.

Most glazing contractors are built for a single metro. They have local crews, local supplier relationships, and a project management process that works when the job site is 40 minutes away. Put them on a project in another state and everything that worked locally - the supply chain, the permitting relationships, the crew availability - stops working.

A national glazing contractor is built differently from the ground up. The logistics infrastructure, the supplier network, the project coordination model - all of it is designed to operate across state lines without the coordination failures that plague subs managing remote projects manually.

ACG is that contractor. Three Florida offices. A coordination platform that handles submittal tracking, material procurement sequencing, and installation coordination in real time - whether the project is in Tampa or Lafayette. Tennessee work is material supply and consulting, coordinated from Florida.

National Reach.

ACG's national reach starts with its Florida foundation: three offices - West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa - covering Florida from the Gulf to the Atlantic. That Florida infrastructure isn't just a home market. It's the operational base from which ACG deploys nationally.

Florida's construction environment is uniquely demanding. High-Velocity Hurricane Zone requirements, Florida Product Approval documentation, NOA certification processes, coastal exposure ratings that affect every system specification - Florida contractors who operate at the top of the market develop technical and operational capabilities that exceed what most states require. National Operations

The Coordination Infrastructure
That Makes National Glazing
Actually Work.

Managing a glazing scope remotely - from Florida for a project in Louisiana - is an operational challenge that manual project management can't solve at scale. Submittal deadlines shift. Material lead times change. Installation windows move. On a locally managed project, a superintendent can walk next door to the PM's office. On a national project, every coordination gap becomes a schedule risk.

ACG's platform eliminates those gaps. Submittal deadlines are tracked against the GC's master schedule in real time. Material procurement is sequenced against approved shop drawings, not generic lead-time assumptions. Installation windows are coordinated with the GC's live schedule and updated as that schedule evolves. When any milestone slips, the platform flags it proactively - before it becomes the GC's problem.

This is how ACG managed the StayApt Lafayette scope from Florida. Same coordination model. Same proactive communication standard. Same submittal quality. The geography changed. The execution didn't.

Procore-based tracking covers every submittal milestone against the GC's master schedule. Nothing slips without proactive notification - days before it becomes a field problem.

02

Material orders are placed against approved shop drawings, sequenced to the installation schedule - not a static "order everything at once" model.

03

Multi-Project Coordination
