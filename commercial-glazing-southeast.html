Southeast Glazing Requirements

Coastal Exposure.
Variable Codes.
One Standard.

Southeast commercial glazing isn't uniform. The Gulf Coast — from Pensacola to New Orleans to Corpus Christi — has hurricane exposure requirements that rival South Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. The Atlantic Coast from Miami to Wilmington carries similar coastal exposure designations that affect every window and door specification.

Move inland to Atlanta, Nashville, Birmingham, or Charlotte and the code requirements change — but the need for proper system specification, complete submittals, and professional installation doesn't. Every Southeast state has its own product approval process, structural engineering requirements, and building department inspection standards.

ACG navigates all of it. Our Florida HVHZ expertise is the highest standard in the country. When we work in Louisiana or Alabama or Georgia, we bring that specification depth to a market where most local subs are operating well below it. The GC gets the benefit of the most demanding technical standard in the industry, applied to their project wherever it's located.

AI-Managed Scheduling

Managing glazing scopes across multiple states simultaneously is an operational challenge that manual project management can't solve at scale. When you have projects in Florida, Georgia, and Louisiana running in parallel, every coordination gap in one state can cascade into schedule failures in the others.

ACG's AI-managed scheduling platform was built for exactly this challenge. Every submittal deadline, every material procurement window, every installation coordinate is tracked against the GC's live master schedule in real time. When something shifts — and in construction, something always shifts — the platform flags it proactively across all active projects, in all active states.

The result: GC superintendents across the Southeast get the same proactive coordination from ACG that they get on a local Florida project. The geography changed. The execution standard didn't.

Common Questions

Southeast Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve commercial glazing projects across the Southeast? +

Yes. ACG covers the full Southeast — Florida (three offices, home market), Georgia, Louisiana, Alabama, Tennessee, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Texas. Our Southeast coverage follows our GC partner network across the region.

What makes Southeast commercial glazing different from other regions? +

The Southeast has unique requirements driven by coastal hurricane exposure along the Gulf Coast and Atlantic seaboard, varying state building codes, and impact zone designations that change by county. ACG's HVHZ expertise from Florida is the highest glazing specification standard in the country — and it translates directly to the most demanding Southeast coastal markets.

Which GC partners does ACG work with in the Southeast? +

ACG partners with Rycon Construction (StayApt Suites Lafayette, Louisiana), Verdex Construction, and Hooks Construction. These are regional and national builders whose project flow spans multiple Southeast states.

How does ACG coordinate multi-state Southeast projects? +

ACG uses AI-managed scheduling to coordinate submittals, material procurement, and installation across simultaneous Southeast projects. The platform tracks every milestone against the GC's master schedule in real time and flags slippage proactively — the same coordination standard that runs ACG's Florida offices, applied regionally.

Does ACG handle the full Division 08 scope in Southeast states? +

Yes. ACG provides the complete Division 08 scope across the Southeast: impact windows and doors, commercial storefronts, curtainwall, window wall, fire-rated assemblies, automatic entrances, and specialty glazing. One sub, every opening, from the Gulf Coast to the Carolinas.
