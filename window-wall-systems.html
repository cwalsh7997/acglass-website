Window wall is the dominant glazing system for Florida's multifamily and hospitality market. ACG has installed more window wall per year than any other system — and built our reputation on delivering it on schedule.

Window Wall Expertise

Florida's Window Wall
Specialist. Period.

Window wall sits between storefront and curtainwall — it spans slab-to-slab but bears its loads directly on the floor slabs rather than the building structure. This distinction matters enormously for the coordination required between glazing, structural, and waterproofing.

ACG has mastered the window wall coordination sequence. We work with your structural engineer to understand slab edge conditions. We coordinate with your waterproofing contractor to establish the air and water barrier interface before installation begins. We sequence our work with your framing and drywall trades to avoid the conflicts that cause expensive rework.

For South Florida's HVHZ market, window wall must be impact-rated. ACG installs ESWindows and Slimpact window wall systems that meet large missile impact requirements — with full Miami-Dade NOA compliance and complete submittal packages.

System Types

Slab-Edge Window Wall

The standard Florida multifamily window wall system.

Slab-edge window wall bears on the floor slab at each level, allowing independent floor-to-floor installation and adjustment for concrete construction tolerances. Thermally broken aluminum framing with structural silicone or wet seal options. Compatible with most Florida multifamily concrete frame construction.
