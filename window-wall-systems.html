FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a window wall system?

Window wall is a non-structural glazing system that spans floor-to-floor between concrete slabs in high-rise buildings. Unlike curtainwall, window wall sits between floor slabs rather than hanging in front of them. It is the dominant system for multifamily and hospitality high-rise construction.

What manufacturers does ACG work with for window wall?

ACG has deep installation experience with ESWindows and Eurowall window wall systems. We are also capable of installing other manufacturer systems based on project specifications.

Does ACG install window wall for multifamily projects?

Multifamily high-rise is ACG's primary window wall market. We understand the unit-count-driven scheduling, floor-by-floor logistics, and waterproofing coordination these projects demand.

Can ACG supply window wall materials without installation?

Yes. ACG offers supply-only window wall packages for contractors who have their own crews. We handle specification, engineering, procurement, and delivery.
