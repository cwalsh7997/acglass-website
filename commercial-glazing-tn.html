Building code context

Tennessee follows the International Building Code (IBC), most recently 2018 edition adopted at the state level with local jurisdictional amendments. Tornado-zone wind-load requirements share substantial engineering DNA with Florida HVHZ — structural glazing, impact-rated assemblies, signed/sealed product approvals. ACG's Florida HVHZ background transfers directly.

Licensing

Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors requires state-level contractor licensing for commercial work contracted directly with the owner at $25,000+ (and specialty subcontractor licensing for electrical, plumbing, mechanical, HVAC, roofing, and masonry above threshold). Glazing is NOT on the mandatory specialty subcontractor list — most glazing scope falls under the general contractor's license. ACG will pursue Tennessee BC-B commercial licensing in 2026 for direct-to-owner projects and larger structural glazing scope.
