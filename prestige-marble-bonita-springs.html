Prestige Marble & Designs

A premium stone and tile showroom at 25171 Bernwood Dr in Bonita Springs, FL. ACG installed the complete ESWindows storefront and window package across both levels for Fort Construction.

Location Bonita Springs, FL GC Fort Construction Systems ESWindows storefront & windows Completed 2024
01
Sheet A-011 · The project

A showroom lives on natural light.

Prestige Marble & Designs is a two-story commercial building combining a high-end retail showroom on the ground floor with office and warehouse space above. The building required a glazing package that matched the brand's premium positioning while delivering the natural light a showroom lives on.

Fort Construction managed the build and brought ACG in to execute the complete ESWindows storefront and window package across both levels. ACG supplied and installed ESWindows dark-framed commercial windows across the entire facade — the ground-floor storefront windows flanking the main entry, the upper-level commercial windows running the full length of both elevations, and the high-bay clerestory windows inside the showroom that flood the space with natural light.

The interior shot is the proof of concept: light pouring through the dark-framed windows at the back of the showroom, bouncing off the polished marble floors and illuminating the full display floor. A showroom for a luxury marble company needs its glazing done right — every window consistent, every frame clean, every seal correct. That's what ACG delivered.
