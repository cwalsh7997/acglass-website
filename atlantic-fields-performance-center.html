The Project

Atlantic Fields is a private luxury community in Hobe Sound, FL — and the Performance Center is its crown jewel. A high-end fitness and wellness facility designed with dramatic glass to blur the line between interior and the Florida landscape surrounding it.

ACG was engaged to deliver the complete storefront and curtainwall glazing package for the building — from the expansive glass walls that frame the fitness floor to the entry storefront system and exterior glazed openings throughout.

The Scope

The Performance Center required precision coordination across multiple glazing systems — commercial storefront aluminum framing at the entries, curtainwall sections along the main fitness areas, and large fixed glazing units throughout the facility.

Every system had to perform at Florida code standards while delivering the clean, minimal aesthetic the architect specified. Glass selection, frame finishes, and sightline dimensions were all held to tight tolerances.

The Result

A facility that looks as good as it performs. Floor-to-ceiling glass on the main elevation connects the workout floor directly to the lush landscape outside. The storefront entries are crisp, the curtainwall sightlines are clean, and every unit passed inspection first time.
