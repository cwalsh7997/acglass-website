Full Glazing Package for a World-Class Performance Center

The Atlantic Fields Performance Center demanded a glazing solution that matched its architectural ambition â a modern, resort-caliber wellness and fitness facility set on a premier golf course in Hobe Sound, Florida. ACG delivered the complete curtainwall and storefront glazing package, engineering seamless transitions between indoor training environments and the expansive golf course landscape.

The design intent was uncompromising: floor-to-ceiling glass walls that dissolve the boundary between the fitness center, spa facilities, and the natural environment surrounding the building. Every opening required precise coordination with the structural steel and wood-clad facade system to maintain clean sightlines and zero-tolerance alignment.

ACG's scope included the full-height curtainwall system wrapping the fitness center, spa plunge pool enclosure with impact-rated glazing, custom storefront entry systems, and operable sliding glass panels for the outdoor terrace areas.
