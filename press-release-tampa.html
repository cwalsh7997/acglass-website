American Commercial Glass Opens Third Florida Office in Tampa, Expands Commercial Glazing Services to Gulf Coast Market

Tampa, FL — April 16, 2026

American Commercial Glass, Inc. (ACG), a licensed commercial glazing subcontractor specializing in storefronts, curtainwall systems, and impact-rated glass, announced the opening of its third Florida office in Tampa at 3031 N Rocky Point Dr W, Suite 600.

The Tampa office expands ACG's service footprint to the Gulf Coast, covering Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, and Polk counties. ACG now operates from three locations: West Palm Beach (headquarters), Naples (Southwest Florida), and Tampa (Gulf Coast).

"Tampa Bay is one of the fastest-growing commercial construction markets in the Southeast," said Connor Walsh, President of ACG. "Opening a local presence here allows us to better serve the general contractors and developers building in this market — with the same 48-hour bid turnaround and on-site execution they expect from us in South Florida."

ACG handles the full Division 08 glazing scope on commercial projects — storefronts, curtainwall, window wall, impact-rated glazing, automatic entrance systems, fire-rated glass, and interior partitions. The company has completed 350+ projects and installed over 1 million square feet of commercial glass across Florida.

The company works with manufacturers including ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Euro-Wall Innovations, PGT, Allegion, and TGP, and has partnered with general contractors such as West Construction, Pirtle Construction, Hooks Construction, Rycon Construction, Sisca Construction, Proctor Construction, and Ahrens Companies.

ACG recently completed high-profile projects including the Eau Palm Beach Resort & Spa (Forbes Five-Star, multi-phase glazing renovation), Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale (waterfront restaurant storefront), and the Wave Food Hall in Cocoa Beach.

The company holds Florida CGC License #1531993 and is certified as a Woman-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE) and Small Business Enterprise (SBE).

About American Commercial Glass

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a licensed Division 08 commercial glazing subcontractor serving all of Florida from offices in West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa. ACG specializes in storefronts, curtainwall systems, impact-rated glazing, window wall, automatic entrance systems, and interior glass installations.
