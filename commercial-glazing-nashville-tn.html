Why Nashville next

Nashville sits at the geographic and economic center of the Southeast and Midwest's fastest-growing commercial construction corridor. Within a 300-mile radius are 10 major commercial markets — from Memphis to Cincinnati, Atlanta to Louisville — that have been consistently underserved by national glazing contractors capable of delivering Florida-grade impact, structural, and high-performance assemblies.

ACG's Nashville office will run on the same standards as our three Florida offices — Florida CGC qualification, manufacturer-authorized installation only, AAMA InstallationMasters trained crews, and the same submittal and quality processes that have produced 350+ delivered projects, 1M+ SF of installed glass, and zero OSHA recordables.

Same standard. New region.

The Nashville office will install the same manufacturer lineup as our Florida offices: ESWindows (impact storefront, curtainwall), Euro-Wall (multi-slide, bi-fold, pivot, DirectSet), PGT (impact-resistant), Allegion (commercial hardware, automatic operators), TGP (fire-rated glazing), and Slimpact (impact-rated steel framing).

Tennessee tornado zones and Kentucky/Ohio severe-weather markets share substantial requirements with Florida HVHZ — structural glazing engineering, impact-rated assemblies, and authorized-installer documentation. ACG's Florida HVHZ background transfers directly.
