Why commercial glazing in Panama City is different.

Panama City is rebuilding. Hurricane Michael hit Mexico Beach and Panama City as a Category 5 on October 10, 2018 - the strongest landfall in the contiguous US since Andrew. Seven years later, every commercial reconstruction project in Bay County goes through Bay County Builders Services on a code-current basis, and every storefront, entrance, and impact system needs current Florida Product Approval numbers tied to 140 mph design winds. ACG carries the impact NOAs, anchor calcs, and Florida-licensed crew capacity to bid Panama City reconstruction and new commercial work without the supply-chain gaps that have slowed local-only glaziers since 2018.

Architectural context: Panama City is a city literally rebuilt by Hurricane Michael in October 2018 - Cat 5, 160 mph sustained winds. Every commercial glass system installed since 2019 is post-Michael construction, which means current FBC compliance, latest Florida Product Approvals, and a level of code scrutiny from Bay County inspectors that the rest of the Panhandle hasn't seen.

Panama City commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is your team familiar with post-Hurricane Michael code requirements in Bay County?

Yes. Every Panama City and Bay County bid we send accounts for the current 140 mph design wind speed, WBDR impact requirements, and Bay County Builders Services permitting practices established post-Michael.

Can you handle the impact-rated curtain wall on a multi-story Panama City project?

Yes. We work with Tecnoglass / ESWindows on Florida-approved impact curtain wall systems certified for the 140 mph open-coast wind loads Bay County requires.

Do you do Tyndall Air Force Base subcontract work?

We bid Tyndall as a specialty glazing subcontractor under qualifying GCs. ACG is not a prime federal contractor, but we work the Tyndall rebuild as a sub for prime GCs holding the federal contracts.

How is pricing for Panama City compared to South Florida?

Material costs are similar - impact glass is impact glass. Labor and mobilization run slightly higher on Panhandle projects vs. our home WPB market because of crew travel, which we disclose in the bid letter rather than burying in the unit prices.

What lead time should I expect for Panama City impact glazing?

10-14 weeks from PO for standard impact storefront packages. 16-20 weeks for custom curtain wall or oversized impact glass. We share the manufacturer's confirmed ship date in the bid letter so your schedule isn't a surprise.
