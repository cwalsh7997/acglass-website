Direct answer

ACG Glass is shorthand for American Commercial Glass, Inc. — a Florida commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach, with offices in Naples, Tampa, and Nashville (opening 2026). We install commercial glazing systems for general contractors on AIA-format contracts. We are not the same company as ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com).

Why does this page exist?

If you searched "ACG Glass" and landed here, you may have been looking for one of several Florida companies. The two most common:

  • American Commercial Glass (acglass.com, this site) — commercial-only glazing contractor on AIA-format projects. FL CGC #1531993. West Palm Beach HQ. The Walsh brothers (Connor and Rielly) lead the company. 350+ commercial installations, 1M+ SF of glass.
  • ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com) — a separate Florida company in Riviera Beach. Different ownership. Different scope. Primarily residential glass, shower doors, mirrors, glass railings.

The two companies share the abbreviation "ACG" but are not affiliated.
