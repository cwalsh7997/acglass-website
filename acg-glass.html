ACG Glass

"ACG Glass" is the short-form name for American Commercial Glass, Inc. — a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor based in West Palm Beach. We install storefront, curtainwall, window wall, hurricane impact, and fire-rated commercial glazing across Florida and the Southeast. Florida CGC #1531993.

Direct answer

ACG Glass is shorthand for American Commercial Glass, Inc., a Florida commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach, with offices in Naples and Tampa. We install commercial glazing systems for general contractors on AIA-format contracts. We are not the same company as ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com).

Why does this page exist?

If you searched "ACG Glass" and landed here, you may have been looking for one of several Florida companies. The two most common:

  • American Commercial Glass (acglass.com, this site) — commercial-only glazing contractor on AIA-format projects. FL CGC #1531993. West Palm Beach HQ. Owner-operated by the Walshes — Rielly Walsh (CEO, majority owner) and Connor Walsh (President).
  • ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com) — a separate Florida company in Riviera Beach. Different ownership. Different scope. Primarily residential glass, shower doors, mirrors, glass railings.

The two companies share the abbreviation "ACG" but are not affiliated.

What ACG Glass (American Commercial Glass) installs

  • Commercial storefront systems
  • Commercial curtainwall (stick-built, unitized, SSG)
  • Window wall for multifamily and mid-rise
  • Hurricane impact glazing — HVHZ NOA-listed
  • Fire-rated glazing — UL-listed 20- to 120-minute assemblies

  • Allegion (Von Duprin, Steelcraft) — hurricane-rated commercial hardware
