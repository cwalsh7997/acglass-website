Coconut Grove Context

Commercial glazing in Coconut Grove, Miami-Dade County.

What gets built in Coconut Grove. Coconut Grove is Miami's oldest neighborhood, a waterfront village south of downtown built around hotels, restaurants, retail, marinas, and a steady run of mixed-use and Class-A office development around CocoWalk and the bayfront. Its commercial construction is hospitality- and retail-led: hotels and restaurants that face the water, boutique retail, office over retail, and the amenity-rich mixed-use that has reshaped the district.

What ACG does in Coconut Grove. Hospitality and restaurant storefront, retail storefront for boutique and mixed-use frontage, curtain wall and window wall for office and mid-rise, folding and multi-slide glass walls for restaurants and hotel amenity spaces that open to the bay, all-glass and aluminum entrances, glass railings for terraces and balconies, and the impact-rated windows and doors Miami-Dade requires. Single-source across the full Division 08 scope - frame, glass, hardware, submittals.

HVHZ and Miami-Dade NOA. Coconut Grove is in Miami-Dade County's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. Every commercial storefront, window, door, curtain wall, and folding system must carry a current Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) for the exact configuration installed, with large-missile impact testing and design-pressure documentation. Waterfront exposure pushes the design pressures up, and ACG's submittal package includes the NOA, install instructions tied to the approval, and engineer-stamped shop drawings where required.

Served from West Palm Beach. Coconut Grove is served out of ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters, which runs the South Florida and Miami-Dade market. Connor Walsh and the WPB office keep project management and field crews staged for Miami-Dade work, including the marina-adjacent access, valet-heavy site conditions, and phased occupancy that hospitality work in the Grove involves.

Who we work with. ACG bids directly to general contractors, hotel and hospitality ownership groups, restaurant operators, retail tenants, and developers active in Coconut Grove. We are active on Procore and BuildingConnected. The 48-hour bid turnaround fits the front-end schedule, and ACG locks rough opening dimensions in pre-construction. Lead times run 8-16 weeks from approved shop drawings depending on system, plus field installation.
