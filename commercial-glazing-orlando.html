Code Compliance

Orlando & Orange County Building Code Requirements

Orlando and Orange County fall within Florida’s Wind-Borne Debris Region, requiring impact-rated glazing or approved protective systems on all commercial projects. The Florida Building Code 7th Edition (2023) governs construction with design wind speeds of 130–150 mph depending on exposure category.

While not in the HVHZ, Orlando’s code requirements are significant. ACG brings HVHZ-level expertise from our South Florida projects to every Orlando job, ensuring systems exceed minimum code requirements. All glazing carries Florida Product Approvals with full engineering packages for Orange County permit review, which typically runs 10–15 business days.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG provide impact glazing in Orlando?

While Orlando is not in the HVHZ, many projects still require impact-rated or wind-borne-debris-rated glazing based on the Florida Building Code wind speed maps. ACG installs code-compliant systems for all Central Florida wind zones.

What types of projects does ACG serve in Orlando?

ACG serves the full range of commercial construction in Orlando: hotels, resorts, multifamily, retail, office, medical, institutional, and mixed-use developments.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Orlando?

ACG installs impact-rated storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000 series), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals for Orange County permitting.

What is the typical timeline for an Orlando glazing project?

Storefront projects typically run 2–4 weeks for installation. Window wall and curtainwall range from 4–10 weeks depending on scope. Orange County permit review runs 10–15 business days; ACG submits complete engineering packages to minimize revision cycles and keep projects on schedule.

Does ACG handle theme park and hospitality glazing in Orlando?

Yes. ACG provides glazing packages for Orlando’s hospitality and entertainment market, including resort hotels, convention centers, restaurants, and entertainment venues along International Drive and the theme park corridors. Our team coordinates with hospitality-specific schedules and phasing requirements.

Does ACG provide value engineering for Orlando projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering during preconstruction to help GCs and owners optimize glazing budgets without compromising code compliance. This includes alternative system selections, glass makeup optimization, and installation sequence adjustments that can reduce overall project costs.
