Common Questions

Orlando Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve the Orlando area? +

Yes. ACG serves Greater Orlando from our West Palm Beach and Tampa offices. scheduling eliminates the distance penalty — coordination, submittals, and crew deployment operate identically to markets where we have a dedicated office. We are active in the Central Florida construction market and familiar with Orange County's permitting and inspection processes.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Orlando? +

ACG installs the full commercial glazing scope — impact windows and doors, aluminum storefront systems, window wall, curtainwall, fire-rated glass assemblies, automatic entrances, and interior glazing. Systems include ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. One sub. Every opening.

Are impact windows required for commercial buildings in Orlando? +

Orlando and Orange County fall under Florida Building Code Section 1609 wind requirements. While the area is not within the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), the wind exposure category still demands tested, approved glazing systems for most commercial building types. ACG specifies systems engineered to the exact requirements for your project location and occupancy classification.

How long does a glazing scope take in the Orlando area? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. coordination means lead times, crew scheduling, and submittal tracking are handled proactively, not reactively. No vague allowances. No hidden exclusions.

What areas near Orlando does ACG serve? +

ACG serves the full Greater Orlando metro: Orange County (Downtown Orlando, Lake Nona, Dr. Phillips, Windermere, Winter Park, Maitland), Seminole County (Lake Mary, Altamonte Springs, Sanford, Oviedo), Osceola County (Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Celebration), and Lake County (Clermont, Leesburg). We also cover the I-4 commercial corridor and UCF Research Park area.
