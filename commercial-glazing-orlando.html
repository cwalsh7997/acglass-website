Common Questions

Orlando Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve the Orlando area? +

Yes. ACG serves Greater Orlando from our West Palm Beach and Tampa offices. AI-managed scheduling eliminates the distance penalty — coordination, submittals, and crew deployment operate identically to markets where we have a dedicated office. We are active in the Central Florida construction market and familiar with Orange County's permitting and inspection processes.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Orlando? +

ACG installs the full commercial glazing scope — impact windows and doors, aluminum storefront systems, window wall, curtainwall, fire-rated glass assemblies, automatic entrances, and interior glazing. Systems include ESWindows, CGI Windows, YKK AP, Trulite, PGT Innovations, Eurowall, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. One sub. Every opening.

Are impact windows required for commercial buildings in Orlando? +

Orlando and Orange County fall under Florida Building Code Section 1609 wind requirements. While the area is not within the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), the wind exposure category still demands tested, approved glazing systems for most commercial building types. ACG specifies systems engineered to the exact requirements for your project location and occupancy classification.

How long does a glazing scope take in the Orlando area? +
