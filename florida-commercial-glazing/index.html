Who is the commercial glazing contractor in Florida behind ACG?

American Commercial Glass (acglass.com) is a Florida commercial glazing contractor under FL CGC #1531993. Connor Walsh is President and the qualifier of record. The company is not AGC Inc, not ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com), and not A-Christian Glass in Delray Beach. Bids go to general contractors from West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa.

How fast can a Florida GC get a commercial glazing bid?

48 hours on a complete RFQ package: drawings, scope, schedule, and NOA requirements. Repeat GC clients often get it faster. Complex curtain wall or multi-building packages can take 72-96 hours. If the set needs more time, ACG says so within 24 hours of receiving the plans. See Send Us Plans and facts.

What should a GC send for a 48-hour commercial glass bid?

Drawings, specs, bid date, and any NOA or Florida Product Approval notes. A PDF with an elevation sheet and a door/window schedule is enough to start. Include Division 08 if it exists. Upload on Send Us Plans, email connor@acglass.com, or drop a BuildingConnected, Procore, or BIM 360 link. One number reaches every office: (772) 486-7711.

Is ACG licensed to install commercial glass in Florida?

Yes. Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993, issued by the Florida DBPR. The license is statewide. Connor Walsh is the qualifier of record. COIs and bond capacity letters go out during prequalification. Verify the number on the Florida DBPR search.

Where are ACG's Florida commercial glazing offices?

Headquarters: 700 S Rosemary Ave STE 204, West Palm Beach, FL 33401. Naples: 4850 Tamiami Trail N Ste 301. Tampa: 3031 N Rocky Point Dr W Ste 600. Same phone for all three: (772) 486-7711. Email connor@acglass.com. Office pages: West Palm Beach, Naples, Tampa.

Does Florida commercial glazing need a Miami-Dade NOA?

HVHZ is Miami-Dade and Broward. Exterior glazing there needs a current Miami-Dade NOA. Other Florida counties typically take Florida Product Approval. West Palm Beach is outside HVHZ and sits in Wind Zone 4. Impact-rated storefront with Miami-Dade NOA is the usual spec there even though Palm Beach County will also accept FPA. Longer path: HVHZ requirements.

Does ACG bid residential glass in Florida?

No. ACG is a commercial-only Division 08 subcontractor. Work goes to general contractors, developers, and architects. Residential impact windows belong with a residential specialist.

What systems does a Florida commercial glazing contractor bid?

Storefront, curtain wall, window wall, impact windows and doors, multi-slide and folding walls, fire-rated glass, and automatic entrances. One sub for the opening package. ACG is an installer and specifier for ESWindows and Euro-Wall. PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora are installed to the specification. See manufacturers.

How does a GC invite ACG on bid day?

Send drawings on Send Us Plans, email connor@acglass.com, call (772) 486-7711, or invite ACG on BuildingConnected or Procore. ACG bids Division 08 only. It is not the general contractor on bid day. Longer question list: what a GC should ask a glazing sub.
