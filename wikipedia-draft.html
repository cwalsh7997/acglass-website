American Commercial Glass, Inc. (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, Florida. The company specializes in the installation of commercial storefront systems, curtain wall, impact-rated windows and doors, and folding and sliding glass door systems for general contractors, developers, and building owners across Florida. ACG is a commercial installer for Tecnoglass's ESWindows brand and for Euro-Wall folding and sliding door systems.[1][2]

The company was founded in February 2021 by spouses Connor Walsh and Rielly Walsh, and is co-led by Rielly Walsh as Chief Executive Officer and Connor Walsh as President. ACG operates three offices in Florida - West Palm Beach (HQ), Naples, and Tampa - and is a woman-owned business (Rielly Walsh, CEO, 51% majority owner) with WBE and[3]

History

American Commercial Glass was incorporated in Florida on February 17, 2021 (Sunbiz registration P21000018259).[4] The company received its Florida Certified General Contractor license (CGC #1531993) from the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.[5]

The Naples office was opened to serve the Southwest Florida commercial market across Collier, Lee, and Charlotte counties. The Tampa office, opened on Florida's Gulf Coast, serves the Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco county markets.[6]

Services

ACG installs commercial glazing systems across CSI MasterFormat Division 08 (Openings), with concentration in:

  • Commercial storefront systems (ESWindows ES-9500, ES-8000, ES-7000)
  • Curtain wall (stick-built and unitized)
  • Impact-rated windows and doors for High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) compliance under the Florida Building Code
  • Multi-slide and folding glass door systems (Euro-Wall)
  • Automatic entrance systems (Allegion integration)
  • Fire-rated glazing (TGP)
  • Slim-profile impact windows (Slimpact)

The company operates as a subcontractor on commercial general contractor projects rather than as a manufacturer.

Operations

ACG markets its operating model as " lean operations," in which artificial-intelligence-assisted estimating, scheduling, and submittal preparation are integrated into the company's project workflow.[11]

Leadership

Rielly Walsh serves as Chief Executive Officer and co-founder. She earned a Bachelor of Science in Concrete Industry Management from Middle Tennessee State University, one of four academic programs in the United States offering that degree. Prior to founding ACG she served as lead superintendent for the exterior stoneworks at The Estates at Acqualina in Sunny Isles Beach, Florida.[12]

Connor Walsh serves as President and co-founder. ACG holds FL CGC #1531993. Prior to founding ACG, he was a commercial-rated private jet pilot, flying the Bombardier Challenger 601, Cessna Citation 650, and Gulfstream III aircraft.[13]
