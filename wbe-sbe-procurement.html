Diversity certifications

WBE + SBE — what the certifications unlock.

These designations are recognized by federal agencies, state DOTs, county procurement offices, and prime contractors with contractual diversity goals. Once ACG's certifications are issued, prime contractors will be able to count ACG subcontract spend toward participation requirements. Until then, ACG identifies as woman-owned with certification in process.

WBE — in process

Women's Business Enterprise

WBE certification, once issued, qualifies a company for set-aside and preference programs at the federal, state, and local level. For ACG this is expected to support:

  • Prime contractor diversity participation goals on GC pre-qualification submissions
  • County and municipal procurement diversity scoring where WBE status earns evaluation points
  • Federal WOSB / EDWOSB program eligibility as a pathway being pursued (subject to NAICS size standards)
  • Florida DOT DBE preference programs as a pathway being pursued

Certifying agency & certificate number: published when certification is issued

SBE — in process

Small Business Enterprise

SBE certification is recognized by local government procurement programs, school boards, and county construction programs across Florida. Once issued, it is expected to support:

  • Local government set-asides at the county and municipal level (Palm Beach, Collier, Hillsborough, and others)
  • Florida public-school district SBE participation requirements
  • SBE subcontractor line-item credit on prime contractor bids to counties and municipalities
  • Preferred-vendor list eligibility for county procurement offices with SBE participation mandates
