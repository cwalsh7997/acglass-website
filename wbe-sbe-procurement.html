Diversity Certifications

WBE + SBE — what the certifications unlock.

ACG holds both Women-owned Business Enterprise (WBE) and Small Business Enterprise (SBE) certifications. These designations are actively recognized by federal agencies, state DOTs, county procurement offices, and prime contractors with contractual diversity goals.

WBE Certified

Women-Owned Business Enterprise

ACG is certified as a Women-owned Business Enterprise, qualifying the company for set-aside and preference programs at the federal and state level. Prime contractors on government projects with WBE participation goals can count ACG subcontract spend toward their required diversity percentages.

  • Federal small-business set-asides under the SBA Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) program where applicable
  • Florida DOT Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE) preference programs for state-funded transportation construction
  • Prime contractor diversity participation goals on GC pre-qualification submissions
  • County and municipal procurement diversity scoring where WBE status earns evaluation points
  • U.S. SBA WOSB and EDWOSB program eligibility (subject to NAICS size standards)
Certifying agency & certificate number: Available on request
SBE Certified

Small Business Enterprise

ACG's SBE certification is recognized by local government procurement programs, school boards, and county construction programs across Florida. SBE status provides a direct preference mechanism in bid evaluation and qualifies ACG for local small-business set-aside contracts.

  • Local government set-asides at the county and municipal level (Palm Beach, Collier, Hillsborough, and others)
  • Florida public-school district project preferences — school boards regularly allocate SBE participation requirements
  • Priority consideration in local small-business development and mentor-protégé programs
  • SBE subcontractor line-item credit on prime contractor bids to FDOT, counties, and municipalities
  • Preferred-vendor list eligibility for county procurement offices with SBE participation mandates
Certifying agency & certificate number: Available on request

Compliance Capabilities

Labor, safety, and materials compliance.

Davis-Bacon Prevailing Wage

ACG can perform on federally funded construction projects subject to the Davis-Bacon and Related Acts (DBRA). Payroll documentation and certified payroll submissions are available through standard compliance workflows.

Section 3 (HUD Projects)

ACG maintains readiness for Section 3 reporting requirements on HUD-funded projects. Section 3 documentation and workforce reporting can be produced for applicable public housing and community development projects.

Buy American Act Materials

ACG sources glazing materials and aluminum framing systems through manufacturers with U.S. supply chains and can document country-of-origin for Buy American Act compliance on applicable federal contracts.

OSHA 30 — 100% Field Crew

Every ACG field crew member holds OSHA 30-hour certification. Zero OSHA recordable incidents since 2021. Experience Modification Rate (EMR) documentation available on request.

COI & Insurance Documentation

Standard additional-insured language on all GC contracts. Certificate of Insurance turnaround: 24 hours via Acrisure direct. General Liability, Workers Compensation, Auto, and Umbrella on file.

Essential Facility Code Compliance

Florida Building Code Risk Category IV (essential facilities) including HVHZ provisions. ACG has completed EOC and public-safety projects requiring FBC Ch 16 essential-facility wind load compliance.
