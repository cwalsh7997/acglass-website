The Market

Siesta Key is a commercial glazing market.

Hurricane Milton made landfall at Siesta Key on October 9, 2024 as a Category 3 storm with sustained winds of 120 mph and storm surge across the island. The rebuild and hardening cycle since then has defined the Siesta Key commercial glazing market. Every new commercial permit on the island requires full Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance, Exposure Category D design pressures, and documented impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems.

Siesta Key's commercial footprint is concentrated in Siesta Key Village (restaurants, bars, beach retail), Crescent Beach (boutique hospitality, beach clubs), and Point of Rocks (resort condos). Pricing runs at a premium to inland Sarasota due to Exposure D requirements, salt-air-tolerant finish specifications, and logistics (one road onto the island with height and weight restrictions for material delivery). Many Siesta Key projects specify HVHZ-level Miami-Dade NOA products for added margin even though the island is not HVHZ.

ACG's ESWindows flagship partnership gives Siesta Key GCs and owners access to Exposure D-rated commercial storefront, window wall, and impact sliding door systems with anodized aluminum or PVDF powder-coated finishes tested for coastal salt-air environments. For the beach clubs, rooftop bars, and waterfront amenity spaces that define Siesta Key's hospitality rebuild, we install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems — the premium moving-wall product on Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale and Atlantic Fields.

Siesta Key FAQ

Common questions — answered.

How did Hurricane Milton affect Siesta Key commercial buildings?

Hurricane Milton made landfall at Siesta Key on October 9, 2024 as a Category 3 storm. Many commercial buildings on the island sustained significant damage from wind, wind-driven rain, and storm surge. The rebuild cycle since late 2024 has required full Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance with Exposure D design pressures. Many rebuilds specify HVHZ-level NOA products for additional resilience.

Does Siesta Key require impact windows on rebuild permits?

Yes, in almost every case. Sarasota County and the barrier-island jurisdictions require impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening per FBC 8th Edition. Post-Milton, many permits are requiring upgraded impact performance even on rehabilitation projects where partial compliance would have historically been accepted.

What design pressures apply to Siesta Key commercial glazing?

Siesta Key is entirely in Exposure Category D. DP60 to DP80+ is typical depending on building height, location on the island, and exposure to Gulf or Sarasota Bay. Florida Product Approval documentation must explicitly confirm Exposure D suitability. Shop drawings should show the calculated design pressures for each opening.

How much do commercial impact windows cost on Siesta Key?

Siesta Key pricing runs 15-20% above inland Sarasota rates. Typical storefront $75-$135/SF, window wall $85-$155/SF, curtainwall $100-$200/SF. Add 5-10% for logistics on the island (single-road access with height/weight limits). Salt-air-tolerant finishes (anodized aluminum or PVDF powder-coat) are standard.

Can ACG provide the full FBC documentation package for Siesta Key projects?

Yes. Every ACG project includes Florida Product Approval numbers, DP rating certifications, shop drawings, installation affidavits, and insurance-carrier documentation. Post-Milton, insurance carriers are requiring full documentation on rebuild and new construction — and the pre-glazed factory documentation from our ESWindows partnership makes this faster than field-glazed alternatives.
