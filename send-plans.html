FAQ

Before you send it over.

What's the real turnaround on a scoped proposal? +

48 hours on standard commercial projects. For complex curtainwall or multi-building scopes, 72-96 hours. If it's going to take longer, we tell you within 24 hours of receiving the plans.

What file size limit? +

9 MB total through the form. If your drawing set is bigger, share a Procore, BuildingConnected, or BIM 360 link in the Notes field, or email it to connor@acglass.com and reference the form submission.

Do you bid jobs outside Florida? +

Florida only for install. Supply-only arrangements are possible on certain manufacturer packages (ESWindows, Euro-Wall) for out-of-state GCs. Submit the form and we'll tell you on the call.

Preferred plan format? +

PDF drawings with an elevation sheet and a door/window schedule is ideal. Full CD set in PDF works. DWG files accepted. If there's a spec section 08, include it; we'll read it cover-to-cover.
