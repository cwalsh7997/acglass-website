Why GCs come back

The four metrics
that actually
matter on glass.

A glazing sub doesn't get called back because of a logo or a brochure. It happens when four numbers come in clean across the project. We design every job around them.

01

First-pass inspection clearance

Submittals built to the county reviewer's standard from day one - Miami-Dade NOA, Broward HVHZ, Palm Beach product approval. No second-round comments. No re-anchoring after the rough opening fails.

02

On-time turnover

Glass is on the critical path. We sequence to your schedule, not ours. Lead times locked at award. Material staged before crew mobilization. Substantial completion handed over on the date in the contract.

03

Accurate submittals

Shop drawings that match what's installed. Product data sheets, warranties, NOAs, anchor calcs - submitted as a complete package, version-controlled, RFI turnaround inside 24 hours.

04

Zero callback claims

Punchlist closed at turnover. No leaks at year one. No glass replacements two years in. The kind of finish that doesn't show up in your warranty queue two GCs later.

How a bid becomes a turnover

The four stages
between plan set
and certificate of occupancy.

Stage 01

Bid in 48 hours

Plans hit our intake. We return a scoped bid with takeoff, system specs, and lead times - usually inside two business days. No "bid is in the queue" silence.

Stage 02

Submittals + approvals

Shop drawings, product data, NOAs, anchor calcs - submitted complete on day 7. Reviewer comments addressed inside 48 hours. Approved before mobilization.

Stage 03

Material + install

Lead times locked at award. Crew mobilizes when frames are ready. Glass crated and protected through finishes. Punchlist tracked weekly with the GC.

Stage 04

Turnover + warranty

Inspection clean on first pass. Closeout package delivered: O&M, warranties, as-builts. We answer the phone for the next ten years.
