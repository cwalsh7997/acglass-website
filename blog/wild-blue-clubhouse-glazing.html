ACG's Naples crews delivered the glazing envelope at Wild Blue Clubhouse working direct with the ownership team. Wild Blue golf and lakeside community in Estero

Rielly Walsh · August 24, 2025 · 11 min read

The project.

Wild Blue Clubhouse is located in Estero, Lee County, Florida. Wild Blue golf and lakeside community in Estero. The development was led by Wild Blue community. ACG was retained direct by the ownership team — no general contractor layer between the design intent and the field crew.

The site sits within Lee County jurisdiction, which enforces Lee County HVHZ (post-Ian) — hardened code. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 172 mph design pressure for Lee coastal, with Florida Product Approval (FPA), Miami-Dade NOA accepted. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Estero market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Wild Blue Clubhouse included clubhouse storefront and pool deck doors. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows storefront, Euro-Wall multi-slide — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Estero location pulled supervision out of our Naples office.
