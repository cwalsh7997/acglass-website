Cudjoe Key Fire Station.

ACG's West Palm Beach crews delivered the glazing envelope at Cudjoe Key Fire Station working direct with the ownership team. Florida Keys fire station post-Irma rebuild

Connor Walsh · November 28, 2025 · 7 min read

The project.

Cudjoe Key Fire Station is located in Cudjoe Key, Monroe County, Florida. Florida Keys fire station post-Irma rebuild. The development was led by Monroe County Fire-Rescue. ACG was retained direct by the ownership team — no general contractor layer between the design intent and the field crew.

The site sits within Monroe County jurisdiction, which enforces Florida Keys HVHZ — strictest in state. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 180 mph design pressure for Keys exposure, with Miami-Dade NOA required. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Cudjoe Key market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Cudjoe Key Fire Station included fire station envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows impact, fire-rated TGP — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Cudjoe Key location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
