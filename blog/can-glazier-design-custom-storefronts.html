Can a Commercial Glazier Design Custom Storefronts?

Custom commercial storefronts in Florida: custom mullion profiles, oversize lites, premium PVDF finishes, integrated lighting. What a commercial glazier can engineer with the architect.

By Connor Walsh, President of American Commercial Glass · May 24, 2026 · 7 to 10 minute read

Yes. A Florida commercial glazier works with the architect to design custom storefronts - custom mullion profiles, oversize lites, premium finishes, integrated lighting, and structural silicone facades. The glazier does not replace the architect; the glazier engineers the assembly and qualifies the spec.

What a glazier can customize

Custom mullion sightlines. Stock ESWindows ES-9500 has a 2.25-inch sightline. Custom dies can drop sightline to 1.75 inch for a slimmer profile, or expand to 4+ inches for a structural look.

Oversize lites. Stock storefront tops out around 5 feet wide by 10 feet tall. Custom lites to 8 feet wide by 14 feet tall are achievable with specialty aluminum systems and oversized laminated glass.

Custom PVDF finishes. AAMA 2605 PVDF Kynar 70/30 is available in 100+ standard colors plus custom-match. 2-tone finishes are achievable (interior color different from exterior).

Structural silicone glazing. Frameless or flush-glazed appearance using Dow Corning 995 or Sika SikaSil WS-305 structural sealant. Eliminates the captured glazing channel.

What the glazier needs from the architect

Sealed shop drawings on custom storefront require structural calcs against ASCE 7-22 wind pressure and FBC 1626 impact criteria. The architect provides the design intent; the glazier (with their engineer-of-record) qualifies it structurally and provides the sealed submittal package.

Lead time on custom storefront

Custom dies take 8-12 weeks to mill. Custom PVDF finish takes 8-12 weeks to coat. Custom glass takes 4-10 weeks depending on coating and lamination. The longest item sets the schedule. Custom storefront typically adds 8-12 weeks to overall project timeline versus stock systems.

Frequently asked questions

Does ACG do custom storefront design?

ACG works with the architect to engineer custom storefronts. We are not the architect of record. We qualify the spec structurally, provide sealed shop drawings, and self-perform installation.

What's the lead time on custom?

8-12 weeks longer than stock systems. Custom dies, custom finish, custom glass all run in parallel where possible. Order on signed contract - not on permit issuance - to compress 2-3 weeks.
