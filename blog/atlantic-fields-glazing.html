Atlantic Fields.

Atlantic Fields sits in Hobe Sound, Martin County — 1,500-acre Discovery Land community; Tom Fazio 18-hole + 10-hole short; 317 estate homes; lots from $6M; borders Atlanti.... ACG executed the glazing scope under Proctor Construction.

Rielly Walsh · August 28, 2025 · 11 min read

The project.

Atlantic Fields is located in Hobe Sound, Martin County, Florida. 1,500-acre Discovery Land community; Tom Fazio 18-hole + 10-hole short; 317 estate homes; lots from $6M; borders Atlantic Ridge Preserve. The development was led by Discovery Land Company + Becker Holding. Proctor Construction served as general contractor.

The site sits within Martin County jurisdiction, which enforces Florida Building Code (FBC) coastal. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 160 mph design pressure for Martin coastal exposure, with Florida Product Approval (FPA), Miami-Dade NOA accepted. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Hobe Sound market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Atlantic Fields included multi-phase development glazing; sales center, golf house, performance center. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Euro-Wall, ESWindows — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to Proctor Construction's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Hobe Sound location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
