Gulfside Twelve.

Gulfside Twelve sits in Naples, Collier County — Gulf-front Naples residence. ACG executed the glazing scope direct with the ownership team.

Connor Walsh · March 24, 2026 · 8 min read

The project.

Gulfside Twelve is located in Naples, Collier County, Florida. Gulf-front Naples residence. The development was led by Beachfront development. ACG was retained direct by the ownership team — no general contractor layer between the design intent and the field crew.

The site sits within Collier County jurisdiction, which enforces Collier County HVHZ (post-Ian update). Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 172 mph design pressure for Naples coast, with Florida Product Approval (FPA) accepted; Miami-Dade NOA accepted. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Naples market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Gulfside Twelve included beachfront luxury envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Euro-Wall multi-slide, PGT impact — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Naples location pulled supervision out of our Naples office.
