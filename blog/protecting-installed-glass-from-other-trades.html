Quick Answer

Once glass is in, other trades can scratch it faster than anyone can argue about it. Protect critical lites, post notice, and photograph condition at install completion and again at handover. Why protection is a schedule item

Welding, grinding, stucco, painting, and material staging near finished glass create damage that looks like a glazing defect at punch. Put protection and notice on the schedule the same week glass installs.

Films and coverings

Use only protection methods compatible with the glass coating and frames. Some films leave residue or stain Low-E if left beyond manufacturer limits.

Handover photo protocol

  • Wide elevation shots keyed to mark numbers
  • Detail shots of any pre-existing scratches noted at install
  • Date/time and weather note if relevant to open openings
  • Store with the closeout package

How to ask for a protection plan

Ask for a written protection and photo protocol in the glazing scope. Send elevations and the construction schedule around glass install. ACG: (772) 486-7711. FL CGC #1531993.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you protect installed commercial glass from other trades?

Identify high-risk elevations, apply compatible protection, post notice, photograph condition at install completion, and re-inspect before removing protection.

Is this the same as glass cleaning guidance?

No. Cleaning/maintenance is operations. This page covers construction-period protection and condition recording.

Who usually owns protective film removal?

Assign it in the subcontract. Do not leave film past manufacturer limits.

What should a GC request at buyout?

A written protection plan, notice protocol, and condition-photo deliverable tied to the glass install schedule.
