ASTM E1105 vs AAMA 501.2:
Specifying the Right Field Water Test

Method selection distinctions from official standards language. No invented pressures or spray frequencies.

Quick Answer

Pick the field water-test method that matches the specification intent before you schedule a hose. ASTM E1105 is a standardized field water penetration test method with defined apparatus and procedures in the standard. AAMA 501.2 is a quality-assurance hose method commonly used for installed fenestration checks. Do not invent pressures or spray rates here. Cite the current standard and the project specification.

Start from specification intent

Read the project specification section that names the field test. Note whether the intent is quality-assurance sampling during construction, forensic diagnosis after a leak, or contractual acceptance testing. The method name, sampling rate, and pass/fail criteria belong in the specification or a written testing plan, not in memory.

Selection checklist

  • Does the specification name ASTM E1105, AAMA 501.2, both, or another method?
  • Is the purpose QA during install, acceptance, or forensic diagnosis?
  • Who hires the testing agency, and who remediates failures?
  • Which openings are in the sample set, and who protects adjacent finishes?
  • Are product-approval / shop conditions consistent with the assembly being tested?

What to prepare in the field

Access, water source, power, interior protection, and a written sequence so the test does not become an uncontrolled wetting of unfinished spaces. Coordinate Division 07 transitions before blaming the glazing alone for a fail.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between ASTM E1105 and AAMA 501.2?

They are different published methods used for different field water-check intents. Read the project specification and the current official standards for apparatus and acceptance criteria. This page does not invent pressures or spray rates.

Can I use AAMA 501.2 if the spec names ASTM E1105?

Not as a silent substitute. Obtain written direction before changing the specified method.

Who usually hires the testing agency?

Follow the project specification and general conditions. Do not assume the glazing sub always pays for third-party testing.

Where should numeric test parameters come from?

Only from the current official standard text and any written project modifications, never from memory or this article.
