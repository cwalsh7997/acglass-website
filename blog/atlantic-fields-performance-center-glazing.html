Project Press Release · Hobe Sound, FL

Atlantic Fields Performance Center.

Atlantic Fields Performance Center sits in Hobe Sound, Martin County — Performance/wellness amenity within Discovery Land's 1,500-acre Atlantic Fields. ACG executed the glazing scope under Proctor Construction.

Connor Walsh · October 17, 2025 · 12 min read

The project.

Atlantic Fields Performance Center is located in Hobe Sound, Martin County, Florida. Performance/wellness amenity within Discovery Land's 1,500-acre Atlantic Fields. The development was led by Discovery Land Company. Proctor Construction served as general contractor.

The site sits within Martin County jurisdiction, which enforces Florida Building Code (FBC) coastal. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 160 mph design pressure for Martin coastal exposure, with Florida Product Approval (FPA), Miami-Dade NOA accepted. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Hobe Sound market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Atlantic Fields Performance Center included wellness/performance facility envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Euro-Wall, ESWindows storefront — manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to Proctor Construction's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Hobe Sound location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
