Why pass-throughs are their own opening type

A pass-through sits on a counter. Staff lean through it. Guests order through it. Weather hits a smaller opening than a full glass wall, but splash, insects, and after-hours security still matter. Full-height folding or multi-slide walls are a different brief.

Up-swing options

Up-swing (awning-style or gas-strut assisted) panels lift into an open position above the counter. Typical tradeoffs:

  • Clear counter when open; headroom and soffit clearance must be drawn
  • Single large pane or segmented; weight and hardware matter for daily staff use
  • Closing force and latch for end of night should be staff-friendly
  • Screen / insect strategies are project-specific add-ons, not assumed here

Folding options

Folding pass-throughs accordion panels to one or both sides of the counter opening. Tradeoffs:

  • Can leave a wider clear width when stacked correctly
  • Need stacking space at the ends of the counter opening
  • More hinges and weather seals to maintain than a simple up-swing
  • Good when the design wants a nearly full counter opening without a single heavy leaf

Sliding options

Horizontal sliding pass-throughs move panels along a track. Tradeoffs:

  • Often simple for staff to operate sideways
  • Need pocket or bypass space equal to the sliding leaf (or leaves)
  • Track cleanliness is a daily issue at bars (ice, sugar, salt)
  • Partial open positions can support staged service

Frequently Asked Questions

Which pass-through type is best?

There is no universal winner. Match operation to service flow, overhead vs side clearance, and how staff close the opening after hours.

Are pass-throughs the same as full glass walls?

No. Pass-throughs are counter-height openings with different stacking, hardware, and cleaning demands.

Do you publish DP or prices here?

No. Ratings and prices belong to current product documents and project quotes.

What should I send?

Bar elevation, counter section, opening sizes, preferred operation, and material-only vs furnish-and-install.
