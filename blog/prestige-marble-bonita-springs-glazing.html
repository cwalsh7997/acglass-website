Prestige Marble Bonita Springs.

Prestige Marble Bonita Springs is the kind of project that lands in a portfolio for one reason: the work is correct. Prestige Marble showroom ACG delivered the glazing direct with the owner.

Rielly Walsh · November 23, 2025 · 8 min read

The project.

Prestige Marble Bonita Springs is located in Bonita Springs, Lee County, Florida. Prestige Marble showroom. The development was led by Prestige Marble. ACG was retained direct by the ownership team — no general contractor layer between the design intent and the field crew.

The site sits within Lee County jurisdiction, which enforces Lee County HVHZ (post-Ian) — hardened code. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 172 mph design pressure for Lee coastal, with Florida Product Approval (FPA), Miami-Dade NOA accepted. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Bonita Springs market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Prestige Marble Bonita Springs included showroom storefront. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows storefront — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Bonita Springs location pulled supervision out of our Naples office.
