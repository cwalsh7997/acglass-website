Prestige Marble Bonita Springs.

Prestige Marble Bonita Springs is the kind of project that lands in a portfolio for one reason: the work is correct. Prestige Marble showroom ACG delivered the glazing for general contractor Fort Construction.

Rielly Walsh · November 23, 2025 · 8 min read

The project.

Prestige Marble Bonita Springs is located in Bonita Springs, Lee County, Florida. Prestige Marble showroom. The development was led by Prestige Marble. ACG worked as the glazing subcontractor to general contractor Fort Construction.

The site sits within Lee County jurisdiction. Lee County is a wind-borne debris region, not the HVHZ. The design wind speed is set for the site from FBC Figure 1609.3(1) (Risk Category II). Florida Product Approval is the code path. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Bonita Springs market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Prestige Marble Bonita Springs included showroom storefront. ACG specified, supplied, and installed CGIC SS3500 storefront.

The scope was sequenced to the general contractor's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Bonita Springs location pulled supervision out of our Naples office.
