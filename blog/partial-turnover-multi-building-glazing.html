Quick Answer

On multi-building jobs, turn over one building at a time with its own inspection, punch, and record set. Do not wait for campus-wide completion to organize as-builts and warranties. Track each building's acceptance package so early buildings do not lose paperwork while later buildings are still installing.

Why partial turnover needs its own tracker

Campus and multi-building schedules often occupy Building A while Building C is still in fabrication. If punch and closeout wait for the last building, early buildings enter service without complete records, and warranty start logic gets fuzzy.

Inspection and punch sequence

  1. Define what "building complete" means for glazing (all openings, or a named phase).
  2. Hold a building-level inspection with the GC and design team as required by the contract.
  3. Close punch items that affect weatherproofing before owner occupancy of that building.
  4. Carry cosmetic items on a dated list with responsibility and access notes.
  5. Do not mix Building A punch into Building C's list without clear labels.

Records that must not wait

  • Approved shop drawings for that building's configurations
  • Product-approval documents for installed systems
  • Condition photos and punch clearance
  • Warranty documents indexed to the building
  • Facilities asset-register rows for that building

Frequently Asked Questions

How should multi-building glazing jobs handle partial turnover?

Track inspection, punch, as-builts, and warranties per building. Do not wait for campus-wide completion to file early-building records.

Is this the same as a global project timeline?

No. The timeline article covers phases. This page covers building-level acceptance and document control.

When do warranties start on a partial turnover?

Follow the contract and manufacturer terms. Do not assume a single campus-wide start date without written confirmation.

What should a GC send for phased glazing turnover planning?

Building release sequence, opening counts by building, and whether early buildings will be occupied before later buildings complete.
