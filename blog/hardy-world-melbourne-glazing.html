Hardy World 4.0.

ACG's West Palm Beach crews delivered the glazing envelope at Hardy World 4.0 working direct with the ownership team. Hardy World retail expansion 4.0

Connor Walsh · October 25, 2025 · 12 min read

The project.

Hardy World 4.0 is located in Melbourne, Brevard County, Florida. Hardy World retail expansion 4.0. The development was led by Hardy retail. ACG was retained direct by the ownership team — no general contractor layer between the design intent and the field crew.

The site sits within Brevard County jurisdiction, which enforces Florida Building Code (FBC) coastal. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 150 mph design pressure, with Florida Product Approval (FPA). ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Melbourne market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Hardy World 4.0 included retail storefront. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Melbourne location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
