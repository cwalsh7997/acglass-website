City of Haines Emergency Operations Center.

City of Haines Emergency Operations Center sits in Haines City, Polk County - Haines City emergency response center. ACG executed the glazing scope for general contractor Pirtle Construction.

Rielly Walsh · March 02, 2026 · 8 min read

The project.

City of Haines Emergency Operations Center is located in Haines City, Polk County, Florida. Haines City emergency response center. The development was led by City of Haines. ACG worked as the glazing subcontractor to general contractor Pirtle Construction.

The site sits within Polk County jurisdiction, which enforces Florida Building Code (FBC) inland. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 140 mph design pressure, with Florida Product Approval (FPA). ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Haines City market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on City of Haines Emergency Operations Center included eoc envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Aldora impact-rated storefront.

The scope was sequenced to the general contractor's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Haines City location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
