Waxin's West Palm Beach.

Waxin's West Palm Beach sits in West Palm Beach, Palm Beach County — 230 Clematis Street, downtown WPB. ACG executed the glazing scope direct with the ownership team.

Connor Walsh · August 30, 2025 · 7 min read

The project.

Waxin's West Palm Beach is located in West Palm Beach, Palm Beach County, Florida. 230 Clematis Street, downtown WPB. The development was led by Waxin's restaurant. ACG was retained direct by the ownership team — no general contractor layer between the design intent and the field crew.

The site sits within Palm Beach County jurisdiction, which enforces Palm Beach County HVHZ — enforced from Jupiter through Boca Raton. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 170 mph design pressure for coastal exposure, with Palm Beach County accepts Miami-Dade NOA, plus Florida Product Approval (FPA). ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the West Palm Beach market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Waxin's West Palm Beach included storefront opening glass. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Euro-Wall opening system — manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the West Palm Beach location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
