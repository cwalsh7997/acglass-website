Best Commercial Storefront
Systems for Florida (2026)

A straight comparison of the top systems used in Florida — ESWindows, YKK AP YES 45, Kawneer Trifab, Oldcastle FG 501T, and Arcadia AA3200.

Connor Walsh · 2026-06-10 · 9 min read

Florida's storefront market is dominated by five systems you see on commercial projects from Tampa to Miami. ACG installs all of them. Here's a straight comparison based on real project experience — not manufacturer marketing.

The Five Systems

1. ESWindows (Various Series)

ESWindows is a Florida-based manufacturer based in Medley, with operations built around HVHZ compliance. Their storefront and impact product line is specifically engineered for South Florida wind conditions. If you're building in Miami-Dade or Broward counties, ESWindows is the first system to evaluate.

Strengths: Deep HVHZ product approval library, fast lead times (manufactured domestically), strong support from Florida glazing contractors, competitive pricing on impact-rated configurations.

Considerations: Less familiar to GCs in Central and North Florida. Product line is more focused on impact systems than standard non-impact.

2. YKK AP YES 45 (and YES 40/50)

YKK AP is the commercial glazing arm of the global YKK Group. The YES 45 (2.5-inch nominal depth) is the workhorse of YKK's commercial storefront line. It's one of the most thermally-performed systems in its price class, with an integrated thermal break design that consistently meets Florida energy code without upgrade.

Strengths: Excellent thermal performance, broad product approval coverage, reliable supply chain, available from multiple glazing distributors statewide, strong NFRC documentation.

Considerations: Mid-price tier — not the lowest-priced, not the most expensive. Lead times can stretch to 8–10 weeks when market is busy.

3. Kawneer Trifab 451T (and 501T)

Kawneer (an Arconic company) is the most widely installed commercial storefront system in Florida. The Trifab 451T (2-inch depth, non-thermally broken) and 451T TB (thermally broken) are in thousands of Florida commercial buildings. The 501T covers the larger 4.5-inch depth market.

Strengths: Universal contractor familiarity — virtually every Florida glazing sub has installed Kawneer. Widest distributor network. Fastest delivery on standard configurations. Inspectors know the system well.

Considerations: Thermal performance on non-TB versions is adequate but not exceptional. On high-performance energy projects, the thermally broken version (451T TB) or a competitor with more robust thermal break design may be preferred.

4. Oldcastle BuildingEnvelope FG 501T

Oldcastle (now part of CRH) produces the FG 501T — a 4.5-inch thermally broken storefront system that competes with Kawneer's 501T in the larger-depth market. The FG 501T is particularly well-regarded for high wind zone applications and large glass spans.

Strengths: Strong performance at large spans (up to 10 feet), good Florida product approval coverage including some HVHZ configurations, competitive pricing for mid-market commercial.

Considerations: Distribution is less extensive than Kawneer or YKK — availability varies by region in Florida.

5. Arcadia AA3200

Arcadia's AA3200 is a 2-inch nominal depth storefront popular on budget-sensitive commercial projects and tenant improvements. It's a reliable, code-compliant system at a competitive price point. Not a thermal break system — specify only for climate-controlled spaces where energy code calculations can be met with high-performance glass (which is possible in some configurations).

Strengths: Lowest price tier among quality systems, fast lead times, widely available.

Considerations: No thermal break version available in this series. Energy code compliance depends entirely on glass performance. Not suitable for high wind zone applications without engineering review.

What ACG Recommends by Project Type

  • HVHZ / Miami-Dade projects: ESWindows first (deepest NOA library), then YKK AP or Kawneer with verified NOA coverage for your specific configuration.
  • Standard Florida coastal commercial: Kawneer 451T TB (thermally broken) or YKK AP YES 45 — both have broad FL PA coverage and are familiar to inspectors statewide.
  • High wind / large spans: Kawneer 501T or Oldcastle FG 501T — 4.5-inch depth systems engineered for high-pressure conditions.
  • Budget tenant improvements / non-HVHZ: Arcadia AA3200 or Kawneer 451T standard. Verify energy code compliance with your energy consultant before specifying non-thermal-break systems.
  • High energy performance (LEED, high HVAC efficiency projects): YKK AP YES 45 with Low-E IGU — best whole-assembly U-value in its class at the mid-market price point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best commercial storefront system for Florida?

For HVHZ projects (Miami-Dade and Broward), ESWindows is the top choice — their systems are designed specifically for South Florida wind conditions with the deepest NOA library. For general Florida commercial projects, Kawneer Trifab 451T TB and YKK AP YES 45 are the most widely installed and well-understood systems. The best choice depends on your wind zone, budget, and whether you need HVHZ compliance.

Which storefront systems have Miami-Dade product approval?

ESWindows, YKK AP YES 45, Kawneer Trifab 451T, and Oldcastle FG 501T all have Miami-Dade NOA coverage for specific configurations. Product approval covers specific combinations of frame depth, glass size, glass type, and wind pressure — verify your specific configuration is within the approved limits. Your glazing contractor should provide NOA documentation at submittal.

Is Kawneer or YKK better for Florida commercial projects?

Both are excellent. Kawneer has broader contractor familiarity and faster delivery on standard configurations. YKK AP YES 45 has stronger thermal performance and is preferred on energy-focused projects. For HVHZ, ESWindows is often the better choice. In practice, specify by performance requirements and let your glazing contractor recommend the system they know best — installation quality matters more than brand.
