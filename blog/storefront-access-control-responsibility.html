Scope boundary: This article does not explain how to bypass locks, defeat readers, clone credentials, or alter access software. It only assigns who typically provides power, cable, hardware, and commissioning on a commercial storefront door.

Why storefront access control needs a matrix

Electrified storefront doors sit at the seam of Division 08, Division 26, and Division 28 (or the project’s security package). When the seam is blank, the GC discovers the gap after the frame is glazed: no power at the header, no EPT or electric hinge, no final connection, no programming window.

Fill the matrix at buyout, not at punch.

Responsibility matrix (template)

Mark one primary owner per row. Typical parties: Owner, GC, Electrical, Security / low-voltage, Glazing (Division 08), Hardware supplier. This is a template, not a record of any ACG job, and not legal advice.

Power

Line-voltage to the operator or power supply is rarely a glazing invent. Put it on the electrical drawings and the door elevation. If the elevation shows an electrified opening and the electrical sheets are silent, stop and RFI before fabrication release.

Glazing still needs to know voltage at the hardware (often low-voltage after a power supply) so the correct electric hinge, strike, or exit device is released.

Wiring and transfers

Separate three cable problems:

  • Backbone from the access panel or power supply to the door vicinity
  • Transfer through the frame (EPT, electric hinge, loop)
  • Final landing on the electrified device

Different trades often own each segment. Write the segments into the matrix. Do not assume the glazing crew pulls backbone cable because they set the door.

Electrified hardware

When the door hardware set lists electrified locksets, strikes, exit devices, or operators, Division 08 usually furnishes and installs those devices as part of the storefront package. Readers, panels, and credential systems usually sit with security.

Coordinate hand, swing, stile width, and prep with the approved shop drawings. A reader that does not fit the stile is a design coordination failure, not an install surprise.

Programming and commissioning

Programming means configuring the access system and owner credentials. That work belongs to the security integrator or owner path named in the contract. Glazing commissions the mechanical door and its specified hardware function.

Hold a joint function demo: door opens, latches, closes, and the access system behaves per the written sequence of operations.

Again: this page does not provide instructions for bypassing, spoofing, or defeating access control.

Coordination checklist

  • Door schedule marks electrified openings and hardware set numbers
  • Elevation shows power transfer and reader location
  • Electrical sheets show line-voltage rough-in
  • Security sheets show panel, cable, and device ownership
  • One matrix assigns power, wiring, hardware, programming
  • Joint commissioning date on the master schedule

How to ask for a coordinated quote

Send elevations, the door and window schedule with hardware sets, and a filled or blank copy of the matrix above. State material-only or furnish-and-install for the glazing package. Call out which doors are electrified.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who usually provides line-voltage power for storefront access control?

Line-voltage power to the door location or power supply is typically electrical-trade scope unless the subcontract says otherwise. Confirm receptacle, dedicated circuit, and disconnect requirements on the door elevation and electrical drawings before buyout.

Who runs low-voltage wiring to electrified storefront hardware?

Low-voltage from the access panel or power supply to the hinge, transfer, and hardware is often security or electrical low-voltage scope. The glazing trade usually provides the door, frame prep, and electrified hardware when those items are in Division 08. Assign cable ownership in writing.

Who programs credentials and commissions the reader?

Access-control programming and owner credential setup belong to the security integrator or owner IT path named in the contract. Glazing does not commission readers or issue credentials unless that work is explicitly added. This article does not provide bypass or credential instructions.

How should a GC request a coordinated storefront access-control quote?

Send elevations, the door schedule with hardware sets, and a one-page responsibility matrix marking power, wiring, hardware, and commissioning owners. State material-only vs furnish-and-install for the glazing package.
