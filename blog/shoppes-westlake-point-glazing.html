Shoppes at Westlake Point.

Shoppes at Westlake Point is the kind of project that lands in a portfolio for one reason: the work is correct. Westlake mixed-use community retail ACG delivered the glazing under SISCA.

Connor Walsh · October 12, 2025 · 7 min read

The project.

Shoppes at Westlake Point is located in Westlake, Palm Beach County, Florida. Westlake mixed-use community retail. The development was led by Westlake retail. SISCA served as general contractor.

The site sits within Palm Beach County jurisdiction, which enforces Palm Beach County HVHZ — enforced from Jupiter through Boca Raton. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 170 mph design pressure for coastal exposure, with Palm Beach County accepts Miami-Dade NOA, plus Florida Product Approval (FPA). ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Westlake market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Shoppes at Westlake Point included retail center. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to SISCA's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Westlake location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
