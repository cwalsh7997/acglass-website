What Commercial Glazing Actually Costs in Florida (2026)

Commercial glazing in Florida is priced per square foot of installed system, not per square foot of glass alone. The number includes material, framing, glazing, hardware, field labor, crane or lift equipment where needed, sealants, and shop drawings. It does not typically include structural substrate, perimeter flashing, or the architect's engineering fees. What Drives Pricing Up or Down

The ranges above are wide because the variables are real. When a GC asks "what does glazing cost" on a specific project, six factors push the number within that range.

1. Glass Specification

On curtainwall work, that glass cost is the single biggest driver of the final per-SF number.

2. Framing System and Depth

A 2" x 4-1/2" storefront extrusion costs dramatically less than a 2-1/2" x 7-1/2" curtainwall frame, even before glazing. Anodized finishes run less than Kynar-coated AAMA 2605 finishes, which run less than custom colors.

3. HVHZ vs Non-HVHZ

Projects within the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (Miami-Dade and Broward counties) require Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) certified products with specific installation procedures, anchor schedules, and testing documentation. Installation labor also takes longer in HVHZ because anchor inspections and submittal requirements are stricter.

4. Floor Level and Access

Ground-floor and second-story glazing can often be installed from the ground with standard scissor or boom lifts, which is baseline pricing.

5. Pre-Glazed vs Stick-Built Storefront

This is a meaningful cost and schedule factor that most pricing articles ignore. Pre-glazed storefront systems arrive at the jobsite as complete, factory-assembled units with glass already set — no field glazing required. Stick-built systems, by contrast, arrive as extrusion lengths that are cut, assembled, and glazed on site. Pre-glazed field install is roughly 50% faster because crews are setting finished units rather than building and glazing them. Material cost for pre-glazed runs slightly higher, but the labor savings typically more than offset it, and the quality is more consistent because factory conditions control humidity, temperature, and seal integrity better than any construction site. ACG installs ES-8000 storefront pre-glazed as standard.

6. Scale

Small projects (under 3,000 SF of glazing) price at the high end of the range because fixed costs — mobilization, shop drawings, submittals, engineering — are spread across less area. Projects over 20,000 SF often price at or below the midpoint because those fixed costs amortize out.
