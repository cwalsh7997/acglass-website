Manufacturer Comparison

PGT vs ESWindows
for Commercial Florida

A fair side-by-side. PGT WinGuard, EnergyVue, and Architectural. ESWindows ES-8000, ES-9000, and SGD-2020. Where each wins on Florida commercial projects.

Connor Walsh, ACG · 2026-04-22 · 12 min read
Quick Answer

PGT is U.S.-manufactured (Venice, FL), strong on residential-spec impact lines, and dominates SW Florida residential. ESWindows is Colombian-engineered, dominates South Florida commercial high-rise (ES-7000, ES-8000, ES-50 storefront), and offers larger spans, better thermal performance, and tighter unitized assemblies. ACG installs both. For commercial you usually want ESWindows; for residential infill you usually want PGT.

PGT Innovations and ESWindows are two of the most recognized impact-rated window and door manufacturers serving Florida. Both produce legitimate, NOA-listed, commercial-capable product lines, and both have a long track record on Florida commercial and residential projects. They are not, however, the same kind of manufacturer, and they tend to win on different project profiles. This guide is a fair side-by-side — PGT WinGuard commercial, EnergyVue, and PGT Architectural versus ESWindows ES-8000, ES-9000, and SGD-2020 — written from the perspective of a Florida commercial glazing subcontractor (ACG) that works extensively with ESWindows but respects what PGT does well.

Who These Manufacturers Are

PGT Innovations

PGT Innovations (Nokomis, Florida) is one of the largest impact-rated fenestration manufacturers in North America. Founded in 1980, the company built its reputation on WinGuard — the flagship residential and light-commercial impact window line — and has expanded over decades into additional brands including CGI, Eze-Breeze, Western Window Systems, and others. PGT's strengths are large-volume residential manufacturing capacity, a broad dealer network across Florida, and a well-established set of product approvals. In recent years PGT has expanded its commercial offering through PGT Architectural products targeted at mid-rise and light commercial applications.

ESWindows

ESWindows (Tecnoglass, Colombia, with U.S. distribution through ESWindows LLC in Miami, Florida) is a vertically integrated glass and fenestration manufacturer that specializes in commercial storefronts, window walls, curtainwall, and large-scale sliding glass door systems. The U.S. business is concentrated in commercial and high-end residential Florida work — roughly 70%+ of Tecnoglass revenue originates from Florida projects. ESWindows is the default specification on many Florida mid- to large-scale commercial projects because of the match between the product line and the commercial use case.

Both manufacturers produce legitimate code-approved products. The right question is not which is "better" globally — it is which is better for a specific project's scale, use case, and schedule.

Where PGT Wins

PGT has real strengths that deserve to be called out directly.

Residential and Small Commercial Volume

For single-family homes, townhouse projects, small mixed-use, and ground-floor retail under 30,000 SF, PGT's WinGuard and CGI lines are well-proven, widely approved, and supported by an extensive Florida dealer network. A small-format commercial project can often be served faster and at lower-priced packages by PGT distribution channels than by ordering a specialty commercial line.

Dealer Accessibility

PGT products are available through dozens of Florida dealers, which simplifies procurement for residential GCs and small commercial work. Service parts, replacement glazing, and warranty handling are all widely accessible.

Energy-Focused Residential

The EnergyVue line was specifically developed for Florida energy-code residential applications and carries strong energy ratings competitive with any impact window in the category.

Established Approval Library

PGT has one of the most extensive NOA and Florida Product Approval libraries among impact manufacturers — meaning that for virtually any common residential opening size, there is an approved PGT configuration ready to specify.

Where ESWindows Wins

Mid- to Large-Scale Commercial

For commercial projects above 30,000 SF of glazing — retail centers, hotels, medical office, mid-rise mixed-use, multifamily wrap — ESWindows is the default commercial platform in Florida. The ES-8000 pre-glazed storefront, ES-9000 window wall, and ES-7000 curtainwall are specifically built for commercial DP loads, sight-line requirements, and thermal performance.

Pre-Glazed Storefront Manufacturing

The ES-8000 system is pre-glazed at the ESWindows Miami facility. Frames arrive on site with glass already installed, tested, and sealed. This reduces field labor, accelerates close-in schedules, and improves quality compared to field-glazed alternatives. For a commercial GC running a tight schedule, pre-glazed storefront is a meaningful differentiator — and it is a primary reason ACG standardizes on ESWindows for commercial storefront work.

Large Openings and Commercial Sliders

The SGD-2020 commercial sliding glass door handles opening sizes and panel weights beyond typical residential slider capacity. This matters on hospitality, restaurant, and Class A commercial projects with large-format openings. ESWindows also offers multi-slide and lift-and-slide configurations for specialty applications.

Window Wall and Curtainwall Capability

ESWindows offers both window wall (ES-9000) and curtainwall (ES-7000) with commercial-grade thermal breaks, structural silicone glazing options, and custom extrusion capability. PGT does not compete in the window wall or curtainwall category at meaningful scale.

Vertical Integration

Tecnoglass manufactures its own glass (insulated and laminated), aluminum extrusions, and hardware in an integrated facility. That vertical integration produces consistent lead times, tighter tolerances, and single-source accountability on commercial specs. For a GC or architect dealing with a commercial schedule, that consolidation materially reduces coordination risk.

Approval Documentation and Code Compliance

Both manufacturers carry robust Florida Product Approval (FL number) and Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) libraries. For Florida commercial work, the practical comparison is:

  • PGT: strongest NOA coverage on residential and light-commercial window and door sizes; large library of approved configurations out of the box
  • ESWindows: deepest NOA and FL number coverage on commercial storefront, window wall, and sliding glass door; larger opening sizes and higher DP ratings than most PGT approvals

On any project above 30,000 SF of glazing, the approval match is better with ESWindows because the NOAs cover commercial-scale openings. On residential or small commercial, the approval match is better with PGT because the NOAs cover the openings without custom engineering.

Approval documents matter beyond code compliance. Insurance carriers, wind mitigation inspectors, and prospective buyers all read the approval trail on commercial properties. See our commercial glazing warranties guide for the documentation framework.

Pricing Context

Both manufacturers sell commercial product in roughly the same pricing range. Apples-to-apples specifications on similar opening sizes with similar DP ratings and glass makeups are usually within 5–15% of each other at the product level. The larger cost drivers on a Florida commercial project are typically installation complexity, site conditions, schedule pressure, and the quality of the glazing subcontractor — not the manufacturer markup.

Full pricing ranges for Florida commercial impact glazing — across both manufacturers and the general market — are in our how much does impact glass cost commercial buildings Florida article.

Warranty and Service

PGT and ESWindows both offer commercial glazing warranties in the 10-year range on glass seal failure and frame finish, with shorter periods on hardware and operators. The more important factor on commercial projects is the glazing subcontractor's workmanship warranty, which typically runs 1–2 years and covers the installation itself. Manufacturer warranty only kicks in if the installation is done per the product approval — which is why approval-referenced shop drawings and documented installation are non-negotiable regardless of which manufacturer is specified.

How ACG Chooses Between Them

ACG is a Florida commercial glazing subcontractor, and ESWindows is our flagship commercial product partner. That partnership reflects where our project mix sits — mid- to large-scale commercial, retail centers, hotels, medical, mixed-use, and multifamily wraps where the commercial fit of ES-8000, ES-9000, SGD-2020, and ES-7000 is strongest. Representative ACG projects using ESWindows systems include Wave Food Hall (Cocoa Beach), KLUS Lighting (Vero Beach), Lake Park Innovation, Dale Mabry Retail (Tampa), Bobcat Treasure Coast, Baron Shoppes Tradition, Harbour Cay II, and Villa L'Onz (Riviera Beach).

For smaller commercial projects, owner-occupied retail under 30,000 SF, or residential adjacencies to a commercial scope, PGT WinGuard and PGT Architectural are legitimate specifications. Those projects typically run through PGT's dealer network rather than a commercial glazing subcontractor like ACG. When a commercial client asks for PGT on a mid- to large-scale project, we document the fit tradeoff (lead time, commercial sight lines, DP range, pre-glazed advantage) and let the owner and architect decide.

Quick Decision Framework

When specifying impact-rated glazing on a Florida commercial project, a simple decision frame:

  1. Residential or small-commercial under 30,000 SF: PGT WinGuard, CGI, or PGT Architectural are strong specifications. Broad dealer network, large NOA library, proven products.
  2. Mid- to large-scale commercial over 30,000 SF: ESWindows ES-8000 storefront, ES-9000 window wall, SGD-2020 sliders, or ES-7000 curtainwall are the better commercial fit. Pre-glazed installation advantage, larger opening approvals, higher DP options, vertically integrated manufacturing.
  3. Class A repositioning or mixed-use: ESWindows for commercial storefront and window wall; consider PGT for any residential tower component within the project.
  4. Lead time under 12 weeks: Tight. Both manufacturers typically quote longer. Plan project schedules accordingly. For deeper discussion see our commercial hurricane impact windows pillar article.
