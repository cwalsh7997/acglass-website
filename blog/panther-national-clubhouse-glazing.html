Project Press Release · Palm Beach Gardens, FL

Panther National Clubhouse.

The Palm Beach Gardens envelope at Panther National Clubhouse was a Euro-Wall DirectSet storefront package. ACG quoted, supplied, and installed it under Verdex Construction.

Rielly Walsh · January 03, 2026 · 7 min read

The project.

Panther National Clubhouse is located in Palm Beach Gardens, Palm Beach County, Florida. Jack Nicklaus + Justin Thomas course; first new private gated golf community in Palm Beach County in nearly 20 years; Avenir community. The development was led by Dominik Senn, with architectural design by Max Strang of STRANG with Peacock + Lewis Architects. Verdex Construction served as general contractor.

The site sits within Palm Beach County jurisdiction, which enforces Palm Beach County HVHZ — enforced from Jupiter through Boca Raton. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 170 mph design pressure for coastal exposure, with Palm Beach County accepts Miami-Dade NOA, plus Florida Product Approval (FPA). ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Palm Beach Gardens market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on Panther National Clubhouse included 60,000 sq ft modern golf clubhouse glazing envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Euro-Wall DirectSet storefront, multi-slide, Vistafold, pivot — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to Verdex Construction's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Palm Beach Gardens location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
