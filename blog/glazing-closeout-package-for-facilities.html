Why warranties are not enough

A warranty tells you who to call when something fails within a term. It does not reliably tell a future project manager the Low-E surface, spacer, interlayer, finish code, or product-approval configuration needed to match a broken lite five years later.

Closeout package contents

  • As-built opening list keyed to elevations
  • Approved shop drawings and product data
  • Product-approval documents for installed configurations
  • Glass and finish sample approvals (if used)
  • Warranty documents with start dates as defined by the contract
  • Maintenance guidance references from manufacturers
  • Asset register spreadsheet or PDF matching the fields above

Turnover meeting checklist

Walk representative openings, confirm label photos exist, confirm the register matches installed marks, and name the owner contact who stores the package.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a glazing closeout package include for facilities teams?

As-builts, approved product data, product-approval documents, warranties, maintenance references, and an opening-by-opening asset register with makeup and finish fields.

Is a warranty binder the same as a closeout package?

No. Warranties are one part. Matching future glass needs makeup, approval, and finish data that warranties often omit.

When should the asset register be created?

Start during submittals and finalize at turnover, while labels and shop drawings are still easy to reach.

What should an owner request at bid time?

Explicitly list the facilities closeout / asset-register deliverable in the glazing scope.
