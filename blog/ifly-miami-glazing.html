iFLY Miami Indoor Skydiving.

ACG's West Palm Beach crews delivered the glazing envelope at iFLY Miami Indoor Skydiving on Parkway Construction's schedule. Indoor skydiving experience facility

Connor Walsh · January 25, 2026 · 9 min read

The project.

iFLY Miami Indoor Skydiving is located in Miami, Miami-Dade County, Florida. Indoor skydiving experience facility. The development was led by iFLY Holdings, with architectural design by iFLY facility design. Parkway Construction served as general contractor.

The site sits within Miami-Dade County jurisdiction, which enforces Miami-Dade HVHZ. Commercial glazing scopes in this jurisdiction are designed for 175+ mph design pressure, with Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) required. ACG's commercial glazing license (FL CGC #1531993) and active product approval listings cover the Miami market.

ACG's scope.

The glazing package on iFLY Miami Indoor Skydiving included exterior storefront + wind tunnel viewing glass. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows, custom impact — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to Parkway Construction's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Miami location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
