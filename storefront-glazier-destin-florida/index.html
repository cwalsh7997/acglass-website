Why commercial glazing in Destin is different.

Destin is one of the busiest commercial glazing markets in the Panhandle and one of the most demanding. 140 mph design winds, full WBDR coverage, salt-fog corrosion on every aluminum extrusion, and developers running fast schedules between spring break and Memorial Day. ACG bids Destin commercial work as a Florida-licensed specialty subcontractor with the impact glass, anchor calcs, and Florida Product Approvals you need to clear permitting at Okaloosa County and the City of Destin without delays.

Architectural context: Destin commercial work splits between coastal resort retail (Destin Commons, HarborWalk Village, Baytowne Wharf) and high-end restaurant/medical along US-98 — both demand impact glazing rated for 140 mph open-coast exposure and the chloride-corrosion finish work that comes with year-round salt fog.

Destin commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Destin in the Wind-Borne Debris Region?

Yes. Coastal Okaloosa County is full WBDR — 140+ mph design winds. Every storefront, entrance, and window we quote in Destin defaults to Florida Product Approval impact compliance, Missile Level C on inland projects and Missile Level D on direct-exposure coastal.

Can you handle Sandestin and the 30A corridor from a Destin project?

Yes. Sandestin commercial (Baytowne Wharf, Grand Boulevard) and the 30A corridor (Miramar Beach, Santa Rosa Beach, Seaside, WaterColor, Rosemary, Alys Beach, Inlet Beach) are inside our standard Panhandle service zone. We bid those markets the same way we bid Destin proper.

Do you supply Euro-Wall folding doors on Destin restaurant projects?

Yes. ACG installs Eurowall folding and sliding systems and we specify their impact-rated folding and sliding systems on Destin restaurants and bayfront resort projects where the operator wants the full-opening Gulf-facing elevation.

How does your pricing compare to a local Destin glazier?

We don't compete on price — we compete on speed, completeness, and Florida-wide manufacturer relationships. Local Destin glaziers may quote cheaper on a small storefront, but when you need 80 impact units delivered on a permit deadline, we have the supply chain to do it.

What's your liability and bonding coverage for Destin work?

Florida-licensed and insured. Florida CGC #1531993 statewide. Workers' comp on every project. Certificates of insurance available on request.
