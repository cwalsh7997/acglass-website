1.02The scope we bid

ACG bids the openings package. On a federal job that usually means the following sections, priced together so there is one subcontractor holding the glass and the frames that carry it.

We also price the interface work that federal packages tend to leave stranded between trades: interior glazing and glass partitions, roller shades, magnetic glass marker boards, and louver-to-frame interfaces where the louver ships under mechanical but has to land in our opening.

Note

1.03Hardened and high-performance openings

Federal openings ask for things a retail storefront never does: forced entry resistance, ballistic ratings, blast response, and domestic sourcing. Those are separate product lines, not upgrades to a standard system.

  • Forced entry, ballistic and attack resistant. Norshield, US Bullet Proofing and Insulgard assemblies where a rated window, door or transaction opening is specified.
  • Aluminum framing and windows on federal packages. Kawneer Trifab 451T for thermally broken framing, Wausau for aluminum window assemblies including blast and hardened series.
  • High wind and impact. ESWindows and PGT impact-rated systems, tested to Florida and HVHZ protocols, which is what coastal federal sites in the Southeast are designed to.
  • Fire-rated. Technical Glass Products framed and frameless fire-rated glazing for rated corridors and stair enclosures.

Exclusion we always state in writing

Independent blast and structural glazing engineering, when the specification requires a delegated engineer of record, is carried as a separate identified line or excluded from our base bid. It is never buried in a lump sum and discovered at submittal.

1.04How we bid a federal job

The difference between a good glazing sub and a problem on a federal job is almost never the glass. It is whether the proposal told the truth about what was in it. Ours do four things every time.

  • Inclusions and exclusions in writing, on the face of the bid. Delegated engineering, badging and base access fees, special inspections, and anything furnished by others is named, not implied.
  • Bond stated, not hedged. Payment and performance bond available at 2.5% of contract value on request, from our surety, against our $3M single project capacity.
  • One authoritative proposal. If a number or a scope changes before award, we issue a corrected proposal that supersedes the prior one in writing. There is never a question about which document governs.
  • 48 hours on a documented scope. Send drawings, the Division 08 spec sections and the bid date. If the set is incomplete, we tell you what is missing the same day instead of guessing and repricing later.

1.07Two delivery models

How we serve a federal project depends on where it is.

  • Furnish and install. Self-performed throughout Florida, from West Palm Beach through the Keys and across to the Gulf Coast, run out of West Palm Beach, Naples and Tampa.
  • Furnish only. Engineered and approved openings packages shipped to the prime's installer outside Florida. We have delivered furnish-only packages to projects in Louisiana and Tennessee.

We would rather tell you which model fits your site than promise crews we do not have standing in your state.
