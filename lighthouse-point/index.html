Lighthouse Point Context

Commercial glazing in Lighthouse Point, Broward County.

Lighthouse Point commercial glazing context. Lighthouse Point is in Broward County, in Florida's South Florida market. It is in Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), which means every commercial opening — storefront, impact window, curtainwall, all-glass entrance — must carry Miami-Dade NOA documentation for the specific configuration installed. Exposure category for Lighthouse Point commercial work is typically D for direct ocean or Gulf-front sites.

What we install in Lighthouse Point. ACG handles the full Division 08 commercial glazing scope: storefront systems for ground-floor retail and restaurants, all-glass entrance doors with hardware and operator coordination, hurricane impact windows from ESWindows, PGT, and Slimpact, and glass railing systems for terraces and balconies. Every installation includes the manufacturer NOA or FL# documentation, engineer-stamped shop drawings where the project requires, and a field inspection log the GC can use to close out the scope at certificate of occupancy.

Who we work with in Lighthouse Point. ACG bids directly to general contractors active in the Lighthouse Point market, restaurant operators specifying Euro-Wall folding walls and impact storefronts, hospitality and hotel ownership groups, healthcare systems, private clubs, and commercial developers. We are active on Procore and BuildingConnected for Lighthouse Point project invites, and the 48-hour bid turnaround that ACG runs on commercial work fits the Lighthouse Point operator and GC timeline at the front of the project.
