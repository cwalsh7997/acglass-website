The 48-Hour Scope Commitment

Send drawings Monday — you have a detailed Division 08 scope letter Wednesday. Not a phone-call quote. Not an allowance-stuffed budget number. A line-item scope with system recommendations, named manufacturer per opening category, NOA / Florida Product Approval cross-references, and an explicit exclusion list.

What "48-hour scope" actually means. ACG's streamlined estimating platform processes drawings, runs takeoffs, matches openings to approved manufacturer systems, and writes a structured scope letter. A human PM reviews and signs every scope letter before it leaves. The 48-hour clock starts when complete drawings, spec, and a clear scope-of-bid request are in hand.

Approved Manufacturers (Seven Only)

ACG holds direct manufacturer certification with six commercial systems suppliers. These are the only systems that appear in ACG's scope letters. Approved-equal substitution is handled through formal RFI and submittal — never quietly swapped on the field.

  • ESWindows / Tecnoglass — impact storefront, curtainwall, structural systems. Authorized commercial installer.
  • Euro-Wall — multi-slide, bifold, pivot, lift-and-slide. Authorized commercial installer.
  • PGT — impact windows and doors. WinGuard and EnergyVue lines.
  • Allegion — commercial door hardware and automatic entrance components.
  • TGP — fire-rated glass and framing. Pilkington Pyrostop / Pyrobel.
  • Slimpact — slim-profile impact systems for high-design openings.
ACG does not install CGI Windows, Wausau, Viracon, or Arcadia. If your spec calls for one of those, send the spec — ACG provides approved-equal documentation and a basis-of-design substitution package for review.
