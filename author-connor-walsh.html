Background

Connor Walsh is the President of American Commercial Glass. He holds Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993 — the credential required to legally pull permits for commercial glazing scopes in the State of Florida.

Before founding ACG in 2020 with his wife Rielly Walsh, Connor flew commercial jets professionally. A 2017 traumatic brain injury ended his aviation career and pulled him back into construction full-time. He founded Liberty Impact in 2014, scaling it from $400K to $10M in annual revenue before selling. He also founded GoGym, a portable training company, and completed the 75 Hard mental toughness program twice.

At ACG, Connor authors the technical content on acglass.com — the Florida code reference articles, the glazing system comparisons, the bid-process guides. The work is reviewed against Florida Building Code 2023, Miami-Dade NOA criteria, and ACG's 350+ project field data.

Connor is a member of Arete Syndicate, an executive growth community led by Andy Frisella and Ed Mylett.
