About

I'm Connor Walsh, President and co-founder of American Commercial Glass, Inc. — a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, with offices in Naples and Tampa. We're opening Nashville in Q3 2026.

ACG is a Division 08 subcontractor. We self-perform commercial storefront, curtain wall, impact-rated window, multi-slide door, and fire-rated steel storefront installations across Florida. We're an authorized installer for ESWindows, Euro-Wall, TGP, Allegion, PGT, and Slimpact.

What I write about

I write about commercial glazing as a working subcontractor — not as a pundit, marketer, or product brochure. My focus areas:

  • The future of commercial glazing — how AI-managed operations, faster bid turnaround, and software-defined submittals are changing a trade that hadn't fundamentally evolved since the 1990s
  • HVHZ & NOA compliance — Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone code, Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance, and what's coming as design pressure thresholds keep ratcheting up
  • ESWindows commercial systems — why we standardize on ESWindows for FL impact and what we've learned across 350+ installations
  • Division 08 economics for GCs — bid clarity, scope coordination, schedule predictability, and why the glazing sub usually determines whether a commercial job hits its closeout date
  • AI in construction operations — what's hype, what's working (we ship daily with sub.ai, jobcost.ai, and a CFO agent), and what comes next

Background

I run ACG day-to-day with my wife Rielly Walsh (CEO). We compete on speed, reliability, and AI-augmented operations — not price. Our customers are commercial general contractors bidding $50K–$2M+ projects, plus commercial developers and restaurant/hospitality owners who hire glaziers direct for Euro-Wall folding-door scope.

I'm a regular guest writer on commercial glazing topics. If you're an editor at a trade publication or a podcast covering construction, AI in trades, or Florida commercial real estate, you can reach me at connor@acglass.com.
