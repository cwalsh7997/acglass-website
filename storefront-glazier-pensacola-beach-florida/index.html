Pensacola Beach commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Pensacola Beach the same as Pensacola?

Adjacent but distinct. Pensacola Beach is a barrier island in the Gulf — Escambia County. Pensacola proper is on the mainland Pensacola Bay. Both are Escambia County, both 150 mph design wind, both WBDR.

What finish does Pensacola Beach commercial typically require?

Kynar 500 PVDF over standard Class I anodize on direct beachfront elevations — salt fog will dull standard anodize in 5–7 years. Kynar holds 15+.

Lead time on Pensacola Beach commercial?

10–14 weeks for impact storefront with PVDF finish — the PVDF coat is custom and adds 2–4 weeks vs. standard anodize.

Can you handle hotel commercial glazing?

Yes. Hotel ground-floor retail and restaurant impact storefront is inside our standard scope.

Who permits Pensacola Beach commercial?

Escambia County BCC permits commercial work on Santa Rosa Island. We pull permits as the Florida-licensed prime/sub of record.
