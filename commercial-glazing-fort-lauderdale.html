HVHZ in Broward County

Why HVHZ Experience Matters
for Broward GCs.

Broward County is entirely within Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) - the same designation that covers all of Miami-Dade County. For GCs bidding and building in Fort Lauderdale, this is the single most important compliance factor in the glazing scope. A glazing sub without proven HVHZ documentation experience will cost you time, money, and plan review re-submissions.

The core requirement: every glazing system installed in Broward County needs Miami-Dade NOA (Notice of Acceptance) - not just a Florida Product Approval (FL#). NOA approval is issued by Miami-Dade County's Building Code Compliance Office (BCCO) and requires testing under TAS (Testing Application Standard) protocols. TAS 201 tests large missile impact resistance; TAS 202 tests uniform static air pressure; TAS 203 tests cyclic wind pressure loading. These protocols exceed the ASTM test standards used for standard FL approvals.

The failure mode ACG frequently inherits: a glazing sub awarded the Broward County scope shows up with materials carrying only FL approval numbers. Broward plan review flags the documentation as insufficient for HVHZ. Re-submittal takes weeks. The project schedule slips. The GC takes the pain. ACG prevents this - our submittal packages are NOA-compliant from day one, built specifically for Broward County plan review standards.

HVHZ Compliance Checklist
  • ✓ Miami-Dade NOA numbers for all installed systems
  • ✓ TAS 201/202/203 test reports on file
  • ✓ Engineer-of-record signed & sealed shop drawings
  • ✓ Wind load calculations per ASCE 7 and FBC HVHZ
  • ✓ Anchor design per manufacturer's NOA conditions
  • ✓ Broward County plan review submission-ready package

Is Broward County part of the HVHZ zone? +

Yes. All of Broward County is within Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ).
