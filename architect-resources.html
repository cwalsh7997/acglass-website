How to Use This Page

Everything specifiers need. Direct PDFs.

Specification documents are organized by manufacturer and system. Every link opens the verified-current PDF directly from the manufacturer's server — no forms, no login walls, no stale reprints.

For CAD details, Revit families, or specific test reports not listed, contact Connor direct. Most manufacturer CAD packages are login-gated and ACG can pull them for an active project in under a business day.

Specifier Services

Direct from the installer.
No distributor middle layer.

01

Budget Direction at SD/DD

Per-square-foot budget ranges for system selection before CDs. ACG provides ranges based on actual Florida pricing, not national averages.

02

System Selection Review

Confirm ESWindows or Euro-Wall system fit for your specific project exposure, height, wind load, and code requirements before spec release.

03

CAD & Revit Support

Shop-drawing-ready CAD details and Revit families available from ACG for active projects. Faster than pulling from manufacturer login-gated portals.

04

Performance Mock-Up Coordination

AAMA 501.1 dynamic water and thermal testing coordinated at approved Florida labs. Mock-up details specified in shop-drawing phase.

05

AIA Lunch & Learn

ACG delivers HSW-approved continuing education on Florida Division 08 best practices. Scheduled on request at your office.

06

Submittal Package Review

Pre-submittal review of proposed manufacturer packages. ACG flags NOA gaps, DP mismatches, and coordination conflicts before GC submits.
