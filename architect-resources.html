Before Specification or Bid Release

Close the coordination gaps.

Opening and product

Match the opening marks, elevations, operation and system names across the plans, schedule and proposed product package.

Glass and performance

Identify the specified glazing makeup and project performance criteria. Compare them with the selected configuration in the manufacturer and approval documents.

Substrate and water management

Coordinate the supporting construction, perimeter conditions, waterproofing transitions and sill with the relevant project trades.

Door hardware and controls

Review the hardware sets, operator category, electrical scope and access-control interfaces together. Use the automatic entrance coordination checklist for that opening review.

Submittal responsibilities

Identify who supplies each product document, shop drawing, calculation, sample and review response. Track unresolved comments before fabrication release.

Bid and installation scope

Send the project address, plans, schedules, addenda and bid date. Identify access, sequencing and work by other trades so the proposed scope can address them.

For broader system coordination, use the commercial storefront guide, curtainwall guide and architect and GC resource index.

ACG reference guides: aluminum storefront specification guide and aluminum curtainwall specification guide. Use the selected manufacturer's current documents for the product package.
