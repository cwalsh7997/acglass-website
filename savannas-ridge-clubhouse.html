Savannas Ridge Clubhouse

The amenity and leasing center for the Savannas Ridge community in Fort Pierce, FL - one of the most glass-intensive clubhouse scopes ACG has delivered. Every glazing system in the building is ESWindows, installed by ACG.

Location Fort Pierce, FL GC Mattamy Homes Systems ESWindows - every system Market Treasure Coast
01
Sheet A-011 · The project

Glass from the entry tower to the pool deck.

The Savannas Ridge Clubhouse is a standalone building combining a double-height entry foyer, a dining and gathering room, leasing offices, and a pool-facing lounge - all wrapped in an ESWindows glazing package that defines the building's character from every angle.

ACG supplied and installed the complete ESWindows package across the entire building: the two-story curtainwall and storefront at the main entry tower, large fixed picture windows across the exterior elevations, multi-panel folding glass doors connecting the dining room to the pool deck, commercial windows in the lounge and leasing areas, and interior storefront glass partitions for the private offices.

The interior foyer shot says everything - double-height ESWindows glass walls and entry doors, sunlight cutting across the polished floor, the pool and landscape visible through every pane. That's what a well-specified and properly installed ESWindows package looks like from inside a building.

Glass-intensive by design
