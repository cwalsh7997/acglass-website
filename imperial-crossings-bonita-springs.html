Imperial Crossings · Bonita Springs, FL
The project

Hundreds of openings. One pace.

Imperial Crossings is a large-scale luxury apartment community in Bonita Springs, FL — a master-planned development featuring both mid-rise apartment buildings and attached townhome-style units set around a pond, greenways and resort amenities.

This is the kind of project that requires a glazing sub with real multifamily experience: hundreds of window and door openings across multiple building types, tight construction sequencing, and Florida impact code requirements across every single unit.

ACG supplied and installed the complete PGT impact window and door package across the entire development — every unit in every building, the mid-rise towers and the townhome blocks alike. Delivered for Build, a large-scale multifamily GC that runs complex, multi-phase communities — ACG matched their pace from groundbreaking through closeout.

Scope notes

Multifamily at development scale.

Two building types

Mid-rise apartment buildings and attached townhome blocks, each with its own sequencing — and a consistent installation standard across both.

Impact code, every unit

PGT impact-rated windows and doors on every opening in the development — clean bronze frames set into the CMU block openings, properly shimmed and sealed.

Consistency and speed

On a development with this many openings, consistency and speed matter as much as quality. ACG delivered all three, including the floor-to-ceiling gabled glass entry at the clubhouse.
