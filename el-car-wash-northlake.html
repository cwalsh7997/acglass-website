The Project

El Car Wash is a premium express car wash chain expanding rapidly across South Florida. Their Northlake Boulevard location in West Palm Beach is a purpose-built commercial facility featuring a multi-level building with retail and amenity space on the upper level above the wash tunnel.

Hooks Construction managed the project and brought ACG in to deliver the ESWindows commercial storefront and window package — keeping the glazing scope consistent with the brand's clean, premium aesthetic across both levels of the building.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the complete ESWindows package: the retail-level storefronts on the main building facing Northlake Boulevard, the tower element glazing, and all commercial windows throughout the upper-level amenity spaces. The ESWindows dark-framed systems maintain the clean contrast the El Car Wash brand specifies across all their locations.

Retail rollout clients like El Car Wash need consistency across locations and a glazing sub who can execute to brand standards without variation. ACG delivered to spec, on time, for Hooks on this one.

The glazing package on El Car Wash Northlake included storefront and tunnel glazing. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows — manufactured by ACG's six approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Lake Park location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
