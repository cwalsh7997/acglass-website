Wynwood Context

Commercial glazing in Wynwood, Miami-Dade County.

What gets built in Wynwood. Wynwood is the arts and dining district just north of downtown Miami, a former warehouse quarter rebuilt into galleries, restaurants, bars, breweries, retail, creative-office, and an increasing wave of mixed-use development. Its commercial construction is restaurant-and-retail-heavy and design-forward: operators want open, transparent fronts, folding and sliding glass walls that open the dining room to the street, and storefront that reads as architecture, not just an envelope.

What ACG does in Wynwood. Restaurant and bar storefront, folding glass walls and multi-slide doors that open spaces to the sidewalk, retail and gallery storefront, all-glass and aluminum entrances, interior glass partitions for creative-office fit-outs, and the impact-rated windows and doors that Miami-Dade requires. We work the full Division 08 scope single-source — the frame, the glass, the hardware, and the submittal package — which keeps fast-turning restaurant and retail buildouts on schedule.

HVHZ and Miami-Dade NOA. Wynwood is in Miami-Dade County's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, the strictest envelope jurisdiction in the country. Every commercial storefront, window, door, and folding wall must carry a current Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) for the exact configuration installed, with large-missile impact testing and design-pressure documentation. The open, glass-forward fronts operators want still have to meet that bar, and ACG's submittal package includes the NOA, install instructions tied to the approval, and engineer-stamped shop drawings where required.

Served from West Palm Beach. Wynwood is served out of ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters, which runs the South Florida and Miami-Dade market. Connor Walsh and the WPB office keep project management and field crews staged for Miami-Dade work, including the tight site logistics, after-hours, and phased access that an active urban district like Wynwood demands.

Who we work with. ACG bids directly to general contractors, restaurant and hospitality operators, retail and gallery tenants, and developers active in Wynwood. We are active on Procore and BuildingConnected. The 48-hour bid turnaround fits the fast front-end of restaurant and retail work, and ACG locks rough opening dimensions in pre-construction so install runs on schedule. Lead times run 8–16 weeks from approved shop drawings depending on system.
