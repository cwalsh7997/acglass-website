Florida Commercial Glass & Glazing Statistics (2026)

Industry stats, hurricane glass adoption, code milestones, and construction benchmarks for Florida commercial glazing — each figure sourced and dated. Compiled from US Census, BLS, the Florida Building Code, NOAA, and American Commercial Glass project records. Free to cite — attribution to American Commercial Glass appreciated. Last reviewed July 2026.

120+
Hurricane landfalls

Florida has recorded more hurricane landfalls than any other US state — roughly 120–125 since 1851.

2
HVHZ counties

Miami-Dade and Broward are the two High-Velocity Hurricane Zone counties, where large-missile impact glazing is mandatory.

~200 mph
Peak design wind

Ultimate design wind speeds reach roughly 200 mph in the Florida Keys under ASCE 7-22 — among the highest in the US.

1992
Impact-code origin

Hurricane Andrew drove the impact-glazing standards that became the statewide Florida Building Code in 2002.

Hurricane exposure & code

Florida's glazing market is shaped by wind. The state's exposure and its code response are the two variables that decide which products a commercial building can use.
