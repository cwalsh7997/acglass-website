Direct answer

Commercial storefront glass: $55–$110 per square foot installed. Commercial curtainwall: $95–$180 per square foot installed. Add ~20% for HVHZ (Miami-Dade / Broward) NOA assemblies. Vistafold and multi-slide doors: $400–$700 per linear foot ($180–$320/SF equivalent). All ranges include framing, glass, hardware, sealants, and standard installation. Add 10–15% for engineering, permitting, and water testing.

What drives commercial glass cost

1. Impact rating

Impact-rated glass uses laminated PVB or SentryGlas® interlayer between two glass lites. This adds 20–30% to material cost versus monolithic tempered. In HVHZ (Miami-Dade NOA), the framing must also be NOA-listed, which restricts manufacturer choice and adds 10–15% to framing cost.

2. Glass make-up

Insulated glass units (IGUs) cost 35–50% more than monolithic glass. Adding Low-E coating: +$8–$12/SF. Tinted or spandrel: +$4–$8/SF. SentryGlas® laminated (vs PVB): +20%.

3. Framing system

Storefront aluminum (1¾"–2" deep) is the most economical. Curtainwall with pressure cap, captured glazing: mid-range. Structural silicone glazed (SSG), unitized curtainwall, or specialty finishes (anodized, custom paint): highest. ESWindows, Euro-Wall, ESWindows-equivalent commercial lines all fall within these ranges depending on series.

4. Installation access

Ground-level work is baseline. Swing-stage from roof: +$3–$6/SF. Crane setup for unitized panels: $4,000–$12,000/day. Tight urban access: +5–10%. Historic-rehab adjustments are project-specific and not included in standard rates.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a typical commercial glazing project cost in Florida?

A mid-size commercial impact storefront (5,000–10,000 SF, single-story) runs $400,000–$1.1M in Florida in 2026, depending on HVHZ status, region, and glass spec.

Is curtainwall worth the premium over storefront?

Curtainwall is required when you span floor-to-floor without intermediate horizontal supports, when you exceed ~10 ft height, or when wind load engineering demands structural silicone. For one-story retail or restaurant up to 10 ft, storefront is the correct system and saves 30–40% versus curtainwall.

Why does HVHZ cost 20% more?

HVHZ assemblies must hold a Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA), which requires Large Missile Impact + cyclic-pressure testing for the full assembly (frame + glass + anchorage). Only a subset of manufacturers and product configurations carry NOAs, and they cost more.

What is not included in $/SF rates?

Crane and swing-stage rental, after-hours premium, historic rehabilitation adjustments, demolition of existing glazing, and lead/asbestos abatement are project-specific and quoted separately. The $/SF rates above include framing, glass, hardware, sealants, standard installation, and project supervision.
