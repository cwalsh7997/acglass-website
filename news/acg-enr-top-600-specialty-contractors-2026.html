American Commercial Glass Named to ENR's 2026 Top 600 Specialty Contractors

WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA · OCTOBER 29, 2026

WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA · OCTOBER 29, 2026

American Commercial Glass (ACG), a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, has been named to Engineering News-Record's 2026 Top 600 Specialty Contractors list. ACG joins the list at No. 600 in ENR's October 26, 2026 issue.

"Being considered alongside the firms on this list means something to us," said Connor Walsh, President of ACG. "Earning a place in ENR's rankings is a milestone we don't take lightly."

About the ranking

ENR ranks specialty contractors across the United States by the construction revenue they report for the prior year. The 2026 list is based on 2025 revenue. The full ranking is available to ENR subscribers at enr.com.

For interviews or photography requests, contact press@acglass.com.

About American Commercial Glass: American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa. Founded in 2021 and led by Rielly Walsh (CEO) and Connor Walsh (President), ACG installs commercial storefront, curtain wall, impact-rated glazing, and entrance systems for general contractors across Florida. ACG has completed more than 350 commercial projects and installed more than 1,000,000 square feet of glazing.
