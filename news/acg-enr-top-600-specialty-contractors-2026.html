West Palm Beach, Fla. · October 29, 2026

American Commercial Glass (ACG), a commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, has been named to Engineering News-Record's 2026 Top 600 Specialty Contractors list. The ranking appears in ENR's October 26, 2026 issue and is ACG's first year on the list.

ENR's Top 600 ranks specialty contractors across the United States by the construction revenue they report for the prior year. The 2026 ranking is based on 2025 revenue.

“Being considered alongside the firms on this list means something to us. Earning a place in ENR's rankings is a milestone we don't take lightly.”

Connor Walsh, President, American Commercial Glass

Built in Florida since 2021

ACG started in West Palm Beach in February 2021 as a commercial glazing subcontractor for general contractors. Since then the company has completed more than 350 commercial projects across Florida, installed more than 1,000,000 square feet of glazing, and opened offices in Naples and Tampa.

“American Commercial Glass is a young company,” Walsh said. “We started in 2021, and we've built it into a commercial glazing contractor serving Florida across storefront, curtain wall, impact systems, and specialty door packages.”

ACG's portfolio includes the Atlantic Fields Golf House in Hobe Sound, Eau Palm Beach Resort & Spa in Manalapan, Gulfside Twelve in Fort Myers Beach and the Cudjoe Key Fire Station in the Florida Keys.

What ACG installs

ACG furnishes and installs Division 08 glazing for general contractors: storefront and entrance systems, curtain wall and window wall, impact-rated windows and doors for Florida's high-velocity hurricane zones, folding and sliding glass walls, fire-rated glazing and interior glass. The company holds Florida certified general contractor license CGC #1531993.

For more information about American Commercial Glass, visit acglass.com.

About American Commercial Glass

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach, with offices in Naples and Tampa. Founded in 2021, ACG is led by Rielly Walsh, CEO, and Connor Walsh, President. ACG installs storefront, curtain wall, window wall, impact-rated glazing and entrance systems for general contractors across Florida. The company has completed more than 350 commercial projects and installed more than 1,000,000 square feet of glazing. Learn more at acglass.com.

About the ENR Top 600

Engineering News-Record (ENR) publishes the Top 600 Specialty Contractors ranking each year. Firms are ranked by the construction revenue they report for the prior year. The full 2026 list is available to ENR subscribers at enr.com.
