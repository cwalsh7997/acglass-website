American Commercial Glass Marks Five Years of Florida Commercial Glazing Delivery

WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA · MAY 9, 2026

WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA · MAY 9, 2026

American Commercial Glass (ACG), a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, marks five years of commercial glazing delivery since its founding in February 2021. The firm now operates Florida operations: West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa.

"We've kept the same standards from day one," said Connor Walsh, President of ACG. "Florida CGC #1531993, a real bid review on every job, and zero compromise on the manufacturers we install. The reputation we've built isn't from chasing volume - it's from doing the work the way Florida commercial glazing is supposed to be done."

Project mix

ACG's project portfolio spans hospitality, multifamily, healthcare, retail, and Class-A office construction across Florida's three biggest commercial markets. Notable recent installations include:

  • Atlantic Fields Golf House - Hobe Sound. A flagship hospitality glazing installation in Martin County.
  • Eau Palm Beach Resort. Glazing work supporting the Forbes 5-Star resort's ongoing renovation program.
  • Wild Blue Clubhouse - Estero. A signature SW Florida hospitality build delivered out of the Naples office.
  • Cudjoe Key Fire Station and Haines City Emergency Operations Center. Critical-facility glazing including impact-rated assemblies.

Three-office Florida coverage

ACG opened its founding West Palm Beach headquarters in 2021, added a Naples regional office in 2023, and opened its Tampa office in 2024. The three-office structure gives the firm local crews and project managers in Florida's three largest commercial markets, eliminating day-trip logistics for installations and field service calls.

Manufacturer credentials

The firm is an installer for ESWindows and Euro-Wall, and holds installation experience with PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

For project tour requests, photography, or interviews on Florida commercial glazing, contact press@acglass.com.

About American Commercial Glass: American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa. Founded in 2021 by Connor Walsh (President) and Rielly Walsh (CEO), ACG specializes in commercial storefront, curtainwall, multi-slide, and impact-rated glazing for hospitality, multifamily, healthcare, retail, and Class-A office projects across Florida. ACG is an installer for ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora. The firm delivers commercial glazing across Florida.
