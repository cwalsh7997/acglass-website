American Commercial Glass Surpasses 350 Florida Commercial Projects in Five Years

WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA · MAY 9, 2026

WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA · MAY 9, 2026

American Commercial Glass (ACG), a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, has surpassed 350 completed commercial projects totaling more than one million square feet of installed glass since the company's founding in 2020. The milestone was reached with zero OSHA recordable incidents over the company's five-year operating history.

"We've kept the same standards from project one to project 350," said Connor Walsh, President of ACG. "Florida CGC #1531993, a real bid review on every job, and zero compromise on the manufacturers we install. The track record we've built isn't from chasing volume — it's from doing the work the way Florida commercial glazing is supposed to be done."

Project mix

ACG's project portfolio spans hospitality, multifamily, healthcare, retail, and Class-A office construction across Florida's three biggest commercial markets. Notable recent installations include:

  • Ocean Prime — Fort Lauderdale. An entirely Euro-Wall envelope including DirectSet, multi-slide, and Vistafold systems for the dining terrace.
  • Atlantic Fields Golf House — Hobe Sound. A flagship hospitality glazing installation in Martin County.
  • Eau Palm Beach Resort. Glazing work supporting the Forbes 5-Star resort's ongoing renovation program.
  • Wild Blue Clubhouse — Estero. A signature SW Florida hospitality build delivered out of the Naples office.
  • Cudjoe Key Fire Station and Haines City Emergency Operations Center. Critical-facility glazing including impact-rated assemblies.

Three-office Florida coverage

ACG opened its founding West Palm Beach headquarters in 2020, added a Naples regional office in 2023, and opened its Tampa office in 2024. The three-office structure gives the firm local crews and project managers in Florida's three largest commercial markets, eliminating day-trip logistics for installations and field service calls.

Bonding and credentials

ACG carries $3 million single project / $6 million aggregate bonding capacity and is qualified by Connor Walsh under Florida Certified General Contractor license #1531993, issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. The firm holds authorized-installer status with ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact.
