ACG Opens Tampa Office, Expands Commercial Glazing Coverage to I-75 Corridor

TAMPA, FLORIDA · MAY 9, 2026

TAMPA, FLORIDA · MAY 9, 2026

American Commercial Glass (ACG) has opened its third Florida office, located at 3031 N Rocky Point Dr W, Suite 600, in Tampa. The Tampa office expands the firm's commercial glazing coverage from its West Palm Beach headquarters and Naples regional office to serve Hillsborough, Pasco, Pinellas, Sarasota, and Manatee counties along Florida's I-75 corridor.

"Tampa Bay's commercial market is moving faster than most people realize," said Connor Walsh, President of ACG. "Multifamily, hospitality, and Class-A office projects on the I-75 corridor have been growing for years. We weren't going to keep day-tripping crews from West Palm. The right way to serve a market is to be in it."

Local crew, local response

The Tampa office houses an estimating team and field installers based in Tampa Bay. Same-day site visits are available across Hillsborough and Pinellas counties; the office covers the I-75 corridor from Sarasota north to Pasco County and west into Pinellas.

Same standard, three offices

Every ACG office operates on the same submittal process, quality expectations, and communication standards. The office that handles a project depends on geography, not on which office is more convenient. ACG's three-office structure was deliberately built so general contractors and architects in any Florida market work with a local team, not a long-distance subcontractor.

About American Commercial Glass: American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa. Founded in 2021 and led by Rielly Walsh (CEO) and Connor Walsh (President), ACG specializes in commercial storefront, curtainwall, multi-slide, and impact-rated glazing for hospitality, multifamily, healthcare, retail, and Class-A office projects across Florida. ACG is an installer for ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora. The firm delivers commercial glazing across Florida.
