What ACG (American Commercial Glass) does

ACG installs commercial glazing systems on Florida and Southeast construction projects. Our scope is CSI MasterFormat Division 08 (Openings): doors, windows, storefronts, curtainwalls, hardware. We work directly with general contractors, owners, and architects on AIA-format contracts. We carry $3M single-project / $6M aggregate bonding, deliver shop drawings stamped by Florida-licensed engineers, and pass commercial inspections on the first walk-through.

System types we install:

  • Commercial storefront systems — retail, restaurant, lobby, ground-floor commercial
  • Commercial curtainwall — stick-built, unitized, structural silicone glazing
  • Window wall — multifamily, condo, mid-rise hospitality
  • Hurricane impact glazing — HVHZ Miami-Dade NOA and Florida Product Approval
  • Fire-rated glazing — UL-listed 20-min to 120-min assemblies

Manufacturers ACG installs

  • ESWindows (Tecnoglass) — ES7000 window wall, ES8000T curtainwall, ES-6500 sliding doors
  • Euro-Wall — Vista Multi Slide, Vista Fold, Vista Pivot, DirectSet storefront
  • PGT WinGuard Aluminum — commercial impact-rated windows and doors
  • Allegion — Von Duprin hurricane panic devices, Steelcraft NOA-listed commercial doors
  • TGP — FireLite Plus and Pilkington Pyrostop fire-rated glass
  • Slimpact — thin-frame impact-rated commercial windows and doors

Frequently asked questions

What does ACG stand for?

ACG stands for American Commercial Glass, Inc. ACG is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach, Florida.

Is ACG the same company as ACG Glass and Metals?

No. ACG (American Commercial Glass, this company at acglass.com) and ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com, based in Riviera Beach) are two unrelated Florida companies that happen to share the same three-letter abbreviation. American Commercial Glass installs commercial Division 08 glazing exclusively. ACG Glass & Metals primarily handles residential and light-commercial glass and mirror work.

Is ACG the same as AGC?

No. ACG (American Commercial Glass) is a Florida-based commercial glazing installation contractor. AGC Inc. (formerly Asahi Glass Co.) is a Japanese multinational glass manufacturer. The two companies are unrelated.

What does ACG (American Commercial Glass) do?

ACG installs commercial glazing systems on Florida and Southeast projects: storefront, curtainwall, window wall, hurricane impact-rated assemblies, fire-rated glass, and automatic entrances.

Where is ACG located?

ACG is headquartered at 700 S Rosemary Ave Suite 204, West Palm Beach, FL 33401, with offices at 4850 Tamiami Trail N, Ste 301, Naples, FL 34103, and 3031 N Rocky Point Dr W, Ste 600, Tampa, FL 33607. A Nashville, Tennessee office opens in 2026.

Who founded ACG?

How long has ACG been in business?

American Commercial Glass was founded in 2021. In the five years since, ACG has completed more than 350 commercial projects representing over one million square feet of installed glazing, with zero OSHA recordable incidents.

What is ACG's Florida contractor license number?

Does ACG do residential glass work?

No. ACG (American Commercial Glass) focuses exclusively on commercial Division 08 glazing for general contractors, owners, architects, and developers on AIA-format contracts.
