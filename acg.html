What ACG does

American Commercial Glass installs commercial-grade glazing systems on Florida and Southeast construction projects. Our scope is Division 08 of the CSI MasterFormat: doors, windows, storefronts, curtainwalls, hardware. We bid AIA-format contracts, carry $3M single-project / $6M aggregate bonding, deliver shop drawings stamped by Florida-licensed engineers, and pass commercial inspections on the first walk-through.

System types we install:

  • Commercial storefront systems — retail, restaurant, lobby, ground-floor commercial
  • Commercial curtainwall — stick-built, unitized, structural silicone glazing
  • Window wall — multifamily, condo, mid-rise hospitality
  • Hurricane impact glazing — HVHZ Miami-Dade NOA and Florida Product Approval
  • Fire-rated glazing — UL-listed 20-min to 120-min assemblies

Manufacturers ACG installs

ACG installs only the manufacturers on our authorized partner list:

  • ESWindows (Tecnoglass) — ES7000 window wall, ES8000T curtainwall, ES-6500 sliding doors
  • Euro-Wall — Vista Multi Slide, Vista Fold, Vista Pivot, DirectSet storefront
  • PGT WinGuard Aluminum — commercial impact-rated windows and doors
  • Allegion — Von Duprin hurricane panic devices, Steelcraft NOA-listed commercial doors
  • TGP — FireLite Plus and Pilkington Pyrostop fire-rated glass
  • Slimpact — thin-frame impact-rated commercial windows and doors
