Treasure Coast Market

Port St. Lucie: One of Florida's
Fastest-Growing Cities.

Port St. Lucie consistently ranks among Florida's fastest-growing cities — and the commercial construction pipeline that follows population growth is substantial. The Tradition commercial district is one of the most ambitious mixed-use developments in the Treasure Coast region, generating a multi-year pipeline of retail, office, medical, and multifamily construction that requires qualified glazing subs with proper Florida credentials.

Cleveland Clinic Tradition Hospital and its expanding medical campus represent the kind of healthcare construction program that demands fire-rated glass assemblies, complex curtainwall coordination, and submittals that pass St. Lucie County plan review the first time. The Digital Domain campus adds corporate office and studio construction to the mix. Industrial development on Gatlin Boulevard keeps the commercial glazing pipeline active year-round.

The US-1 commercial corridor from Fort Pierce through Stuart runs active retail and professional office construction. Jensen Beach and Hobe Sound serve a commercial and boutique commercial market with specific design requirements. Vero Beach's commercial redevelopment and healthcare sector add Indian River County volume to the Treasure Coast glazing market.

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is 60 miles south — familiar territory for our team. ACG has completed multiple Treasure Coast projects, including work in Hobe Sound, Port St. Lucie proper, and Stuart-area commercial. We're not making occasional trips to the Treasure Coast — it's part of our active service area.

Common Questions

Port St. Lucie Glazing FAQ.

How far is ACG from Port St. Lucie? +

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is approximately 60 miles south of Port St. Lucie via I-95 or the Florida Turnpike. ACG serves the Treasure Coast — St. Lucie, Martin, and Indian River counties — as a primary service area, not an occasional out-of-market trip. We have completed multiple projects in the Treasure Coast area and our team is familiar with St. Lucie County's building department permit and inspection process.

What Treasure Coast projects has ACG completed? +

ACG has completed multiple commercial glazing projects on the Treasure Coast: Project LIFT in Hobe Sound (commercial storefront), the Ginsberg Eye Center (medical impact systems), and Hulett Environmental in Port St. Lucie (commercial storefront). These projects demonstrate ACG's experience across the medical, commercial, and institutional construction verticals that define the Treasure Coast market.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Port St. Lucie projects? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. The Treasure Coast's Atlantic coastal exposure requires wind-rated systems with proper Florida product approvals. ACG specifies systems that meet St. Lucie, Martin, and Indian River county building code requirements — recommendations driven by the project spec and design intent, not by what's most convenient to stock.

Does ACG serve Fort Pierce, Stuart, and Vero Beach as well as Port St. Lucie? +

Yes. ACG serves the full Treasure Coast — Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, Stuart, Hobe Sound, Jensen Beach, and Vero Beach. St. Lucie, Martin, and Indian River counties are all within ACG's active service area. If you're building anywhere on the Treasure Coast, ACG can deliver the full glazing scope — storefronts, impact windows, window wall, curtainwall, and fire-rated glass.

How quickly can ACG turn around a scope for a Treasure Coast project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. ACG's AI-managed estimating process eliminates the manual bottlenecks that slow down other glazing subs. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions, no three-week delay. Send plans Monday, scope Wednesday.
