Scope narrative

Aspen Dental is a national dental practice brand operating through a franchise and corporate location model, with standardized store designs that are adapted to local code requirements in each state and jurisdiction. Florida locations must meet Florida Building Code requirements, including impact glazing where the site's location falls within a WBDR or HVHZ designation.

The Edgewater location in Volusia County follows the Aspen Dental retail format: a storefront commercial building with the brand's characteristic glazed facade - large glass lites at the front elevation with the Aspen Dental identity prominently visible. ACG installed the commercial storefront and entry systems for this location, meeting the Florida Building Code requirements for the Edgewater site and coordinating with the project's general contractor.

Retail medical buildings have a specific set of entry requirements at variance from standard retail: accessibility compliance for all entries (ADA), secure internal partitioning from patient areas to reception, and standard commercial traffic-volume hardware at entries. ACG's entry system specification for the Aspen Dental location addressed these requirements within the impact-rated commercial entry product line.

Edgewater is south of New Smyrna Beach in Volusia County - an area with coastal proximity that influences the site's wind exposure classification and the applicable impact glazing requirement. ACG confirmed the WBDR or non-WBDR designation for the specific Edgewater site and specified storefront systems meeting the applicable code provision.

Technical highlights

Technical highlights of the Aspen Dental Edgewater glazing scope:

  • Retail medical storefront: Aluminum storefront with large glass lites at the front elevation - consistent with the Aspen Dental retail format and providing the brand visibility the location required.
  • ADA-compliant entry: Commercial entry systems meeting ADA accessibility requirements - door width, hardware height, opening force, and threshold conditions per Florida Accessibility Code.

Frequently asked questions

What is Aspen Dental?

Aspen Dental is a national dental practice brand with locations across the United States. ACG installed the commercial storefront glazing package at the Aspen Dental location in Edgewater, Volusia County, Florida.

What glazing is standard for medical retail buildings in Florida?

Medical retail outpatient buildings in Florida - dental practices, urgent care, vision centers - typically use standard commercial aluminum storefront with large glass panels for brand visibility and interior light. In coastal Florida WBDR jurisdictions, storefront must be impact-rated with Florida Product Approval. Entry systems must meet ADA accessibility requirements.

What ADA requirements apply to commercial entry glazing?

ADA accessibility requirements for commercial entries include minimum clear opening width (32 inches minimum, 36 inches preferred), maximum opening force (5 lbs for interior, 8.5 lbs maximum for exterior with closer), lever or loop handles (no round knobs), and thresholds not exceeding 0.5 inches in height (1/4 inch for new construction). Impact-rated aluminum entry systems are available that meet all ADA requirements while providing FBC impact compliance.

Does Edgewater, FL require impact storefront?

Edgewater is in Volusia County; whether impact glazing is required depends on the specific site's position relative to the WBDR boundary. Volusia County includes both coastal WBDR areas and inland non-WBDR areas. ACG confirms the applicable code requirement for each Volusia County project's specific location.

Medical retail glazing includes ADA-compliant entry systems, large storefront for brand visibility, and Florida impact compliance where the site's location requires it.
