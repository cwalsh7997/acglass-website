The Sarasota Market

One of Florida's Fastest-Growing
Mid-Market Metros.
ACG Is Already Here.

Sarasota is not just a retirement corridor — it's one of the most active commercial construction markets in the Florida Gulf Coast. Downtown Sarasota's mixed-use redevelopment has accelerated through the mid-2020s, with new residential towers, office conversions, and retail projects transforming the bayfront and the core.

The UTC (University Town Center) corridor is one of the most commercially active retail strips in Southwest Florida — regional and national tenants continue to infill the corridor with new pads, expansions, and interior renovations. Every one of those GCs needs a glazing sub who can hit the schedule and submit clean.

The I-75 industrial and logistics corridor south of Sarasota is generating consistent warehouse, flex industrial, and distribution center work. Sarasota Memorial Health Care System and HCA's Sarasota Doctors Hospital have both been active in renovation and expansion programs. Luxury multifamily along the bayfront continues to demand high-spec curtainwall and window wall.

Lakewood Ranch — one of the fastest-growing master-planned communities in the United States — straddles Manatee and Sarasota counties and is generating years of commercial, retail, medical office, and mixed-use glazing work. ACG's position between our Naples and Tampa offices means Lakewood Ranch GCs get two crews available, not one.

Common Questions

Sarasota Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve Sarasota and Manatee County commercial projects? +

Yes. ACG actively serves Sarasota and Manatee counties, primarily from our Naples office located approximately 60 miles south of downtown Sarasota. For large projects and north county work, we also deploy from our Tampa office. We have established supply relationships throughout the Sarasota-Manatee metro and experience with both county building departments.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Sarasota? +

ACG installs ESWindows, CGI Windows, PGT Innovations, YKK AP, Trulite, Eurowall, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. For Sarasota curtainwall and window wall projects, we work with YKK AP and Eurowall systems with full structural engineering coordination. System selection is always driven by project specification and architect's intent — not convenience.

What code requirements apply to Sarasota commercial glazing? +

Sarasota County is not within Miami-Dade's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ), but Florida Building Code Chapter 16 wind requirements apply, and coastal proximity places most of the county within the wind-borne debris region. Impact-rated or wind-rated glazing systems are commonly required or architect-specified. ACG's submittal packages include Florida Product Approvals, NOA documentation, and EOR coordination calibrated to Sarasota County's plan review expectations.

How close is ACG's Naples office to Sarasota? +

ACG's Naples office is approximately 60 miles south of downtown Sarasota — about 60 minutes via I-75 or US-41. Sarasota is a priority service area for that office, and we additionally deploy from our Tampa office for north county and Lakewood Ranch projects. GCs in the Sarasota-Manatee market effectively have coverage from two directions.

How quickly can ACG turn around a Sarasota glazing scope? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and competitive pricing — within 48 hours. Our AI-managed operations support fast, accurate takeoffs for everything from a single storefront to a full mixed-use curtainwall package. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions.
