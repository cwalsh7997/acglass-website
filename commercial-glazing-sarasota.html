Our Services

Commercial Glazing Services in Sarasota

Full-service glazing solutions for Sarasota County’s coastal commercial construction market — from waterfront condos to cultural institutions.

Impact Storefront Systems

FBC-approved impact storefront for Sarasota's coastal retail and commercial properties. ES-8000 and ES-9000 systems with laminated impact glass meeting Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements for Sarasota County.

Curtainwall & Window Wall

High-performance curtainwall and window wall systems for Sarasota's waterfront high-rises and mixed-use developments. Engineered for coastal wind loads and salt-air durability along the Gulf Coast.

Sliding & Folding Glass Walls

Eurowall MultiSlide and VistaFold systems for Sarasota's resort-style hospitality and luxury residential projects. Impact-rated operable walls that maximize Gulf views while meeting Florida Building Code.

Impact Windows

Florida Product Approved impact windows for Sarasota commercial, institutional, and multifamily projects. Laminated glass assemblies engineered for the Gulf Coast wind zone with superior thermal performance.

Fire-Rated Glazing

TGP and SAFTI fire-rated glazing systems for Sarasota healthcare facilities, educational buildings, and commercial corridors. Complete fire-rated assemblies meeting UL certification requirements.

Automatic Entrances

Allegion and Stanley automatic entrance systems for Sarasota's hotels, medical centers, and commercial buildings. Sliding, swinging, and revolving door systems with ADA compliance and hurricane-rated options.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG have medical office and healthcare project experience?

Yes. ACG provides commercial glazing for Sarasota medical facilities, including specialized systems for healthcare environments with specific infection control, durability, and aesthetic requirements.

What glazing is suitable for Sarasota's waterfront retail projects?

Sarasota's waterfront environment requires corrosion-resistant, high-performance glazing with excellent material finishes. ACG specifies and installs premium systems suitable for coastal retail applications.

Can ACG handle cultural institution renovations?

Yes. ACG has experience with cultural facility upgrades, including historic renovation coordination, specialized glazing for display areas, and tight scheduling around operational constraints.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Sarasota?

ACG installs impact storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet Sarasota County Wind-Borne Debris Region requirements.

What is the typical timeline for a Sarasota commercial glazing project?

Timelines vary by project scope. Sarasota County permitting typically takes 2-4 weeks for commercial glazing. Standard storefront projects run 6-10 weeks from approved submittals; curtainwall and window wall systems require 12-16 weeks. ACG provides detailed schedules during pre-construction to align with your overall project timeline.

Does ACG offer value engineering for Sarasota projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering during pre-construction to optimize glazing specifications for Sarasota's code requirements and coastal conditions. This includes system alternatives that meet performance requirements at competitive pricing, energy code optimization for Florida's climate zone, and coordination with architects to maintain design intent while controlling costs.
