Lake Park Innovation Center · West Palm Beach, FL
The project

Three buildings. Every bay matching.

Lake Park Innovation Center is a three-building light industrial and commercial campus in West Palm Beach, FL — developed as a multi-phase small-bay flex project offering units from 1,000 to 5,000 SF for light industrial, office and commercial tenants.

Three buildings. Dozens of individual tenant bays. Hundreds of storefront openings. This is exactly the kind of multi-building scope where glazing coordination can fall apart fast — sequencing across buildings, consistent profiles across dozens of openings, and a GC that needed every building glazed on its own TCO schedule.

ACG supplied and installed the complete ESWindows commercial storefront package across all three buildings — the dark-framed storefront entrance systems at each tenant bay, the fixed glazed side lites, and the transom windows above each entry. Consistent profile and finish across every single bay on the campus.
