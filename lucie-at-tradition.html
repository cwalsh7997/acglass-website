The Project

Lucie at Tradition is a multifamily apartment community located in the master-planned Tradition development in Port St. Lucie, FL — a fast-growing Treasure Coast market that has seen significant residential construction activity over the past several years.

The community clubhouse and leasing office serves as the development's first impression — the building where prospective residents tour, sign leases, and form their initial opinion of the property. ACG was brought in to deliver the ESWindows storefront and window package for this building.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the complete ESWindows commercial storefront and window package — the main entrance storefront system at the leasing office entry, fixed windows across the front and side elevations, and all secondary glazed openings throughout the building envelope.

The clean white aluminum profiles complement the building's coastal Florida architectural language. ESWindows systems deliver the impact performance required by Florida code while maintaining the aesthetic the developer specified. Installed on schedule, no punchlist.
