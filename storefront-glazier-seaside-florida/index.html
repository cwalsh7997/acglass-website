Seaside commercial glazing questions, answered.

Who reviews Seaside commercial submittals?

Seaside has an Architectural Review Board (ARB) that reviews all commercial alterations, additions, and new construction. Reviews focus on architectural language, storefront proportions, materials, and finishes.

What finishes does Seaside typically approve on commercial storefront?

Dark bronze anodize, oil-rubbed bronze, matte black, or custom paint colors approved by the ARB. Standard mill finish or polished aluminum is rarely approved.

Is impact glazing required at Seaside?

Yes — coastal Walton County is WBDR (135 mph design wind). All commercial windows and storefronts require Florida Product Approval impact certification.

Can your team match historically-correct profile widths for Seaside?

Yes. We specify narrow-sightline impact storefront systems (Kawneer Trifab, YKK YES 45 FI, or equivalent) with applied muntins or true-divided-lite glass to match the ARB-approved language.

How tight is a typical Seaside commercial timeline?

Seaside operators target spring break openings. Working backward, PO must be in by early October for a March opening. Tighter timelines force air-freight on glass, which we'll quote but rarely recommend.
