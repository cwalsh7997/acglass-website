Texas Market

Texas: America's
Second-Largest Construction Market.

Texas is the second-largest commercial construction market in the United States, driven by energy, technology, healthcare, and sustained population growth that has made the state's major metros among the most active construction environments in the country. For a national commercial glazing contractor with operations, Texas represents one of the most important markets in the US.

Houston is the energy capital of the world, and its commercial construction reflects that dominance. ExxonMobil's Spring campus — among the most extensive corporate campuses in the US — Chevron's headquarters expansion, Shell's Woodcreek campus, and BASF's regional facilities are generating substantial corporate office and industrial glazing demand. The Galleria and Post Oak high-rise corridor is one of the most active commercial real estate markets in the country. And the Texas Medical Center — one of the world's most extensive medical complexes with over 60 institutions — is undergoing a multi-decade, multi-billion dollar expansion program that represents among the most extensive ongoing healthcare glazing markets in the US.

Dallas and the Metroplex is Texas's most diversified commercial construction market. Uptown Dallas and the Deep Ellum mixed-use corridor are running high-rise and mid-rise glazing scopes. The Frisco and Plano corporate campus corridor — home to Toyota's North American headquarters, Liberty Mutual, PGA America, JPMorgan Chase, and dozens of other major corporate relocations — is generating some of the most significant commercial glazing opportunities in the country. The DFW Airport commercial development zone adds logistics, hospitality, and commercial glazing demand.

Austin's technology boom has produced a construction pipeline unmatched in the state. Tesla's Gigafactory, Apple's 133-acre Austin campus, Oracle's new headquarters, Samsung's Taylor semiconductor fabrication facility, and the continuous stream of technology company expansions are generating enormous glazing demand across corporate, industrial, and mixed-use project types. The Domain, East Austin's mixed-use explosion, and the downtown Austin high-rise market add hospitality, retail, and office glazing scopes.

San Antonio's Pearl District redevelopment and the UTSA campus expansion are the anchors of a growing commercial construction market. The I-10 and I-35 corridor commercial development, military base commercial construction, and the healthcare market anchored by University Health and Methodist Hospital generate consistent glazing demand in the Alamo City.

ACG's national operations platform enables Texas project coordination from its Southeast offices. Texas building code — the International Building Code with state amendments — governs commercial glazing, with energy code requirements driving thermally broken aluminum and high-performance glass on all new commercial construction. Gulf Coast Texas (Houston's southern corridors, Galveston) faces hurricane wind exposure requiring tested glazing assemblies. ACG's Florida coastal expertise applies directly to Texas Gulf Coast project requirements.

Texas Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve Texas commercial construction projects? +

Yes. ACG is a national commercial glazing contractor with operations enabling full project coordination for Texas scopes from our Southeast offices. Houston, Dallas, Austin, San Antonio, and statewide Texas projects are welcome. We follow our GC partners wherever they build.

How does ACG coordinate Texas projects from the Southeast? +

ACG's scheduling and operations platform enables full project coordination from Florida to Texas. Estimating, submittal management, procurement, and field scheduling are coordinated remotely — tied to your actual construction sequence. ACG has completed projects in multiple states and the platform removes geographic constraints from project coordination.

Can ACG handle Houston Texas Medical Center glazing projects? +

Yes. The Texas Medical Center's expansion is among the most extensive ongoing healthcare construction programs in the world. ACG brings healthcare glazing specialization — fire-rated assemblies, infection control glass, automatic entrance systems, and clinical glazing — to Houston's medical campus projects. We understand the specific requirements of hospital construction environments.

What glazing systems does ACG install on Texas commercial projects? +

ACG installs ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT BuildingEnvelope, PGT Window, TGP fire-rated glass, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. Texas projects are specified to IECC energy code requirements, local AHJ plan review standards, and the project's architectural specifications.

How quickly can ACG turn around a scope for a Texas project? +

Send us your drawings and ACG returns a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. Houston, Dallas, Austin, San Antonio, or anywhere in Texas: same 48-hour turnaround commitment.
