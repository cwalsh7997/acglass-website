Texas Market

Texas: America's
Second-Largest Construction Market.

Texas is the second-largest commercial construction market in the United States, driven by energy, technology, healthcare, and sustained population growth that has made the state's major metros among the most active construction environments in the country. For a national commercial glazing contractor with AI-managed operations, Texas represents one of the most important markets in the US.

Houston is the energy capital of the world, and its commercial construction reflects that dominance. ExxonMobil's Spring campus — one of the largest corporate campuses in the US — Chevron's headquarters expansion, Shell's Woodcreek campus, and BASF's regional facilities are generating substantial corporate office and industrial glazing demand. The Galleria and Post Oak high-rise corridor is one of the most active commercial real estate markets in the country. And the Texas Medical Center — the world's largest medical complex with over 60 institutions — is undergoing a multi-decade, multi-billion dollar expansion program that represents one of the largest ongoing healthcare glazing markets in the US.

Dallas and the Metroplex is Texas's most diversified commercial construction market. Uptown Dallas and the Deep Ellum mixed-use corridor are running high-rise and mid-rise glazing scopes. The Frisco and Plano corporate campus corridor — home to Toyota's North American headquarters, Liberty Mutual, PGA America, JPMorgan Chase, and dozens of other major corporate relocations — is generating some of the most significant commercial glazing opportunities in the country. The DFW Airport commercial development zone adds logistics, hospitality, and commercial glazing demand.

Austin's technology boom has produced a construction pipeline unmatched in the state. Tesla's Gigafactory, Apple's 133-acre Austin campus, Oracle's new headquarters, Samsung's Taylor semiconductor fabrication facility, and the continuous stream of technology company expansions are generating enormous glazing demand across corporate, industrial, and mixed-use project types. The Domain, East Austin's mixed-use explosion, and the downtown Austin high-rise market add hospitality, retail, and office glazing scopes.

San Antonio's Pearl District redevelopment and the UTSA campus expansion are the anchors of a growing commercial construction market. The I-10 and I-35 corridor commercial development, military base commercial construction, and the healthcare market anchored by University Health and Methodist Hospital generate consistent glazing demand in the Alamo City.

ACG's AI-managed national operations platform enables Texas project coordination from its Southeast offices. Texas building code — the International Building Code with state amendments — governs commercial glazing, with energy code requirements driving thermally broken aluminum and high-performance glass on all new commercial construction. Gulf Coast Texas (Houston's southern corridors, Galveston) faces hurricane wind exposure requiring tested glazing assemblies. ACG's Florida coastal expertise applies directly to Texas Gulf Coast project requirements.
