Why ACG

A glazing company that actually performs.

ACG exists because the glazing industry needed a contractor who actually performs. We built an operation around accountability, communication, and technical discipline — and we hire people who share those values.

We're growing. We're winning the projects that matter — complex commercial builds, high-profile developments, demanding GCs who know the difference between a reliable sub and a problem. And we're looking for people who want to grow with us.

You'll work directly with Rielly and Connor Walsh, the owners who founded the company in 2021 and still price, plan, and close out every project. Offices in West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa, operating throughout Florida.
