ESWindows vs PGT impact windows - commercial Florida comparison

ESWindows and PGT (PGT Industries) are both Florida-Product-Approval and Miami-Dade NOA qualified impact window manufacturers. ESWindows is the choice for architectural commercial (multifamily, hospitality, office) with premium aluminum profiles. PGT WinGuard line is broader residential-commercial-blend coverage with deeper distribution.

Which is the better choice for commercial Florida projects?

ESWindows for architectural commercial - multifamily, hospitality, office, healthcare - where premium aluminum profile, custom finish, and tighter sightlines matter. PGT for commercial-residential-blend projects where the project benefits from PGT's broader distribution network and faster lead-time on stock profiles.

Does ACG install both?

Yes. ACG carries direct manufacturer relationships with ESWindows. PGT is sourced through distribution. We bid the spec and recommend the right product for the project type.
