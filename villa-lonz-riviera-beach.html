Villa L’Onz Riviera Beach

Twelve Bermuda- and Key West-inspired townhomes for the Riviera Beach CRA — ACG is furnishing and installing the complete ES Windows impact-rated glazing package under All Site Construction.

Location Riviera Beach, FL Sector Multifamily · Townhomes GC All Site Construction Status Installation underway — March 2026
01
Sheet A-101 · Project

Twelve units. Every opening impact-rated.

Villa L’Onz brings twelve Bermuda- and Key West-inspired townhomes to 120 W 11th St in Riviera Beach — a development by the Riviera Beach CRA with two townhouse buildings designed by REG Architects: eight two-story units and four three-story units, built by general contractor All Site Construction.

The colorful gabled facades — pink, cream, blue, and yellow — celebrate South Florida’s architectural heritage while delivering the modern impact-rated performance required for Palm Beach County’s High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) designation.

ACG is the Division 08 glazing subcontractor, furnishing and installing the complete impact-rated glazing package throughout all 12 units: ES Windows single-hung impact windows, plus impact-rated entrance doors and sliding glass doors — 24 major glazed openings across the development. Glazing installation is underway as of March 2026.

Sheet A-301 · Scope notes

HVHZ performance, heritage character.

HVHZ compliant

Every opening is engineered to Palm Beach County’s High Velocity Hurricane Zone requirements — Florida’s most demanding wind and impact standards, for the coastal Riviera Beach location.

24 major glazed openings

One impact-rated entrance door and one sliding glass door per unit across all 12 units — plus ES Windows single-hung impact windows throughout both buildings.

Submittal through punch

ACG manages the full glazing scope — submittals, approvals, material procurement, scheduling, and installation — coordinating with All Site Construction, REG Architects, and ES Windows from first drawing to final punch.

Specified to the architecture

Each opening was specified to complement the Bermuda and Key West architectural character — modern performance behind heritage facades.
