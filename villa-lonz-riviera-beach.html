The Project

ACG is the Division 08 glazing subcontractor for Villa L’Onz — a 12-unit Bermuda/Key West-inspired townhouse development by the Riviera Beach Community Redevelopment Authority (CRA) at 120 W 11th St in Riviera Beach, FL. The project features 2 townhouse buildings designed by REG Architects, with 8 two-story units (Unit A) and 4 three-story units (Unit B), built by General Contractor All Site Construction.

Villa L’Onz brings colorful, gabled facades in pink, cream, blue, and yellow to Riviera Beach — a Bermuda and Key West-inspired aesthetic that celebrates South Florida’s architectural heritage while delivering modern impact-rated performance required for Palm Beach County’s High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) designation. ACG is furnishing and installing the complete impact-rated glazing package throughout all 12 units.

ACG Scope

ACG is the Division 08 glazing subcontractor for Villa L’Onz — responsible for furnishing and installing the complete impact-rated glazing package throughout all 12 units across both townhouse buildings.

  • 01
    Impact Windows ES Windows impact-rated single hung windows throughout all 12 units. HVHZ compliant per Palm Beach County requirements — engineered to meet Florida’s most demanding wind and impact standards for the coastal Riviera Beach location.
  • 02
    Entrance Doors & Sliding Glass Doors Impact-rated entrance doors and sliding glass doors, one each per unit — 24 major glazed openings total across the 12-unit development. Each opening engineered for HVHZ compliance and specified to complement the Bermuda/Key West architectural character.
  • 03
    Full Division 08 Glazing Scope Complete coordination with All Site Construction, REG Architects, and ES Windows from submittal through final punch. ACG manages the full glazing scope — submittals, approvals, material procurement, scheduling, and installation across both buildings and all 12 units.
Project Status — Glazing Installation Underway

Glazing installation is actively underway as of March 2026.
