Inlet Beach commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Inlet Beach the same as Rosemary Beach?

Adjacent but distinct. Inlet Beach is the eastern unincorporated area of Walton County including 30Avenue and The Hub at WaterSound; Rosemary Beach is the immediately-adjacent New Urbanist town with its own ARB. We work both.

Who is the dominant developer at Inlet Beach?

St. Joe Company. St. Joe owns and develops most of the major commercial at Inlet Beach including 30Avenue, The Hub at WaterSound, and the surrounding WaterSound master plan.

Is Inlet Beach in the WBDR?

Yes. Coastal Walton County is full WBDR — 135 mph design wind, impact glazing required on all exposed elevations.

Can you supply storefront systems for 30Avenue inline retail tenants?

Yes. Standard impact storefront packages (ESWindows / Tecnoglass) are inside our regular bid scope. We've quoted comparable inline retail at WaterColor Town Center and Pier Park.

Lead time on Inlet Beach commercial?

Standard 8–12 weeks for in-stock impact storefront, 14–18 for custom curtain wall or oversized impact glass.
