What "AI-Managed" Actually Means

This Is Not a Chatbot.
This Is Operational Infrastructure.

When ACG says AI-managed, it means the operational functions that determine whether a glazing scope succeeds or fails are handled by a real-time coordination platform — not Excel spreadsheets, not calendar reminders, not phone calls that happen too late. Here's exactly what it does:

01
Submittal Management

Submittal Deadline Tracking Tied to Master Schedule

Every submittal deadline — shop drawings, product approvals, NOA documentation — is tracked against the GC's actual master schedule, not a static internal calendar. When the GC's schedule moves, the platform recalculates every downstream glazing milestone automatically. Submittal delays that were invisible in the old model surface days before they become the GC's problem.

02
Procurement

Material Procurement Sequencing Based on Approved Drawings

Material orders are placed against approved shop drawings, sequenced to the installation schedule in the field. Not "order everything at contract execution and hope the schedule holds." Each procurement action is tied to an approval status and a field need date. Lead times are calculated against reality, not assumptions. Material arrives when it's needed — not six weeks before or six weeks after.

03
Installation

Installation Window Coordination with GC's Live Schedule

ACG's installation windows are coordinated against the GC's live schedule — not set in stone at contract and ignored when the schedule moves. When concrete delays a floor, the glazing installation window moves with it. When a milestone accelerates, ACG knows in advance and adjusts crew deployment. The GC doesn't have to chase ACG to get coordination. ACG comes to them with the update.

04
Risk Management

Proactive Flagging When Any Milestone Slips

The moment any milestone — approval, procurement, delivery, installation — shows risk of slipping, the platform surfaces it. Not after the GC's superintendent calls wondering where the glazing is. Before the slip becomes a schedule failure. Proactive flagging is the single most valuable capability ACG's AI platform provides to GC partners. It turns reactive problem-solving into proactive schedule management.

05
Multi-Project

Multi-Project Coordination Across Simultaneous Scopes

ACG often runs multiple glazing scopes simultaneously — Florida and Louisiana, Tampa and Georgia, West Palm Beach and Nashville. Manual project management breaks down at this scale. The AI platform coordinates all active scopes in parallel without the coordination failures that happen when PMs are managing too many projects with too many spreadsheets. Every project gets the same real-time coordination attention regardless of how many projects are running simultaneously.

National Operations

Why AI Coordination Is
the Only Way to Scale
Nationally.

Managing glazing scopes across multiple states simultaneously is an operational challenge that manual project management cannot solve at scale. When ACG executed the StayApt Suites scope in Louisiana from Florida, the coordination challenge was real: submittal packages needed to meet Louisiana approval timelines, material procurement had to account for Gulf Coast freight logistics, and installation coordination was happening across a 600-mile distance from the project coordinator to the job site.

Manual coordination at that distance — phone calls, email chains, spreadsheet updates — introduces the exact coordination failures that cause schedule problems. By the time the issue surfaces in a manual system, the damage is already done. The AI platform eliminates the delay. Issues surface when they're still solvable, not when they're already a superintendent problem.

This is why ACG's national model works when most regional subs fail when they try to go national: the operational infrastructure scales with the geography. It doesn't degrade with distance.
