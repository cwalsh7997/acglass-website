Permit and code context

Franklin is in Williamson County, Tennessee. Permits issue through the local municipal codes office. Wind code: ASCE 7-22 with Tennessee state amendments - design wind speeds 90-115 mph Risk Category II. Impact-rated glazing is not required by code.

Energy code: IECC Climate Zone 4A. Typical commercial fenestration target: U-factor ≤ 0.42, SHGC ≤ 0.40. ACG carries Tennessee-rated low-E glass products and aluminum framing approved for the energy compliance path.
