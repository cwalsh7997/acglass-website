The Market

Miami is a commercial glazing market.

Miami-Dade County is HVHZ (High Velocity Hurricane Zone) — the most stringent wind-load design zone in the United States. Every commercial opening in Miami requires impact-rated glazing with Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA), not just Florida Product Approval. Testing must include TAS 201 (large missile impact), TAS 202 (cyclic pressure), and TAS 203 (uniform static air pressure) per the Miami-Dade test protocol.

Miami's commercial glazing market is one of the most sophisticated in the United States. Brickell's Class A office towers, downtown Miami luxury hotels, Wynwood mixed-use, Coconut Grove hospitality, Miami Beach hospitality — every project requires architect-quality finish specifications, NOA documentation, and general contractors expect seamless Procore submittal workflows.

ACG serves Miami with our ESWindows flagship partnership. ESWindows is Colombian-headquartered and operates a high-capacity commercial glazing testing facility in Latin America, with extensive Miami-Dade NOA inventory across the 8000 storefront series, 9000 window wall series, and SGD-2020 impact sliding doors. Our Miami commercial glazing projects benefit from ESWindows's direct NOA engineering support and factory pre-glazed capability that compresses installation timelines on tight urban schedules.

Miami FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Does Miami require HVHZ-rated impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Miami and Miami-Dade County are in Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ). Every commercial opening must use products with Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) — not just Florida Product Approval. Testing must include TAS 201 (large missile), TAS 202 (cyclic pressure), and TAS 203 (uniform static air pressure).

What is the difference between Florida Product Approval and Miami-Dade NOA?

Florida Product Approval (FL#) covers most of Florida outside HVHZ. Miami-Dade NOA is the enhanced approval required inside HVHZ (Miami-Dade + Broward). NOA testing includes TAS 201/202/203 protocols which are more stringent than ASTM E1886/E1996. All NOA products carry FL# approval; not all FL# products have NOA.

What ESWindows products have Miami-Dade NOA?

ESWindows holds Miami-Dade NOA across the 8000 commercial storefront series, 9000 window wall series, and SGD-2020 impact sliding door system. Every NOA-rated product ships with documentation packages including the NOA number, large missile test certification, and installation requirements. ACG provides this documentation on every Miami project.

Does ACG have Miami commercial project experience?

Yes. ACG has delivered iFly Miami (experiential retail/entertainment) and other Miami-Dade commercial projects. Our ESWindows partnership provides the HVHZ-specific product depth and documentation rigor Miami projects require.
