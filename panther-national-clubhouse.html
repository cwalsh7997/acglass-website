Panther National Clubhouse

The 60,000+ SF centerpiece of the first new private gated golf community in Palm Beach County in nearly two decades. ACG is executing the complete Euro-Wall envelope for Verdex Construction — every opening on the building.

Location Palm Beach Gardens, FL Status Under construction GC Verdex Construction Systems All Euro-Wall
01
Sheet A-011 · The project

Every opening on this building is Euro-Wall.

Panther National is the first new private gated golf community to open in Palm Beach County in nearly two decades. The 400-acre community is centered around an 18-hole championship course co-designed by Jack Nicklaus and Justin Thomas, with the clubhouse serving as the highly anticipated centerpiece still to come.

The 60,000+ square foot, two-story clubhouse was designed by Miami-based architect STRANG in partnership with Peacock + Lewis Architects, with Verdex Construction as the general contractor. Per Max Strang's brief, the clubhouse is meant to “blur the line between indoors and out” with full-height windows, organic materials, and a soft, neutral palette.

ACG is supplying and installing the complete Euro-Wall package across the entire clubhouse envelope — not a piecemeal scope. DirectSet storefront wraps the fixed glazed elevations, multi-slide doors open the dining and lounge spaces to the course, Vistafold folding doors dissolve the indoor/outdoor transitions, and pivot doors handle the architectural entry moments. A single-manufacturer envelope — all Euro-Wall — is what makes the architect's promise real.
