The Project

Panther National is the first new private gated golf community to open in Palm Beach County in nearly two decades. Founded by Swiss developer Dominik Senn, the 400-acre community is built around an 18-hole championship course co-designed by Jack Nicklaus and Justin Thomas — the course opened for play in November 2023 and the clubhouse is the centerpiece still to come.

The 60,000+ square foot, two-story clubhouse was designed by Miami-based architect Max Strang of STRANG in partnership with Peacock + Lewis Architects and Decorators Unlimited. Verdex Construction is the general contractor. ACG is executing the complete Euro-Wall envelope across the entire clubhouse — DirectSet storefront, multi-slide doors, Vistafold folding doors, and pivot doors.

The Glazing Scope

ACG is supplying and installing the complete Euro-Wall package across the entire clubhouse envelope — not a piecemeal scope. Every opening on this building is Euro-Wall: DirectSet storefront wraps the fixed glazed elevations, multi-slide doors open the dining and lounge spaces to the course, Vistafold folding doors dissolve the indoor/outdoor transitions, and pivot doors handle the architectural entry moments. Per Max Strang's brief, the clubhouse is meant to “blur the line between indoors and out” with full-height windows, organic materials, and a soft, neutral palette. A single-manufacturer envelope — all Euro-Wall — is what makes that promise real.

The scale is significant: 60,000 sq ft of conditioned space on a clubhouse that will house a Michelin-inspired restaurant, private dining and wine bar tower, fashion and golf pro shop, full-service locker rooms, an elevated fitness center, and event spaces — all overlooking the Nicklaus + Thomas course. Every glazed opening had to meet Palm Beach County wind-load requirements and deliver the visual precision a 60,000 sq ft architect-led clubhouse demands.

Why This Matters

Private golf-club construction in South Florida is one of the most visible and most demanding markets in commercial glazing. When Verdex Construction needed a Division 08 sub for Panther National — the first private gated golf community in Palm Beach County in 20 years, co-designed by the greatest golfer of all time — they called ACG. The scope, the client profile, and the visibility of the project set a new benchmark for what our team delivers.

The golf course: “The Big Cat”

The 18-hole championship course was crafted through the collaboration of Jack Nicklaus and Justin Thomas. Head Professional Adam Schafer has said the design team moved 2.5 million cubic feet of dirt to shape the course, which can stretch up to 8,000 yards from the back “JT” tees and drops to roughly 4,500 yards at the forward tees.

Elevation change reaches 48 feet in spots — an unusual feat in South Florida — and the course includes an 87-yard-long island green at the par-3 15th, the longest green Nicklaus has ever designed.

The location

Panther National sits at 10991 Panther National Blvd, Palm Beach Gardens, FL 33412, about 20 miles northwest of Palm Beach International Airport. The community borders a 2,400-acre protected conservation area to the north, meaning the clubhouse views are preserved in perpetuity.

It is 20 minutes from downtown West Palm Beach — the same drive time as ACG's headquarters at 700 S Rosemary Ave, which is one of the reasons Verdex selected a local Division 08 contractor for this envelope.

FAQ

Panther National Clubhouse, answered.

Who is building the Panther National Clubhouse?

Verdex Construction is the general contractor. American Commercial Glass (ACG) is the glazing contractor, executing the curtainwall, window wall, and Euro-Wall system package across the 60,000 sq ft clubhouse envelope.

Who designed the Panther National Clubhouse?

Miami-based architect Max Strang of STRANG designed the clubhouse in partnership with Peacock + Lewis Architects and Planners, LLC. Interior design is by Decorators Unlimited.

Who designed the Panther National golf course?

The 18-hole championship course was co-designed by Jack Nicklaus and Justin Thomas. It opened for play in November 2023 and plays up to 8,000 yards. Panther National is Justin Thomas's debut as a golf course designer.

Who developed Panther National?

Panther National was founded and developed by Dominik Senn — a Swiss former world-class alpine ski racer and founder of the 4sports & Entertainment sports management agency. The 400-acre community is the first new private gated golf community to open in Palm Beach County in nearly 20 years.

Where is Panther National located?

10991 Panther National Blvd, Palm Beach Gardens, FL 33412. The community occupies 400 acres inside the 4,752-acre Avenir master-planned development, adjacent to a 2,400-acre protected conservation area — about 20 miles northwest of Palm Beach International Airport.

How big is the Panther National Clubhouse?

60,000+ sq ft on two stories. It will house a Michelin-inspired restaurant, a private wine bar and dining tower, a fashion and golf pro shop, full-service locker rooms, an elevated fitness center, event spaces, and a courtyard. Expected completion: late 2026.

What glazing systems are being installed on the Panther National Clubhouse?

All glazing on Panther National is Euro-Wall. ACG is installing the complete Euro-Wall envelope: Euro-Wall DirectSet storefront on fixed elevations, Euro-Wall multi-slide doors opening the dining and lounge spaces to the course, Euro-Wall Vistafold folding doors at the indoor-outdoor transitions, and Euro-Wall pivot doors at the architectural entries. The envelope is engineered to Palm Beach County wind-load requirements.

When will the Panther National Clubhouse open?

Expected completion is late 2026 / Q4 2026. Vertical construction on the clubhouse commenced in May 2025 and the envelope package — including ACG's glazing scope — topped out in Q1 2026.

Case Study · Modern Golf Clubhouse / Max Strang Design

How ACG delivered it.

The Challenge

Panther National is the first private gated golf community to open in Palm Beach County in nearly two decades — Swiss developer Dominik Senn's 400-acre flagship, anchored by a Jack Nicklaus + Justin Thomas championship course inside the Avenir planned community.

The 60,000 sq ft clubhouse envelope — designed by architect Max Strang in partnership with Peacock + Lewis and Decorators Unlimited — called for long exterior glazing elevations facing the golf course, Michelin-inspired dining spaces that open to the outdoors, and full-height fenestration supporting a design brief that explicitly blurs the line between indoors and out. Every opening had to meet Palm Beach County wind-load requirements and integrate with Verdex Construction's trade-stack schedule while residential estate homes were already selling into the community.

ACG Solution

ACG was awarded the complete Euro-Wall envelope: Euro-Wall DirectSet storefront wrapping the fixed elevations, Euro-Wall multi-slide doors on the course-facing dining and lounge spaces, Euro-Wall Vistafold folding doors at the indoor-outdoor transitions, and Euro-Wall pivot doors at the architectural entry moments. Single-manufacturer envelope, coordinated detailing throughout.

ACG staged the envelope install in sequence with Verdex’s exterior close-in window, providing scaffolding-compatible details and coordinating shop drawings with the structural steel package ahead of delivery. Aerial drone documentation during construction confirms the structural tolerances and finish standard the design intent called for.
