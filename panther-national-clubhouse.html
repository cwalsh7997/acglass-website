The Project

Panther National is one of the most anticipated private golf club developments in South Florida — a Nicklaus Design championship course set within an exclusive Palm Beach Gardens community. The clubhouse is the centerpiece: a monumental travertine-clad structure with a sweeping upper terrace, a lower arrival level with golf cart staging, and a glazing scope that will be visible from the 1st and 18th fairways.

Verdex Construction is the general contractor. ACG is executing the Eurowall curtainwall and window wall package — the full-height glazing that spans the upper elevation of the clubhouse, filling the entire dining and lounge level with glass. When this building is complete, the Eurowall system will be the first thing every member sees from the course.

The Glazing Scope

ACG is supplying and installing the complete Eurowall system across the clubhouse's primary glazed elevation — a large-format European curtainwall and window wall system delivering the floor-to-ceiling transparency the design calls for. Eurowall's European-engineered performance meets the Florida energy code requirements and the visual precision a building of this caliber demands.

This is the most architecturally demanding glazing scope ACG has been awarded to date. The scale, the client profile, and the visibility of the project set a new benchmark for what ACG can deliver.

Why This Matters

Private club construction in South Florida is competitive and highly visible. When Verdex Construction needed a glazing sub for Panther National, they called ACG. That's not a coincidence — it's the result of a track record built one delivered project at a time.
