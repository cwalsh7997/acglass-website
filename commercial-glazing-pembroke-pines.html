Common Questions

Pembroke Pines Glazing FAQ.

Is Pembroke Pines in the HVHZ? +

Yes. Pembroke Pines is within Broward County's HVHZ jurisdiction. All commercial glazing systems require Florida Product Approval with NOA documentation. ACG manages the complete submittal package in-house.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Pembroke Pines? +

ACG installs ESWindows, CGI Windows, YKK AP, Eurowall, Slimpact, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. All systems carry the required Broward County HVHZ approvals.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for Pembroke Pines? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and competitive pricing — within 48 hours.
