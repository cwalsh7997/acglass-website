Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG serve St. Petersburg and Clearwater?

Yes. ACG provides commercial glazing services throughout the Tampa Bay metropolitan area, including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Largo, Brandon, and Sarasota.

Does Tampa require impact-rated glazing?

Impact requirements in Tampa Bay are determined by the project's wind zone per the Florida Building Code. Coastal areas and high-rises typically require impact-rated or protected glazing. ACG installs code-compliant systems for all Tampa Bay wind zones.
