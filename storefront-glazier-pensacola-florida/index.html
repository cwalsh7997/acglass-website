Why commercial glazing in Pensacola is different.

Pensacola sits in one of the toughest commercial glazing markets in Florida. The coast pulls 150 mph design wind. Hurricane Ivan in 2004 and Sally in 2020 didn't just rewrite local building practices — they put every storefront, curtain wall, and impact assembly in the metro on a 20-year aging clock. ACG works Pensacola Bay, Cordova, and Perdido Key as a Florida-licensed specialty subcontractor (CGC #1531993) shipping pre-glazed assemblies from our supply network so coastal projects don't sit waiting on a Mobile or Atlanta lead time.

Architectural context: Pensacola mixes National Register historic districts (East Hill, North Hill, Seville Square) with modern commercial on Bayfront Parkway and Cordova — storefront work has to respect Historic Pensacola Preservation Board reviews downtown while meeting 150-mph coastal wind loads.

Pensacola commercial glazing questions, answered.

Are you licensed to install commercial storefronts in Escambia County?

Yes. ACG holds Florida CGC #1531993, a statewide certified general contractor license that covers all 67 Florida counties including Escambia. We pull permits in our own name.

Pensacola is in WBDR — do you supply impact-rated systems?

Yes. Coastal Escambia is in the Wind-Borne Debris Region (140+ mph). Every storefront, entrance, and window assembly we quote in Pensacola defaults to Florida Product Approval / FBC Section 1626 impact compliance with Missile Level D where required. We carry the NOAs on file.

How quickly can you get a Pensacola project bid?

Within 48 hours of receiving plans and specs. The bid letter includes system selection, NOA references, lead time, and anchor calc summary. We don't quote in ranges — every Pensacola bid has a real number.

Who handles install if your office isn't in Pensacola?

ACG runs its own field crews under our Florida license. For Pensacola work we field a crew from our Florida operations or partner with a Panhandle installer working under ACG's license, supervision, and insurance. We don't broker to a third party — every install carries our name and warranty.

What's the longest a Pensacola storefront project should take from bid to install?

Standard storefront and entrance package: 8–12 weeks from PO to install for in-stock systems. Custom curtain wall or large impact glass orders run 14–18 weeks. We share the manufacturer's confirmed ship date inside the bid letter, not after PO.
