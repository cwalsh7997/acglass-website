The Project

A new commercial retail building on Dale Mabry Highway in Tampa - one of the city's highest-traffic commercial corridors. The building's modern design pairs white brick with warm wood-tone cladding panels and a clean flat roofline, anchored by ESWindows dark-framed storefronts that run the full length of both elevations.

Wallace Construction Group managed the build and brought ACG in to execute the complete ESWindows glazing package - a Tampa project that reinforces ACG's growing presence in the West Coast Florida market.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the ESWindows commercial storefront package across both building elevations: the main entry system with the full-height black-framed double doors and fixed lites, the large fixed window banks flanking the entry on the front elevation, and the full-height storefront system on the second elevation.

The entry composition - symmetrical black frames, clean glazing, centered on the building face - is exactly what a retail tenant on Dale Mabry expects when they open their doors on day one. Consistent frames, consistent reveals, no punch list items at TCO.

The glazing package on Dale Mabry Retail Tampa included retail storefront. ACG specified, supplied, and installed ESWindows storefront - manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to Wallace Construction's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Tampa location pulled supervision out of our Tampa office.
