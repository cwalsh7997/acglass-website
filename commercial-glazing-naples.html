Code Compliance

Naples & Collier County Building Code Requirements

Naples and all of Collier County fall within Florida's Wind-Borne Debris Region, requiring impact-rated glazing or approved protective systems on every commercial project. The Florida Building Code 7th Edition (2023) governs all construction, with design wind speeds of 150 mph along the Gulf coast.

ACG's experience in the Miami-Dade HVHZ — the most stringent code zone in the U.S. — means every Naples project benefits from that higher standard. All glazing systems carry Florida Product Approvals or Miami-Dade NOAs as required, with full engineering packages for permit. Salt air corrosion resistance is specified on every coastal project. Collier County plan review typically runs 10–20 business days; ACG submits complete engineering packages to minimize revision cycles.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG serve luxury hospitality and resort projects in Naples?

Yes. ACG provides comprehensive glazing for Naples resort properties, including curtainwall, storefront, and custom glazing systems for high-end hospitality and residential projects.

What impact requirements apply to Naples commercial projects?

Collier County requires impact-rated glazing for most commercial projects due to hurricane considerations. ACG installs Miami-Dade-approved impact systems and can source specialized glazing for luxury applications.

Can ACG handle fast-track resort renovations?

Yes. ACG has experience with expedited hospitality projects, including phased installations and coordination with resort operations to minimize downtime.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Naples?

ACG installs impact-rated storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000 series), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet Collier County wind load requirements.

What is the typical timeline for a Naples glazing project?

Timelines vary by system complexity. Storefront projects typically run 2–4 weeks for installation, while window wall and curtainwall projects range from 4–10 weeks depending on scope. ACG provides detailed schedules during preconstruction and coordinates with GC milestones to maintain project flow.

Does ACG provide value engineering for Naples projects?

Yes. ACG regularly provides value engineering options during preconstruction to help GCs and owners optimize glazing budgets without compromising code compliance or performance. This includes alternative system selections, glass makeup optimization, and installation sequence adjustments that can reduce overall project costs.
