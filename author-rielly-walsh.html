Background

Rielly Walsh is the CEO of American Commercial Glass. She holds a BS in Concrete Industry Management from Middle Tennessee State University — one of the few college programs anywhere that trains concrete and structural professionals from a materials-up perspective.

Her concrete-and-stone background is the unusual edge in glazing leadership: most exterior envelope executives have never worked the structure from the other side. Rielly has. She reads a building the way the GC does — in layers, sequences, and interfaces. The peak in the field, walking Panther National with the Verdex team, is the photo that captures her job.

Rielly joined ACG in 2022 to launch the company into its current chapter. She owns strategy and long-term vision — the Naples and Tampa office expansions, the AI-managed operations stack that distinguishes ACG from legacy subcontractors, and the manufacturer-partner relationships with ESWindows and Euro-Wall.
