The Project

Westlake Shopping Center is an occupied multi-tenant retail center in Hialeah, FL — an active, functioning property where tenants were open and the parking lot was in use throughout the entire project. The existing storefront glazing package had aged out. The scope: full demo of every system across the center, and new ESWindows commercial storefront installed throughout, bay by bay.

This isn't a new construction punch list. This is a live-fire retrofit — the kind of work that tests a glazing contractor's sequencing, site discipline, and coordination with an occupied building. ACG was brought in by TGSV Enterprises to execute it from start to finish.

The Scope

ACG demolished the existing storefront systems across the entire shopping center — every tenant bay, every frame, every piece of glass. We prepped and cleaned each opening, then installed new ESWindows commercial storefront systems throughout, maintaining matching profiles and sightlines across the full facade run.

The result is a building that looks brand new from the street. The aerial shot doesn't lie — consistent glazing language from end to end, clean transitions at every bay, and a facade that looks like it was glazed on day one rather than retrofitted 20 years in.

Why This Scope Matters

Demo-and-replace work in an occupied retail environment requires permits for both demolition and installation, sequencing around tenant operating hours, a clean site maintained in an active parking lot, and no gaps in the building envelope while work is in progress. ACG managed all of it — no tenant complaints, no permit delays, delivered for TGSV Enterprises on schedule.
