Panama City Beach commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Panama City Beach in the WBDR?

Yes. PCB is full WBDR — coastal Bay County, 140 mph design wind. Every storefront, entrance, and window we quote in PCB defaults to Florida Product Approval impact compliance, Missile Level D on direct-exposure beachfront.

Can you handle Pier Park inline tenant storefront packages?

Yes. Pier Park inline tenants run standard storefront packages (narrow-sightline ESWindows systems, or equivalent) with impact glazing and Bay County permitting. We bid those packages routinely.

Do you specify Kynar 500 finishes on PCB beachfront work?

We recommend Kynar 500 PVDF over standard Class I anodize on direct beachfront elevations — the salt fog cycles will dull a standard anodize finish in 5–7 years. Kynar holds color and gloss 15+ years. We disclose the cost delta in the bid.

How is permitting handled in Panama City Beach?

City of Panama City Beach Building Department handles inside city limits; Bay County Builders Services on unincorporated areas. We pull permits in our own name as the Florida-licensed prime/sub of record.

What's the lead time for spring-break-deadline PCB projects?

Tight. If your PCB project needs to be open by spring break and you're not in PO by November 1, we'll tell you straight — the lead time math doesn't work and we won't pretend it does. That honesty is part of why GCs keep coming back.
