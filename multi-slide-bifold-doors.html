What are commercial multi-slide and bi-fold door systems?

Commercial multi-slide and bi-fold doors are large-span glazed opening systems that stack or pocket to connect interior and exterior space. FAQ

Questions GCs ask before they bid.

Is Euro-Wall available in HVHZ / Miami-Dade NOA configurations?+

Yes. Euro-Wall carries active Miami-Dade NOA on multiple configurations. ACG installs Euro-Wall in Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Martin, Lee, and Collier counties. HVHZ-appropriate configuration is confirmed per project.

Can these systems handle Florida wind-driven rain?+

Yes, when specified and installed correctly. Drainage is the entire game on multi-slide systems. ACG details the sill pan, drainage path, and threshold-to-interior-floor transition during shop-drawing phase - this is where most failures originate.

Folding vs. multi-slide vs. lift-and-slide - what’s the difference?+

Folding walls stack against one jamb in an accordion pattern (best for wide openings with a pocketing target). Multi-slide walls stack in overlapping tracks (best for medium openings). Lift-and-slide uses an oversized sliding mechanism where panels lift slightly to clear the sill on movement (best for very heavy single panels or luxury residential).

How long do these systems take to install?+

Depends on configuration. A typical 20-ft folding wall with 6 panels: 3-4 days field install after rough opening is ready. Oversized lift-and-slide systems can take longer due to panel weight and mechanical alignment.

Does the Euro-Wall warranty apply to ACG installs?+

Yes. Because ACG installs Euro-Wall systems in Florida, the manufacturer warranty is intact on every system we install. Warranty service is coordinated through ACG and Euro-Wall directly.

Related questions

What is the difference between multi-slide and bifold doors?

Multi-slide doors glide panels sideways behind one another and can pocket into a wall; bifold doors fold like an accordion to one side for a wider clear opening. Both are operable glass walls. American Commercial Glass installs Euro-Wall multi-slide and bifold systems, including hurricane-rated HVHZ configurations.

Which is better for restaurants, multi-slide or bifold doors?

Bifold doors open an entire wall for indoor-outdoor dining and offer multiple locking points and easy accessibility; multi-slide doors give wider unobstructed views when closed. Restaurant glazing is American Commercial Glass's signature specialty - ACG installs Euro-Wall folding and sliding systems with ADA-compliant thresholds and Florida hurricane-code compliance.

Are operable glass walls hurricane rated?

They can be. American Commercial Glass installs Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems in hurricane-rated HVHZ configurations that meet Florida wind-borne debris and High-Velocity Hurricane Zone requirements. ACG has Miami-Dade NOA compliance experience and matches each operable glass wall to the project's wind-load and code requirements.
