Code Compliance

Florida Building Code Requirements for Lakeland

Lakeland and Polk County fall within the Wind-Borne Debris Region under the Florida Building Code (FBC 7th Edition, 2023). While design wind speeds are lower than coastal areas at 120-130 mph, all commercial glazing must still meet impact-resistance standards or be protected by approved missile-impact systems.

As an inland market between Tampa and Orlando along the I-4 corridor, Lakeland benefits from lower wind speed requirements compared to coastal Florida. However, impact glazing remains mandatory throughout Polk County. ACG engineers every glazing package to meet the specific wind speed and code requirements for each project location.

All ACG impact systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet the large-missile impact test (ASTM E1886/E1996) required throughout the Wind-Borne Debris Region. Our team manages shop drawings, product approvals, and engineering calculations to streamline the permitting process with the Polk County building department.
