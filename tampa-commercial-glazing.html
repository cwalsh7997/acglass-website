FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical bid turnaround time for commercial glazing in Tampa?

ACG provides commercial glazing bids within 48 hours of receiving plans. Our AI-managed intake system reviews plans, builds the takeoff, and delivers pricing faster than traditional glazing subcontractors.

Does ACG work on residential projects?

No. ACG is a commercial-only subcontractor. We partner with general contractors, developers, and architects on Division 08 scopes. For residential impact windows, we recommend a residential specialist.

What types of commercial buildings does ACG work on in Tampa?

We install glazing on commercial and hospitality projects including restaurants, hotels, office buildings, multifamily residential, healthcare facilities, fitness centers, country clubs, retail centers, and institutional buildings.

Is ACG licensed and insured in Florida?

Yes. ACG holds Florida CGC1531993 and carries full general liability and workers compensation insurance. We are a Woman-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE) and Small Business Enterprise (SBE).

What manufacturers does ACG specify in Tampa?

ACG partners with ESWindows, Eurowall, YKK AP, CGI, Allegion, TGP, and Slimpact. We specify based on project requirements — HVHZ compliance, Miami-Dade NOA, budget, and architectural intent.
