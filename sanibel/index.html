Sanibel Context

Commercial glazing in Sanibel, Lee County.

What gets built on Sanibel. Sanibel is a barrier island in Lee County, off Fort Myers, known for resorts, inns, restaurants, retail, and the institutional buildings that serve a destination community. After Hurricane Ian came ashore nearby in 2022, much of the island's commercial building stock entered a rebuilding and hardening cycle - resorts, restaurants, retail centers, and civic buildings being rebuilt or retrofitted to current code. That rebuild is hospitality-heavy and impact-glazing-heavy by nature.

Why impact glazing leads here. Sanibel is in one of the highest design-wind regions in the continental U.S. Coastal Lee County commercial buildings face ultimate design wind speeds near the top of the Florida Building Code maps - the same Category 5 exposure that drives the strictest envelope decisions in the state. Much commercial glazing on the island is specified to large-missile impact and high design-pressure standards, frequently at the Miami-Dade NOA grade even where Florida Product Approval would technically suffice, because owners and engineers want the highest-tested assemblies on a barrier island. ACG specifies, documents, and installs impact-rated storefront, windows, doors, and sliding systems to that standard.

What ACG does in Sanibel. Impact-rated storefront and curtain wall for resort and retail frontage, impact windows and doors for inns, restaurants, and offices, large impact-rated multi-slide and folding glass walls for hospitality spaces that open to the water, all-glass and aluminum entrances, and glass railings for terraces and amenity decks. We carry product approvals current and tie install instructions to the specific approval for each opening - the documentation a coastal jurisdiction inspects closely.

Served from Naples. Sanibel is served from ACG's Naples office on Tamiami Trail, which runs the Southwest Florida market day to day. Naples and Lee County are ACG's home coast for impact-zone hospitality and coastal commercial work, and the office keeps project management, supervision, and crews staged for barrier-island logistics - including the access and staging constraints that island work carries.

Who we work with. ACG bids directly to general contractors, resort and hospitality ownership groups, restaurant operators, and commercial property owners rebuilding on Sanibel. We are active on Procore and BuildingConnected. The 48-hour bid turnaround fits the front-end schedule on rebuild and new-construction work, and ACG locks rough opening dimensions in pre-construction. Lead times run 8-16 weeks from approved shop drawings depending on system, plus field installation.
