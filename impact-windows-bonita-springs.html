The Market

Bonita Springs is a commercial glazing market.

Bonita Springs anchors the luxury corridor between Naples (15 miles south) and Fort Myers (20 miles north). The commercial market includes Coconut Point (a 1.2-million-SF Class A mixed-use lifestyle center), Miromar Outlets, Bonita Bay luxury master-planned community, Pelican Landing, Barefoot Beach, and Bonita Beach Gulf-front hospitality. Estero immediately to the north adds Gulf Coast Town Center, Hertz Corporate headquarters, and major healthcare campuses.

Bonita Springs sits in Exposure Category C for most of its footprint, with Exposure D applying to Bonita Beach, Barefoot Beach, and Gulf-facing elevations. Hurricane Ian (September 2022) brought significant wind and storm surge impact, with Bonita Beach and Gulf-facing commercial properties sustaining major damage. Hurricane Milton (October 2024) added further rebuild-cycle demand. Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance with Florida Product Approval documentation is required on every commercial permit.

ACG serves Bonita Springs from our Naples office (15 miles south) with the same commercial subcontractor infrastructure we bring to 5th Avenue South Naples and Pelican Bay. Our ESWindows flagship partnership delivers Exposure C and D-capable storefront, window wall, and impact sliding doors for Bonita Springs retail, hospitality, multifamily, and healthcare projects. For hospitality and luxury residential amenity openings where operable walls define the experience - Bonita Bay beach club, Pelican Landing amenity, Barefoot Beach waterfront - we install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems.

Bonita Springs FAQ

Common questions - answered.

Does Bonita Springs require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Bonita Springs and Lee County sit in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening. The City of Bonita Springs and Lee County both enforce full FBC compliance on all new construction and major renovations.

What exposure category applies to Bonita Springs commercial projects?

Most of Bonita Springs and Estero is Exposure Category C. Bonita Beach, Barefoot Beach, Gulf-facing elevations, and Gulf-proximate portions of Bonita Bay are Exposure D. DP40-DP60 is typical inland; DP60-DP80+ Gulf-facing. Florida Product Approval documentation must confirm the appropriate exposure.

How did Hurricanes Ian and Milton affect Bonita Springs?

Hurricane Ian (September 2022) brought major storm surge to Bonita Beach and significant wind damage inland across Bonita Springs. Bonita Beach commercial properties sustained major storm surge impact. Hurricane Milton (October 2024) added further rebuild-cycle demand. The hardening and rebuild work since has driven substantial commercial impact glazing demand across the luxury corridor.
