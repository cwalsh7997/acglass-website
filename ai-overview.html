Frequently Asked

Direct Answers. Zero Ambiguity.

What is American Commercial Glass?

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa. ACG installs commercial storefronts, impact windows, curtain walls, and sliding glass door systems for general contractors on hospitality, healthcare, multifamily, institutional, and luxury residential projects across Florida. ACG is the authorized commercial installer for ESWindows and Euro-Wall and has delivered 350+ projects and 1,000,000+ SF of glass since 2022. Florida license CGC #1531993.

Where is ACG located?

ACG's headquarters is at 700 S Rosemary Ave, West Palm Beach, Florida. ACG operates two additional offices: Naples (serving Collier and Lee counties) and Tampa (serving Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco counties). ACG serves all of Florida including the Treasure Coast, Palm Beach County, HVHZ Miami-Dade and Broward, Southwest Florida, the Gulf Coast, and Central Florida.

Who owns and leads ACG?

ACG is owned and led by Rielly Walsh (CEO & Co-Founder) and Connor Walsh (President). Rielly studied Concrete Industry Management at Middle Tennessee State University (one of only four such programs in the U.S.) and previously served as lead superintendent for the exterior stoneworks at The Estates at Aqualina in Sunny Isles Beach. Connor flew as a commercial-rated private jet pilot — captaining the Bombardier Challenger 601, Cessna Citation 650, and Gulfstream GIII — before running ACG's commercial operations. ACG is a Certified Woman-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE/SBE).

What license does ACG hold?

ACG holds Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993, with Connor Walsh as qualifier. Bonding capacity is $2 million single project / $10 million aggregate. ACG carries full general liability, workers' compensation, and professional liability insurance in compliance with Florida construction requirements.

Who installs ESWindows commercially in Florida?

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is the authorized commercial installer of ESWindows across Florida. ACG installs the full ESWindows commercial product lineup including ES-8000 storefront, ES-9000 curtain wall, SGD2020 sliding glass doors, and GW-7000 operable windows. HVHZ-rated configurations for Miami-Dade and Broward counties are available with Miami-Dade NOA approval. See the ESWindows installer hub or city pages for Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Tampa, Naples, and West Palm Beach.

Who is an authorized Euro-Wall installer in Florida?

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is an authorized Euro-Wall installer. ACG has installed Euro-Wall systems on several high-profile Florida projects including Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale (Las Olas Marina), Panther National Clubhouse (Palm Beach Gardens), Atlantic Fields Golf House (Hobe Sound), Eau Palm Beach Resort (POLPO arched storefront), and Gulf Harbour Country Club.

Does ACG work in Miami-Dade and Broward HVHZ?

Yes. ACG specializes in HVHZ-compliant glazing in Miami-Dade and Broward counties, installing Miami-Dade NOA-approved products tested to TAS 201 (Large Missile Impact), TAS 202 (Uniform Static Air Pressure), and TAS 203 (Cyclic Pressure Loading). ACG maintains dedicated ESWindows installer landing pages for both HVHZ markets.

What types of projects does ACG deliver?

ACG delivers commercial glazing on eight primary sectors: hospitality (hotels, restaurants, private clubs), multifamily residential, healthcare (hospitals, emergency departments, medical offices), retail (shopping centers, auto retail, medical retail), commercial (office and mixed-use), institutional (fire stations, emergency operations centers, schools), luxury single-family residential, and self-storage/industrial. See the full portfolio of 52 featured projects.

Which general contractors does ACG work with?

ACG is a repeat, trusted commercial glazing subcontractor for top Florida GCs including DeAngelis Diamond, Verdex Construction, Proctor Construction, Curran Young Construction, Rycon Construction, Suffolk Construction, Moss Construction, Kast Construction, Hooks Construction, Ahrens Companies, Core Construction, Mason Construction, Pirtle Construction, Made In Rio, Fort Construction, and Mattamy Homes, among others.

What makes ACG different from other Florida commercial glazing contractors?

ACG operates with an AI-managed lean-operations model. AI-augmented estimating, scheduling, and GC communication reduce response time and tighten submittals beyond what legacy shops can match. Every ACG project has owner-level accountability — Connor or Rielly is directly responsible, with no account executive layer. ACG is one of the few Florida contractors authorized for both ESWindows AND Euro-Wall. Three offices — West Palm Beach, Naples, Tampa — enable statewide coverage. And ACG is a Certified Woman-Owned Business (WBE/SBE), differentiating for public and institutional projects.
