The Market

Lakewood Ranch is a commercial glazing market.

Lakewood Ranch is America's #1 best-selling master-planned community per RCLCO. The commercial and multifamily build-out across Manatee and Sarasota Counties includes medical campuses, corporate parks, mixed-use retail, hospitality, and thousands of Class A multifamily units. Every new commercial opening must comply with the Florida Building Code 8th Edition — and for most Lakewood Ranch projects, that means impact-rated glazing.

Lakewood Ranch sits inland in Exposure Category C for most of the community footprint, with portions near Waterside in Exposure D. Florida Product Approval (FL#), ASTM E1886/E1996 missile impact testing, and design pressure ratings appropriate to the site are required. ACG provides the full FBC documentation package on every project.

ACG installs ESWindows 8000-series storefront, 9000-series window wall, and SGD-2020 impact sliding doors across Lakewood Ranch. For premium hospitality and amenity projects — the clubhouses, pool bars, and rooftop spaces that define Lakewood Ranch's lifestyle communities — we also install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems, the same product on Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale and Atlantic Fields.

Lakewood Ranch FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Does Lakewood Ranch require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Lakewood Ranch is within Florida's wind-borne debris region, and the Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening. Most developers choose full impact glazing for aesthetic and operational reasons.

What products does ACG install in Lakewood Ranch?

Our primary product partner is ESWindows (8000-series storefront, 9000-series window wall, SGD-2020 impact sliding doors). For premium hospitality and amenity spaces, we also install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems. All products carry Florida Product Approval and appropriate DP ratings.

How much do commercial impact windows cost in Lakewood Ranch?

Typical ranges are $65-$180 per square foot installed depending on system type. Storefront $65-$120, window wall $75-$140, curtainwall $90-$180. A mid-size retail rollout runs $170,000-$360,000. Lakewood Ranch projects in Exposure C typically run at the mid-range; Waterside and Gulf-proximate sites in Exposure D run 10-15% higher.

Which Lakewood Ranch neighborhoods has ACG quoted or worked in?

ACG is active across the entire Lakewood Ranch commercial district including Waterside Place, Main Street at Lakewood Ranch, UTC, Lakewood Ranch Corporate Park, and the various Class A multifamily communities. For specific project references, contact connor@acglass.com with your scope.

What is ACG's bonding capacity?

$2M single project / $10M aggregate. Capacity letters available on request. This covers the vast majority of Lakewood Ranch commercial and multifamily projects.
