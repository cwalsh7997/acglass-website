Common Questions

Kissimmee Glazing FAQ.

Does Kissimmee require impact windows on commercial projects? +

Kissimmee is not in the HVHZ, but Florida Building Code requires wind-resistant glazing systems for Osceola County's exposure category. ACG ensures every system meets FBC wind design requirements for the project's specific wind zone.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Kissimmee? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, Slimpact, TGP fire-rated glass, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. System selection is driven by the project spec, wind zone, and architect's design intent.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for Kissimmee? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours.

Does ACG handle permits and submittals for Osceola County? +

Yes. ACG manages the full submittal package — shop drawings, Florida product approvals, and engineer-of-record coordination. Our packages are built to pass Osceola County plan review without revisions.
