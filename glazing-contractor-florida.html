The Role

What a Commercial
Glazing Contractor Does.

In commercial construction, the glazing contractor — often listed as the glazing subcontractor or Division 08 sub — is responsible for every glass and aluminum opening on the building. That scope includes storefront entrances, curtainwall facades, window wall systems on multifamily towers, impact-rated windows and doors, fire-rated glass assemblies, automatic entrance systems, and interior glass partitions.

The glazing contractor's work begins during the bid phase: reviewing construction documents, identifying the specified systems, pricing the scope, and flagging specification issues before contract award. After award, the glazing sub takes the drawings through shop drawing production, Florida product approval verification, submittal processing, and eventually installation and inspection.

In Florida's construction environment — with wind-borne debris requirements, Miami-Dade NOA compliance in the HVHZ, and strict product approval processes — a licensed glazing contractor is a technical partner, not just a labor vendor.

Common Questions

Glazing Contractor Florida FAQ.

What does a glazing contractor do on a commercial project? +

A commercial glazing contractor is the Division 08 subcontractor on a commercial construction project. Their scope includes storefront systems, curtainwall facades, window wall, impact windows and doors, fire-rated glass assemblies, automatic entrances, and interior glass partitions. Beyond installation, the glazing sub produces shop drawings, manages Florida product approvals and NOA compliance, coordinates the submittal process, and handles field inspection coordination. In Florida, where wind-borne debris requirements and HVHZ product approval add regulatory complexity, the glazing contractor is a technical partner — not just a labor crew.

Does ACG cover all of Florida as a glazing contractor? +

Yes. ACG's three offices — West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa — allow us to cover the full state with local crews and local knowledge. South Florida and the Treasure Coast are served from West Palm Beach. Southwest Florida from Naples. The Tampa Bay area and Central Florida from our Tampa office. For large projects elsewhere in the state, we deploy from the closest office. We've completed 350+ commercial projects across Florida's healthcare, education, retail, mixed-use, and government sectors.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Florida? +

ACG installs the full commercial glazing scope: aluminum storefront (ESWindows, YKK AP, Eurowall), curtainwall (Eurowall, Kawneer, YKK AP), window wall for mid-rise multifamily, impact-rated windows and doors (CGI, Slimpact, ESWindows), fire-rated glass assemblies (TGP SuperLite I & II, Pyroswiss), automatic entrance systems (Dorma, Allegion), and interior glass partitions. System selection is driven by the project specification and applicable Florida Building Code requirements — not by what's most convenient or lowest-priced to stock.

How do GCs hire a commercial glazing contractor in Florida? +

Most GCs engage a glazing contractor by sending the construction document set and requesting a scope and price during the bid phase. For Florida projects, verify that your glazing sub holds an active Florida contractor's license, has demonstrable Florida product approval experience, can produce PE-stamped shop drawings for curtainwall and high-wind applications, and has a track record of on-time submittal delivery.
