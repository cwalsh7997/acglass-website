The Clearwater Market

Clearwater Is One of
Tampa Bay's Most Active
Commercial Markets.

Clearwater is not simply a beach town — it's a major commercial construction market within the Tampa Bay metro, with multiple distinct submarkets generating consistent glazing scope. The downtown Clearwater redevelopment has accelerated through the 2020s, with mixed-use projects transforming the core around Cleveland Street and the bluff overlooking Clearwater Harbor.

The US-19 commercial corridor is one of the most consistently active retail and commercial development strips in all of Pinellas County — running north through Palm Harbor and south through Largo, with constant infill, renovation, and new construction. National and regional tenants, medical office, and service retail are building and rebuilding along this corridor year-round.

Clearwater Beach remains one of the top hospitality and commercial development zones in Florida — hotel renovations, new resort construction, and beachfront commercial projects demand glazing systems that are properly tested and certified for direct Gulf exposure. Mease Countryside and Mease Dunedin hospitals anchor a medical construction corridor through Safety Harbor and Dunedin that continues to generate healthcare renovation and expansion work.

The Safety Harbor and Dunedin commercial strips, the Tarpon Springs commercial district, and the Palm Harbor retail corridor all represent consistent smaller-scale commercial glazing scope that ACG's Tampa crew handles efficiently — the same crew doing the large hotels is also qualified for the smaller storefronts.

Common Questions

Clearwater Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG have crews available for Clearwater commercial projects? +

Yes. ACG's Tampa office serves Clearwater and all of Pinellas County with dedicated local field crews, established supply relationships, and direct familiarity with Pinellas County's building department and inspection process. We're not making day trips — Clearwater is a core part of our Tampa office service area.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Clearwater? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. For Clearwater Beach hospitality projects, we commonly work with higher-spec PGT and Euro-Wall curtainwall systems with enhanced coastal sealant details. System selection always follows project spec and architectural intent.

Are impact-rated systems required for Clearwater commercial buildings? +

Pinellas County is not in Miami-Dade's HVHZ, but Clearwater's coastal location means that most of the city — and especially Clearwater Beach — falls within Florida's wind-borne debris region. Impact-rated systems are required for these locations, and are commonly specified throughout the rest of the county as well. ACG's submittal packages are engineered for Pinellas County plan review requirements from the first submission.

Does ACG serve Dunedin, Safety Harbor, Tarpon Springs, and Palm Harbor? +

Yes. ACG's Tampa office serves the full North Pinellas County commercial market — Clearwater, Dunedin, Safety Harbor, Tarpon Springs, Palm Harbor, Countryside, and Largo (north Pinellas). These communities represent active commercial construction markets with retail, medical, mixed-use, and hospitality projects that fall squarely in our service area.

How fast can ACG return a scope for a Clearwater project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. From a Dunedin retail storefront to a Clearwater Beach hotel curtainwall, our operations support fast, accurate takeoffs for any project size. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions.
