Why commercial glazing in Tallahassee is different.

Tallahassee is a different Panhandle market — interior, not coastal, so the wind load drops to 115 mph and the WBDR doesn't apply. But the specification rigor goes UP, not down. State of Florida DMS jobs, FSU and FAMU university work, and Tallahassee Memorial Hospital expansions all carry detailed shop-drawing review, thermal performance specs that beach jobs never see, and aesthetic constraints from historic district commissions downtown. ACG bids Tallahassee as a Florida-licensed specialty glazing sub with the engineering and submittal practices state and university work requires.

Architectural context: Tallahassee is the seat of Florida state government and home to FSU and FAMU — the commercial glazing work splits between historic preservation downtown (Cascades, Capitol Hill), university science and admin building curtain wall, large healthcare campus expansions, and Killearn/Northeast suburban retail. Leon is interior (not WBDR), but state DMS jobs and university projects have their own specification rigor.

Tallahassee commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Tallahassee in the Wind-Borne Debris Region?

No. Interior Leon County is not WBDR — design wind speed is 115 mph and impact glazing is not code-required. That said, many state, university, and healthcare projects spec impact glass anyway for security or program-protection reasons. We'll quote both compliant base and impact alternates.

Have you worked on Florida State University or FAMU projects?

ACG is a Florida-statewide specialty glazing subcontractor — we bid FSU, FAMU, and FSU Panama City work as a sub to prime GCs holding the contracts. Education work has its own submittal rigor and we structure our packages to clear it on first review.

Do you handle State of Florida DMS specifications?

Yes. DMS state-spec jobs (LEED requirements, specific thermal performance, security glazing) come with prescriptive specifications. We respond with shop drawings, performance calcs, and FL Product Approval references that match the spec section by section.

What's your experience with historic district downtown projects?

Tallahassee's downtown historic district has design review on storefront alterations. We bid those projects with the historically-correct profile width, sightline, and finish (often anodized to match adjacent original ironwork) and submit to the design review board as part of our scope.

How quickly can a Tallahassee project be bid?

Standard 48 hours from drawings and specs. State and university work with detailed performance specs may take 5–7 business days for the full submittal package, but we'll send the lump-sum number and lead time within 48 hours and follow with the submittal binder.
