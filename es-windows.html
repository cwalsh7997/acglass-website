The Scale
3.6 million square feet of manufacturing - one campus, one supply chain, one standard. - Tecnoglass · Barranquilla, Colombia

ESWindows is the U.S. commercial brand of Tecnoglass Inc. (NYSE: TGLS) - the first Colombian company to list on Nasdaq and the second-largest glass fabricator serving the United States. They don’t buy glass, frame, or finishes from someone else. They own the entire supply chain.

From their vertically integrated 3.6 million square foot complex in Barranquilla - including their Alutions aluminum extrusion plant and their Soft Coat Low-E glass facility - every profile, every glass unit, and every finish is produced, tested, and shipped under a single quality system. For a Florida commercial GC, that means: no finger-pointing between suppliers, predictable lead times, and specs that hold.

They’ve been building to Miami-Dade HVHZ standards since 2002 - one of the earliest manufacturers to do so. When the scope calls for impact-rated, blast-rated, or oversized commercial glazing at a real price, ESWindows is the answer for more Florida GCs than any other single manufacturer.

ACG furnishes and installs ESWindows on Florida commercial scopes. Statewide code path and office map: Florida commercial glazing hub. Office keepers: West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa. Operable-wall specs: Euro-Wall. Send plans for 48-hour budget pricing.
