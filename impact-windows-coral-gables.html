The Market

Coral Gables is a commercial glazing market.

Coral Gables is one of South Florida's most architecturally distinguished commercial markets - the historic Mediterranean Revival city designed by George Merrick, now home to corporate headquarters (Hooters of America, Bacardi USA, etc.), luxury retail along Miracle Mile, The Shops at Merrick Park, and boutique hospitality including the Biltmore Hotel.

Coral Gables sits in Miami-Dade HVHZ. Every commercial opening requires Miami-Dade NOA-approved impact-rated glazing with TAS 201/202/203 testing. Coral Gables additionally enforces some of the most rigorous architectural review standards in Florida - the Historic Preservation Board and Development Review Board both review commercial projects, and storefront details must comply with the city's Mediterranean architectural vocabulary.

ACG's ESWindows flagship partnership delivers HVHZ-rated commercial storefront, window wall, and impact sliding doors with the architectural-grade finishes Coral Gables projects require - bronze anodized, custom PVDF powder-coat, specified aluminum colors. Miami-Dade NOA documentation is standard with every project. For historic preservation projects where architectural detail matters as much as performance, our coordination with architects and historic preservation consultants is part of the workflow.

Coral Gables FAQ

Common questions - answered.

Does Coral Gables require HVHZ impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Coral Gables is in Miami-Dade County HVHZ. Every commercial opening requires Miami-Dade NOA-approved impact-rated glazing with TAS 201/202/203 testing. Coral Gables additionally enforces architectural review standards via the Historic Preservation Board and Development Review Board.

What Coral Gables architectural standards affect commercial glazing?

Coral Gables enforces Mediterranean Revival design standards throughout its commercial districts. Storefront details, aluminum finish colors, frame profile widths, and architectural integration all receive review. ACG's ESWindows partnership provides the finish options and architectural-grade frame profiles Coral Gables architects typically specify.

Does ACG coordinate with Coral Gables historic preservation?

Yes. ACG works through the general contractor and owner's architect on Coral Gables historic district and architecturally sensitive projects. We provide specification-quality product submittals, sample finishes, and installation documentation that aligns with the city's review requirements.

Does ACG have HVHZ Miami-Dade commercial project experience?

Yes. Our Miami-Dade portfolio includes iFly Miami and related HVHZ commercial projects. For Broward HVHZ, ACG carries complete Euro-Wall NOA documentation. Full HVHZ product depth and NOA documentation on every Coral Gables project.
