Industrial Glazing

Industrial Glazing That
Keeps Up with the Schedule.

Industrial and logistics construction runs on aggressive schedules. The GC is managing dozens of scopes simultaneously and needs subs who self-manage. ACG fits that profile — we show up when we say we will, we coordinate with adjacent trades proactively, and we don't create problems that require the superintendent to babysit.

Industrial glazing scopes typically include front office storefront, dock door vision lite systems, mezzanine office glazing, fire-rated glass for hazmat areas, and the occasional specialty scope for manufacturing facilities.

South Florida industrial is in HVHZ territory — Doral, Medley, Miramar, and the industrial parks throughout Miami-Dade and Broward counties all require HVHZ-rated storefront and glazing systems. ACG brings the same FBC compliance to industrial that we bring to every other sector.
