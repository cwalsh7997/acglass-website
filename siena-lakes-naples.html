Sheet A-011 · The project

Interiors that residents live with every day.

Siena Lakes is a large-scale continuing care retirement community developed by Erickson Senior Living — one of the most active senior living developers in the country. The Naples campus features resort-style amenities, multiple dining venues, fitness centers, and hundreds of residences across a walkable, interconnected layout.

For a senior living project of this scale, interior doors and glass aren't an afterthought — they define the feel of hallways, common spaces, and private residences. The light transmission, the hardware, the way a door closes: all of it matters, because the interior environment directly affects quality of life for residents every day.

ACG handled the interior door and glass package — the complete interior glazing scope across the community's common areas, corridors, and amenity spaces. This is a high-unit-count, precision-coordination scope: every opening had to meet Erickson's specifications while fitting a construction schedule shared by multiple trades across a large, multi-building campus. Erickson builds to a consistent national standard across every campus, which means every subcontractor has to perform. ACG delivered the scope on schedule and to spec.
