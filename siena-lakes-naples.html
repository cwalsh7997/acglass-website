The Project

Siena Lakes is a large-scale continuing care retirement community developed by Erickson Senior Living — one of the country's leading senior living developers. The Naples campus features resort-style amenities, multiple dining venues, fitness centers, and hundreds of residences across a walkable, interconnected layout. It's the kind of project where the interior environment directly affects quality of life for residents every day.

For a senior living project of this scale, interior doors and glass aren't an afterthought — they define the feel of hallways, common spaces, and private residences. The light transmission, the hardware, the way a door closes: all of it matters.

The Scope

ACG handled the interior door and glass package for Siena Lakes — the complete interior glazing scope across the community's common areas, corridors, and amenity spaces. This is a high-unit-count, precision-coordination scope. Every opening had to meet Erickson's specifications while fitting within a construction schedule shared by multiple trades across a large, multi-building campus.

ACG's operations model — lean management, AI-assisted coordination, no excess overhead — is exactly what makes a scope like this executable at scale without surprises.

The Team

Delivered for Erickson Senior Living, one of the most active senior living developers in the country. Erickson builds to a consistent national standard across every campus, which means every subcontractor on a Siena Lakes project has to perform. ACG delivered the interior glazing scope on schedule and to spec.
