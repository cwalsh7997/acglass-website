Walton County commercial glazing market.

Walton County is home to the 30A luxury beach-town corridor - Santa Rosa Beach, Seaside, WaterColor, WaterSound, Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, Inlet Beach, and Miramar Beach (Sandestin). Commercial glazing here runs through Architectural Review Boards on most beach-town projects AND has to meet 130-140 mph coastal wind loads with full WBDR impact certification. ACG bids Walton County commercial as a Florida-licensed glazing sub with the architectural detail and impact NOA library the market requires.

Walton County permitting and code notes.

  • Walton County BCC permits unincorporated areas (including most 30A submarkets).
  • City of DeFuniak Springs and City of Freeport have their own building departments.
  • Coastal Walton is full Wind-Borne Debris Region (WBDR) - impact glazing required.
  • Beach-town ARCs / ARBs (Seaside, Rosemary Beach, WaterColor, Alys Beach, WaterSound) review storefront submittals before permitting.
