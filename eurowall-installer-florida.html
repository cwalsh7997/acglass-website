About the Manufacturer

About Euro-Wall

Euro-Wall is a North Port, Florida-based manufacturer of premium multi-slide and bi-fold glass wall systems. Their product line is designed for applications where creating a seamless transition between indoor and outdoor spaces is the architectural intent — country clubs, resort dining rooms, restaurant storefronts, clubhouses, and commercial projects.

Euro-Wall systems feature heavy-duty aluminum framing, precision-engineered hardware, and glass panel sizes capable of spanning wide openings without sacrificing structural performance. Their "wall vista fold" bi-fold system, in particular, is a flagship product for hospitality and country club applications — it allows an entire wall of a dining room or ballroom to open completely to an outdoor terrace or pool deck.

Because Euro-Wall manufactures from North Port, FL, ACG's coordination with their team happens on a direct, local basis. Factory visits, pre-installation coordination, and technical reviews are practical and efficient — an advantage on complex hospitality projects with demanding design teams.

Systems ACG Installs

Euro-Wall Systems
ACG Installs

Wall Vista Fold — Bi-Fold Glass Wall

The Wall Vista Fold is Euro-Wall's signature bi-fold system. Panels fold and stack to one or both sides, opening an entire wall face. Used by ACG at Gulf Harbour Yacht & Country Club for the club's indoor-outdoor dining renovation. The system accommodates large glass panels, creating an unobstructed opening between interior and exterior spaces.

Multi-Slide Door Systems

Euro-Wall's multi-slide systems allow multiple large glass panels to slide and stack, creating wide openings with minimal visible framing when open. ACG has installed these on resort and clubhouse projects where the design intent is a full indoor-outdoor connection — Eau Palm Beach Resort and Panther National Clubhouse are both multi-slide scopes.

Hurricane-Rated Options

Euro-Wall manufactures hurricane-rated configurations for Florida's coastal markets. ACG specifies hurricane-rated Euro-Wall systems where wind load requirements and local building code mandate impact performance — including coastal Palm Beach and Fort Myers markets where ACG's Euro-Wall projects are concentrated.

Why ACG Specifies Euro-Wall

The Case for
Euro-Wall

When an architect specifies a bi-fold or multi-slide glass wall system on a commercial project in Florida, Euro-Wall is one of ACG's primary recommendations. Here is why.

Florida Manufacturer. Euro-Wall manufactures in North Port, FL. That means shorter lead times, no international shipping delays, and the ability to arrange factory visits with Euro-Wall's engineering and sales team — a practical advantage on complex projects with custom specifications.

Hurricane-Rated Options. Florida's coastal markets require hurricane-rated glazing systems in most applications. Euro-Wall manufactures hurricane-rated configurations that meet Florida Building Code requirements for both interior and exterior applications.

Proven at the Top of the Market. ACG's Euro-Wall reference list includes Forbes Five-Star Eau Palm Beach Resort, Panther National Clubhouse, and Gulf Harbour Yacht & Country Club. These are reference projects that reflect the product's performance and ACG's installation quality.
