Common Questions

Hialeah Glazing FAQ.

Is Hialeah in the HVHZ? +

Yes. Hialeah is in Miami-Dade County and within Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ). All commercial glazing systems must carry Miami-Dade NOA documentation. ACG manages the full NOA submittal package in-house.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Hialeah? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, Slimpact, TGP fire-rated glass, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora — all with Miami-Dade NOA documentation required for Hialeah and Miami-Dade County projects.

Does ACG have experience in Hialeah specifically? +

Yes. ACG completed the Westlake Hialeah storefront retrofit project — a full impact storefront replacement in Hialeah — and has active South Florida crews familiar with the Hialeah building department and Miami-Dade plan review process.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for a Hialeah project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions.
