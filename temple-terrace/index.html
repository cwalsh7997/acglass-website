Temple Terrace Context

Commercial glazing in Temple Terrace, Hillsborough County.

What gets built in Temple Terrace. Temple Terrace is a built-up suburban city in Hillsborough County, just northeast of Tampa near the University of South Florida. Its commercial development is dominated by retail and neighborhood shopping centers, multifamily and student-housing apartments, medical office, and the mixed-use redevelopment moving through the Fowler Avenue, 56th Street, and Temple Terrace Highway corridors. The USF-adjacent submarket keeps a steady pipeline of apartment, retail, and institutional construction.

What ACG does in Temple Terrace. We handle the full Division 08 commercial envelope. Aluminum storefront for retail and restaurant frontage, window wall and punched openings for multifamily and student housing, curtain wall for mid-rise office and mixed-use, all-glass and aluminum entrances for lobbies and retail, glass railings for balconies and amenity decks, and interior glass partitions for office and medical fit-outs. Single-source from the frame to the hardware to the submittal package.

Served from Tampa. Temple Terrace is minutes from ACG's Tampa office at Rocky Point. That proximity keeps project management, field supervision, and crew scheduling close to the work. Connor Walsh opened the Tampa office to commit ACG to the Tampa Bay market on a single-source, owner-led basis — the same way the company runs West Palm Beach and Naples.

Wind and code. Temple Terrace is outside Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone but remains in a hurricane wind region. Hillsborough County commercial glazing is designed to the Florida Building Code under ASCE 7, typically Exposure B or C with ultimate design wind speeds around 150 mph for Risk Category II buildings, and documented through Florida Product Approval. ACG keeps approval documentation current and ties install instructions to the specific approval for each opening, which matters on multifamily where the unit count multiplies every detail.

Who we work with. ACG bids directly to general contractors, multifamily and student-housing developers, retail and restaurant operators, and commercial property owners active in Temple Terrace. We are active on Procore and BuildingConnected for project invites. The 48-hour bid turnaround fits the front-end schedule, and ACG locks rough opening dimensions in pre-construction. Lead times run 8–16 weeks from approved shop drawings depending on system, plus field installation scaled to the building.
