Healthcare Glazing

Life-Safety Glazing for
Healthcare Facilities. Done Right.

Healthcare glazing is where code compliance becomes life safety. Fire-rated corridor vision panels. Nurse station sliding windows. Patient room glazing with safety glass. Infection control-compatible sealants and framing profiles. ICU isolation room glazing with acoustic performance.

ACG has worked on hospital expansions, medical office buildings, outpatient surgical centers, and specialty medical facilities throughout Florida. We understand the specific code requirements that apply to healthcare occupancies — and we coordinate with the building's infection preventionist and facilities team during construction to ensure our installations are compatible with operational requirements.

TGP fire-rated glass systems are our primary specification for healthcare corridor vision panels and fire-rated wall assemblies. We maintain full UL certification documentation and installation compliance records for your AHJ and Joint Commission review.

Fire-Rated Systems

Hospital Fire-Rated Glazing

UL-certified fire-rated glass for healthcare corridor and stairwell applications.

Healthcare facilities require fire-rated glazing throughout — corridor vision panels, stairwell enclosures, elevator lobbies, and separation walls between occupancy types. ACG installs TGP fire-rated assemblies with full UL certification and documentation. Ratings from 20 to 120 minutes. Full code analysis provided before submittal.
