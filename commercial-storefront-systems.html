Commercial Storefront
Systems That Perform.

Your storefront is the first thing people see. It's also the first line of defense against wind, water, and forced entry. ACG delivers storefront systems that do both — with architectural precision and code compliance that passes inspection the first time.

ACG Storefront Approach

Storefront Done Right,
From Day One..

Commercial storefront sounds straightforward — until it isn't. Slab edge conditions that weren't coordinated with the structural engineer. Head and sill details that conflict with the waterproofing spec. Anchor conditions that weren't thought through at submittal. Glass types that don't match what the energy model required.

ACG reviews drawings before we commit to a price. We flag conflicts during buyout, not during installation when they're $50,000 change orders. Our shop drawings are complete — head, sill, jamb, transition conditions all detailed and coordinated with adjacent trades.

We use Eurowall aluminum framing systems, specifying thermally broken options for projects with energy compliance requirements and standard systems where thermal break isn't needed. Anodized or powder-coated finishes in standard and custom colors. Tempered, laminated, or insulated glass units as the project demands.

System Options

Thermally Broken Storefront

Energy-compliant framing for projects requiring improved thermal performance.

Eurowall thermally broken aluminum framing systems eliminate the heat bridge between interior and exterior aluminum, improving energy performance and preventing condensation in temperature-differential climates. Available in standard extruded profiles and custom configurations.

HVHZ-Rated Storefront Systems

Impact-rated storefront for South Florida and coastal markets.

Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone requires impact-rated storefront on most commercial applications. ACG installs ESWindows and Slimpact-rated storefront systems that meet large missile impact testing requirements. Full compliance with FBC Section 1609 and Miami-Dade NOA listings.
