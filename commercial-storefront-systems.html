FAQ

Questions GCs ask before they bid.

Impact vs. non-impact storefront — when is each appropriate?+

Impact storefront is required in HVHZ counties (Miami-Dade, Broward) and typically specified on the first two floors of any coastal commercial building regardless of county. Non-impact storefront is used elsewhere in Florida for interior elevations and buildings set back from the coast. ACG installs both.

What is ES-8000 vs ES-9000?+

ESWindows ES-8000 is the impact-rated storefront system for HVHZ and coastal applications. ES-9000 is the thermally broken version for higher-performance energy code compliance. ACG is an authorized Florida dealer for both.

Does ACG handle door hardware?+

Yes. ACG coordinates and supplies door hardware through our Allegion partnership. Panic devices, exit hardware, closers, electrified hardware, and ADA-compliant thresholds are part of our standard storefront scope.

Can ACG work with a GC’s preferred architect and specifier?+

Yes. ACG regularly collaborates with architects from schematic design through CDs, providing system recommendations, budget direction, and manufacturer submittals. We are familiar with Kobi Karp, Discovery Land design teams, and most major Florida commercial firms.

How fast can ACG turn around a storefront bid?+

48 hours for standard commercial storefront packages. 72–96 hours for complex mixed-use or multi-elevation projects.
