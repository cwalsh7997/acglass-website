Okaloosa County commercial glazing market.

Okaloosa County covers some of the busiest commercial glazing markets in the Panhandle - Destin and Fort Walton Beach on the coast (140 mph, full WBDR), Niceville on the bay (130 mph, still WBDR), and Crestview in the interior (125 mph, outside WBDR). Eglin AFB and Hurlburt Field anchor a significant military-adjacent commercial market. ACG bids Okaloosa as a Florida-licensed glazing sub with impact NOA coverage and the federal-sub experience the Eglin perimeter requires.

Okaloosa County permitting and code notes.

  • Okaloosa County Growth Management permits unincorporated areas.
  • City of Destin, City of Fort Walton Beach, City of Niceville, City of Crestview, City of Valparaiso, and Town of Cinco Bayou have their own building departments.
  • Coastal Okaloosa is full WBDR; interior (Crestview, north of I-10) is outside WBDR.
  • Eglin AFB and Hurlburt Field commercial work is federal sub-contract territory - separate procurement standards.
