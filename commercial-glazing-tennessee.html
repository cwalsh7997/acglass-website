Tennessee Market

Tennessee: One of the Southeast's
Fastest-Growing Markets.

Tennessee has emerged as one of the Southeast's fastest-growing and most dynamic commercial construction markets. Nashville's sustained population growth, corporate relocations, and massive technology investment have produced one of the most active high-rise and mixed-use construction pipelines in the country.

Nashville's SoBro and Gulch neighborhoods are running some of the Southeast's most complex glazing scopes. The Gulch's mixed-use explosion, anchored by Amazon and AllianceBernstein's campus construction, is producing mid-rise and podium commercial glazing scopes. Nashville's suburban markets — Brentwood, Franklin, and Cool Springs — are seeing major corporate campus and office park construction from healthcare, financial services, and technology companies relocating from higher-cost markets.

Memphis is one of the Southeast's most active healthcare construction markets. The Methodist Le Bonheur Healthcare System, Baptist Memorial Health Care, and the St. Jude Children's Research Hospital expansion programs are among high-volume ongoing healthcare glazing scopes in the country. St. Jude's multi-decade expansion plan alone represents hundreds of millions of dollars in construction work. Healthcare glazing requires specialized expertise — fire-rated assemblies, infection-control glass partitions, and entrance systems engineered for high-traffic clinical environments.

Knoxville's commercial construction is anchored by the University of Tennessee's ongoing campus growth and the surrounding healthcare district. The downtown Knoxville commercial revitalization is generating mixed-use, retail, and hospitality glazing scopes. Chattanooga's downtown North Shore redevelopment has transformed the city into one of the most active mid-size construction markets in Tennessee, with mixed-use, corporate, and hospitality glazing projects concentrated in the downtown core.

Tennessee building code requires thermally broken aluminum framing and high-performance glass on all new commercial construction — driven by the state's energy code requirements. ACG engineers every system to meet Tennessee's code standards and the specific local AHJ's plan review requirements, whether that's Nashville Metro, Shelby County, or Knox County.

ACG coordinates material supply and pre-construction consulting from Florida for Tennessee scopes. Our estimating, submittal management, procurement, and delivery and local-installer coordination teams manage Tennessee projects as efficiently as local Florida work — because the platform removes geographic constraints from project coordination.

What are Tennessee's commercial glazing code requirements? +

Tennessee follows the International Building Code with state amendments. Tennessee's energy code requires thermally broken aluminum framing and high-performance glass on all new commercial construction. Nashville and Memphis operate in standard wind zones — not coastal high-wind exposure. ACG engineers every system to meet Tennessee's code standards and the specific local AHJ plan review requirements.

Can ACG handle Nashville high-rise curtainwall projects? +

Yes. ACG has experience with high-rise curtainwall systems used in Nashville's SoBro and Gulch corridor. Our scheduling platform sequences curtainwall work to match multi-story construction schedules, and our submittal teams handle structural engineering coordination required for high-rise glazing systems. Nashville tower projects are welcome.
