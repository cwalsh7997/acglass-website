TGP (Technical Glass Products) — Florida Product Approvals & Miami-Dade NOAs

Reference table of TGP (Technical Glass Products) commercial glazing systems that ACG is authorized to install. Cells marked ‘pending verification’ have not been hand-confirmed against the manufacturer’s current portal listing — ACG verifies every approval on every project bid.

Portal note. Technical Glass Products is not listed in the Florida Product Approval portal under that name. TGP fire-rated glazing is regulated by IBC/NFPA test methods (UL 9, UL 10B, UL 263, NFPA 252, NFPA 257) rather than the Florida wind-load product-approval system. ACG verifies TGP listings against UL Online Certifications Directory and Intertek Directory of Listed Products for each project.
Verification policy. Every cell on this page that is not ‘pending verification’ has been hand-confirmed against the Florida Product Approval portal or Miami-Dade NOA portal on the ‘last verified’ date shown. Approval numbers change. For binding bid use, always cross-reference with the source portal before submittal. ACG does not certify the contents of this page for permit submission — we certify only the project-specific sealed engineering we deliver with each bid.
