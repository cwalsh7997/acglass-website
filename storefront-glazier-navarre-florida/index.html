Navarre commercial glazing questions, answered.

Is Navarre Beach in WBDR?

Yes. Coastal Santa Rosa County is full WBDR — 140 mph design wind, impact glazing required.

Does Santa Rosa County have its own permitting?

Yes. Santa Rosa County Development Services permits commercial work outside the City of Milton and Town of Gulf Breeze.

Lead time on Navarre commercial?

Standard 8–12 weeks for impact storefront.

Can you handle small-restaurant scope at Navarre Beach?
