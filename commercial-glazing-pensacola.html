The Pensacola Market

Military, Gulf Coast, and a
Booming Panhandle Market.

Pensacola is the gateway to Florida's Panhandle — a market unlike any other in the state. The military presence here is massive: Naval Air Station Pensacola is one of the Navy's largest installations, Eglin Air Force Base is the largest military installation in the world by area, and the broader DOD footprint drives sustained government and institutional construction that keeps the Escambia County market active regardless of economic cycles.

The Downtown Pensacola waterfront redevelopment has injected significant private-sector construction energy into the core — mixed-use, hospitality, retail, and office projects that require commercial storefront and curtainwall expertise. The Pensacola Medical District brings healthcare glazing demand. The Airport Business Park serves a growing light industrial and office market adjacent to Pensacola International.

Pensacola Beach and the Perdido Key corridor represent the hospitality and residential end of the Panhandle market — Gulf Coast tourism drives hotel, restaurant, and retail construction along US-98 that requires impact-rated glazing throughout. Gulf Breeze, Milton, Pace, and Navarre are expanding rapidly as Santa Rosa County's residential growth drives commercial development outpacing many inland Florida markets.

Coastal exposure is the defining code consideration for Pensacola glazing. Escambia and Santa Rosa counties sit on the Gulf Coast — impact-rated systems are required throughout. Wind design pressures are among the highest in the state. ACG engineers submittal packages specifically to Northwest Florida's wind zone requirements, with Florida product approvals and NOA documentation built for Escambia County plan review.

ACG's statewide reach extends to Northwest Florida via AI-managed logistics — delivering the same scope depth and 48-hour responsiveness that GCs across Florida rely on, without requiring a permanent Panhandle office.

Does ACG serve the Florida Panhandle and Northwest Florida? +

What impact-rated glazing requirements apply in Pensacola? +

Pensacola and Escambia County are in a coastal high-wind zone under Florida Building Code — impact-rated products are required throughout. Wind design pressures are among the highest in the state. ACG specifies ESWindows, CGI, and Eurowall impact systems engineered to the specific design wind pressure for each project, with Florida product approvals and NOA documentation built for Escambia and Santa Rosa County plan review.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Pensacola? +

ACG installs ESWindows, CGI, and Eurowall impact windows and doors; YKK AP commercial storefront; Eurowall curtainwall and window wall; Slimpact; TGP fire-rated glass assemblies; and Allegion automatic entrance hardware. All systems are specified to meet FBC wind requirements for Northwest Florida's coastal exposure zones — with verified product approvals for Pensacola-area wind design pressures.
