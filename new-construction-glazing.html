Preconstruction to Punchlist

Embedded in the Project From Day One

ACG's value on new construction projects starts long before installation. During preconstruction, we provide budgets, system recommendations, value engineering options, and constructability reviews that help the GC and owner make informed glazing decisions.

Once the project is awarded, ACG manages the full glazing scope: shop drawings, engineering, procurement, material staging, installation, quality inspections, and punchlist. One point of accountability from first budget to final walkthrough.

Full-Scope Capability

Every System. Every Building Type.

New construction projects require the widest range of glazing capability. A single project may include storefront, curtainwall, window wall, impact windows, automatic entrances, interior glass, and fire-rated assemblies. ACG provides all of these under one contract.

This full-scope capability means the GC has a single glazing partner who understands the entire building envelope — not five subcontractors who each know one piece.
