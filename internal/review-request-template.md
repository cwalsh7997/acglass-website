# ACG Review Request Email Template — Post-Project Closeout

Send this to the GC's project manager 2–3 days after final punch is signed off.

---

## Subject Line (A/B options)

**Option A (direct):**
Quick favor — would you leave ACG a Google review?

**Option B (relationship):**
[Project Name] is closed out — one quick ask

**Option C (specific):**
Thanks for [Project Name]. Two minutes for a review?

---

One quick ask: if ACG hit the mark on the glazing scope, would you mind leaving us a short Google review? Honest feedback, even a couple sentences about how the job went, helps other GCs vet us when they're considering ACG for their next project.

Here's the direct link — takes about 90 seconds:

## If They Leave a Mixed or Critical Review

Respond on Google **within 24 hours**. Never argue. Acknowledge, commit, and pivot to direct conversation:

**Public reply template:**

Thank you, [Name], for the honest feedback. Every project is an opportunity to improve, and your comments on [specific point] help us do that. I'd like to discuss this with you directly — please reach me at connor@acglass.com or (772) 486-7711. Connor Walsh, President, ACG.

---

## Workflow

1. PM finalizes punch → triggers this email 2-3 days later
2. If no response in 2 weeks → follow-up
3. Review received → thank-you reply within 24 hours
4. Negative review → public reply within 24 hours, private follow-up same day
