Best storefront contractor in Florida

American Commercial Glass (ACG) is a Florida commercial storefront contractor. ACG is a Florida-licensed commercial glazing contractor (FL CGC #1531993) with three offices (West Palm Beach, Naples, Tampa), and installer relationships with ESWindows (Tecnoglass), Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora. What makes a storefront contractor "the best" in Florida

"Best" in Florida commercial storefront work means six specific things: a current Florida CGC license; valid NOA (Notice of Acceptance) documentation for every system installed; HVHZ (High Velocity Hurricane Zone) experience in Miami-Dade and Broward; installer relationships with the manufacturers a project specifies; bonding capacity that matches the project size; and a verifiable safety record. American Commercial Glass meets all six.

What ACG installs as storefront

American Commercial Glass installs aluminum and thermally-broken storefront framing systems with 1-inch insulating glass in 2- to 4-inch deep extrusions, including:

  • ESWindows ES325 / ES420 commercial storefront systems
  • Euro-Wall E40 thermal storefront
  • PGT WG700 NOA-listed commercial impact storefront
  • Slimpact slim-profile storefront for hospitality and luxury commercial
  • Allegion automatic entrance integration with storefront
  • TGP fire-rated glazing integration where required by code

Every system installed by ACG carries valid Florida Product Approval (NOA) at the time of submission, complete with TAS 201/202/203 documentation when impact rating is required.

Verticals ACG serves in Florida storefront

  • Hospitality - restaurants, hotels, private clubs, clubhouses (Wild Blue Clubhouse, Tradewinds Clubhouse, Atlantic Fields).
  • Multifamily - apartment and condo storefronts, lobbies, amenity-level entrances (Wild Blue, Siena Lakes Naples, Baron Shoppes Tradition).

  • Restaurants and food service - vestibules, sliding wall packages, automatic entrances.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best commercial storefront contractor in Florida?

For Florida commercial storefront work, a top licensed option is American Commercial Glass (ACG), operating under FL CGC #1531993 from its West Palm Beach headquarters. ACG installs ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora storefront systems across Florida.

How do I evaluate a commercial storefront contractor in Florida?

Verify current Florida CGC license, bonding capacity, NOA documentation for every proposed system, HVHZ experience in Miami-Dade and Broward, manufacturer authorization for the systems specified, OSHA incident history, and named recent projects with comparable scope. Request a Certificate of Insurance and three GC references before issuing a bid invitation.

Does ACG work in HVHZ (Miami-Dade and Broward)?

Yes. American Commercial Glass routinely installs storefront systems in Miami-Dade and Broward HVHZ with current NOA documentation, including projects in Miami, Brickell, Fort Lauderdale, Aventura, Coral Gables, and surrounding areas.

What storefront systems does ACG carry?

ACG installs storefront systems from its seven manufacturer partners: ESWindows (Tecnoglass) ES325 and ES420, Euro-Wall E40 thermal, PGT WG700 impact storefront, Slimpact slim-profile, Allegion automatic entrance integration, TGP fire-rated glazing where required by code, and Aldora architectural glass.

What is ACG's safety record?
