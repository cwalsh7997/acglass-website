The Market

Palm Beach County is coastal. Your glass should be, too.

Palm Beach County runs 47 miles along the Atlantic. The entire county sits in Florida's wind-borne debris region, and every commercial building permit filed today requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems. If your building predates 2002, the windows may not be up to current code. If your windows were installed before 2010, they may not meet the tightened Florida Building Code 8th Edition requirements that followed Hurricane Irma.

ACG installs commercial-grade impact windows — not the residential systems you see advertised on the radio. Our primary product partner is ESWindows, whose 8000-series storefront, 9000-series window wall, and SGD-2020 sliding door systems are factory pre-glazed, large-missile tested, and Florida Building Code approved for every Palm Beach County jurisdiction.

We work directly with general contractors, owners, and property managers on projects ranging from single-suite retail retrofits to multi-story office towers and multifamily amenity buildings. Every installation is performed by ACG's own crews, documented to spec, and delivered with the FBC approvals and NOA documentation insurance carriers require.

FAQ

Palm Beach County impact windows — common questions.

Are Palm Beach County impact windows HVHZ rated?

Palm Beach County is not part of the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) — that designation only covers Miami-Dade and Broward. However, all commercial impact windows in Palm Beach County must meet Florida Building Code wind load requirements and ASTM E1886/E1996 missile impact testing. Many ACG projects opt for HVHZ-rated systems anyway for added protection.

How much do commercial impact windows cost in Palm Beach County?

Commercial impact window costs typically range from $65 to $180 per square foot installed, depending on system type (storefront, window wall, curtainwall), glass makeup, and opening size. A small storefront retrofit ranges $55,000–$110,000; a mid-size retail rollout ranges $170,000–$360,000.

How long does impact window installation take?

A single-suite retail storefront typically installs in 2–3 weeks once material lands on site. Larger projects like multi-story offices or multifamily buildings can take 8–16 weeks depending on size, phasing, and occupancy constraints.

Can ACG install impact windows on occupied commercial buildings?

Yes. ACG regularly installs commercial impact windows on occupied buildings throughout Palm Beach County. We coordinate scheduling, temporary weather protection, and phased installation to minimize tenant disruption.

Do Palm Beach County impact windows qualify for insurance discounts?

Yes. Most Florida commercial property insurance carriers offer meaningful premium discounts for buildings with code-compliant impact windows — typically 10–30% off windstorm premiums. ACG provides the FBC approval numbers and installation certifications insurance carriers require.
