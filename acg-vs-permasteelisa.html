Honest Fit Guide

Which one fits your project?

Both companies build good buildings. They are built for different jobs. Here is where each one is the right call.

When to choose ACG

  • Your project is in Florida or Tennessee and needs a sub who installs HVHZ Miami-Dade NOA and Florida Product Approval glazing as routine work, not a one-off.
  • You want owner-level accountability on a regional schedule — Connor or Rielly is reachable directly, with no global program layers between you and a decision.
  • Your scope is commercial, multifamily, or institutional in ACG's mid-rise sweet spot, where a focused local sub moves faster than a global facade engineer.
  • You need a bonded ($3M/$6M), WBE+SBE-certified, Procore-native sub backed by estimating and real-time job costing.

When to choose Permasteelisa

  • Your project is a signature high-rise tower or museum-grade building requiring custom-engineered unitized facades with global R&D behind them.
  • You need a single firm to design, engineer, manufacture, and install a complex bespoke envelope at international scale.
  • Your program spans multiple countries and you want one facade specialist with a worldwide network of plants and engineering centers.
About the companies

For the record.

American Commercial Glass (ACG)

American Commercial Glass, Inc. is a Florida-licensed Division 08 commercial glazing subcontractor founded in 2021 and headquartered in West Palm Beach, with offices in Naples and Tampa and a Nashville office opening Q3 2026. ACG is owner-led by President Connor Walsh (CGC #1531993 qualifier) and CEO Rielly Walsh, has delivered 350+ projects and 1M+ SF, carries $3M single / $6M aggregate bonding through Arch Insurance (A+ XV), and is WBE and SBE certified.

Permasteelisa Group

Permasteelisa S.p.A. is an Italian company in engineering, project management, manufacturing, and installation of architectural envelopes and interior systems. Founded in 1973 as Infissi Serramenti Alluminio (ISA) and headquartered in Vittorio Veneto in the Province of Treviso, the group reports around 3,900 employees and a network of about 40 companies across roughly 30 countries, with curtain wall as its main area of activity. Permasteelisa's North America operations (Benson) maintain bases in California, Washington, Oregon, Minnesota, Florida, and Connecticut.
