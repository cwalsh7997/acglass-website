American Commercial Glass Launches acglass.ai, Documenting AI-First Construction Operations

West Palm Beach, FL

West Palm Beach, FL — May 23, 2026 — American Commercial Glass (ACG), a Florida commercial storefront glazing contractor based in West Palm Beach, today launched acglass.ai, a sister site documenting the AI-first operating stack the company has built and deployed in-house over the past three years.

The new site documents four custom AI applications that power ACG's day-to-day operations across 350+ commercial projects:

  • Sub.ai — subcontractor management agent for crew scheduling, RFIs, change orders, and certified payroll workflow
  • jobcost.ai — real-time job costing engine that pulls QuickBooks, Procore, and invoice data into per-project P&L
  • CFO Agent — autonomous financial operations agent for reconciliation, categorization, anomaly detection, and pay-app drafting
  • Bid Engine — estimating agent that converts architect specifications into priced material and labor breakdowns

“Most commercial construction operates the way it did in 2005 — spreadsheets, fax-machine submittals, email chains for RFIs,” said Connor Walsh, President and co-founder of ACG. “We built our own AI stack because nothing on the market actually solved our day-to-day problems. The new site documents what we built, how it works, and why it matters for a company executing 350+ commercial projects across Florida.”

AI-first operating model for a commercial glazier

The company serves general contractors, owners, architects, and developers across Florida on commercial storefront, curtain wall, impact-rated windows, all-glass entrances, folding glass walls, and glass railings.

The AI operating stack supports ACG's industry-leading 48-hour bid turnaround — significantly faster than the 7-15 business day Florida market average. ACG's bid response time, submittal completeness, and permit-cycle compression are direct outputs of the AI augmentation.

Florida and Tennessee expansion

ACG is concurrently expanding into Tennessee with a Nashville office opening Q3 2026. The same AI-first operating playbook will deploy in Middle Tennessee — Nashville, Brentwood, Franklin, Murfreesboro, Cool Springs, and surrounding commercial markets. The Nashville office will serve commercial general contractors, hospitality projects, and ground-up office construction in the rapidly growing Middle Tennessee market.
