AI-Managed Operations in Commercial Glazing

How a Florida contractor runs bid-to-closeout on custom agents — what we built, what failed, and what GCs can expect from an AI-managed glazier.

By Connor Walsh, President & Co-founder · American Commercial Glass · 12 pages

Download PDF (12 pages) Talk to Connor
Executive Summary

Glazing is the second-most-delayed trade. Most glazers run on email. We don't.

Commercial glazing carries some of the longest lead times on a jobsite — custom fabrication, manufacturer dependencies, owner-direct versus GC ambiguity. Most glazing subs still run their submittal log, RFI tracker, and cost-to-complete out of spreadsheets and inboxes. ACG runs four custom AI agents in production that touch every job we take on.

"We're not an AI company that happens to install glass. We're a glazing contractor that built the tools nobody else would build for us."
— Connor Walsh, President

This white paper documents what we built, where it works, where it failed, and what general contractors and owners should expect when they hire an AI-managed glazier. It is written for GCs, owners, architects, and the construction-tech reporters covering this shift.

The Stack

Four agents. One glazing company.

/ 01

Sub.ai

Subcontractor coordination and bid management. Five-lens priority engine, nightly re-score, automated bid-tracking, manufacturer-aware lead-time enforcement.

/ 02

jobcost.ai

Real-time job costing and margin tracking. Pulls from QuickBooks + Procore, surfaces variance the moment a PO drifts from the estimate.

/ 03

CFO Agent

Autonomous CFO assistant. AP/AR triage, cash-runway forecasting, Florida 90-day Notice-to-Owner enforcement, anomaly detection.

ESWindows Dealer Portal

Manufacturer-integrated ordering. We built this when we got tired of waiting. Reduces delivery reschedules and storage-fee exposure.
