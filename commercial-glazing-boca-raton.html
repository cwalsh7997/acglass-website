Commercial Glazing Services in Boca Raton

Impact Storefront Systems

FBC-approved impact storefront for Boca Raton's upscale retail and commercial properties. ES-8000 and ES-9000 systems with laminated impact glass meeting coastal Palm Beach County wind load requirements up to 175 mph.

Curtainwall & Window Wall

High-performance curtainwall and window wall systems for Boca Raton's luxury high-rises and mixed-use developments. Engineered for HVHZ coastal wind loads with salt-air corrosion-resistant finishes.

Sliding & Folding Glass Walls

Impact Windows

Fire-Rated Glazing

Automatic Entrances

Allegion and Stanley automatic entrance systems for Boca Raton's corporate campuses, medical facilities, and luxury retail. Sliding, swinging, and revolving door systems with ADA compliance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG have corporate office park experience in Boca Raton?

Yes. ACG provides comprehensive glazing for Boca Raton office parks and corporate headquarters, including multi-building installations, tenant improvement coordination, and Class A finishes.

What impact requirements apply to Boca Raton commercial projects?

Boca Raton is outside the HVHZ but still requires impact-rated glazing for certain building heights and locations per the Florida Building Code. ACG provides code-compliant systems for all Boca Raton applications.

Can ACG handle luxury retail and hospitality projects?

Yes. ACG specializes in premium retail and hospitality glazing, including luxury material selection, custom finishes, and precise detail coordination for high-end projects.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Boca Raton?

ACG installs impact storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals and meet Palm Beach County impact requirements.

What is the typical timeline for a Boca Raton commercial glazing project?

Timelines vary by scope. Palm Beach County permitting typically takes 2-4 weeks for commercial glazing. Standard storefront projects run 6-10 weeks from approved submittals; curtainwall and window wall systems require 12-16 weeks. ACG provides detailed schedules during pre-construction.

Does ACG offer value engineering for Boca Raton projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering during pre-construction to optimize glazing specifications for Palm Beach County's code requirements and luxury market standards. This includes system alternatives that meet performance requirements at competitive pricing, energy code optimization, and coordination with architects.
