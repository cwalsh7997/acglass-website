Boca Raton Market

One of South Florida's Most
Active Commercial Markets.

Boca Raton punches above its size in Florida commercial real estate. The Boca Raton Innovation Campus (BRIC) has redefined what a technology and life sciences campus looks like in South Florida - and the tenant fit-outs and commercial support construction surrounding it keep the glazing pipeline full. The Glades Road corridor runs high-volume retail and mixed-use at a pace that rivals any submarket in the state.

Palmetto Park Road's commercial redevelopment, the massive Town Center at Boca Raton corridor, and the Downtown Boca push toward mixed-use density all represent multi-year construction pipelines that require reliable glazing subs with HVHZ credentials and proven submittal execution. Corporate campus construction - ADT, Modernizing Medicine, Bluegreen Vacations, and others - adds a steady flow of high-spec commercial work.

Boca Raton is not in the HVHZ. That designation is Miami-Dade and Broward only. Coastal Boca Raton is a wind-borne debris region, so Florida Product Approval is the code path. ACG's West Palm Beach team manages the full documentation stack for Palm Beach County plan review.

ACG's West Palm Beach HQ is 35 miles north - a straight shot down I-95. We are not an out-of-market sub making occasional trips south. We serve Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Deerfield Beach, and the full South Palm Beach County construction market as a matter of routine.

Common Questions

Boca Raton Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG install impact glazing in Boca Raton? +

Yes. ACG installs impact glazing throughout South Palm Beach County. Boca Raton is not in the HVHZ. Coastal Boca Raton is a wind-borne debris region, so Florida Product Approval is the code path. ACG specifies and installs HVHZ-compliant systems and submits complete NOA packages with shop drawings - built to pass Palm Beach County plan review without revisions. We handle HVHZ compliance every week.

How far is ACG's West Palm Beach office from Boca Raton? +

ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters is approximately 35 miles north of Boca Raton - 30 to 40 minutes via I-95 or the Florida Turnpike. ACG serves Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Deerfield Beach, Boynton Beach, and Coconut Creek as a matter of routine - not as occasional trips from a distant office. We're Palm Beach County. Boca Raton is part of our primary market.

How fast can ACG turn around a scope for a Boca Raton project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope - system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown - within 48 hours. ACG's estimating process eliminates the manual bottlenecks that cause delays at other glazing subs. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions, no three-week wait. Send plans Monday, have a complete scope Wednesday.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Boca Raton? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. For Boca Raton projects, ACG specifies systems with Florida Product Approval. Miami-Dade NOA documentation is used when the specification calls for it. Recommendations are driven by the project spec and architect's design intent - not by what's most convenient or lowest-priced to stock.

Does ACG handle shop drawings and submittals for Boca Raton commercial glazing? +

Yes. ACG manages the complete submittal package - shop drawings, Florida product approvals, NOA documentation, and engineer-of-record coordination. Our submittal packages for Palm Beach County are built to the building department's standards and submitted complete - minimizing revisions and back-and-forth that cost your schedule. We also track submittals proactively so your GC team isn't chasing us for status updates.
