The Market

Lakewood Ranch is a commercial glazing market.

Lakewood Ranch is America's #1 best-selling master-planned community according to RCLCO for eight consecutive years. Spanning 33,000+ acres across Manatee and Sarasota Counties, it generates more new construction than any single community in Florida. The commercial build-out - retail villages, medical campuses, office parks, hospitality, and thousands of multifamily units - requires a commercial glazing subcontractor with the capacity and product depth to deliver at scale.

The Lakewood Ranch commercial district includes Waterside Place, Main Street at Lakewood Ranch, University Town Center (UTC), the Lakewood Ranch Medical Center campus, and the Lakewood Ranch Corporate Park. Dozens of new mixed-use and multifamily developments are currently active or in pre-construction. Every commercial permit requires Florida Building Code compliance with wind-load design for Exposure Category C, and select buildings fall in Exposure D.

ACG is the commercial glazing subcontractor Lakewood Ranch general contractors call when they need a Florida-licensed (CGC #1531993), ESWindows-flagship, commercially-focused glazing sub that provides performance and payment bonds on projects that require them. We serve Lakewood Ranch from our Tampa office (45 miles north) with the same estimating, project management, and crew depth as our South Florida headquarters.

Lakewood Ranch FAQ

Common questions - answered.

Does Lakewood Ranch require impact windows on new commercial construction?

Yes. Lakewood Ranch spans Manatee and Sarasota Counties and both fall within Florida's wind-borne debris region. The Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening. Most GCs and developers in Lakewood Ranch opt for full impact glazing rather than shutters for aesthetic and maintenance reasons.

What commercial projects has ACG delivered near Lakewood Ranch?

ACG has delivered glazing on multiple Florida master-planned community projects including Wild Blue Clubhouse (Naples), Atlantic Fields Performance Center (Hobe Sound), and Tradewinds Hobe Sound. These are the same community types found throughout Lakewood Ranch.

Does ACG have a Tampa Bay area office?

Yes. ACG operates: West Palm Beach (HQ), Naples, and Tampa. The Tampa office serves Lakewood Ranch (45 miles south) with same-day site visit capability, pre-construction meetings, and daily coordination on active projects.

What is ACG's bonding capacity for Lakewood Ranch projects?

ACG's bond capacity letters are available on request. This is sufficient for the vast majority of Lakewood Ranch commercial and multifamily projects, including amenity buildings, mid-size retail rollouts, medical office, and multifamily phases.

What makes ACG different from a Lakewood Ranch local glazing contractor?

ACG is commercial-only (no residential), ESWindows flagship partner (pre-glazed storefront capability), Procore-active with VIP GCs (DeAngelis Diamond, Proctor, Curran Young, Rycon), and Florida CGC-licensed (full general contractor license, not just specialty). That level of commercial infrastructure is uncommon for Gulf Coast glazing contractors at any scale.
