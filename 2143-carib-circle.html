The Project

2143 Carib Circle is a luxury custom residence built by High Tide Properties — a modern waterfront home designed around glass. Floor-to-ceiling impact windows, oversized multi-slide patio doors, and a glass-wrapped second floor that opens the entire living space to the pool deck and waterway beyond.

High Tide needed a glazing contractor who could execute the full ES Prestige window and door package without delays, rework, or coordination headaches. ACG delivered.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the complete ES Prestige impact-rated window and door system — including the massive multi-panel sliding glass doors on the ground floor, fixed picture windows, casement and awning windows throughout, and the frameless glass shower enclosures.

Every opening in this home is impact-rated. Every unit was coordinated with the structural openings, verified against shop drawings, and installed to manufacturer specification.

The Result

Clean sightlines from every room. Hurricane protection on every opening. A home that looks like it belongs in an architecture magazine — because the glass was installed by a team that treats residential work with the same discipline we bring to commercial high-rises.

The glazing package on 2143 Carib Circle included luxury residential envelope. ACG specified, supplied, and installed Euro-Wall multi-slide, PGT impact — manufactured by ACG's seven approved partner lines: ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.

The scope was sequenced to ownership's field schedule, with weekly coordination meetings, RFI turn-around inside 24 hours, and submittals tracked against the construction critical path. ACG's three-office Florida footprint (West Palm Beach HQ, Naples, Tampa) keeps a project supervisor within driving distance of every active job site, and the Singer Island location pulled supervision out of our West Palm Beach office.
