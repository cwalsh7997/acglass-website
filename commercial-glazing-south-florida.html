South Florida HVHZ

The Most Demanding
Glazing Market in the Country.

Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties — Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone — represent the most stringent commercial glazing environment in the United States. Large missile impact testing, FBC Section 1609 wind design, and local code amendments create a compliance landscape that trips up subs who don't live in it.

ACG was born in this market. West Palm Beach is our headquarters. We've submitted hundreds of HVHZ glazing packages across the tri-county region. We know what passes first review and what doesn't — and we engineer our submittals accordingly.

From Miami's Brickell corridor to Fort Lauderdale's downtown towers to Palm Beach's luxury estates and commercial facilities — ACG is South Florida's glazing sub of record for GCs who can't afford schedule failures.
