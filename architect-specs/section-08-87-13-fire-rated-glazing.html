Fire-Rated Glazing

Fire-rated glazing assemblies (UL 9, UL 10B, UL 263) for 20-, 45-, 60-, 90-, and 120-minute ratings in commercial Florida construction.

Download .txt All Spec Sections Copied to clipboard 
PART 1 GENERAL

1.1 SUMMARY
A. Section includes fire-rated glazing assemblies for openings requiring 20-, 45-, 60-, 90-, or 120-minute fire ratings.

1.2 REFERENCES
A. UL 9 — Fire Tests of Window Assemblies.
B. UL 10B / UL 10C — Fire Tests of Door Assemblies.
C. UL 263 — Fire Tests of Building Construction and Materials.
D. NFPA 252, NFPA 257.
E. ASTM E2010, E2074.
F. ANSI Z97.1.
G. International Building Code (IBC) and Florida Building Code 8th Edition.

1.3 PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
A. Fire-Resistance Rating: As shown on Drawings; verified by current UL listing.
B. Hose-Stream Test: Pass per UL 9 / UL 10B as required by rating.
C. Safety Glazing: ANSI Z97.1 / 16 CFR 1201 Category II.

1.4 SUBMITTALS
A. Product Data, UL Listing, Shop Drawings, Samples, Installer Certification.

PART 2 PRODUCTS

2.1 MANUFACTURERS
A. Technical Glass Products (TGP) — basis of design (FireLite, Pilkington Pyrostop, FireFrames).
B. Slimpact — impact-rated steel framing with fire glazing where impact + fire required.
C. Other manufacturers with current UL listing matching project requirements, approved prior to bid.

2.2 GLAZING
A. 20-Minute Rating: TGP FireLite Plus or equivalent ceramic glazing.
B. 45- to 120-Minute Rating: Pilkington Pyrostop or equivalent multi-layer intumescent glazing.
C. Frame: Stainless steel or galvanized steel hollow-metal, fabricated to match UL listing.

PART 3 EXECUTION

3.1 INSTALLATION
A. Install per UL listing and manufacturer instructions. Substitution of any component voids the rating.
B. Use only listed glazing tape, sealants, and gaskets.

3.2 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
A. Maintain UL labels visible on each lite of glazing and each frame.

3.3 WARRANTY
A. Manufacturer's standard warranty on glazing and frame.
