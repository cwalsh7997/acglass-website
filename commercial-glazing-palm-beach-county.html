Impact Storefront Systems

Curtainwall & Window Wall

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ACG provide county-wide service across all Palm Beach County?

Yes. ACG operates from our West Palm Beach headquarters and provides rapid response, unified project management, and coordinated delivery across all Palm Beach County markets.

How does ACG coordinate multi-site projects across Palm Beach County?

ACG manages supply coordination, delivery logistics, and installation scheduling across multiple Palm Beach County sites. We maintain unified project management and quality standards county-wide.

What's the typical response time for Palm Beach County bids?

ACG typically provides preliminary budgets within 24-48 hours and detailed bids within 3-5 business days for Palm Beach County commercial projects, with rapid turnaround available for expedited requests.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Palm Beach County?

ACG installs impact storefront (ES-8000/ES-9000), curtainwall, window wall, sliding and folding glass walls (Eurowall MultiSlide, VistaFold), fire-rated glazing (TGP/SAFTI), and automatic entrances (Allegion/Stanley). All systems carry Florida Product Approvals meeting Palm Beach County's HVHZ and WBDR requirements.

What is the typical timeline for a Palm Beach County commercial glazing project?

Timelines vary by scope and location within the county. Permitting typically takes 2-4 weeks. Standard storefront projects run 6-10 weeks from approved submittals; curtainwall and window wall systems require 12-16 weeks. ACG provides detailed schedules during pre-construction.

What are the code differences between coastal and inland Palm Beach County?

Coastal Palm Beach County (east of I-95) falls under HVHZ-equivalent requirements with large missile impact testing and 175 mph design winds. Inland areas are in the Wind-Borne Debris Region with small missile testing and 150 mph winds. ACG engineers systems for both zones.

Does ACG offer value engineering for Palm Beach County projects?

Yes. ACG provides value engineering to optimize glazing specifications for Palm Beach County's dual-zone code requirements. This includes system alternatives that meet performance requirements at competitive pricing, energy code optimization, and coordination with architects to maintain design intent.
