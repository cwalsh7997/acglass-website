Palm Beach County Is Where
ACG Was Built.

Palm Beach County is home to ACG's West Palm Beach headquarters — the company's founding market with more than 5 years of continuous project history. No other glazing sub in Florida has a deeper concentration of completed commercial projects in Palm Beach County than ACG. That depth of local knowledge isn't marketing — it's reflected in the time it takes to get a submittal through plan review, the relationships with local inspectors, and the accuracy of our scope estimates from day one.

The Palm Beach County commercial market spans an extraordinary range of project types and regulatory environments. The coastal corridor — from Boca Raton's barrier island north through Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Lake Worth, WPB, Palm Beach, North Palm Beach, Juno Beach, and Jupiter Island — falls within the High Velocity Hurricane Zone. HVHZ requires specific product approvals, NOA documentation, and third-party test reports that most out-of-county subs do not have current relationships to assemble properly.

The inland markets — the Florida's Turnpike corridor, the western communities of Wellington and Royal Palm Beach, the Congress Avenue business district, the i-95 commercial strip — follow standard Florida product approval requirements, but the pace and competition of these markets demands the 48-hour scope turnaround that ACG's AI-managed operations enable.

From the Downtown WPB and CityPlace redevelopment to Palm Beach Gardens' Medical City, from Boca Raton's Innovation Campus to Jupiter's Abacoa — ACG has installed glazing in every major submarket in this county.

Palm Beach County Glazing FAQ.

Where is ACG's headquarters in Palm Beach County? +

ACG is headquartered in West Palm Beach — the company's founding market. The WPB office has served as ACG's operational base since the company was founded in 2021, and it serves as the hub for all Palm Beach County commercial glazing operations, from Boca Raton in the south to Jupiter and the Treasure Coast gateway in the north. This is not a regional expansion — it's where ACG was built.

Does ACG hold HVHZ certification for Palm Beach County coastal projects? +

Yes. ACG is HVHZ-certified with full NOA documentation capability. The HVHZ covers coastal zones throughout Palm Beach County — from Boca Raton's barrier island north through Delray Beach, Lake Worth, WPB, Palm Beach, North Palm Beach, Juno Beach, and Jupiter Island. Our estimating team identifies the applicable wind zone for every project and assembles the correct submittal package from the start — no reactive corrections at plan review.

What is ACG's project track record in Palm Beach County? +

Palm Beach County is ACG's founding market with 5+ years of continuous project history — the densest concentration of completed commercial glazing projects in any ACG market. The portfolio spans Downtown WPB and CityPlace, the WPB Medical District, Palm Beach Gardens' Corporate Center and Medical City, Boca Raton's Innovation Campus, Jupiter's Abacoa, Delray Beach Atlantic Ave, and Boynton Beach's healthcare corridor.

What areas of Palm Beach County does ACG cover? +

ACG's WPB headquarters covers the full Palm Beach County market: West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Tequesta, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Lake Worth, Royal Palm Beach, Wellington, Greenacres, and all points between. The coastal corridor from Boca to Jupiter falls within HVHZ zones; inland communities follow standard Florida product approval requirements. One sub for the entire county.

How quickly can ACG turn around a scope for Palm Beach County? +

Send us your plans and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions. This is ACG's home market; we don't need time to research the area, the building department, or the code requirements. The scope comes back fast because we already know the answers.
