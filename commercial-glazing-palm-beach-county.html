Palm Beach County HQ

The Glazing Sub That
Calls Palm Beach Home.

ACG's headquarters is in West Palm Beach. That makes Palm Beach County our primary market — and our deepest one. We know every building official, understand every local code amendment, and have project history across every submarket from the western agricultural communities to the oceanfront enclaves.

Palm Beach County's HVHZ designation requires the most stringent impact glazing in the country. Our systems are tested, certified, and installed to those requirements — and our submittal packages are thorough enough to pass without revision cycles that derail schedules.

From the massive Quantum Park commercial development in Boynton Beach to the luxury residential towers along Flagler Drive in West Palm Beach to the Palm Beach Gardens commercial corridor to the master-planned communities in Wellington — ACG has built across every Palm Beach County market.
