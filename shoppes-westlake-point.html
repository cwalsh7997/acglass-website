Shoppes at Westlake Point

A new multi-tenant retail center in Westlake — one of Palm Beach County's fastest-growing master-planned communities. ACG delivered the complete ESWindows storefront glazing package for Sisca Construction.

Location Westlake, FL GC Sisca Construction Systems ESWindows storefront Completed 2025

Sheet A-011 · The project

Every bay says the same thing: come in.

Shoppes at Westlake Point brings service, dining, and retail tenants to a rapidly expanding residential population near Wellington and Loxahatchee that's been waiting for walkable commercial space. Every bay in a retail strip tells the same story through the glass — and the storefront systems have to be clean, consistent across every tenant space, and built to handle South Florida's sun and storm exposure without showing their age a year later.

ACG delivered the complete storefront glazing package — ESWindows storefront framing, entrance doors, and all glass across every tenant bay. Multi-tenant retail means every opening has to match the next one while accommodating different tenant configurations: some with full-height glass, others with awning overhangs and outdoor seating areas. ESWindows storefront delivers the clean aluminum sightlines and impact performance retail developers in Palm Beach County expect, and ACG managed the full scope from submittals through punch list.

Built for Sisca Construction — a commercial general contractor with a strong portfolio of retail, restaurant, and mixed-use projects across South Florida. Sisca builds at the pace retail developers need, and ACG matched that schedule. The project joins Baron Shoppes of Tradition in ACG's growing portfolio of retail strip and multi-tenant storefront work — from single-tenant buildouts to full-center glazing packages.
