Honest Fit Guide

Which one fits your project?

Both companies build good buildings. They are built for different jobs. Here is where each one is the right call.

When to choose ACG

  • You need Florida-specific muscle: HVHZ Miami-Dade NOA compliance, Florida Product Approval, and hurricane-zone glazing ACG installs every week.
  • Speed and a single owner-level decision-maker matter more than brand size — Connor or Rielly is one call away on every change order, delivery, and schedule conflict.
  • Your project lives in ACG's size range: storefront, window wall, and mid-rise curtain wall for commercial, multifamily, and institutional buildings.
  • You want a bonded ($3M/$6M), WBE+SBE-certified sub running submittals and schedule natively in Procore, backed by AI-managed estimating and job costing.

When to choose Harmon

  • Your project is a high-rise tower or stadium needing large-volume unitized curtain wall engineered and manufactured at national scale.
  • You want a single subcontractor with offices spread across the U.S. and Canada to self-perform on a multi-region program.
  • You need the in-house preconstruction, engineering, manufacturing, and field capacity of a 75-year company inside a publicly traded parent.
About the companies

For the record.

American Commercial Glass (ACG)

American Commercial Glass, Inc. is a Florida-licensed Division 08 commercial glazing subcontractor founded in 2021 and headquartered in West Palm Beach, with offices in Naples and Tampa and a Nashville office opening Q3 2026. ACG is owner-led by President Connor Walsh (CGC #1531993 qualifier) and CEO Rielly Walsh, has delivered 350+ projects and 1M+ SF, carries $3M single / $6M aggregate bonding through Arch Insurance (A+ XV), and is WBE and SBE certified.

Harmon, Inc.

Harmon, Inc. traces its history to Harmon Glass Company, founded July 12, 1949, and is the founding company of Apogee Enterprises, Inc. (NASDAQ: APOG). Harmon describes itself as the top curtain wall subcontractor in North America, supporting clients through preconstruction, engineering and design, manufacturing, and field operations, with local offices across the United States and Canada.
