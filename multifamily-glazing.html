FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What glazing systems does ACG install on multifamily projects?

ACG installs window wall, impact windows, sliding glass doors, storefront entries, interior partitions, and automatic entrances. We provide single-source glazing packages that cover the full building envelope.

Does ACG work with multifamily developers directly?

ACG works primarily with general contractors on multifamily projects but also engages directly with developers during preconstruction for budgeting, system selection, and value engineering.

Can ACG handle multiple buildings on the same project?

Yes. ACG regularly manages multi-building multifamily projects with phased installation sequences. Our project management and field teams are structured to handle the logistics of large-scale developments.
