Multifamily Glazing

On a 200-unit tower, glazing is
a schedule problem first.

Multifamily glazing is schedule-sensitive in a way other sectors aren't — there are 200 units to close out, and every week of delay costs the developer carrying costs on a fully constructed building that can't be occupied.

ACG approaches multifamily glazing with the sequencing discipline it demands. We coordinate our installation schedule with your framing and waterproofing trades from day one. We sequence unit delivery to align with your punch list and TCO schedule. We don't leave 40 units with glazing punch items when the project is trying to close.

For South Florida multifamily, every system we install is HVHZ-rated and Miami-Dade NOA compliant. ESWindows and Slimpact impact window wall systems for the tower floors. Impact sliding doors for balconies. Impact storefronts for the amenity levels.

Why sequencing matters

Glazing sits on the critical path to your TCO.
