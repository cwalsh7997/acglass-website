About ACG

Built in Florida. Scaling nationally.

Built Because GCs
Deserved Better.

ACG was founded after watching too many glazing subs fail the contractors who trusted them. Missed shop drawing deadlines. Material that showed up wrong. Crews that no-showed on install day. Change orders for scope that was clearly in the drawings. We've seen every version of it — and we built ACG to be the opposite.

Every project gets a dedicated project manager who actually answers the phone. Our leadership walks jobsites personally. We submit shop drawings when we say we will. We coordinate with your waterproofing, framing, and stucco trades proactively — not after you've sent us three emails asking for an update.

The result? GCs who use us once, use us on every project after. Developers who request us by name. And a track record of 500+ completed projects, 50M+ SF of glass installed, with repeat clients who trust us to protect their schedules, budgets, and reputations.

What GCs Get From ACG

The Problems We Solve

01

Your Schedule Is Sacred

Your glazing sub blows a deadline and suddenly your drywall, paint, and flooring trades are stacked up waiting. We commit to dates and hit them — because we know every day we're late costs you money and credibility with your owner.

02

No Scope Gaps. No Surprises.

We review drawings thoroughly before we bid. If there's a conflict between architectural and structural, we flag it during buyout — not during install when it's a $200K change order. Our submittals are complete, our shop drawings are accurate, and our pricing covers what it says it covers.

03

Communication That Actually Works

You shouldn't have to chase your glazing sub for a status update. Our PMs send weekly progress reports, attend your OAC meetings prepared, and respond to RFIs within 24 hours. When there's a problem, we tell you first — with a solution attached.
