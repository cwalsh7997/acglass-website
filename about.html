The Company

Built Because GCs Deserved
a Glazing Sub That Actually Performed.

ACG was founded in South Florida on a straightforward premise: general contractors deserved a glazing subcontractor that operated with the same level of professionalism they brought to the job. Not a residential glass shop that occasionally takes commercial work. Not a sub that submits shop drawings when they get around to it and shows up to install when the schedule still works for them. A contractor, in the real sense of the word.

The commercial construction market in South Florida is demanding. Permitting is rigorous. Wind codes are among the strictest in the country. Building departments know the difference between a submittal that's been assembled by someone who understands the code and one that was thrown together to get through plan review. GCs working in this market have been burned enough times to know that the lowest-priced glazing bid is rarely the lowest-priced glazing scope when the job is done.

ACG grew from a single West Palm Beach office into a three-office Florida operation over the past 5+ years, delivering 350+ commercial projects across multifamily, hospitality, healthcare, retail, office, and institutional sectors. That growth happened because GCs who used ACG once came back for the next project — and the one after that. Read more about how we work with general contractors.

We're still a lean operation. No sprawling corporate structure. No accounts that get handed off to a junior rep after the contract is signed. The same people who bid your project are the people who deliver it.

What Makes ACG Different

AI-Managed. Lean-Operated.
No Middlemen.

ACG uses AI-managed project operations — and it's worth being specific about what that means, because "AI" gets attached to a lot of things that don't deserve the label.

ACG's AI-managed system is a project management infrastructure that tracks every active job in real time across all three Florida offices. It isn't a chatbot. It isn't a marketing claim. It's the architecture that makes it possible for a lean team to manage a high volume of concurrent commercial projects without things falling through the cracks.

Real-Time Submittal Tracking

Every submittal is logged, tracked, and flagged when it's at risk of missing a deadline. Shop drawing schedules, AHJ review windows, material approval dependencies — all monitored without relying on a coordinator's memory.

Proactive Schedule Coordination

The system generates alerts when installation windows are approaching and preconditions haven't been confirmed. Your superintendent gets contacted by ACG before there's a problem — not after one has developed.

Material Procurement Tied to Approvals

Orders are placed against approved shop drawings, not assumed lead times. The system tracks material status from order to delivery and triggers proactive notification if a manufacturer's schedule changes.

Closeout Documentation Management

As-built drawings, FL Product Approval certificates, warranty documents, and lien waivers are tracked and assembled automatically as milestones are completed — not assembled at closeout from a pile of loose files.

Lean Management Model

ACG runs a flat management structure. When a project is awarded, a single project coordinator is assigned — and that person is your point of contact for shop drawings, submittals, material status, scheduling, and closeout. You're not calling a general office line and hoping someone picks up who knows your job.

There's no account manager who hands off to a project manager who hands off to a field supervisor who hands off to a crew chief. The coordinator knows the job because they've been on it from kickoff. When you have a question, you call the person who has the answer.

The Team

Professional. Responsive.
Accountable.

ACG's team is built around a simple hiring standard: people who understand commercial construction, communicate proactively, and take ownership of the results. That's harder to find than it sounds in a trade where the default is to wait for the GC to ask before providing an update.

Project coordinators at ACG come from construction backgrounds — they've been on jobsites, they understand the language of a master schedule, they know what it means when your super says the openings won't be ready until next Tuesday. They're not order-processors reading from a script. They're construction people managing a scope.

Field crews are experienced commercial glaziers — not residential installers making a jump to commercial work. There's a meaningful difference between installing a residential window and installing a commercial curtainwall system to engineered shop drawings on a building with an occupied lower floor. ACG's crews understand that difference and are selected accordingly.

The culture ACG has built is straightforward: when something goes wrong — and in construction, something always eventually goes wrong — you tell the GC immediately, with a solution. Not after you've tried to fix it quietly and made it worse. Not after three emails asking for a status update. Immediately, in writing, with a path forward. GCs who've worked with ACG consistently mention this as the thing that separates us from the other glazing subs they've used.

Three Florida Offices

Where ACG Operates.

Having local offices matters in commercial glazing. Local field crews, local supply relationships, and direct familiarity with local building departments make a meaningful difference in how smoothly a project runs.

Headquarters — Founded Here
West Palm Beach, FL

ACG's home base and founding market. South Florida's construction market is among the most technically demanding in the country — high wind speeds, complex permit processes, HVHZ compliance in southern areas, and a sophisticated GC community that expects genuine professionalism from their subs. ACG built its processes and standards here, and it shows in the work.

From West Palm Beach, ACG serves Palm Beach County, Broward County, Miami-Dade County, and the Treasure Coast — from Jupiter to Homestead and across the I-95 corridor. ACG also operates as a national commercial glazing contractor for GC partners who build outside Florida.

Southwest Florida Coverage
Naples, FL

Southwest Florida is a different market than South Florida — a mix of high-end residential-scale commercial, healthcare, hospitality, and the explosive growth along Collier, Lee, and Charlotte counties. The Naples office positions ACG to serve this corridor with local crews and direct familiarity with Collier County's building department processes.

Coverage extends through Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Marco Island, and across the Southwest Florida construction market. For complex coastal projects, proximity to the work matters.

Tampa Bay and I-75 Corridor
Tampa, FL

Tampa Bay is one of Florida's fastest-growing construction markets — Water Street Tampa, the Wesley Chapel commercial corridor, the suburban explosion in Brandon, Riverview, and east Hillsborough, and continued multifamily and mixed-use growth across the bay in Clearwater and St. Petersburg. ACG's Tampa office is active across all of it.

Local field crews mean no day trips from South Florida. Local familiarity with Hillsborough County's building department means submittals built to pass review the first time. Coverage extends through Pasco, Pinellas, Sarasota, and Manatee counties.

The Mission

One Goal: Be the Glazing Sub
GCs Call Back.

ACG's mission isn't complicated: be the glazing sub that general contractors call back for the next job. Not because of price — competitive pricing is table stakes, not a differentiator. Because of execution.

ACG measures success in three ways. Zero callbacks — meaning the work is done right the first time, the punch list is closed before the GC walk, and warranty claims are a rarity rather than a budget line. Zero missed TCOs — meaning the glazing scope never holds up a certificate of occupancy. And repeat clients — GCs who used ACG on one project and came back for the second, third, fifth, tenth.

The relationships ACG has built in Florida's commercial construction community are the result of 350+ projects where the job got done, the schedule was protected, and the GC didn't have to chase their glazing sub for a status update. That's not marketing — it's the record.

This is a business built for the long term. ACG has no interest in winning projects by undercutting and then making it up on change orders. The business model only works if GCs want to work with ACG again — and that only happens if the execution warrants it.
