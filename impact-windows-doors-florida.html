HVHZ Requirements

Florida Impact Standards
Are the Most Demanding
in the Country.

Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone — covering Miami-Dade and Broward counties, with portions of other coastal counties — imposes the most stringent impact glazing requirements in the United States. Large missile impact testing (9-pound 2x4 at 50 fps), pressure cycling requirements, and water infiltration testing all must be passed before a product gets a Notice of Acceptance.

Beyond HVHZ, Florida's entire coastal construction environment demands wind-rated glazing. FBC Section 1609 wind design requirements apply statewide, with specific exposure categories that increase requirements for buildings near the coast.

ACG works exclusively with products that have valid Florida Product Approval listings. We don't install products that are 'probably rated' or 'similar to the tested product.' Every window and door we install has a current approval listing that matches the project conditions.

Slimpact

Slimpact Low-Profile Impact Systems

Minimal sight-line impact systems for modern architectural aesthetics.

Slimpact systems deliver the narrow sight lines of standard aluminum framing with the impact performance Florida's coastal markets require. The slim profile allows architects to specify impact protection without the heavy framing that traditional impact products require. ACG installs Slimpact systems with full HVHZ compliance and Miami-Dade NOA documentation.
