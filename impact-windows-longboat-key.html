The Market

Longboat Key is a commercial glazing market.

Longboat Key is a 12-mile barrier island spanning Manatee and Sarasota Counties. The market is one of Florida's most affluent, with ultra-luxury condos, resort hospitality (Longboat Key Club), St. Armands Circle retail, and Class A multifamily commanding the highest per-square-foot pricing on Florida's Gulf Coast. Hurricane Milton (October 2024) brought direct impact to the island, and Hurricane Ian (2022) preceded it. Every commercial structure must comply with Florida Building Code Exposure D design pressures.

Longboat Key's commercial market is a specialty within a specialty. Projects require premium aesthetics (specified aluminum finishes, large architectural openings, minimal sightlines), Exposure D performance (DP60-80+ with laminated SGP or PVB interlayers for direct Gulf exposure), and experienced commercial subcontractors — the residential shops that handle the island's single-family market are not configured to deliver on commercial specifications.

ACG's flagship partnerships with ESWindows (commercial storefront, window wall, SGD-2020 impact sliding doors) and Euro-Wall (folding and multi-slide systems) fit Longboat Key's market profile exactly. The Euro-Wall system on Atlantic Fields is the same premium moving-wall product we install on Longboat Key hospitality amenity spaces, restaurant patios, and beach-facing residential amenity areas. ESWindows 9000-series window wall at DP80+ handles the design pressures on Gulf-facing elevations.

Longboat Key FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Does Longboat Key require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Longboat Key spans both Manatee County (Town of Longboat Key north) and Sarasota County (south). Both sit in Florida's wind-borne debris region and Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutters on every commercial opening. The Town of Longboat Key's permitting standards are among the most rigorous in the state.

What design pressures apply to Longboat Key commercial projects?

The entire island is Exposure Category D with direct Gulf exposure. Expect DP60 to DP80+ design pressures depending on building height and location. Select taller or more-exposed buildings may require higher ratings. Florida Product Approval documentation must explicitly cover Exposure D conditions.
