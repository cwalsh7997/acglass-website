The Cincinnati commercial market

Cincinnati's commercial market is anchored by Procter & Gamble's downtown headquarters, the University of Cincinnati and Cincinnati Children's medical campus, and the rapidly expanding Over-the-Rhine adaptive-reuse district. Glazing demand skews institutional and Class-A office curtainwall.
