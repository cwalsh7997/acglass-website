Doral Market

Miami-Dade's Industrial Backbone,
Built to HVHZ Standard.

Doral is the commercial and industrial backbone of Miami-Dade County. The NW 87th Ave and Doral Blvd corridor is home to Amazon fulfillment operations, Burger King's global headquarters, and hundreds of corporate distribution centers. CityPlace Doral has added a dense mixed-use layer on top of the industrial base. The Palmetto (SR-826) and Florida's Turnpike carry the construction traffic to prove it.

Every glazing project in Doral falls within Miami-Dade County's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. That means every storefront, curtainwall, and impact window system must carry a valid Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) and be installed by a contractor who understands HVHZ submittal and inspection requirements from the inside out.

ACG's West Palm Beach office serves Doral and the broader Miami-Dade industrial market. From tilt-wall distribution centers to Class A office campuses at Trump National Doral to the high-bay industrial storefronts that define the Airport West submarket — ACG has the HVHZ certifications, the manufacturer relationships, and the commercial-only experience to deliver these scopes correctly.

Common Questions

Doral Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG carry HVHZ certification for Doral commercial glazing projects? +

Yes. ACG is HVHZ-certified and installs NOA-approved glazing systems throughout Miami-Dade County, including Doral. Every system we specify carries the appropriate Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance and is engineered for the County's stringent wind load requirements. HVHZ compliance is built into our standard process — not an add-on.

What NOA-approved glazing systems does ACG install in Doral? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, and Slimpact systems — all with Miami-Dade NOA approval. System selection is driven by the project specification, structural engineering requirements, and the GC's schedule — not convenience or stocking preferences.

Which ACG office serves Doral and the NW 87th Ave corridor? +

ACG's West Palm Beach office serves Doral and the broader Miami-Dade market. We're routinely active on projects along the Palmetto (SR-826), the Turnpike corridor, and the NW 87th Ave industrial spine. We have established local supply relationships in Miami-Dade and the office proximity keeps response times tight.

Does ACG have experience with Doral's large-scale industrial storefront projects? +

Yes. Doral's tilt-wall distribution centers, flex industrial buildings, and corporate campus developments are among the project types ACG handles most frequently. High-bay storefronts, oversized aluminum framing, and large-lite impact systems are standard for us — not edge-case scopes. We understand the GC expectations in this market.

How quickly can ACG turn around a glazing scope for a Doral project? +

Send us your drawings and we return a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, NOA references, and a complete Division 08 scope breakdown — within 48 hours. Our estimating process is built for commercial contractor timelines. No vague allowances, no hidden exclusions.
