Louisiana Market

Louisiana's Unique
Glazing Demands.

Louisiana is one of the most technically demanding states for commercial glazing in the country. The entire Gulf Coast region requires hurricane impact systems equivalent to Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone — driven by the same Category 4 and Category 5 storm exposure that shaped Florida's most stringent building codes. ACG brings proven HVHZ expertise to every Louisiana project.

New Orleans' French Quarter and Central Business District are active commercial glazing markets. The Warehouse District's ongoing redevelopment into mixed-use commercial space, the Mid-City corridor's retail and medical build-out, and the Metairie and Kenner suburban commercial corridors all generate consistent glazing demand. Historic New Orleans also requires sensitivity to the city's architectural fabric — ACG has experience matching storefront systems to preservation requirements.

Baton Rouge is Louisiana's second major market — anchored by one of the most significant petrochemical industrial corridors in the world. ExxonMobil, Shell, BASF, and dozens of supporting industrial businesses have massive facilities along the Mississippi River corridor. The corporate offices, control buildings, administrative facilities, and supporting commercial infrastructure adjacent to these operations generate sustained glazing demand. Louisiana State University's campus expansion and the downtown Baton Rouge revitalization add institutional and mixed-use scopes.

Lafayette is Louisiana's oil and gas services capital. The Camellia Boulevard and Ambassador Caffery commercial corridors are growing rapidly as energy services companies, healthcare providers, and retail developers expand in Acadiana. ACG has completed a project in Lafayette — StayApt Suites Lafayette, a full glazing package delivered for Rycon Construction — proving our ability to coordinate and deliver in Louisiana's Acadiana region.

Lake Charles has seen dramatic post-hurricane reconstruction and industrial expansion. The LNG export terminal construction has generated enormous commercial and industrial glazing demand in the region. Shreveport and Monroe serve as commercial centers for North Louisiana, with healthcare, government, and mixed-use construction activity separate from the coastal storm exposure requirements.

ACG's Florida coastal expertise translates directly to Louisiana Gulf Coast projects. Our teams have years of experience specifying, submitting, and installing hurricane impact glazing systems that meet the most demanding wind zone requirements — from Florida's HVHZ to Louisiana's equivalent coastal exposure categories.

Louisiana Glazing FAQ.

Has ACG completed commercial glazing projects in Louisiana? +

Yes. ACG has completed commercial glazing projects in Louisiana, including StayApt Suites Lafayette — a full glazing package delivered for Rycon Construction in Lafayette, LA. This project demonstrates ACG's proven ability to coordinate and deliver complete glazing scopes in Louisiana's Gulf Coast construction market.

What are Louisiana's Gulf Coast hurricane glazing requirements? +

Louisiana's Gulf Coast requires hurricane impact glazing systems equivalent to Florida's High Velocity Hurricane Zone. New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Lafayette, and Lake Charles all require impact-rated or tested-and-labeled window and door assemblies with engineer-of-record documentation. ACG brings deep Florida HVHZ expertise to Louisiana's identical requirements.

How does ACG coordinate Louisiana projects from Florida? +

ACG's AI-managed operations platform enables full project coordination from our Florida offices. Estimating, submittal management, procurement, and field scheduling are coordinated remotely — tied to your actual construction sequence. We have proven this model with the StayApt Suites Lafayette project and follow our GC partners wherever they build.

What glazing systems does ACG install on Louisiana projects? +

ACG installs ESWindows, CGI Windows, YKK AP, Kawneer, Eurowall, TGP fire-rated glass, and Allegion entrance hardware. All systems are specified to Louisiana's wind zone and hurricane exposure requirements, with engineer-of-record coordination for AHJ plan review.

How quickly can ACG turn around a scope for a Louisiana project? +

Send us your drawings and ACG returns a detailed scope — system recommendations, quantities, and competitive pricing — within 48 hours. Our AI-managed estimating platform handles Gulf Coast wind zone specifications efficiently so your Louisiana bid package stays on schedule.
