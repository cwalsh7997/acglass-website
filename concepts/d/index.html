— The Position

Glass is not a commodity.
It's a trust contract.

For five years, American Commercial Glass has installed the exterior envelope on Florida's most demanding commercial projects — hospitality, country clubs, multifamily, healthcare, and the buildings where the glass is the point.

Speed and reliability are not slogans. A glazing sub that loses a week on submittals costs the GC a month on schedule. A glazing sub that mis-orders a Euro-Wall configuration costs the owner a season. The work happens before the silicone bead — in the prequalification, the NOA cross-reference, the field-measure tolerance check — and that is where we win.

ESWindows. Euro-Wall. PGT. Allegion. TGP. Slimpact. Six approved manufacturer partnerships. Three Florida offices. Nashville Q3 2026. FL CGC #1531993.
