Glass is not a commodity. It's a trust contract.

For five years, American Commercial Glass has installed the exterior envelope on Florida's most demanding commercial projects — hospitality, country clubs, multifamily, healthcare, and the buildings where the glass is the point. A glazing sub that loses a week on submittals costs the GC a month on schedule. A glazing sub that mis-orders a Euro-Wall configuration costs the owner a season.

"Speed and reliability are not slogans. They are the work itself — the prequalification, the NOA cross-reference, the field-measure tolerance check, the human PM signing every scope letter before it leaves." — Connor Walsh, President · May 2026

That is where we win. Not on price, not on filler — on the operational discipline of being unambiguously prepared, every time, on every project. Seven approved manufacturer partnerships. Three Florida offices. One named PM per job. Submittal through closeout.

The next decade of Florida commercial construction will demand more from glazing subcontractors, not less. Energy code tightening. HVHZ requirements expanding statewide. Manufacturer lead times stretching. The firms that prepare now — with disciplined operations, certified field crews, and direct manufacturer relationships — are the ones GCs and owners will keep awarding work to.
