Systems That Last.

A failing automatic door on a high-traffic commercial entrance is not a minor punch list item — it's a liability. ACG installs Allegion automatic entrance systems with the mechanical quality and installation precision that commercial building owners demand.

Allegion Systems

Allegion-Powered Entrances.
ADA Compliant. Every Time.

ACG is an authorized Allegion installation contractor. Allegion makes the Assa Abloy, Schlage, and Von Duprin hardware brands — plus the LCN door closers and SARGENT commercial locks that specify on virtually every commercial project.

Automatic sliding entrances, swing door operators, and revolving door systems are all in ACG's scope. We coordinate the structural rough-in, the electrical rough-in, and the final installation in sequence with your project's schedule.

ADA compliance is non-negotiable on commercial entrances. ACG's installations meet all ADA requirements — approach clearances, door width, operating force, and timing — with documentation available for your inspector and building official.
