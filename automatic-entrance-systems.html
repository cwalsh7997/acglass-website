Entrances that keep working.

A failing automatic door on a high-traffic commercial entrance isn’t a punch-list item — it’s a liability. ACG installs automatic sliding, swing, and all-glass entrance systems with Allegion operators and hardware, on the same subcontract as the glazing around them.

One sub owns the whole opening.

ACG installs Allegion automatic entrance operators and door hardware — the LCN closers, Von Duprin exit devices, and Schlage locks that show up on virtually every commercial hardware schedule — together with the storefront, all-glass entrance, or vestibule glazing that surrounds them.

Automatic sliding entrances, low-energy and full-energy swing operators, and all-glass automatic entrances are all in scope. We coordinate the structural rough-in, the electrical rough-in with your MEP contractor, and the final installation in sequence with your project schedule — so the door, the frame, the glass, and the operator are one subcontract instead of three.

ADA compliance is non-negotiable on commercial entrances. ACG installations meet approach clearances, clear-width, operating-force, and timing requirements — with documentation ready for your inspector and building official. General notes · Accessibility

ADA compliant. Every time.

Approach clearances

Maneuvering clearance at the pull and push sides is set by the door type and approach direction — it gets designed in, not discovered at inspection.

Clear width

Accessible openings require a compliant clear width with the door open 90 degrees — a real constraint on frame and hardware selection that we account for at shop-drawing stage.

Force & timing

Low-energy operators are governed by opening speed, hold-open time, and closing force limits. ACG commissions each operator to spec and documents the settings.

Inspector-ready paperwork

Operator data, sensor layouts, and compliance documentation are delivered with the closeout package — available for your building official on request.
