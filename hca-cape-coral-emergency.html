HCA Cape Coral Emergency · Cape Coral, FL
The project

Critical-care glazing, delivered on schedule.

HCA Florida Cape Coral Emergency is a freestanding emergency room (FSER) — a full-service emergency facility developed by HCA Healthcare as part of their Florida Fawcett Hospital system in Cape Coral, FL.

Healthcare construction is its own discipline: hard deadlines tied to certificate of occupancy, infection control requirements during construction, and a building that needs to perform to the highest standards on day one. ACG was brought in by PHM Group to deliver the complete glazing package — and the facility opened on schedule.

ACG supplied and installed the full package using PGT aluminum framing with Vitro architectural glass — the commercial storefront systems at the main patient entrance, the ambulance bay glazing, fixed window units throughout the building facade, and all secondary glazed openings.
