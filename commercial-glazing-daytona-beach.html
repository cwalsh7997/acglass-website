Daytona Beach Market

More Than the Speedway —
A Growing Commercial Market.

Daytona Beach's identity has always been bigger than motorsports — and the commercial construction market reflects a city diversifying into healthcare, hospitality, retail entertainment, and industrial sectors. AdventHealth Daytona Beach and Halifax Medical Center's expansion program represent multi-phase healthcare construction investments that demand fire-rated glass assemblies, curtainwall coordination with healthcare MEP systems, and submittal packages that satisfy Volusia County's building department.

One Daytona — the mixed-use entertainment and retail development anchored directly across from Daytona International Speedway — has redefined commercial glazing expectations in the Daytona market. The LPGA International commercial corridor and the area surrounding Daytona Beach International Airport generate consistent industrial and office construction. Significant hospitality construction along the beachfront corridor runs through every development cycle.

Ormond Beach and Port Orange commercial growth continues to fill in the Volusia County suburban commercial market — medical offices, retail centers, and mixed-use development that requires a real commercial glazing sub rather than a residential glass shop that occasionally installs storefronts. DeLand and Deltona add inland Volusia County commercial volume to the overall market.

Daytona Beach's Atlantic coastal exposure is the defining factor for glazing system specification. Properties within coastal setback zones require impact-rated systems with specific Florida product approvals. The entire Volusia County market requires glazing systems specified to the Florida Building Code's wind load requirements — and submitted with complete product approval documentation to pass plan review. ACG's team handles this every day.

Daytona Beach Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG serve Daytona Beach and Volusia County commercial construction? +

Yes. ACG serves the Daytona Beach and Volusia County commercial construction market across all major sectors — healthcare, hospitality, retail, industrial, and multifamily. What are Daytona Beach's glazing wind requirements for commercial projects? +

Daytona Beach's Atlantic coastal location creates significant wind loading requirements for commercial glazing. Volusia County and the City of Daytona Beach require glazing systems with Florida product approvals and, in coastal areas, systems tested to stringent wind pressure ratings. Beachside and A1A corridor properties have the most demanding requirements. ACG specifies systems that meet the Florida Building Code's wind load requirements for each specific project location, and submits complete product approval documentation to pass plan review the first time.

What types of commercial glazing projects does ACG handle in Daytona Beach? +

ACG handles the full commercial glazing scope in Daytona Beach — healthcare (AdventHealth Daytona Beach, Halifax Medical expansion), hospitality (beachfront hotel and resort construction), retail and entertainment (One Daytona district), industrial and airport-adjacent commercial, and multifamily residential. From a simple retail storefront on Williamson Boulevard to a multi-story curtainwall system on a beachside hotel, ACG handles it all as a single sub.

What glazing systems does ACG install in Volusia County? +

ACG installs ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, and Allegion entrance hardware. For Daytona Beach's coastal and beachside properties, ACG specifies impact-rated systems with the appropriate Florida product approvals and wind resistance ratings. System recommendations are driven by the project spec, the location's wind zone designation, and the architect's design intent — not by what's most convenient to stock.
