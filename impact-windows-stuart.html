The Market

Stuart is a commercial glazing market.

Stuart is the Martin County seat and the commercial anchor of the central Treasure Coast. The historic downtown Stuart district along the St. Lucie River hosts boutique retail and restaurants. Hutchinson Island, Sewall's Point, and Jensen Beach form the barrier-island luxury corridor with hospitality, Class A condo, and retail commercial. Hobe Sound to the south is home to ultra-luxury communities including Atlantic Fields.

Martin County sits in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Stuart downtown and inland Palm City are Exposure C; Hutchinson Island, Sewall's Point, and St. Lucie Inlet areas are Exposure D. Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance is mandatory. Post-Hurricane Ian (2022) and Hurricane Milton (2024), the Treasure Coast rebuild and hardening cycle has intensified commercial impact glazing demand.

ACG serves Stuart from our West Palm Beach headquarters (45 miles south) with full commercial subcontractor infrastructure. Our ESWindows flagship partnership provides Exposure C and D-capable commercial glazing. Our reference portfolio in the immediate area includes Atlantic Fields Performance Center (Hobe Sound, Proctor Construction) and Baron Shoppes of Tradition (Port St. Lucie, ESWindows retail).

Stuart FAQ

Common questions - answered.

Does Stuart require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Stuart and Martin County sit in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening.

What exposure category applies to Stuart commercial projects?

Inland Stuart, Palm City, Martin Downs are Exposure C. Hutchinson Island, Sewall's Point, and St. Lucie Inlet are Exposure D. Florida Product Approval documentation must confirm the correct exposure. DP40-DP60 inland; DP60-DP80+ barrier island.
