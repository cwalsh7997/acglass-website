All-Glass Entrances in Palmetto Bay, FL.

Palmetto Bay all-glass entrance work. Frameless and minimally framed all-glass entrance systems serve as the architectural moment at retail, hospitality, restaurant, office lobby, and institutional building entries in Palmetto Bay. ACG installs all-glass entrances with the hardware (top and bottom pivots, side patches, automatic openers) coordinated as a single-source assembly with the glass make-up, the operator, and the floor and head condition. The result: an entrance that operates daily for years without alignment issues or hardware service calls.

System options in Palmetto Bay. Common configurations: single or double frameless pivot doors, herculite-style entrances with header and floor patches, automatic sliding doors with full-glass side panels, and pivot doors with sensor-operated openers. In HVHZ Palmetto Bay, impact-rated configurations are required and ACG sources Miami-Dade NOA-approved hardware and glass make-ups.

ACG provides material + labor + submittal documentation + operator coordination in the all-in number. Lead time is 6-10 weeks from approved shop drawings, with 1-2 days of field installation.

Standards & Documentation

Hardware, glass, and operator coordination.

Hardware sources. ACG specifies all-glass entrance hardware from Allegion (Dorma, FSB), CRL, and TGP depending on the project. Pivots are bottom-mounted floor closers or top-pivot offset depending on the floor condition and operating cycle. For high-traffic Palmetto Bay retail or restaurant entries we specify floor closers rated for daily operation cycles in the 200,000+ range.

Glass make-up. In HVHZ Palmetto Bay, all-glass entrance glass make-ups must be Miami-Dade NOA-approved laminated configurations meeting the impact requirements for the project exposure. ACG specifies the appropriate make-up and provides Florida Product Approval (FL#) or NOA documentation as required.

Operator and access control. ACG coordinates with the GC electrical and access control contractors on automatic operator installation, sensor placement, and access control integration. We pull operator specs from the manufacturer and verify power and control runs in pre-construction so the entrance commissions on first try.
