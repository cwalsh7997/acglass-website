Medley Business Park

A multi-bay light industrial and commercial building at 7265 NW 74 St in Medley, FL. ACG supplied and installed the complete ESWindows storefront and window package for Goya Builders — every tenant bay, one consistent run.

Location Medley, FL GC Goya Builders Systems ESWindows storefront Completed 2024

Sheet A-011 · The project

One storefront run. Zero variation.

Medley Business Park is a clean, well-designed multi-tenant facility in Medley's industrial corridor — built to house multiple tenants across a consistent storefront run. Dark-framed ESWindows systems carry across every bay, giving the building a unified commercial identity from the street.

ACG was brought in by Goya Builders to supply and install the complete glass and glazing package — from the entry doors at each tenant bay through to the upper window units on the facade. Each bay received ESWindows dark-framed commercial glazed entry doors, fixed side lites, and upper window units: consistent profiles, consistent alignment, consistent finish throughout the full run.

With multiple identical bays, maintaining uniformity across the run is where the work lives. Every door plumb. Every sill level. Every frame gap tight. The street-view photo at bays 5 and 6 tells the story — clean lines, no variation, exactly what a GC wants to hand over at TCO. On schedule, in spec, zero punch-list drama.
