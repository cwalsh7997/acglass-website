Southwest Florida's commercial construction market has a character that differs meaningfully from South Florida's urban core. Naples and Collier County are defined by high-net-worth residential development, private club compounds, luxury retail on 5th Avenue South and at Mercato, and medical facilities supporting one of the most affluent and elderly populations in the country. The commercial glazing specification in this market tends to run to the premium end of the product range: owners and architects specify ESWindows by product series, not by price bracket, and they expect submittals and installation to match the specification without substitution.

Lee County — Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, and Estero — represents a different commercial construction profile. Multi-tenant retail, medical office campuses, hospitality along the Gulf Coast, and the active post-Ian rebuild are all driving commercial glazing demand in Lee County. Hurricane Ian made a direct landfall on Lee County in September 2022 as a Category 4 storm. The post-Ian inspection environment and the experience of a direct major hurricane landfall have made impact glazing compliance a non-negotiable in Lee County's rebuild and new construction market. ESWindows Florida Product Approval documentation is complete, auditable, and current.

ACG has delivered commercial glazing projects across the Southwest Florida market including Wild Blue Clubhouse and Illumina Fort Myers. These projects reflect the range of commercial sectors in the Collier-Lee market: from private club amenity builds to commercial mixed-use development. The Atlantic Fields Performance Center project further demonstrates ACG's reach in the Southwest Florida institutional and athletic facility market. In each case, ESWindows systems were specified because they deliver the product performance and approval documentation that Southwest Florida commercial construction requires.

The ESWindows SGD-2020 impact sliding glass door is particularly well-matched to the Naples and Marco Island market. Post-Ian Southwest Florida Context

Hurricane Ian changed the market. ESWindows documentation is complete.

Hurricane Ian's Category 4 landfall on Lee County in September 2022 was the costliest hurricane in Florida history in terms of insured losses. The damage to commercial and residential glazing systems was extensive, and the post-Ian rebuild triggered a significant increase in regulatory scrutiny over impact glazing compliance in Lee County. Fort Myers Beach, Bonita Springs, and the entire Lee County coastal zone entered a rebuild cycle that continues through 2026 and into 2027, with impact compliance standards enforced more rigorously than the pre-Ian baseline.

The post-Ian environment has made Florida Product Approval (FL#) documentation and proper installation credentials essential requirements rather than formalities. General contractors in Lee County now face owner and lender requirements for detailed glazing product approval documentation that was sometimes treated casually in the pre-Ian market. ACG's ESWindows submittal packages — complete FL# documentation, installation instructions tied to the approval, and field verification logs — are designed to meet these requirements without the back-and-forth that delays certificates of occupancy.

Collier County's Pelican Bay, Old Naples, and Marco Island markets have not been directly impacted by a major hurricane in recent decades, but they are aware of the exposure. The high-specification owner and design community in Naples tends to specify products by product approval number, not by brand recognition. ESWindows' complete Florida Product Approval and Miami-Dade NOA inventory across the product line gives ACG the documentation basis to satisfy even the most exacting specifications in the Southwest Florida luxury market.

Naples FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Is ACG an authorized ESWindows installer in Naples?

Yes. ACG holds flagship partner status with ESWindows and has a Naples office serving all of Collier and Lee Counties. The full ESWindows commercial product line is available for Southwest Florida projects with complete Florida Product Approval documentation.

Does Southwest Florida require impact-rated glazing for commercial buildings?

Yes. Collier and Lee Counties are in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Florida Building Code requires impact-rated glazing or approved protection on all commercial openings. Post-Hurricane Ian enforcement in Lee County has tightened significantly since 2022. Gulf-facing Exposure D sites require higher design pressure ratings than inland locations.

What ESWindows products does ACG install in Naples?

ACG installs the ESWindows 8000-series storefront (DP30-DP70, factory pre-glazed available), 9000-series window wall (DP50-DP80+), and SGD-2020 impact sliding glass door (DP +90/-150 psf, FL22267). All carry Florida Product Approval for Collier and Lee County submittals.

About ESWindows

ESWindows in Southwest Florida — manufacturer context for Collier and Lee work.

ESWindows is headquartered in Colombia and operates the largest commercial glazing testing facility in Latin America. For Southwest Florida general contractors and developers, that testing depth translates into a complete Florida Product Approval inventory for the commercial product line. The FL# documentation, test reports, and installation instructions for ESWindows 8000-series, 9000-series, and SGD-2020 are complete, current, and organized for building department submission. In the post-Hurricane Ian Lee County market, where building officials have heightened scrutiny on impact glazing documentation, a complete and auditable product approval package is not optional — it is the baseline for a timely permit.

The Naples and Southwest Florida market has a set of product specification expectations that differs from the volume commercial markets in Tampa or Miami. Owners and architects in the Pelican Bay, Old Naples, and Marco Island luxury segment specify by product series and product approval number. A commercial glazing sub who responds to that specification with a substitution — offering a different manufacturer or a lower-rated product series at a reduced price — is not a viable option for this market tier. ACG's flagship ESWindows partnership means the products the architect specified are the products ACG installs, with the factory documentation to confirm it. There is no substitution discussion because there is no substitution.

The ESWindows SGD-2020's performance characteristics are particularly relevant to the Naples and Marco Island market. Gulf-facing amenity openings on Marco Island carry design pressures that standard sliding door products in the DP50-DP60 range cannot satisfy. The SGD-2020's DP +90/-150 psf rating with LMI+SMI certification provides the performance required for the direct Gulf exposure that characterizes Marco Island's oceanfront commercial development. For private clubs and luxury hospitality along the Pelican Bay and Gulf Shore corridors, the SGD-2020 also provides the aesthetic presence that matches the project environment — it is not a contractor-grade product that happens to carry a product approval number.
