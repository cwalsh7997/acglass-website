Replacement Services

When Glass Needs to Be Replaced

Commercial glass fails for many reasons — impact damage, seal failure in insulated units, vandalism, storm damage, code upgrades, or simple aging. ACG provides commercial glass replacement services for storefront, windows, doors, and curtainwall across Florida.

Our replacement services cover single-lite emergency repairs through full-building reglazing projects. ACG carries the manufacturer relationships and product knowledge to source replacement glass quickly, including impact-rated products for HVHZ locations.

Minimizing Downtime and Exposure

A broken storefront or failed window is a security risk, energy loss, and professional liability. ACG provides rapid response for commercial glass emergencies — board-up, temporary protection, and permanent replacement coordinated quickly.

For planned replacement projects — building-wide IGU replacement, impact glazing upgrades, or storefront modernization — ACG provides detailed scoping, phased installation plans, and minimal disruption to building operations.
