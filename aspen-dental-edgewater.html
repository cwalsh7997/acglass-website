A new multi-tenant retail plaza anchored by Aspen Dental in Edgewater, FL — the complete ESWindows storefront and window package, delivered for MEC Contractors on a tight schedule.

The project

One facade, every tenant configuration.

A mix of healthcare and retail tenants required different storefront configurations across the same building facade. ACG supplied and installed the complete ESWindows commercial glazing package: storefront entrance systems for each tenant bay, fixed commercial windows set into the brick masonry facade, and the full-height storefront glazing on the anchor tenant unit.
