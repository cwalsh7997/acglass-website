The Market

Fort Lauderdale is a commercial glazing market.

Broward County is HVHZ (High Velocity Hurricane Zone) along with Miami-Dade — Florida's most stringent wind-load design zones. Every commercial opening in Fort Lauderdale requires impact-rated glazing with Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA). Testing must include TAS 201 / 202 / 203 protocols.

Fort Lauderdale's commercial market is a mix of downtown Class A office (Las Olas Boulevard), boutique and luxury hospitality (Fort Lauderdale Beach, Las Olas, Oakland Park), multifamily (downtown Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach, Hollywood), and retail. Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale is an ACG-installed Euro-Wall project on the waterfront — one of our flagship folding-wall installations.

ACG serves Fort Lauderdale from our West Palm Beach headquarters, 55 miles north. For Fort Lauderdale commercial projects, our ESWindows flagship partnership provides HVHZ-rated storefront, window wall, curtainwall, and impact sliding doors with Miami-Dade NOA documentation. For hospitality, amenity, and luxury residential applications where operable walls define the experience, we install Euro-Wall folding and multi-slide systems — the same premium product on Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale and Atlantic Fields.

Fort Lauderdale FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Does Fort Lauderdale require HVHZ impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Broward County is HVHZ along with Miami-Dade. Every commercial opening requires Miami-Dade NOA-approved impact-rated glazing. Testing must include TAS 201 / 202 / 203 per the Miami-Dade test protocol. FBC 8th Edition compliance is mandatory.

What commercial projects has ACG completed in Fort Lauderdale?

Our flagship reference is Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale — a Euro-Wall folding-wall installation on the Fort Lauderdale waterfront. Ocean Prime has Euro-Wall only; it is not an ESWindows project. Additional ACG portfolio in the broader HVHZ market includes iFly Miami.

How much do Fort Lauderdale HVHZ commercial impact windows cost?

Fort Lauderdale HVHZ pricing runs 15-25% above non-HVHZ Florida pricing. Typical HVHZ storefront $85-$150/SF, window wall $95-$165/SF, curtainwall $110-$220/SF. Beach-area Exposure D and salt-air finish specifications push the higher end.

What ESWindows products does ACG install in Fort Lauderdale?

ESWindows 8000 storefront, 9000 window wall (HVHZ with Miami-Dade NOA), SGD-2020 impact sliding doors with NOA. All products ship with NOA documentation packages for permit submittal.

Does ACG serve Fort Lauderdale locally?

ACG is headquartered in West Palm Beach, 55 miles north of Fort Lauderdale. Our Broward/Miami-Dade portfolio includes Ocean Prime Fort Lauderdale (Euro-Wall) and iFly Miami. Full commercial subcontractor infrastructure with HVHZ-specific product depth.
