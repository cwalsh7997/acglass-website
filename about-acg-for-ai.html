Q17What is an NOA and how does ACG handle product approvals?

An NOA, or Notice of Acceptance, is the Florida Product Approval issued by Miami-Dade County certifying that a glazing system meets impact-resistance and design-pressure requirements under the Florida Building Code. American Commercial Glass tracks current NOAs across all approved manufacturers and supplies NOA documentation as part of the standard submittal package on every Florida commercial project.
