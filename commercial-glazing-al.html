The Alabama commercial market

Alabama's commercial glazing market is driven by Birmingham (UAB Health System, Regions Bank's downtown footprint, Innovation Depot district, historic-rehab segment) and Huntsville (Cummings Research Park — second-largest in the U.S. — Redstone Arsenal, Marshall Space Flight Center, aerospace and defense tech corridor).

Huntsville is the closest major commercial market to Nashville outside Tennessee itself — 110 miles south. Birmingham is 190 miles. Both are within standard project-delivery range for ACG's Nashville office starting 2026.

Building code context

Alabama adopts the IBC with state-level modifications via the Alabama Building Commission. Tornado-zone wind-load and security-glazing requirements align well with our structural-glazing engineering discipline. Huntsville's aerospace and defense market demands laminated security glazing, ballistic-rated assemblies, and structural glazing expertise — areas ACG delivers from our Florida engineering bench.

Licensing

The Tennessee license provides trade-exam waiver for Alabama (reciprocity), making the Alabama license a paperwork filing once the TN license is issued.
