Building code context

Alabama adopts the IBC with state-level modifications via the Alabama Building Commission. Tornado-zone wind-load and impact-resistance requirements align closely with Florida HVHZ engineering. Huntsville's aerospace and defense market demands security glazing, ballistic-rated assemblies, and structural glazing expertise — all areas ACG delivers in Florida today.

Licensing

Alabama requires state-level general contractor licensing for commercial projects $50,000+, administered by the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors. The Tennessee license provides trade-exam waiver for Alabama (reciprocity), making the Alabama license a paperwork filing once the TN license is issued.
