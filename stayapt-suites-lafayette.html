Sheet A-011 · The project

Same precision. Different state line.

stayAPT Suites is a growing national extended-stay hotel brand built for guests who need a place that functions like an apartment, not a hotel room. The Lafayette, Louisiana location is a four-story, full-service property with a distinctive exterior: dark brick base, blue and white panel accents, and a multi-story curtainwall entry tower that defines the brand's street presence.

This project represents ACG operating outside Florida — a national GC, a national hospitality brand, and a glazing scope that required the same precision and coordination ACG delivers on every project regardless of geography. ACG executed the complete package: the multi-story curtainwall system on the main entry tower — the signature architectural feature visible from the parking lot and the road — the commercial storefront at the lobby entry, and the ESWindows window units on all four floors across both wings.

The twilight photo captures what a well-executed curtainwall looks like when the building is occupied and lit from within: every mullion aligned, every glass unit seated correctly, the lobby glowing behind the glass exactly as the architect intended. Rycon Construction brought ACG to Lafayette because they needed a glazing sub they could trust — ACG delivered.
