Step 2 of 6

How tall is the building?

Height drives both the recommended system and the install cost factor (high-rise needs unitized, cranes, and sequencing).

Low-rise — 1 to 2 stories
Standard storefront installations. Boom lift or ladder access. No height premium.
Mid-rise — 3 to 7 stories
Window wall or stick-built curtainwall. Swing stages and hoisting add to the scope.
High-rise — 8+ stories
Unitized curtainwall standard. Cranes, mast climbers, and sequencing add to the scope.

Step 4 of 6

Where is the project?

Florida HVHZ + wind speed varies wildly by county. Picking the right region applies the right code framing and cost factor.

SE FL HVHZ
Miami-Dade, Broward. Strictest impact glazing. Miami-Dade NOA + TAS 201/202/203. 175+ mph design wind.
Florida Keys (HVHZ)
Monroe County. 190 mph design wind — the most stringent zone in the state. Miami-Dade NOA required.
Palm Beach + Treasure Coast
Palm Beach, Martin, St. Lucie. HVHZ-adjacent — impact rated glazing standard via broader-state product approvals.
SW Florida (coastal)
Collier, Lee, Charlotte, Sarasota coastal. Wind-borne-debris region. 165-170 mph design wind.
Tampa Bay (coastal)
Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco. Wind-borne-debris region, non-HVHZ. 140-150 mph design wind.
Central FL (inland)
Orlando metro, Polk, inland Sarasota. 130-140 mph design wind. Not in WBD region.
North FL / Jacksonville
Duval, Nassau, Clay, Alachua. 130-140 mph design wind. Outside HVHZ.
FL Panhandle (coastal)
Bay, Escambia, Walton. Wind-borne-debris region, 140-160 mph design wind.
Nashville, TN opens Q3 2026
Tennessee. No HVHZ, freeze-thaw climate. IBC + IECC Climate Zone 4A. ACG Nashville office opens Q3.
Step 5 of 6

Special requirements?

Pick any that apply. Each affects the system specification and scope.

Impact / hurricane rated
Required by code in HVHZ. Laminated impact glass and reinforced framing add to the scope.
Stringent energy / SHGC
FBC 8th Ed. Tighter SHGC, U-value, high-perf coatings.
Sound rated (STC)
Laminated, asymmetric IGUs. Common in hospitality, healthcare.
Fire-rated assemblies
TGP-type ceramic, 20 to 180 min ratings. Adds cost on the affected portion.
Blast resistant
GSA / DoD spec. Heavier framing, anchor engineering.
Bird-friendly glazing
Acid-etch or frit pattern. Common in healthcare, government, LEED projects.
