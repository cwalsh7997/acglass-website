Sheet G-301 · General notes

How we work as a government sub.

Pre-bid

We read Division 08 end-to-end, flag AT/FP references, verify TAA sourcing for the specified manufacturers, and confirm design pressures against manufacturer-listed configurations before we quote. If a specified assembly can't meet the requirement, we surface it at the RFI stage — not at submittal.

Submittals

Complete packages assembled to the spec's Division 01 requirements: product data, shop drawings, engineering calcs where required, NOA / FL Product Approval documentation, sealed structural anchorage, and NFRC data where applicable.

Field

Site-specific safety plan and Activity Hazard Analyses at mobilization on federal sites. OSHA 30 supervision. Daily production reports, weekly certified payroll where required, monthly pay applications on AIA G702/G703 or agency forms.

Closeout

Warranty package, O&M manuals, as-built shop drawings, and manufacturer punch-list closeout delivered within the spec-required window — assembled as milestones complete, not scraped together at the end.

Frequently asked

Questions contracting teams ask.

Does ACG hold a Florida general contractor license?

Yes. ACG holds Florida Certified General Contractor license CGC #1531993, which qualifies the company to contract directly on state and municipal construction projects statewide.

What bonding does ACG carry?

ACG is bonded for $3M single / $6M aggregate through Arch Insurance. Performance and payment bonds are furnished on any project that requires them.

Are ACG's glazing manufacturers TAA-compliant?

Yes on both primary lines. Euro-Wall folding and sliding door systems are U.S.-manufactured. ESWindows / Tecnoglass is manufactured in Colombia — a designated country under the Trade Agreements Act (19 U.S.C. § 2511) — with a U.S. finishing and staging facility in Medley, Florida.

Can ACG perform on Davis-Bacon projects?

Yes. Certified payroll workflow is in place. Prevailing wage determinations are pulled at bid stage and priced into the labor estimate.

What AT/FP glazing standards does ACG price to?

Per project spec. Common references are UFC 4-010-01 (DoD Minimum AT Standards for Buildings), ASTM F1642 (test method), ASTM F2248 (equivalent 3-second design loading), and ASTM F2912 (specification). Interlayer selection, standoff distance, and frame anchorage all follow from the project engineer's blast-load calculation. See the AT/FP technical reference.

Where is ACG registered for federal contracting?

SAM.gov registration is in process, along with the associated Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) and CAGE code. Current status is furnished to contracting officers and prime contractors on request.

Has ACG completed fire station and essential-facility projects?

Yes. ACG's verified public-sector past performance includes the Haines City Public Safety Complex & EOC (25,443 SF, GC Pirtle Construction, completed 2025, glass by Aldora), the Cudjoe Key fire station for Monroe County in the Florida Keys, and the Martin County Fire Training facility in Stuart. Fire stations and EOCs are essential-facility (Risk Category IV) buildings under ASCE 7 and the Florida Building Code — higher wind-load design pressures and impact-rated envelope on the perimeter.

Is ACG a woman-owned business?

Woman-owned · Rielly Walsh, CEO and 51% majority owner.

What geographies does ACG cover?

Florida statewide. FL CGC #1531993.
