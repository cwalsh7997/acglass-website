Tradewinds Clubhouse - private club glazing package, Hobe Sound, FL

The Tradewinds Clubhouse is a private club facility in Hobe Sound, Martin County, Florida - a member-focused building whose glazing connects indoor club spaces to the surrounding landscape and natural Florida environment. American Commercial Glass installed the commercial glazing package - impact-rated storefront, curtainwall, and specialty glazing elements consistent with the club's architectural program. Martin County is a Florida WBDR coastal jurisdiction, and all exterior glazing required Florida Product Approval documentation. The Hobe Sound area has become one of Florida's most active private club construction markets, and ACG has multiple completed projects in the community.

Scope narrative

Hobe Sound, on Florida's Treasure Coast in Martin County, has emerged as one of the most active markets for luxury private club development in Florida - with multiple Discovery Land Company properties, the recently completed Atlantic Fields, and other private communities under development in the area. The Tradewinds Clubhouse represents the private club building type in this Hobe Sound market context: a member facility designed to provide a refined indoor-outdoor connection to the natural Florida landscape, with glazing playing a central role in creating that connection.

American Commercial Glass installed the commercial glazing package at the Tradewinds Clubhouse - covering the primary member-facing elevations of the club building. The scope included impact-rated storefront systems at the building's public rooms and dining spaces, specialty glazing elements at view corridors and outdoor terrace transitions, and entry systems at the clubhouse's primary member arrival experience.

Martin County WBDR requirements applied to all exterior glazing at the Tradewinds Clubhouse. ACG prepared Florida Product Approval submittal documentation for all system types - confirming ASTM E1886/E1996 impact compliance at the calculated design pressures for Hobe Sound's coastal exposure - and submitted the complete package to Martin County's building department as part of the permit process.

Private club glazing in the Hobe Sound market has a consistent set of design and technical expectations: high-quality anodized or painted aluminum frames, neutral low-E glass that preserves landscape view quality, wide glazing panels that maximize the view from interior club spaces, and glazed door systems that create an architecturally smooth indoor-outdoor transition. ACG's Hobe Sound project experience - including Atlantic Fields - informs its approach to this local market's design expectations.

Technical highlights

Technical highlights of the Tradewinds Clubhouse glazing scope:

  • View-quality glass: Neutral low-E glass make-up - preserving the natural light quality and view clarity that define the indoor-outdoor connection at a Florida private club in a wooded natural setting.
  • Impact-rated throughout: Florida Product Approval-documented impact systems at all exterior glazed openings, rated to the calculated Martin County WBDR design pressures.
  • Entry systems: Glazed entry door systems at the clubhouse arrival experience - impact-rated hardware with an architectural aluminum finish consistent with the club's design standard.
  • Outdoor transition glazing: Storefront or sliding door systems at terrace transitions - allowing the club to open to outdoor dining or lounge conditions when weather permits, while providing a closed impact-rated envelope during storms.
  • Florida Product Approval: FPA per ASTM E1886/E1996 for all system types at the Hobe Sound Martin County coastal exposure design pressures.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Hobe Sound, FL?

Hobe Sound is a community in Martin County on Florida's Treasure Coast, between Stuart to the north and Tequesta/Jupiter to the south. The area includes Jonathan Dickinson State Park and is known for its natural Florida character. Martin County is a Florida WBDR coastal jurisdiction.

What private club glazing projects has ACG completed in Hobe Sound?

ACG has completed multiple private club glazing projects in the Hobe Sound area, including the Atlantic Fields development (four structures) and the Tradewinds Clubhouse. Hobe Sound is one of ACG's active markets for private club glazing, reflecting the area's continued luxury club development.

What is Martin County's impact glazing requirement?

Martin County is a Florida WBDR coastal jurisdiction. All exterior commercial glazing in Martin County requires Florida Product Approval documentation per ASTM E1886/E1996, with design pressure analysis confirming FPA coverage for the building's exposure category and height. Martin County is not HVHZ - HVHZ applies only to Miami-Dade and Broward counties.

What glazing systems are typical for Florida private club buildings?

Florida private club buildings typically use impact-rated aluminum storefront for dining and lounge spaces, curtainwall for large main-room facades, sliding or folding door systems for terrace transitions, and low-E insulating glass for thermal comfort and Florida Energy Code compliance. All systems require Florida Product Approval for WBDR coastal counties.

Does ACG specialize in private club glazing?

American Commercial Glass has extensive experience with private club and golf community glazing in Florida, including projects in Martin County (Atlantic Fields, Tradewinds), Lee County (Wild Blue), and other Florida markets. Private club glazing is a core segment of ACG's Florida commercial portfolio.
