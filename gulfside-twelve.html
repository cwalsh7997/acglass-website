The Project

Gulfside Twelve is a five-story luxury beachfront condominium on Fort Myers Beach — twelve units directly on the Gulf of Mexico, built by New Age Development as a premium coastal residential product on Lee County's most demanding stretch of coastline.

This is HVHZ territory. Every window and door on this building faces the open Gulf and must perform to the highest impact resistance ratings Florida code requires. There is no margin for error on a beachfront tower that will face direct hurricane exposure for its entire life. ACG specified and installed PGT impact systems from ground floor to penthouse.

The Scope

ACG supplied and installed the complete PGT impact window and door package across all five floors — the large-format fixed windows on each unit facing the Gulf, the impact-rated sliding glass doors opening to the private balconies, and the storefront glazing at the ground-floor lobby and amenity level.

The blue awnings frame every opening. Every floor, every unit, the same PGT profiles — clean dark aluminum frames, high-performance glass, and the impact resistance to stand up to whatever the Gulf sends. The building is built for this coast. The glazing is too.

The Context

Fort Myers Beach was devastated by Hurricane Ian in 2022. New Age Development built Gulfside Twelve as part of the recovery — a premium product designed to prove that Fort Myers Beach can build back stronger. ACG's PGT package on this building isn't just a glazing spec. It's a statement about what resilient coastal construction looks like.
