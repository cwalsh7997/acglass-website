ESWindows 8000-series storefront is the most commonly specified ESWindows product in the Tampa market. Tampa FAQ

Common questions — answered.

Is ACG an authorized ESWindows installer in Tampa?

Yes. ACG holds flagship partner status with ESWindows and has a Tampa office serving Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco Counties. The Tampa team installs the full ESWindows commercial product line including the 8000-series storefront, 9000-series window wall, and SGD-2020 impact sliding doors.

Does Tampa require impact-rated glazing for commercial buildings?

Yes. Tampa Bay falls within Florida's wind-borne debris region. The Florida Building Code 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved protection systems on commercial openings. Gulf-front and Tampa Bay-front sites in Exposure Category D require higher design pressure ratings than inland locations.

Is Tampa in the HVHZ?

No. HVHZ only covers Miami-Dade and Broward Counties. Tampa requires Florida Product Approval (FL#) and ASTM E1886/E1996 impact testing compliance, but not Miami-Dade NOA. This means ESWindows standard Florida Product Approval variants — without the HVHZ premium — are the correct specification for Tampa projects.

About ESWindows

ESWindows in Tampa — manufacturer context for Gulf Coast work.

ESWindows is headquartered in Colombia and operates the largest commercial glazing testing facility in Latin America. For Tampa Bay general contractors, that testing scale translates into a complete and current Florida Product Approval inventory across the 8000-series, 9000-series, and SGD-2020 product families. Every FL# on an ESWindows product is current, the test reports are available, and the installation instructions are the same documents referenced in the product approval. When a Tampa building inspector asks for documentation, the ACG submittal package provides it without the scramble that accompanies manufacturers with incomplete or outdated approval records.

Tampa Bay's post-Hurricane Ian and post-Hurricane Milton environment has sharpened GC and owner awareness of what impact glazing compliance actually requires. Hurricane Ian (2022) and Hurricane Milton (2024) both produced significant glazing failures across the Gulf Coast, and the reconstruction process has reinforced the distinction between products that are truly impact-rated and products that carry nominal certifications. ESWindows 8000-series and 9000-series are engineered for the Florida commercial market's actual design pressures, not for the minimum threshold that allows a product approval number to be printed on a data sheet. The factory pre-glazed 8000-series eliminates one of the most common sources of field glazing failure: improper setting block placement and insufficient glazing compound contact in hot, humid Florida field conditions.

The water and air infiltration performance of a commercial storefront system is determined as much by the installation as by the product approval. ACG's Tampa crews are trained on the ESWindows 8000-series installation requirements specifically — not on a generic storefront installation protocol that approximates the ESWindows detail. The sill flashing, perimeter caulking, and anchor spacing for an ESWindows 8000-series HVHZ storefront are specified in the product approval, and ACG follows those specifications in the field. For Tampa GCs whose owners have experienced glazing leaks on previous projects, that installation discipline is the difference between a five-year-warranty product and a five-year callback problem.
