The Market

Port St. Lucie is a commercial glazing market.

Port St. Lucie has been among the fastest-growing cities in the United States for multiple consecutive years, with population growth driving extensive commercial construction across Tradition, Torino, and St. Lucie West master-planned communities. Commercial construction ranges from Tradition Town Center mixed-use to Mets Stadium-area hospitality to Class A multifamily across the St. Lucie River frontage.

Port St. Lucie and St. Lucie County sit in Florida's wind-borne debris region. Most of the city is Exposure Category C, with limited Exposure D along the Indian River and oceanfront. Florida Building Code 8th Edition compliance is mandatory on every commercial permit. Tradition and Torino new-construction especially has been active, with ESWindows factory pre-glazed storefront shortening retail tenant-improvement schedules.

ACG's reference portfolio in Port St. Lucie is direct: Baron Shoppes of Tradition, a multi-tenant retail development with full ESWindows commercial storefront. We serve Port St. Lucie from our West Palm Beach headquarters (35 miles south) with the full commercial subcontractor infrastructure PSL GCs and developers expect.

Port St. Lucie FAQ

Common questions - answered.

Does Port St. Lucie require impact windows on commercial buildings?

Yes. Port St. Lucie and St. Lucie County sit in Florida's wind-borne debris region. FBC 8th Edition requires impact-rated glazing or approved shutter systems on every commercial opening.

What exposure applies to Port St. Lucie?

Most of Port St. Lucie is Exposure C, with limited Exposure D along Indian River and oceanfront. DP40-DP60 is typical. Florida Product Approval must confirm the correct exposure.

Has ACG completed projects in Port St. Lucie?

Yes. Our flagship PSL reference is Baron Shoppes of Tradition - a multi-tenant retail development with full ESWindows commercial storefront. The same pre-glazed approach works for additional PSL retail, multifamily, and office projects.

Does ACG serve Port St. Lucie locally?

Yes. ACG is headquartered in West Palm Beach (35 miles south of PSL). Our Treasure Coast portfolio includes Baron Shoppes, Atlantic Fields Performance Center, and Tradewinds Hobe Sound. PSL is a core ACG market.
