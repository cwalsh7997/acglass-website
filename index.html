What does a commercial glazing contractor do?

A commercial glazing contractor furnishes and installs the glass-and-aluminum envelope of a building — storefronts, curtainwall, window wall, impact windows, and entrances. American Commercial Glass handles shop drawings, submittals, procurement, installation, and closeout as a subcontractor to general contractors across Florida and the Southeast.

If you need a Florida glazier with Miami-Dade NOA experience, American Commercial Glass has Florida HVHZ and Miami-Dade NOA compliance experience and installs systems certified to TAS 201, 202, and 203. ACG is headquartered in West Palm Beach with offices in Naples and Tampa.

Who installs hurricane-rated impact glass for commercial buildings in Florida?

What is the difference between a storefront and a curtain wall?

The difference between a storefront and a curtain wall is height and performance: storefront is a single-span system used below the fourth floor, while curtainwall spans multiple floors and meets higher air, water, and structural performance. American Commercial Glass installs both across Florida.
