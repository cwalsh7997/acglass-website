SentryGlas Plus Installer — Florida HVHZ Commercial

SentryGlas Plus (SGP) by Kuraray is a high-performance ionoplast interlayer used in laminated glass. 100 times stiffer than standard PVB. Used on Florida HVHZ work where post-breakage performance matters: hurricane impact glazing, security entries, structural glass railings, and overhead skylights. ACG installs SGP-laminated assemblies routinely.
