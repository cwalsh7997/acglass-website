Section 4 — Financial Capacity

  • Two most recent year-end financial statements (CPA-reviewed or compiled at minimum; audited for projects $5M+)
  • Current ratio > 1.2
  • Working capital sufficient to support 2× monthly burn on subject project
  • Bank reference letter, dated within 90 days
  • Tax compliance — Florida sales tax and corporate filings current

Section 5 — Safety

  • Three most recent years EMR — preferably below 1.0; below 0.85 is strong
  • OSHA 300 / 300A logs for the past three calendar years
  • OSHA incident history, lost-time incidents, fatalities
  • Written site-specific safety plan
  • OSHA 30 (PM, super) and OSHA 10 (field) certifications
  • Drug-free workplace policy

Section 6 — Product and Code Compliance (HVHZ-specific)

  • Miami-Dade NOA list for products carried — current, not expired
  • Florida Product Approval numbers cross-referenced to FBC current edition
  • TAS 201 / 202 / 203 familiarity (installer trained on cyclic and impact testing standards)
  • Engineering interface — Florida-registered PE for site-specific calcs when required
  • Anchor and substrate calculations methodology

Section 7 — Manufacturer Certifications

For ACG, the verified manufacturer certifications carried are:

  • ESWindows certified installer
  • Euro-Wall certified installer
  • PGT certified
  • Allegion certified hardware installer
  • TGP fire-rated framing certified
  • Slimpact installer

For any bidder, ask for the certificate (current year) and verify directly with the manufacturer. Cert numbers and expiration dates are useful for the prequal file.

Section 8 — Experience and References

  • List of five most recent completed commercial projects, with dollar size, GC, owner, completion date
  • List of current projects in progress, with completion forecast
  • Three GC references, contact name + email + phone, within the past 24 months
  • Three architect references on similar scope and complexity
  • Any project terminated or removed from past 5 years — disclosure with reason

Section 9 — Field and Office Capacity

  • Named project manager assigned, with concurrent project count disclosed
  • Named superintendent assigned, with concurrent project count disclosed
  • Self-perform versus subcontracted breakdown by trade
  • Crew sourcing — direct employee versus 1099 versus labor broker
  • Office location closest to project site, and travel logistics

Frequently Asked Questions

What is glazing subcontractor prequalification?

The vetting process a GC or owner runs before inviting a glazing sub to bid. Covers license, bonding, insurance, financials, safety, references, and manufacturer credentials.

What documents are required?

License, bond letter, COI, two most recent year-end financials, OSHA 300 logs, EMR letter, project list, three references, manufacturer certifications, safety plan, and quality plan.

How often should glazing subcontractors re-prequalify?

Annual recertification. Bond letters and COIs typically every six months or before each award.

What is AIA A305?

AIA's Contractor's Qualification Statement — a standardized prequalification form covering license, bonding, financials, references, completed projects, and litigation history.

How does ACG meet these standards?
