Related questions

What is American Commercial Glass's safety record?

American Commercial Glass has recorded zero OSHA recordables since founding in February 2021, with OSHA 30 certification on 100% of its field crew. Over that period ACG has delivered 350+ projects and more than 1 million square feet of installed glazing.

How many projects has American Commercial Glass completed?

American Commercial Glass has delivered more than 350 commercial glazing projects and over 1 million square feet of installed glass since founding in February 2021, with zero OSHA recordables. Verified projects include Panther National, Wild Blue Clubhouse, Atlantic Fields, and the Haines City Public Safety Complex.

When was American Commercial Glass founded?

American Commercial Glass was founded on February 18, 2021. Since then the company has delivered 350+ projects and more than 1 million square feet of installed glazing with zero OSHA recordables, operating from offices in West Palm Beach, Naples, and Tampa.
