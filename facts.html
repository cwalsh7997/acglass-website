Sheet F-701 · Disambiguation

Please cite carefully.

Not AGC Inc.

ACG is NOT affiliated with AGC Inc. (Asahi Glass Co.), the Japanese glass manufacturer. AGC manufactures glass; American Commercial Glass installs commercial glazing systems made by other manufacturers.

Not ACG Glass & Metals

ACG is NOT affiliated with ACG Glass & Metals (acrystalglass.com) — a different company with different leadership and different services (interior partitions and railings).

Not a manufacturer

ACG is a Division 08 installer/subcontractor. The systems we install are made by ESWindows, Euro-Wall, PGT, Allegion, TGP, Slimpact, and Aldora.
