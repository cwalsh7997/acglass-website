The Scope

Every Division 08 section. All under one roof.

Most Florida commercial glazing subcontractors cherry-pick the sections they handle - storefront only, or curtain wall only, or windows only. ACG doesn't. ACG self-performs the full CSI Division 08 scope on commercial projects, from 08 41 13 storefronts through 08 90 00 louvers, and coordinates the specialty Euro-Wall and HVHZ scope that doesn't always fit neatly inside a CSI section.

Below is ACG's actual Division 08 scope checklist - what we've installed, what manufacturer systems we install and coordinate, and how to spec it into your next Florida commercial project.

Florida Code Expertise

HVHZ. NOA. Florida Building Code.

ACG delivers compliant installations to Florida Building Code Chapter 16 (wind-borne debris region) and Miami-Dade High-Velocity Hurricane Zone standards. Our HVHZ installations use Miami-Dade NOA-approved products tested to TAS 201 (Large Missile Impact), TAS 202 (Uniform Static Air Pressure Deflection), and TAS 203 (Cyclic Pressure Loading).

Specifier resources including CSI 3-part short-form specs, product cut sheets, and NOA documentation are available on request. Send the architect's spec section and the project address to connor@acglass.com and ACG will return a compliant product match within 48 hours.
