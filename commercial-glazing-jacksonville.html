North Florida Market

Florida's Largest City.
One of Its Fastest-Growing
Construction Markets.

Jacksonville is Florida's largest city by land area and one of the state's most diverse commercial construction markets. Downtown redevelopment is accelerating — mixed-use towers along the St. Johns River, the ongoing Shipyards waterfront project, and new office and hospitality builds are reshaping what was for decades an underbuilt core. The JaxPort expansion is driving industrial and logistics construction along the north corridor.

Healthcare construction is massive and sustained. Mayo Clinic's Jacksonville campus continues to expand. Baptist Health and UF Health are each running multi-phase construction programs spread across the metro. Distribution centers are stacking up along I-95 as the logistics sector treats Jacksonville's port access, land cost, and labor market as a long-term advantage.

St. Johns County — particularly Nocatee and the Ponte Vedra Beach commercial corridor — is experiencing some of the fastest commercial growth in Northeast Florida. ACG operates as a statewide glazing contractor with coordination that allows us to mobilize for North Florida projects without the execution gaps that plague out-of-state subs working an unfamiliar market.

Common Questions

Jacksonville Glazing FAQ.

Does ACG do commercial glazing in Jacksonville? +

Yes. ACG serves Jacksonville and the broader Northeast Florida market as part of our statewide glazing operation. With statewide scheduling infrastructure, we mobilize efficiently for Duval County projects without the coordination gaps that come with out-of-state subs unfamiliar with Florida Building Code and local permitting processes.

What systems does ACG install for Jacksonville commercial projects? +

ACG installs the full commercial glazing scope — impact windows and doors, aluminum storefront systems, window wall, curtainwall, fire-rated glass assemblies, automatic entrances, and interior glazing. Systems include ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall, TGP fire-rated glass, Slimpact, Allegion entrance hardware, and Aldora. One sub. Every opening.

What are the wind code requirements for commercial glazing in Jacksonville? +

Jacksonville's Atlantic coast location puts it under Florida Building Code Section 1609 wind requirements with meaningful exposure demands. Requirements vary by specific project location, building height, and occupancy classification. ACG specifies systems engineered to your project's exact exposure conditions and submits packages built to pass Duval County plan review without revisions.

Does ACG handle submittals and product approvals for Duval County? +

Yes. ACG manages the full submittal package — shop drawings, Florida product approvals, NOA documentation, and engineer-of-record coordination. We know how Florida building departments work. Our submittal packages are complete enough to pass Duval County plan review without revision cycles that delay your schedule.

What areas near Jacksonville does ACG serve? +

ACG serves the full Northeast Florida region: Duval County (Downtown Jacksonville, Riverside, Southside, Mandarin, Arlington, Jacksonville Beach), St. Johns County (Ponte Vedra Beach, Nocatee, St. Augustine), Clay County (Fleming Island, Orange Park), Nassau County (Fernandina Beach, Amelia Island), and Putnam County. We also serve the Daytona Beach market along the coast.
