Where are ACG's Florida offices located? +

ACG operates three Florida offices: West Palm Beach, serving Southeast Florida and the Treasure Coast from the Palm Beaches to Miami-Dade; Naples, serving Southwest Florida from Collier through Sarasota; and Tampa, serving the Tampa Bay metro and surrounding counties. Together, these three offices give ACG local operational presence across the full Florida construction market.

Does ACG serve my city in Florida? +

ACG serves commercial construction projects throughout Florida. Our three offices collectively cover all major Florida markets: the full Southeast Florida corridor from Miami to the Treasure Coast, Southwest Florida from Naples through Sarasota, and the Tampa Bay region including Central Florida. For projects in areas like Orlando, Jacksonville, or the Panhandle, ACG deploys from the nearest office. Send your plans to connor@acglass.com and we'll confirm coverage and return a scope within 48 hours.

How do I contact the nearest ACG office? +

The fastest path is a single contact point: email connor@acglass.com with your plans or call (772) 486-7711. ACG routes all Florida inquiries centrally — you don't need to determine which office handles your area. What commercial glazing services does ACG provide across Florida? +

ACG provides the full commercial glazing scope statewide: aluminum storefront systems (ESWindows, PGT, Euro-Wall), curtainwall (Euro-Wall, ESWindows — including PE-stamped packages for high-wind applications), window wall systems for multifamily, impact windows and doors for Florida's wind-borne debris regions (CGI, Slimpact, ESWindows), fire-rated glass assemblies (TGP SuperLite), automatic entrance systems, and interior glass partitions. One sub, every opening, all of Florida.
