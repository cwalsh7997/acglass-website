Full-Scope Glazing

Every System.
One Sub.

Stop piecing together your glazing package from five different subs. ACG handles the complete scope — impact windows, storefronts, window wall, specialty systems — with one contract, one schedule, and one team that actually coordinates with your other trades.

01

Impact Windows & Doors

HVHZ-rated impact systems engineered to meet or exceed Florida Building Code and large missile impact requirements.

Full range of impact window and door configurations. Laminated glass, structural silicone, thermal breaks. Certified hardware and installation methods. Meets the nation's most stringent hurricane protection codes.

Hurricane Rated Impact Tested FBC Approved HVHZ
02

Storefront Systems

Flush-glazed and thermally broken storefronts with custom finishes and architectural precision.

Commercial-grade framing systems with thermal performance and clean aesthetics. Anodized or powder-coated aluminum in custom colors. Seamless glazing from floor to ceiling, storefront entries, and tempered or laminated glass options.

Flush Glazed Thermal Break Custom Finishes Commercial Grade
03

Window Wall Systems

Slab-to-slab high-performance glazing systems for multifamily, hospitality, and mixed-use development.

Full curtainwall and window wall assemblies. Structural glazing or unitized systems. Impact-rated options for coastal markets. Thermal performance that exceeds code. Coordination with MEP trades and structural systems.

Slab-to-Slab High Performance Impact Rated Unitized
04

Sliding & Multi-Slide Doors

Multi-track pocket and stack-glass systems for seamless indoor-outdoor connectivity.

Heavy-duty aluminum framing with precision hardware. Pocket and stack configurations up to 20+ feet wide. Impact-rated options. Floor or pocket-mount installations. Full range of finishes and glass options.

Multi-Track Pocket & Stack Impact Rated Wide Span
05

Bifold Door Systems

Full-opening aluminum bifold systems that transform interior and exterior spaces.

Lightweight aluminum framing with heavy-duty tracks and rollers. Single or double-acting panels. Impact-rated glass options. Thermal breaks for energy efficiency. Contemporary hardware and custom finishes.

Full Opening Aluminum Frames Impact Options Custom Hardware
06

Fire-Rated Glass

UL-certified fire-rated glazing systems from 20 to 120 minutes.

Transparent and opaque fire-rated glass products. Life-safety rated framing systems. Integration with fire doors and building envelope requirements. Full compliance with IBC and local codes. ACG specializes in healthcare glazing and hospitality and country club glazing where fire-rated systems are a core requirement.

20-120 Min Rated UL Certified Life Safety IBC Compliant
07

Interior Glass Systems

Glass partitions, office fronts, and demising walls for modern interiors.

Frameless and framed partition systems. Acoustic and non-acoustic options. Switchable glass and privacy film. Custom hardware and finishes. Fire-rated interior glazing available.

Glass Partitions Office Fronts Custom Hardware Acoustic Options
08

Supply & Installation

Full project delivery from shop drawings through on-site installation and commissioning.

Coordination with architects, engineers, and general contractors. Plan review and constructability assessment. Shop drawing development and approval. Field installation with quality assurance. Punch-out and final commissioning.

Plan Review Shop Drawings Full Installation Commissioning
