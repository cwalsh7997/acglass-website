Impact Windows & Doors

Florida-code-compliant hurricane impact systems for coastal and HVHZ projects. ACG installs tested, NOA-approved impact windows and doors from ESWindows and Slimpact that meet the nation's most stringent wind and impact requirements.

Impact glazing demands technical precision. Code zones vary, product approvals must align with project conditions, and installation details are inspected rigorously. ACG has the product knowledge and field expertise to get it right every time.

Automatic Entrances

Sliding, swinging, and revolving automatic entrance systems for commercial, hospitality, and healthcare applications. ACG installs Allegion systems that are ADA-compliant, code-certified, and fully integrated with access control and fire safety.

Automatic entrance coordination is critical. ACG handles the detail work proactively — reviewing power requirements, header conditions, and interface details during preconstruction to ensure installation executes smoothly.

Fire-Rated Systems

Fire-rated glazing assemblies for interior and exterior applications requiring code-mandated fire resistance. ACG installs tested, labeled assemblies from Technical Glass Products — fire-protective and fire-resistive solutions rated 20 minutes to 2 hours.
